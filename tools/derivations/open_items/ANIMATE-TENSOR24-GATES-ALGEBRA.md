## 1. The clicks' formulas (what the one engine records at a click)

- **K1, the wind.** The wind is the conserved form of Rule3 for a hand at a Node-clock (hands.wind). For each gear k, with n its notch now, b its notch before, w the teeth count, S the hold and R_j the grips:

      wind_k = X / (4 Gamma^3 G^2) [ 2 ( w (n_k^2 + b_k^2) - S n_k b_k - n_k SUM_j R_j b_jk ) - SUM_j R_j (b - b_j)_k (n + n_j)_k ]

  Here X is the time gear's notch and Gamma the full rate. On a hand of one frequency it is Planck's relation: wind = I sin w, with I = A^2 sin w / T the adiabatic invariant and T the quantum of action. The wind moves only through the six Spokes, as currents: its change at a Node-clock is the sum of the currents through its Meshes. A stopped Node-clock has no wind.
- **K2, the click of a whole.** Where a hand's wind at a Node-clock reaches one whole winding (one quantum: 2 A^2 sin w = T per gear), the carry takes it:
  - on one hand: the chime;
  - at both ends of a Mesh: the fitting, a pair laid;
  - at both ends of a Mesh into the unsprung hand: the unspringing, a pair into light.

  The click's place is the Node-clock where the winding was reached, and its tick is the dial's.
- **K3, the books at a click.** taken + the remainder before - the remainder after = booked. Every winding the click takes is written, and the excess is kept, signed, in the remainder.
- **K4, the face.** Every chime writes the hand's gears now and before. From them:
  - the sweep, sigma = three_now twelve_before - twelve_now three_before (the Wronskian of the hand's two gears); its sign is the charge's sense;
  - the beat, the proper ticks between the hand's crossings (its frequency, so its energy per proper tick);
  - the levels unwound (whole quanta).
- **K5, the momentum at a click.** dP at the take and dP at the lay; the recoil is written on the Mesh's flows.
- **K6, the click of a split.** The twin hands' parts, up and down: each below a whole winding, together at least one. The draw is by the parts' sizes, so the chance is by the wind (Born).
- **K7, the turnover.** The clock's own click: its time gear at the top or bottom of its dial mirrors every fine tooth of its Node-clock, and the ratchet counts it. A stopped Node-clock (the horizon) makes no click until its release, which is a turnover (397 of 397 on the hole 900).
- **K8, the draw's room.** The count of digits the click's draw could read (the vernier's room).

## 2. Between clicks: the one formula (TENSOR24.md section 2)

    W+ (a+ - a) - W (a - a-) = s [ SUM over the six Spokes j of g_j (U_j a_j - a) - D a + J ]

then the carry. Six consequences decide where and when the clicks of section 1 happen:

- **M1, the rewind.** The step is invertible in integers, and the turnover is an involution. Every click can be undone from its note, so no click's record is lost.
- **M2, the Mesh's antisymmetry.** The Mesh term enters its two ends with opposite signs, so the wind's currents are conserved and reach one Mesh per tick (K1's continuity).
- **M3, the hand's phase.** The formula is unchanged under z to i z, so the sweep (K4) is conserved between clicks and summed through them.
- **M4, the dispersion.** For a hand z = A exp(i (k . x - w t)):

      2 W (1 - cos w) = s [ g SUM over the axes of (2 - 2 cos k_a) + D ]

  So the rest beat w_0 that K4 reads follows from the spring D alone, and a packet's speed from dw/dk.
- **M5, the clock's rate.** A hand's beat runs by the local W and s, so a hand where the lapse is lower beats slower between its clicks (a theorem once the rate is the regulator's own gear, cut 4; on the head the rate table is typed, E8).
- **M6, the great clock's course.** On a setting alike everywhere, the A1 part of the regulator, sourced by the stress's trace, is one scalar oscillator: a discrete closed Friedmann universe. It rises, tops, makes the turnover clicks of K7, and runs back. With a black hole in the setting, K7's turnovers come locally at the stopped Node-clocks. The course's length is set by the matter's energy and the coupling; on the head the clamp F1 sets it.
