## The script and its usage line (for the Scribe, on every toys-5 commit)

    cd tools/derivations/co1 && TMPDIR=/dev/shm/tmp python conservation_check.py <world.json> <intervals> [--toy <dir>]

`--toy <dir>` names the folder of lean_full.py (default the folder `toy` beside the script; on toys-5 pass `--toy tools/derivations/toys/lean`). The script subclasses the toy's Node (`Checked(Lean)`, the one override snapshots the board before the close), steps the world for the intervals, reads every law each interval on the pre-state, the state after the step (before the close) and the state after the close, together with that interval's log lines, then runs back to the seed, then runs the 48 on the 3-cube image of the world (at most 30 intervals, as the toy's cube48_test.py does). One line per law, `LAW: held N of N` or `LAW: broken at interval k, Node (x,y,z), the numbers`, the next three breaks under it; a JSON row of readings; one JSON row per interval with clicks (the first twelve); `VERDICT`; exit code 0 only when every law holds. Running time: 19 s on the 3-cube at 20 intervals, about 2 min on the 5-cube at 40 and on the 9-cube at 60 (three runs in parallel), the 48 included.

## How each law is read (the clause, the quantity, the predicate)

1. ENERGY in the clicks (The ledger of exactness, the exact take; The conservation at the click, line by line). For every credit line (birth or body click): (a) the take re-read from the board, f(s) = [w (now^2 - (now + t s)^2) - t s (S before + L)] div W_i at the Node with the toy's own rule integers at the close's reads, equals the line's `taken`, and the light's level stands moved by `levels` toward its before (the last click's at a Node with several); (b) `levels` is the least count paying the walls (f(levels - 1) < booked); (c) taken >= booked; (d) taken == booked, the exact take, counted exact of all; (e) the born pair's two children each at exactly one whole quantum of their unit at their Node after the lay (the share's rise over the close at the frame's weight, in whole units), the book being 2 units per pair.
2. MOMENTUM (The lattice momentum). P_a = 2 num SUM_i (now_(i+a) before_i - now_i before_(i+a)) per row (the light and every plane, re and im summed) on the pre-state, on the rotate's output alone (recomputed from the pre-state with the toy's own turned arrivals), after the writes and the swap, and after the close. The law's letter: at uniform static paces the integer step moves P_a by exactly (2 num / w) SUM_i (r'_i - r_i)(now_(i+a) - now_(i-a)); the check tests that identity to the bit at every interval whose paces are uniform (one content, one factor on every Link) and whose Links carry no fold (phases at (X, 0), odd lines 0, frames 1); at varying paces or folded Links the change is the paces' gradient (the force law) and the turn's work, counted within or beyond the floors' bound num SUM_i |now_(i+a) - now_(i-a)| as a reading, no break. The write's momentum (W-S1 on the light, the held writes) and the exchange's at the turns are read apart. In the clicks: the close's change of P_a per row equals, exactly, what the credit lines book: a take of d levels at i moves P_a by 2 num d (before_(i-a) - before_(i+a)); a lay of A on re.now the same with A; a lay of s A on im.before by 2 num s A (now_(i+a) - now_(i-a)). The credits' sign current J_a = Im(conj z (z(+a) - z(-a))) and the running cosine 2 cos k_a = Re((z(+a) + z(-a)) / z) as a reduced rational are read from the credit lines' frames alone (the decoder's row to land).
3. CHARGE (The sign is the rotation sense; The charge written is the Wronskian over T div 2). W = SUM over the planes and the board of re_now im_before - im_now re_before, plain and weighted by 1 / p_i^2 as the exact rational over the lcm of the distinct p_i^2 (p_i the Link pace at the start content). The rotate's drift of the weighted W within the remainders' and the turn's roundings (every rotated level within 3 of the rational step's: the carried remainder below one, two turned-arrival roundings at most one each over six Links at R / w below 1 / 3): |dW_weighted| <= 3 SUM_i (|re_i| + |im_i|) / p_i^2. At the close: the board's W unchanged exactly; every birth's two children with W of opposite sign summing to 0 (and the senses [-1, 1]). The charge in units of T div 2 as (quotient, remainder) at the start and the end.
4. FLUX on the board (The flux form of Rule3; The conserved form; The tension). F_ij = -F_ji on every Link of the light's own current (the toy's `link_current` from the two ends). Rule3's identity at every untouched Node on every line of every row (the phase row with its flux inside the division, the planes with the toy's turned arrivals, the light, the sign's row, the volume's row with its ten lines): w (a_next - 2 a_now + a_before) + r' - r = SUM_j R_ij (m_ij - a_now) - D a_now (+ the flux), a_next the level before the write; the write's book beside it, wwall x inc + wr' - wr = the numerator recomputed from the pre-state (W_step - W_start for the light, 2 W and J_a for the sign's row, the volume's forms with the light's stepped level and the stress). The exchange at every turned Node (every line the pre-state exchanged and complemented, im conjugated). The light's linear sum L = SUM_i [w (now_i - before_i) + r_i] over the untouched Nodes moved exactly by the pairing term SUM_i SUM_j R_ij (a_j - a_i) and the write w inc alone.
5. COUNT from bang to bang (the owner's standing rule; The click writes on the lattice; The direction). The close's book exact: the state after the close equals the state before it with the logged takes (light.now moved by levels toward before, click after click at a Node) and the logged lays (A on re.now, s A on im.before at the children's Nodes) and nothing else, every line and remainder compared. The quanta in whole units (the light's board share in W_c, each record's in its unit) before and after the close with the booked and the taken beside them (a reading). The run back from the end to the seed, the board hashed at every interval, compared bit for bit.
6. THE 48 on the clicks (The cube's symmetry). The check adds the credits (interval, Node, line, taken, levels, beat, proper, sense) and the refusals (an orientation refusal as its Link with the two ends unordered, since the toy logs it from the lower end; a lay or stop refusal at its Node) through every image, on the 3-cube image of the world for at most 30 intervals. The one-Link reach forward and backward is covered by the toy's reach_test.py (both halves, forward and `step_back`); nothing added.
