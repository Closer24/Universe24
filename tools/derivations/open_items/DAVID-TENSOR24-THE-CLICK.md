## The clock and the links

1. **The clock is the dial and the scalar.** Every gear is a notch with a fine tooth that carries the remainder, and the one act turns every dial by one division: the quotient moves the notch. The clock is what the group leaves in place: the time gear, the rate, the load, the trace and every wind.
2. **The twist is clock under the 24 and not under the 48.** Every turn keeps it and every mirror reverses it, at every link (check 1).
3. **A link is two node-clocks across one spoke.** Every reading of a link is a product of its two ends:
   - the lead (now here times before there, less before here times now there), reversed when the ends are exchanged;
   - the even and the odd sums;
   - the twist (the cross of the two ends' transverse leads).
4. **Anything that travels moves one link a tick.** The next node-clock reads it as its neighbour's gear, and at -1 it is read back. A move and its return give the identity (check 8).
