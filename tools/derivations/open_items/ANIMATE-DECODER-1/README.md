## The files

- **src/tensor24/decoder/records.py,** the record reader. The file is split at the line feed alone, so U+2028, U+0085 and U+2029 stand inside a label. A line that is no JSON object is named with its cause: no JSON, nested past the interpreter's depth, or holding a whole number past its digits (the tool lifts the digits' bound); a path that cannot be opened, a NUL in it among them, is named too. The schema's rules are its guards, checked in order:
  - every line is a JSON object with its kind, a label, each key once;
  - the record holds a header and an end;
  - the header comes first, with a whole major version that is known;
  - the end comes last;
  - every event is of a known kind and stands at a whole round and site;
  - every field a line holds has its shape in the schema's record of its kind (a whole number, a label, a row of the schema's length, or a record of the schema), and a field the schema does not name holds whole numbers and labels alone through its rows and mappings, the schema's first line: a float, a truth value, NaN, null or a row of another length is seen (Sofer's G1 and G2);
  - the header's sides make a board, each side 1 or more;
  - every value stands on the record, read where the line's own record names it: an event's round from 1 to the end's rounds and its site on the board; a whole click's ends' and children's sites on the board and its ends' Spokes among the board's; a count from the first beat on the kinds whose record holds one; a whole click's q, where it holds one, at least 1, and every holding's windings from 0 (the Boss's ruling of 04:4x UTC, 6488101036: a whole click is the carry's quotient at least 1, and the wind a positive form). So an unnamed ends or children on any other line is a field of whole numbers and labels, read. The digests are the runner's, which the decoder never reads (TENSOR24.md);
  - a whole click with ends books in its two ends alone, and one that takes at one end holds no end (G3);
  - the events keep the schema's order.

  A record that breaks a rule is not read, with the cause named in ASCII (a field's name and a kind as Python's ascii() writes them, a board by its sides and never their product); nothing is raised. A field may stand absent where its writer lacks it, and a reading that needs it reads not read, the cause named. The ledger has its own guards: every line a JSON object, at a whole round and train, after the step or the clicks, with its fields of their shape, each place once.
- **src/tensor24/decoder/books.py,** row 15's two readings:
  - **the books at every whole click** (K3), read per train: a part's taken and its laid each hold one holding per train, a train's holdings on one side read as one where each starts where the last ended, and no train stands on both sides. Each part takes (its taken holds a holding), each taken train loses and each laid one gains, its windings and kept together, and over the part the windings and the kept together gain nothing; the click's own train is among those it takes from; where it holds its q, q whole windings left, over both its ends where it takes at both. A take paid from its remainder alone moves no whole winding (the head's credits do so: 2,557 on the chiral and 1,047 on the hole), so a holding is read by its windings and kept together. A whole click that lacks a field its books are read from (its own train, its ends, a part's books, a holding's train, windings and kept), or that books a train twice in a part, is named;
  - **the plan's second reading** (TENSOR24.md section 19, item 8; TENSOR24_PLAN.md section 5, item 8): the wind over every train of the header after the clicks against after the step, at every round with a click, whole or split. A round is read where its step lines and its clicks lines hold the header's trains and no other, each with its whole wind; a header that names no train, or a train card without its index, is named.

  The verdict: not read with no whole click; not shown where a part read fails, the books at a whole click read or the wind at a round read, before any gap, with the gap named beside it (David's order); else not read where a whole click's books are not read, the ledger is absent or not read at a round with a click, or the header names no train or a train card holds no index; else shown. Part C's "the change equals the booked" is each reading held against 0: a click keeps its remainder in the train it took from, so the change over every train is what it booked, 0 where its books close.
- **src/tensor24/decoder/clocks.py,** each Node-clock's ticks counted from its rate lines, read where the lines stand from its first beat with whole counts, each k a bit (the schema's Rate line under the second promise), the counts rising and no k repeated; else not read, the cause named. A Node-clock's lines are read once for all its counts, the ticks before each stretch summed once and each count found by bisection.
- **tools/tensor24_decode.py,** the decoder's tool: the usage line with no argument or more than two (exit 1 + 1), the digits' bound lifted, row 15's verdict, and the whole clicks' ticks with the first not read named, each Node-clock's lines read once. Its numbers are spelt from 0 and 1, as the files' tool spells them.
- **tests/test_the_tensor24_decoder.py,** 75 tests: each broken record and ledger not read with its own cause and read or raising without its own guard, the clicks whose books do not hold them, the wind over the header's trains, the precedence of a failure over a gap, each reading's law failing on its own mutant, the tool run as a program.
- **tests/test_the_tensor24_decoder_cases.py,** 52 tests, its budget 350 lines on Animate's word (decision 26): the twelfth review's cases. Each record read with its unnamed and absent fields; each broken one with its own cause in ASCII; a board past the digits named by its sides; a part that books a train twice, and chained holdings read as one; a whole click without its train; the open books before each gap, the gap named beside; the books at each side of their bounds; the ticks at count 0; the tool over four clicks at two Node-clocks, and its causes in one line of ASCII; the Boss's ruling on A3 and A4 (a whole click at q 0 and at q -3, a take and an end's laid holding below 0, each refused by name, and a turnover at q 0, the time gear's bit, read).
- **tools/tensor24_convert.py,** the converter (Animate's tool, outside src/tensor24): a head record in the schema 1.1. The head's proper time is written as rate lines that give its ticks exactly at each of its clicks, its books as holdings of the amounts it moved, and its winds as the ledger at "clicks" alone. The head's planes are trains 0 to P - 1, their winds, and their lines trains P to 2 P - 1, so a click moves wind between two trains (the Boss's ruling of 04:4x UTC, on Sofer's reading). A whole click's train is the train its windings left; its q is left out, the head counting its levels where its books count amounts. Its numbers are spelt from 0 and 1, as the decoder's tool spells them (Sofer's gate reads both tools under its amendment 4).
- **first_slice.py and first_slice.txt:** the three committed records (examples/records: bodies, chiral and hole) converted, read back by the package, counted against the head's own counts, read for row 15 beside the head's reader, and their whole clicks' ticks read against the head's own proper time.
- **engine_rates.py and engine_rates.txt:** the engine's own beats under random masks (the Mesh test's parted layouts), written as rate lines in a record, read back by the package at every beat.
- **mutants.py and mutants.txt:** 169 source mutants of the decoder and its tool, run against both test files: each guard dropped, each part of each rule, reading and verdict changed, the fifth review's sixteen, the seventh's eighteen and the twelfth's twenty-five among them on this text, and seven for the Boss's ruling on A3 and A4.
- **review_raises.txt** and **review_readings.txt:** the fifth review's decoder_raises.py and the seventh's decoder_readings.py, as run here on this text. The twelfth's row15_and_guards.py stays in the reader's scratch (decision 26). As run here it reads every case as the review asks: A1 and A2 not read, a train booked twice, and A1'' closing; A5 not read, the train lacking; B1 and B2 not shown, the gap named beside; C1 to C7 read; C8, C9 and C10 named; D1 to D3 exit 0, nothing on stderr, one line each. A3, A4 and A6 to A8 are the plan's (Next).

## What it reads

- **Every converted record reads under the schema's rules,** every new guard included, and its counts match the head's: whole clicks 138, 2,745 and 1,327; books 116, 54 and 71; turnovers 1,934, 861 and 1,073.
- **The books, read per train:** all 4,210 converted whole clicks are read, and every one closes (138, 2,745 and 1,327). The head's line is its own train (the Boss's ruling of 04:4x UTC): before it, the head's 2,651 credits into their own plane's line (1,904 on chiral, 747 on hole) stood as one train on both sides of the click and read not read, named "books one train twice in a part".
- **The ticks at the whole clicks,** read from the rate lines, are the head's own at every click: 138, 2,745 and 1,327 of as many.
- **Row 15 reads NOT READ on all three:** the converted ledger, the head's winds once a round at "clicks" alone, is read at 0 of 25, 54 and 47 rounds with a click.
- **The step's change, a reading of its own:** the wind over every train moved on every round with no click (94 of 94, 5 of 5 and 12 of 12).
- **The engine's beats** (engine_rates.txt): the ticks read back right at 1,349, 1,358 and 1,414 beats of as many on three seeds; a Node-clock's lines with the first dropped, and a site with no rate line, read not read with the cause named.
- **The mutants** (mutants.txt): 169, none surviving; the twelfth's twenty-five each die on the second file, and the ruling's seven each on the case written for it.
- **The ticks in one pass:** one Node-clock at half the full rate with a whole click at every round reads in 0.30 s at 4,000 rounds and 1.12 s at 16,000, where the walk per click took 7.4 s at 4,000.
