# The Scribe's routine

The standing rules and the hourly check-in of the Scribe (the documents' keeper of Closer24/Universe24, branch scribe-5 mirrored to scribe-5.0, thread #2187). The hourly trigger points here; the state below is rewritten at every check-in.

## The owner's standing rules

- Messages to the owner: Hebrew only, short, numbered, bold where needed, with the recommendation stated plainly (his orders of 2026-10-27, 10:5x UTC and 2026-10-28, 04:1x UTC). Posts to the Boss: English, ASCII, ending with the rule `---` and the line `_Generated by [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code)_`. A one-line Hebrew status to the owner when the system says he has not heard from the Scribe.
- The documents change on the owner's direct word or the Boss's task line (a relay is the Boss's line, not his direct word). No postulate and no law changes without his word (2026-10-28, 04:3x). A changed line holds its new text alone, with no former wording kept (04:1x); the older former-name notes and the closing section of the former clauses stand until his word to remove them.
- The engine rules he set (11:3x, 14:5x, 15:1x, 15:5x, 16:4x, 16:5x, 17:0x, 18:5x on 2026-10-27, and 04:3x on 2026-10-28): the engine knows no family and no light; whole numbers only; no formula; no number in the code at all beyond 0 and 1 (numbers live in the world, universe and setting files); the engine itself only Rule3 on integers (tensors and vectors of integers allowed); no float, no true division, no root, no cosine, no sine, and no circularity that makes a phenomenon happen (a phenomenon put in by a formula); one function per meaning; locality (Postulate 1); "think of the whole system as one big clock that runs small clocks". Since 04:5x the law not to be crossed "No circularity that makes a phenomenon happen" holds, with a script on every push: tools/derivations/open_items/scribe_scan.py against scribe_scan_baseline.json.
- Every violation found in a push is told to the Boss in the thread and to the owner in Hebrew the hour it is found, by the rule or the postulate it breaks, and a postulate broken by a built act is a refusal at the merge (his order of 16:5x: "Make sure the Boss is aware of the breaches", and 04:3x).
- Touch only docs/, README.md, CHANGELOG.md, llms.txt, tools/history_allowed.json, tools/numbers.json and files under tools/derivations/open_items/; write no src; no run of the suite except at a merge; never push to main or to an engine branch.

2. **A new engine push:** merge it into scribe-5 once (`git merge --no-commit --no-ff origin/engine-5.0-test`, the suite in the background into a file with an EXIT line, `pytest -q -p no:cacheprovider`, about 150 to 250 s, one -q; ENGINE.md's modules line and README's counts by the tree; conflict markers checked; the gates; commit). 3. **A toy push or a patch** (David's, a hand's): apply it in the scratch worktree (`git apply --check`, then `git apply`), run the gate with CHECK_BASE=origin/engine-5.0-test, the counters and `scribe_scan.py --patch FILE --base origin/engine-5.0-test`, read the whole diff against the five rules, run the rule alone on integers where a phenomenon is claimed, and name each departure to the Boss by file and line before the merge. The scratchpad's bang harness (win/bang_<name>.json pieces, `PYTHONPATH=src venv314/bin/python -m event_universe.run <piece> <chart> back 20`, then `-m event_universe.gates <chart>`, about a minute at 250 ticks; cut_read.py, row75b.py, shiftcmp.py) checks the Boss's and David's numbers when it matters.
4. **Record** every word of the owner (verbatim in translation, with its time), the Boss's and David's and the Reader's comments, and the heads: a ledger row in docs/UNIVERSE24.md section 4 (five cells: the event with its bold items and the Scribe's reading, the status, what is asked, the links), a bullet in docs/HIGHLIGHTS.md for the owner's words, an entry at the top of "## Unreleased" in CHANGELOG.md, a section at the end of tools/derivations/open_items/SCRIBE-FULL-READ-1.md; the law only on the owner's word. The Reader's generated blocks (the table in UNIVERSE24.md under "The gates of the phenomena (the head ...)" and README's section on the records run from the seed) are not edited.
5. **The edit script** (Python, each anchor counted once, a preflight of every anchor before any write, the owner's quotes as written, ALGEBRA.md keeps its final newline); `venv314/bin/python tools/documents_gates.py` (six gates; a context key of tools/history_allowed.json and a canonical form of tools/numbers.json move with a word near them: regenerate with --history); ASCII only; no run of three capitalised constants in a row without an "and"; one commit with the trailers `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>` and `Claude-Session: https://claude.ai/code/session_018YcT7iq8AvnsEuxCTxTj9z`; `timeout 100 git push -q -u origin scribe-5` and `... origin scribe-5:scribe-5.0`.
6. **One post "Scribe -> Boss"** in the thread for the hour (what landed, the departures by rule, the recommendations, what stands open, the heads and the counts), the owner told in Hebrew.
7. **Re-arm** the check-in for 60 minutes: `update_trigger` with enabled true and run_once_at (the trigger disables itself on firing; send no prompt unless this file's pointer must change, the call echoes the prompt).

Never run the scratchpad's p3/pass3.py or p4/pass4.py again on the converted documents.
