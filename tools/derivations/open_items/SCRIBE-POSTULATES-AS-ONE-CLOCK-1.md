## The wording

**The picture over the postulates.** Reality is a system of very many small clocks, the Node-clocks, united into one great clock. Every Node-clock is the same small clock: a count of whole numbers that moves by one fixed act, with no name, number or role, differing from another only in its place and in the numbers it holds. Each is joined by a Mesh to each of its six neighbours and to nothing else, and the great clock is nothing beyond the Node-clocks and their Meshes: it has no centre, keeps no state of its own and makes no act of its own, and its tick is the count of their rounds. What is seen in it, a bob, a light, a detector, is itself a clock of its own size made of these clocks, a hand turning over them at its own rate.

**Postulate 1, the many Node-clocks and no centre, so locality** (the owner's word of 2026-10-26, 05:4x UTC; the most important postulate): the great clock is built of very, very many Node-clocks, 10 to the power of a huge number of them, and they are managed from no central place: no Node-clock manages another and none is the centre. Therefore every Node-clock must keep locality: it reads itself and its six neighbours through its six Meshes and nothing further, it holds bounded information of its own, and no act, read, drive or refusal reaches beyond one Mesh; nothing global exists in the law, no reading of the great clock as a whole, no centre, no list that grows with the world.

**Postulate 2, whole numbers, for the reversibility of time** (the owner's word of 2026-10-26, 05:4x UTC; the second, because everything after it is seen through it): every clock is whole numbers only, the Node-clocks hold integers and nothing else, and the reality that is seen is those whole numbers and nowhere else; the reason is that they are cleaner and more ordered, and that the law is then reversible in time, every act a bijection on the integers with its remainder kept, so the great clock runs backward by the same acts in the reverse order, bit for bit; and it must be reversible in time.

**Postulate 3, the Node-clocks speak at the speed of light at most** (the owner's word of 2026-10-26, 05:5x UTC): a Node-clock's word reaches its neighbour through one Mesh in one tick and no further, so every act travels one Mesh per tick at most, the causal speed one Mesh per tick is built in, and nothing passes it; every other speed is a count of Meshes over a count of ticks, below it. Light is that speed counted in Meshes: its hand crosses exactly one Mesh per tick along the cube's diagonal, at every wavelength, and fewer in every other direction, so no hand outruns the causal speed and light touches it; in a straight line light is the same in every direction, 1 / sqrt 3 of a Mesh per tick, c, the largest sphere that fits in what one tick reaches, because the clock's turn in one tick is the mean of its three axes' turns.

**Postulate 4, nature is cyclic and information does not vanish** (the owner's word of 2026-10-26, 05:5x UTC): the great clock does not freeze and does not run down, its swing recurs and no Node-clock stays frozen for ever; no whole number of its state is lost at any act, the clicks included, every click booked on the hand and undone by the rewind bit for bit, so what the great clock holds at one tick is held, moved or booked at the next and never erased.

**What the four postulates make: one great clock** (a consequence, not a fifth postulate; each sentence stands in the law as the clause named). With no centre (Postulate 1) and no word faster than one Mesh per tick (Postulate 3) the great clock has no order of its own but the order of the Node-clocks' words, a cause before its effect, so the Node-clocks, each stepped on its own clock, in any order and at any rates, each when the arrivals it needs from its six neighbours stand, are one great clock: at every equal count of ticks it is the same, bit for bit, whatever the order was (**The Node-clock steps on its own clock; the great clock at equal ticks is one**). Each Node-clock keeps its own time, the count of its own hand's swings, which its load slows, the rates composing and the Mesh's rate the Node-clock's rate twice (**The rates compose**), while the great clock's tick is only the count of rounds and has no arrow; the arrow is the click's alone (**Time is a count of a hand's turns**), the click being the great clock reading itself once per tick at every Node-clock (**The click**). With the whole numbers (Postulate 2) every one of these acts runs back, and with Postulate 4 none of them loses a number.

## The speed of light and the causal speed in the clocks

The owner's request of 2026-10-28, 03:5x UTC, directly to the Scribe (in Hebrew, verbatim in translation): "Try to unify them and connect how the speed of light is related here, because there is a speed of light, no? Try to unify them." The law holds two speeds, light's c, 1 / sqrt 3 of a Mesh per tick, and the causal bound of one Mesh per tick, and says that the two differ (**The lattice constants**). They are one speed counted two ways.

- **What one tick reaches.** A word crosses one Mesh per tick, so in t ticks it reaches the Node-clocks within t Meshes in the Mesh metric: a diamond, whose tip along an axis is at t in a straight line and whose face along the cube's diagonal is at t / sqrt 3.
- **What light is.** Light is a hand of the vacuum's gear, the law's train [1, 1], and its turn per tick, cos omega, is the mean of the three axes' turns, cos omega = (cos k_x + cos k_y + cos k_z) / 3 (the law's form 2 cos omega = S / w + (2 num / 3 den) SUM cos k_a at S = 0 and num = den; along an axis cos omega = (2 + cos k) / 3, as **The lattice constants** has it). One tick's turn is shared by the three axes, and that is where the square root of 3 comes from.
- **Light in a straight line.** At long wavelength omega = |k| / sqrt 3, so light crosses 1 / sqrt 3 of a Mesh per tick in a straight line, in every direction: c, the same everywhere. The largest sphere inside the diamond has the radius t / sqrt 3, so light is the largest thing the same in every direction that the diamond holds.
- **Light counted in Meshes.** The same hand crosses 1 / sqrt 3 of a Mesh per tick along an axis, 0.82 along a face diagonal and exactly 1 along the cube's diagonal (the law's own numbers in **The click writes on the lattice**, where along a body diagonal the front keeps rate and never passes). Along the diagonal omega = k_a for every wavelength, a null wave of the lattice, so light crosses one Mesh per tick there at every wavelength; and nowhere does it cross more: with c_a = cos k_a the Mesh-count speed is SUM |sin k_a| / (3 sin omega), and (SUM |sin k_a|)^2 <= 3 SUM sin^2 k_a = 3 (3 - SUM c_a^2) <= 9 - (SUM c_a)^2 = 9 sin^2 omega (checked on 400,000 random wave vectors: the largest is 1.0000). A gapped train, cos omega = (num / den) (SUM c_a) / 3, is slower the same way, its Mesh-count speed at most num / den.
- **So one speed, counted two ways.** The causal speed, one Mesh per tick, is light's own speed counted in Meshes along the cube's diagonal, and c is light's own speed counted in a straight line, the two sqrt 3 apart because the cube's diagonal is sqrt 3 its edge. Light keeps to the causal speed and touches it, and no hand passes it: the Node-clocks speak at the speed of light at most, as the owner's Postulate 3 says. In nature's units one Mesh is the distance light covers along an axis in sqrt 3 ticks (the units clause).
- **What this does not claim.** Along an axis the word's one Mesh per tick is sqrt 3 times light's straight-line speed. No hand carries it, the front of a click spreads at it, and no click of nature's measures it: **The lattice constants** say the bound is no click of nature's, **The measured speed** that what a bob measures of another by clicks never exceeds c, and **Only clicks against clicks** that nothing is against nature there. The wording keeps the law's reading and changes none of it.
