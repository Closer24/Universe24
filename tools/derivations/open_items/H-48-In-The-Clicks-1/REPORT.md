## 1. The algebra (one page)

**The mirror of a riding line's division.** A riding line divides once per interval at the mean wall W = (w div 2)(X_before + X_now) (interval.py, `divisions`): P_in = W (a_now - a_before) + r - half(W), P_out = P_in + X F, (v, r') = ((P_out + half(W)) div W, (P_out + half(W)) mod W). Under a reversal of the line (a -> -a, F -> -F) two mirrors of the remainder are candidates:

- the ones' complement r* = W - 1 - r (the one rule, rule3.mirrored, the swap's): P_in* = -P_in - 1, so (-P_out - 1 + half(W)) = -(q W + s) + 2 half(W) - 1 = -q W + (W - 1 - s) at even W: the quotient -q and the remainder W - 1 - s at EVERY numerator, s = 0 included (test 1: 10,000 of 10,000 at an even wall and at an odd one);
- the two's complement r* = 2 half(W) - r (= W - r at even W): P_in* = -P_in, (-P_out + half(W)) = -q W + (2 half(W) - s): the quotient -q and the remainder 2 half(W) - s where s > 0, and at s = 0 the quotient 1 - q with the remainder 0 (the law's note; test 2: exact + the defect = 10,000 of 10,000). At an odd W the two are one.

**Which mirror the seed imposes.** Every remainder is born at half(W) (rows.lines_of, node.ride_walls). The image universe (the seed reflected) is also born at half(W). The fixed point of the ones' complement is (W - 1) div 2, an integer at odd W alone; the fixed point of the two's complement is half(W) at every W. W = (w div 2)(X_b + X_n) with w div 2 = 3 den Gamma^2 G^2 is EVEN at Gamma 1,000 (and at any even Gamma, G or den). So the reflected universe's remainders stand to the base's as the two's complement, r* = 2 half(W) - r, exactly, from the first interval on, as long as no division meets s = 0 (chance F / W per division on the line's lattice F, below 10^-9 here).
