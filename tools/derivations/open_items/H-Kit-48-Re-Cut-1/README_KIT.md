The kit's four tools run against the engine itself (src/event_universe: run.py, node.py, the log every record carries) and import no toy. The former tools (cut on David's former lean toy, lean_full.py, which toys-5 no longer holds; three hands in a row could not run them) are kept under kit/former/ for the reading of what they measured and run nowhere. Every tool: integers only, no float; ASCII; absolute paths.

The setting for every command below: `P=/home/user/Universe24/venv314/bin/python`, `W=<your worktree>`, `K=<this kit folder>`, `export PYTHONPATH=$W/src TMPDIR=/dev/shm/tmp`, every run under `nice -n 19`, one run at a time, and before any run above a minute `$P $K/wait_load.py 3` (sleeps until the 1-minute load is below 3, polled every 60 s; no shell loop). The kit's worlds (world_chiral_300.json, the chiral seed of the 5-cube at 300; world_bodies_120.json, two packets on the 7-cube at 120) read their universe files beside them (universe_toy.json, universe_middle.json, copies of tests/worlds'); the engine's own worlds are under $W/tests/worlds (world_hole.json: the hole seed at 60; world_nature.json: the chiral seed at 60; world_bodies.json) and its records under $W/examples/records (hole.json, chiral.json, bodies.json).

1. Run a record (the engine's writer): `cd /dev/shm/tmp/<id>; nice -n 19 $P -m event_universe.run <world.json> <out.json> back 20` (prints the reader's rows at the marks, the run back and the counts; the 5-cube at 60 with back 20 about 12 s; the record is the JSON: world, universe, den, cube, counts, turns, rows, nodes (a Node line per interval: X, content, turned, frozen, held, current), run_back, log (birth, credit, refused events)).
2. Read the gates: `$P -m event_universe.decoder <out.json>` (the engine's own decoder; see src/event_universe/gates.py for the table).
3. The conservation check: `nice -n 19 $P $K/conservation_check.py <record.json> [board off] [out <summary.json>]`: one line per law, "LAW: N of M" (intervals, births or clicks holding of as many), the readings as one JSON line, the verdict "laws held in full: n of 12" last, exit 0 only when every law holds in full. From the log and the frames: the run back (the form), the births' charge (the children's W summed to 0), the lattice momentum (the Node lines' Link currents summed per axis per interval; the births' dP_take + dP_lay_ends = 0; the recoil summed), the energy as the beat per proper interval (per credited Node the beats per 1000 proper as [quotient, remainder]), the count under the exact take (taken + remainder before - remainder after = booked per click, EX-S1; the log's sums against the record's counts), integers (floats found: 0). On the board (the record re-run from its own two files, 12 s for the hole 60): the current's antisymmetry F_ij + F_ji = 0 on every Link of the gap-0 line at every interval, the Node lines' current against the board's, the Wronskian summed over the board unchanged across every interval without a birth and equal to the rows' W total at the marks, the re-run's log and counts equal to the record's. Two laws are readings and not counts (the Links' momentum sum 0 on three axes: on the hole seed 0 of 60, the seed is not at rest; the beats per proper interval): read their numbers.
4. A flagged copy: `nice -n 19 $P $K/make_flagged_copy.py <id> <FLAG_NAME> <replacements.json> src $W/src [check off] [worlds a.json,b.json] [python $P]`: the copy of src written under /dev/shm/tmp/<id>/src_<id> from a JSON list of {"file": "event_universe/<file>.py", "old": "<exact text>", "new": "<exact text>"}, each refused unless its old text occurs exactly once (nothing written on a refusal); then the check that the copy with the flag empty runs the hole 60 and the chiral 60 bit for bit with the head (both run with back 20, the record hashed with the rows' milliseconds struck; the head's records cached under /dev/shm/tmp/<id>/head_<world>.json), EQUAL or APART per world, exit 0 only on every world EQUAL. About 50 s for the check: wait_load first. The flag's own path is run with `PYTHONPATH=/dev/shm/tmp/<id>/src_<id> <FLAG_NAME>=1 ...`.
5. The 48 on the clicks: `nice -n 19 $P $K/clicks_48.py <world.json> <intervals> <out.json> [cube N]`: the world run from the seed and from each of its 48 images (tests/test_the_board.py's IMAGES: the signed permutations about the origin, 24 of det +1 and 24 of det -1), the clicks of each image carried back through the image and compared with the base's (the births with the children's Nodes and senses, the credits with line, sense and beat, the takes taken / booked / levels of every click, the turned Nodes per interval): per image EQUAL (bit for bit), CONJUGATE (every sense negated, the children exchanged) or APART, with the first interval apart and the first apart per part; the summary's counts by verdict and by det. The 3-cube at 60 (cube 3) runs the 49 boards in about 4 minutes, the 5-cube at 60 about 10 minutes: wait_load first. The engine's own 48 test (tests/test_the_board.py, the hole seed at 20 on the 5-cube, the rows and the births alone) is the reference for the rows; this tool reads the clicks.
6. The reach: `nice -n 19 $P $K/reach.py <world.json> <intervals before the change> <x,y,z> <levels added> <out.json> [cube N]`: a world run N intervals, the board copied, one Node's gap-0 level changed on the copy, both run one interval; the births and the refusals of that interval compared Link by Link by the least torus distance of the Link's ends from the changed Node, and every line slot (now, before, rem, wr) that differs counted by distance: the step reaches one Link (nothing differs at distance 2 or more), the lays the Link ends and the packets' width.
7. Integers and the barred words: `grep -n "float\|\b[0-9]\+\.[0-9]\+\b"` over your scripts before the hand-back, and a grep for each barred word of your brief (the words the brief lists; the kit does not spell them).
8. The controls that make an error small: (a) the flag off bit for bit (rule 4's check); (b) the prediction written BEFORE the run (the number expected, then the measured); (c) the conservations in the clicks on every run (rule 3); (d) the same seed for the item and its control; (e) a disagreement with a standing comparison is a measurement to run, never an opinion.
