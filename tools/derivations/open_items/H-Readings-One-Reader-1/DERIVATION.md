## 1. The algebra (one page)

**Step 0, the unification found (the four roads).** The letter holds the Link's readings in four modules (links.py, readings.py, metric.py, core/rule3.link_factor) as fourteen functions, and the row's readings in three (gram.py, metric.py, links.wronskian) as seventeen. Both fold.

*(1) The algebra.* Every reading of a Link at a Port is a function of ONE record of the two ends, read the same from both ends up to the side's sign: the even sum e(a) = a_i + a_j, the odd sum o(a) = side (a_i + a_j) (the other end reads -o), the frame parity f = 1 - 2 odd(e(b)) of the frame bits, and the one cross F(x, y) = x_i y_j - y_i x_j (the other end reads -F). The factor is Q(rounded(e(X_a), 2)) read at the wall Gamma and 0 where either end is frozen (e of the frozen mask); the frame is f; the Link's count is n = K_i - f K_j and its phase pair the orbit at n; the odd part is o(A_a); the fold is the tangent triple of 3 K o(V_a) over Gamma^2; the current of a line is F(now, before); the gradient along an axis is the + Port's arrival less the - Port's; the net current is F at the - Port less F at the + Port; the twist is F of the two ends' net currents on the other two axes; the flux is u.G.(gradient of N). One function, `read_link(node, port, level, frozen, bits, count, odd, flux, rotation, coupling)`, returns the Link's record; the direction is the side's sign on o and on F (the Port's own), and the coupling is the exponent on f and the factor on the count and the odd part (0: the identity by arithmetic, as the engine writes it).

*(2) The matrix.* Every reading of a record row is ONE bilinear x.M.y line by line, M one of the shape's matrices: the Gram G, the charge's GJ, the density's G Gamma_a, the conjugation K: the charge is B.GJ.N; the current on an axis is dN_a.GJ.N with the neighbours read in the Node's frame (K where f = -1); the density is N.G Gamma_a.N; the stress on the axes (a, b) is d_a.G.d_b (the diagonal at a = b, the shear at b = a + 1); the flux is u.G.d_a N; the share's four own parts and the six Link parts are the same bilinear at the lines' own and the neighbours' arguments; the period's ratio is N.G.B over N.G.N; the term is SUM_a coefficient Phi_a (Gamma_a x). The row's gram is NOT a reading of the Link's record: the Link's record reads one line at two ends, the gram reads the lines of one row at one end; the two readers stay two, the row reader taking the Link reader's gradient as its one Link read.

*(3) The cube.* The readers are covariant under the 48 as the engine's are (the same arithmetic); the cross is the one bilinear odd under the exchange of the ends and of the readings; the cyclic order (a, a + 1, a - 1) enters the readers twice as it enters the engine (the twist, the curl: the Hand-In pair's finding, one cross act owed in core/ports.py).

*(4) Rule3.* The readers move no state; the Node adds what they return (NL-S1). Nothing in them branches on a row's kind: the shape picks the matrices, the coupling picks the exponent.

**What the letter holds twice (measured, section 5):** the phases at the coupling 1 (interval.py:123 and :130); the identity Link at the coupling 0 built by arithmetic every interval (interval.py:126-134) and its identity fold (interval.py:149-150); the curl and the term at every interval with no quartet row (interval.py:135, :155-156); gram.share at one Node re-spelt in merger.share_at (merger.py:58-95); the log's entry spelt in eight places.
