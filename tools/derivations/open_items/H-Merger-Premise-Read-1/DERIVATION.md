- read at the interval's START (the state the previous close left): close.meet's current, twist and

  (bodies.credit, bodies.py:147, `gram.charge(row.shape, row.lines)`).

So the premise of a meeting is read before the step and the price of that meeting is paid after it.

## 1. The algebra (one page)

**The current across one Link.** F_ij = SUM over the record's lines of (now_i before_j - before_i now_j)
(links.link_current, links.row_current), and close.meet reads den F (close.py:86).

**A born pair at the lay.** GP-S1 lays the two children by the one lay act (merger.child_at): at the
- end (re_now, re_before, im_now, im_before) = (-A, 0, 0, B), at the + end (A, 0, 0, B), and
close.refuse has already refused the lay where the plane stood at either end, so those four integers
are the whole state. Then
  F_re = (-A)(0) - (0)(A) = 0,  F_im = (0)(B) - (B)(0) = 0,  F = 0 exactly,
and W = re_now im_before - im_now re_before gives -A B at the - end and +A B at the + end, so
W_minus W_plus = -(A B)^2 < 0. Both of close.meet's rest conditions hold for every born pair, at every
A and B and every phase: this is the moving-pair theorem of mb1 and mb2, read at the lay.

**The same pair after one step of Rule3.** The step is one carried division per line at each Node
(interval.division): v = (now - before) + g, g = (rem + X Force) div ws, ws the mean wall times the
row's scale, rem the line's own remainder, the division's carry half(ws) (core/rule3.division_forward).
The step writes now' = now + v, before' = now. At the two ends of a born pair now - before is -A on the
re line at the - end and +A at the + end (antisymmetric) and 0 - B on the im line at both ends
(symmetric), so
  re: now'_minus = -2A + g_minus, before'_minus = -A ; now'_plus = 2A + g_plus, before'_plus = A
  im: now'_minus = -B + g'_minus, before'_minus = 0 ; now'_plus = -B + g'_plus, before'_plus = 0
and the stepped current is, exactly,
  **F' = A (g_minus + g_plus)**,  the im line contributing 0 (both before slots are 0),
so close.meet's reading of it is **den A (g_minus + g_plus)**.

**The cross term's two parts.** g = (rem + X Force) div ws has a momentum part and a force part. The
momentum part is the (now - before) already taken out and is exactly antisymmetric by the charge law.
The force part Force = SUM_j R_j (m_j - a) - (2w - S) a (interval.readings) is antisymmetric in its own
weight term (a = -A against +A) and in its arrival term as far as the Link's record is read the same
from both ends: links.turn_pair is odd in its input and the two ends' levels are negatives on the re
line, so the arrival at the - end is minus the arrival at the + end **except** through the Link's odd
part n_ij, which links.link signs by the Port's side (antisymmetric) and which enters the tangent
triple as (W^2 - n^2, 2 W n, W^2 + n^2): the middle term is odd in n, so the two ends' second turns are
not mirrors and the arrivals part by 2 ts a_im / tw.

**The condition on the row.** F' = 0 exactly when g_minus + g_plus = 0, that is when the two ends'
re-line remainders are equal, the Link's odd part on the born axis is 0 (or the Link's phase sine is
0), and the force's residue does not tip the carried division at one end alone; with equal remainders
and an exactly antisymmetric force the sum lies in {-1, 0}, so even at its best the stepped current is
0 or one level of A. The carriage pair's inequality (a row whose mass D stands against its six reaches
R_j) decides the size of Force and so of g, not its antisymmetry: a heavy row does not make F' smaller.

**The prediction, written before the runs.** The laid current is 0 at every born pair of every record
(the hole 60, the chiral 60, the bodies, the quartet on a carrying leg alike). The stepped current is
den A times a small integer and is 0 only by the rounding's accident, so at most a few births of each
record have a stepped current 0; under reading (b) nearly every born pair is either refused by name
(twist or sense) or taken through the moving branch with its orientation read from the sign of that
integer. The 48 and the run back do not part the readings, because close.click at -1 undoes the close
from the log and the merger's lay reads no sense.

**Step 0's unification in the algebra.** close.meet's current, close.refuse's share and bodies' net
current are three readings of ONE form: the record's levels at the interval's start. The one form is
`den SUM_lines cross(now_i, before_i, now_j, before_j)` on the start lines, with the share and the net
current its contractions. One reading serves the three.

## 5. What each reading costs and buys, in the law's own terms

Reading (a), the pair's own state as the birth laid it:
- MG-S1's premise is true as written ("no current across the Link", "at rest, as the pair at rest"):
  the current is 0 exactly, 33 of 33 and 50 of 50 rest meetings measured, by the theorem and not by a
  rounding.
- SL-S1's refusal by the orientation is read where it means something: a current of 0 with charges of
  the same sign is a true refusal by the orientation, not a rounding's sign.
- MG-S1's "the birth read at -1" holds: the merger's orientation is the birth's own, because the state
  read is the state the birth left. RW-S1 (one act with the direction) keeps its meaning at the Link.
- The charge law holds: the two ends' charges are -A B and +A B exactly, so the selector W_minus
  W_plus < 0 is exact and covariant under all 48 images (the Wronskian is a Node scalar).
- The count of integers is unchanged; no number enters; nothing beyond one Link.

Reading (b), the state at the close:
- MG-S1's premise becomes false at 13 of 14 born pairs on each record: a pair nothing has moved is read
  as carrying a current den A (g_minus + g_plus), which is the carried division's residue at the two
  ends and not a motion.
- SL-S1's refusal by the orientation is then read at that residue: 10 of 33 (hole) and 5 of 50
  (chiral) rest meetings are refused by name `twist` and 4 of 33 and 10 of 50 by name `sense`, with no
  level of the board behind the refusal.
- The exact take is unchanged (the merger's two takes read the levels whole at the end of the interval
  under either reading, and the falls and the walls are the same integers).
- The charge law is bent, not broken: after the step the two ends' charges are -A(B - g'_minus) and
  +A(B - g'_plus), of opposite sign while the two residues stay below B, which is why 10 of 50 chiral
  meetings fall to `sense`.
- The count of integers is unchanged.
- It buys nothing measurable: no birth, no credit, no Node line and no run back changes.

Nature's records favour (a) on every gate the Reader's rows read: the merger as a row (the hole 60 and
the chiral 60 keep their 17 births and their mergers under either reading, but under (a) their
orientation is the birth's own and is the same under every image of the cube), the pair into light (the
merger's children are the same under either reading, so the gate does not part), Bell's cells (empty on
both records under both readings; no pair is clicked at both ends on either). The gate that would part
is the orientation of a merger on a record with more than one: under (b) it is a rounding's sign.
