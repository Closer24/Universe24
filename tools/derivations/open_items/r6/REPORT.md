## 1. The derivation, from the law's clauses by name

**Step 1, the question on the law's letter (The swap at the top; The Node; Rule3 is reversible).** The fifth act is the conditional map at every Node: Z = swap(P) where the trigger T(P) holds (the weak maximum of the triple X0 = P.before, X1 = P.now, X2 = the step's output), Z = step(P) elsewhere. Its bijection rests on the law's identity, "the inverse on a state is the forward act on the swapped and complemented state, every neighbour's level fixed": step(swap(Z)) = swap(step^-1(Z)). The 4.0 tree and the branch hold it bit for bit (Rule3's line is time-symmetric and the write's source D = now^2 - next x before is symmetric in next and before).

**Step 2, the two pre-images (the theorem of this round).** Given the post-state Z at a Node, the forward act has exactly two candidate pre-images there: Q = swap(Z) (a pre-image iff T(Q), the swap of a swap returning Z), and Y = step^-1(Z), the engine's inverse (a pre-image iff not T(Y)). Both read the Node and its six Links alone, and the swap keeps the level now of every Node: swap(Z).now = Z.before = P.now whether the Node turned or not, so every neighbour's level read is the start's (The interval's dependency radius is one Link). In the branch the identity gives Y = swap(step(Q)), so Y.before = step(Q).now and T(Y) = at_top(step(Q).now, X1, X2) = T(Q) by the trigger's symmetry in its outer levels: always one pre-image. Under the ride the identity fails, so T(Q) and T(Y) can disagree: T(Q) and not T(Y) is a **collision** (both pre-images step to Z: the forward interval is not injective); not T(Q) and T(Y) is a post-state no state reaches.

**Step 3, why the identity fails under the ride (The line rides the volume, form A).** The riding momentum weighs the velocity of [k, k+1] by the volume at its earlier end, X_k: P_in = w X0 v_in + r, P_out = w X1 v_out + r'. The forward act on Q (time line now X1, before X2) reads the incoming velocity -v_out at the wall w X2, where it was born at w X1. With the mean-wall complement c = (w/2)(X1 + X2) - 1 - r' the reversed numerator is w X1 (u_before - u_now) + R with R = (w X1 - 1 - r) + w (X1 - X0)(v_in - 1/2) + w (X1 - X2) v_out: Rule3's complement at w X1 plus two asymmetry terms; R leaves [0, w X1) by about [(X1 - X0)(v_in - 1/2) + (X1 - X2) v_out] div X1 levels. That error enters form A's booking of the reversed pair (form A is itself not time-symmetric: F(next, now) - F(before, now) = (next - before)(r - r') / w per Node, the remainders' term) and moves the volume's reversed third level by delta from X0. So the triple read on Q is (X2, X1, X0 + delta).
