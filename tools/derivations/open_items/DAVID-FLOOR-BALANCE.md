## 1. In clocks

- **Two clocks on one band.** Swinging together, the band pulls each back by half its strength more; swinging opposite,

- **The held hand lends a spring.** Through its four-leg tie with each it lends c h^2 to the giver and to the taker
  alike: more when it swings wide, nothing when it passes 0.
- **The opposite swing keeps s - t/2 + c h^2.** Three cases follow, each a fact of the structure:

### 2.4 The square form, reversed bands, and which case holds

- **Big Pic's candidate** (6542519437 item 6): every form of the law is the square of its own cell's value. A band is
  (t/2)(x_i + x_j)^2, and a reversed band (-t x_i x_j) is (t/2)(x_i - x_j)^2. Each square's diagonal gives each of its
  two clocks t/2, so each clock's mass is half the sum of its bands, laid by no one. The form is the sum of the bands'
  squares, the Gram form of their incidence ((t/2)(D + A) for equal plain bands, D the clocks' counts of bands), never
  below 0: the bound holds on every structure.
- **Which case holds is the structure's (DERIVED).** On a connected group the form has a free swing exactly when some x
  not 0 has x_i = -sigma x_j on every band (sigma = 1 for a plain band, -1 for a reversed one), that is, when around
  every loop the product of the -sigma is 1: every loop's parity and reversal agree. That is Harary's balance of the
  network signed by -sigma (F. Harary, "On the notion of balance of a signed graph", Michigan Math. J. 2 (1953)
  143-146): an even loop carries an even number of reversed bands, and an odd loop an odd number. Then the group sits at
  the floor's edge with one free swing, and a group with no loop always does. It holds by its springs alone exactly when
  some loop's parity and reversal disagree: frustration.
- **So:** the click world's single giver-taker band gives the edge, s = t/2, its free swing the opposite swing, held by
  the lend alone. The equilateral triangle of plain two-leg bands gives each clock t, more than half: its swings summing
  to 0 hold t/2 each and its common swing 2t. The plain triangle is the smallest frustrated loop. A reversed odd loop
  sits at the edge, and a reversed even loop holds.
- **The ring rule** (ring_balance.py, exact, rings of 3 to 10 clocks): under the square form a ring's form is
  (t/2)(2 I + A). Then det(A + 2I) = 0 exactly when parity and reversal agree (an even ring with no band reversed, an
  odd ring with one), and 4 otherwise, as the periodic transfer form det(A + 2I) = 2 - 2 (-1)^n sigma says (sigma the
  product of the ring's band signs). By Gershgorin no ring eigenvalue lies below -2, so det(A + 2I) = 0 says the lowest
  is -2: the same rings are those whose edge, with equal masses, sits at the whole band in whole numbers. det(A + I) = 0
  on the plain rings of 3, 6 and 9, where -1 is the lowest only on the triangle (eigenvalues 2, -1, -1).
- **Joining two rings into one** adds their lengths and their reversals: two odd rings, each with one reversal, make an
  even ring with none, the sign rule of two fermions making a boson (a reading, on the owner's lead of 10:15;
  6542620339).
