## 4. Row 46, built (src/tensor24/decoder/bangs.py)

- **The frame:**
  - the load at each round, read from the ledger as G1 proposes;
  - a bottom is a round whose load is no higher than the round before's and lower than the round after's;
  - a bang runs from the first round, or a bottom, to the round before the next bottom, and is whole where that bottom closes it.
- **Reading 1, the clicks:** per bang whole, its click kinds (each whole or split click's kind and train), and the deepest clock its whole clicks lay (the trains of their laid holdings, at the click or at its ends, and of their children).
- **Reading 2, the ledger:** per bang whole, the deepest clock holding a winding at the bang's last round.
- **The depth:**
  - an elementary train is 0, and a composite one deeper than its deepest part, as G2 proposes;
  - a card without its parts, a part no card names, a part that is a row, or parts that bind themselves leave a train's depth unread, and the row names it.
- **The verdict:**
  - not read where the ledger is absent, the header names no time gear, a round holds no load, fewer than two bangs are whole, or a clock read is of no depth;
  - not shown where a later bang shows no more than the last (its click kinds and its deepest clock laid each at least the last's, one of them more), or the deepest clock held falls at a bang's end, the first named;
  - else shown.
- **The tests** (tests/test_the_tensor24_decoder_bangs.py, 19 tests in 194 of its 200 lines):
  - a record of three bangs whose clocks deepen (0, 1, 2), read SHOWN, by the tool too;
  - a bang that shows more by its kinds alone, or by its depth alone;

  - a held clock that falls;
  - eight frames the row cannot read, each named;
