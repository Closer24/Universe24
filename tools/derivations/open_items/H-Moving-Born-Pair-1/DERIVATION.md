### 1.3 Theorem: a pair whose charges sum to 0 carries no current across its Link

The lattice momentum across the born Link is the bilinear P_link = 2 num SUM_lines (now_+ before_- - now_- before_+), the two ends' levels dotted over the lines (take.momentum_of's form, the J hands' key). Write the + child's levels (a_+, b_+) and the - child's (a_-, b_-) as vectors over the lines; the charges are W_+- = a_+- . J b_+-. For equal amplitudes (|a_-| = |a_+|, |b_-| = |b_+|, the one lay for both children) and W_+ + W_- = 0, the pair's current across the Link is 0 for every phase pair: with a_- = lambda R_alpha a_+ and b_- = mu R_beta b_+ (R the rotation by an angle in the (v, J v) plane), W_- = lambda mu W_+ cos(beta - alpha), so lambda mu cos(beta - alpha) = -1 forces beta = alpha + pi (lambda mu = 1) or beta = alpha (lambda mu = -1), and in both cases P_link = -A B (mu sin beta + lambda sin alpha) = 0. The same at the G-form of the quartet, R commuting with J and G. The log confirms it on the head: dP_lay = 0 at every birth where the row stood at nothing (11 of 17 on the hole 60, 7 of 16 on the chiral 60; the rest where a standing record's levels enter the cross term).

So MG-S1's premise, no current across the Link, holds for every lay of a born pair at the Link's two ends whose charges sum to 0: the moving form cannot part the born pair from the merger's premise.
