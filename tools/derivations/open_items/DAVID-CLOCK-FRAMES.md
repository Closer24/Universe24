## 1. In clocks

- **A clock's frame.** A clock's count is read against its frame. Turn the frame and read the count as its negative:
  nothing a clock feels changes, so long as every band at that clock is read turned too. A plain band becomes a
  reversed one, and a Mesh grip becomes a plain band. Each term is a cell's square, and a cell's square is the same in
  either frame.
- **The click world's pair.** One band and no loop. Its sign is a choice of the taker's frame: the swing called
  opposite in one frame is called together in the other, and the swing with no spring of its own is the same swing. So
  the band's sign on the pair is a freedom that nothing reads.
- **A loop.** Around a loop, count each plain band -1 and each reversed band or grip +1, and multiply: the product is
  the same in every frame, the loop's parity. A free swing exists exactly when every loop's parity is +1 (Harary's
  balance, FLOOR 2.4), and an odd loop holds the group by its squares alone.
- **The meshed board.** At each Node-clock a giver and a taker share a band, and each is gripped to its Mesh neighbours.
  The loop through the giver and the taker at two neighbouring Node-clocks carries both bands, and its parity is their
  signs' product. With every band of one sign every such loop is even, and the uniform opposite swing is free on any
  Mesh board (the MIRACLE physicist, the Boss's 6543885489 item 2). One band of the other sign makes the loops through
  it odd, and nothing is free.
- **The free swing weighs.** It moves, and term 5 carries each clock's momentum, so it weighs and it is read. What
  nothing reads is which frame calls it opposite.
- **The write.** Turning a band's sign where no loop passes is a frame turn of one of its clocks: the rest see nothing.
  Turning it on a loop changes the loop's parity: the free swing goes, and the rest see the change. Made at the round
  its pair stands at 0, the turn moves no energy, and a second turn undoes it.
- **Quaternion hands.** The frame is a whole unit of the 24, and a band carries a link k, the taker read in the giver's
  frame. A loop's holonomy, the product of its links, is what no frame turn changes, and a loop holds exactly when its
  holonomy is not 1.

## 2. In the algebra

### 2.1 The frame turn (DERIVED)

- **The square form:** V = SUM w (x_i - e_ij x_j)^2 over the cells, with e_ij = -1 for a plain band, +1 for a reversed
  band and for every Mesh grip, and w > 0: FLOOR 2.4's square form, its signs written in the cell.
- **The frame turn** by eta in {1, -1}^N: x_v -> eta_v x_v and e_ij -> eta_i e_ij eta_j. Each cell becomes
  eta_i (x_i - e_ij x_j), and its square is unchanged. Every form that reads x_v is read in the turned frame: term 5's
  own momenta (W v_in v_out / 2 per clock) are squares and stand unchanged, and each Mesh stretch is a cell with e = +1.
- **The frame-free content of the signs** is, on each loop, the product of its e_ij (FLOOR 2.4's product of the
  -sigma). Two sign lays with the same loop products are one lay in two frames: on a connected group, turn frames along
  a spanning tree until its bands are plain; the bands left are one per independent loop, fixed by the loops' products
  (switching: T. Zaslavsky, "Signed graphs", Discrete Appl. Math. 4 (1982) 47-74; Harary's balance as cited in FLOOR
  2.4). On a group with no loop, every sign lay is one frame's.
- **Checked** by frames.py as polynomials with whole coefficients, at every clock of the pair, the triangle of plain
  bands and the meshed ring of 3: 2 of 2, 3 of 3 and 6 of 6.

### 2.2 The Boss's test: what reads the free swing (DERIVED)

- On a balanced group the free swing is the form's kernel direction (FLOOR 2.4): on the pair, g = -u under a plain band
  and g = u under a reversed one; on a meshed board with every band plain, g_x = -u_x = a at every Node-clock.
- **Term 5 reads it.** On node-all (engine/step.py's docstring), H = W v_in v_out / 2 + SUM_Mesh grip Delta^2 / 2, so
  each clock's own momentum enters. A free swing moving at v carries W v^2 per clock, in every frame. On the probe's
  ring the drifting free swing carries the step's own energy E = W SUM v^2 = 9,600,000,000 at lam 1, with every push 0.
- **So by the test the free swing is read, and it is not superfluous.** It is a swing with no spring of its own; in a
  field reading it would be a massless one, which is not made here.
- What the clicks read of it is not derived here.

### 2.3 The kernel, the floor and the board (DERIVED)

- Under the square form with no own spring laid, the kernel is nonzero exactly when every loop is balanced (FLOOR 2.4).
  A group with no loop always has one, and an odd loop removes it.
- **The meshed board:** N Node-clocks, a band of sign e_x between the giver x and the taker x, and grips around both
  rings. The loop giver_x, taker_x, taker_(x+1), giver_(x+1) has the parity e_x e_(x+1), each grip counting +1, and the
  two rings of grips are even. So the board is balanced exactly when every band has one sign.
- **Checked** at the grain's weights (each band at weight f = 60,000, each grip at the grip 100,000,000): on rings of 3
  to 8 Node-clocks the determinant is 0 with every band plain and with every band reversed, and positive with one or two
  reversed; the uniform opposite swing is in the kernel with every band plain; with an own spring f laid on every clock
  the determinant is positive.
- **So the floor:** on a board whose bands all have one sign, no weight of the square form holds the free swing. An own
  spring laid beyond the square form holds it (FLOOR's s > t/2), and that is a laid number. A loop's odd parity holds
  it with no number laid.

### 2.4 Quaternion hands on the 24 (DERIVED)

- **The 24 whole units:** +-1, +-i, +-j, +-k and (+-1 +- i +- j +- k)/2. frames.py builds them in doubled
  coordinates, every sign on every axis and every sign on all four, and finds them closed under the product. Over them
  2 Re k is -2 once, -1 eight times, 0 six times, 1 eight times and 2 once.
- **The cell and its square:** for a unit k, |g - k u|^2 = |g|^2 + |u|^2 - 2 Re(g* k u), since |k u| = |u|. So the
  band, -2 Re(g* k u), has twice the tie's weight in the one cell, at every unit, and no ratio is laid between them.
  Checked as polynomials in the eight dials for 24 of 24 units.
- **The frame turn** x_v -> w x_v, with k_vj -> w k_vj and k_iv -> k_iv w* at every link at v, keeps the form. Checked
  for all 576 (frame, link) pairs on a triangle, as whole 12 x 12 matrices.
- **The kernel:** on a connected group it is nonzero exactly when every loop's holonomy is 1. A kernel vector meets
  x_i = k_ij x_j on every band; around a loop of holonomy k it meets x = k x at its base; and left multiplication by a
  unit other than 1 fixes no quaternion but 0, since |k - 1|^2 = 2 - 2 Re k > 0 (least 1 over the 23). Checked: the
  triangle's determinant is 0 at k = 1 alone of the 24, and the pair alone, with no loop, has determinant 0 at all 24.
- **Scalar dials with a unit link:** with g and u on the real axis, the cell's square is w (g^2 + u^2 - 2 Re(k) g u).
  Only Re k reaches the dials, so the 24 give five pair forms. The pair sits at the edge where Re k is 1 or -1, and
  holds at the other 22 units, its own weight w being more than half its band 2 w |Re k| (FLOOR 2.1's s > t/2). At
  k = -1 the cell is g + u, the held hand's present cell at |h| = 1.

### 2.5 The write (DERIVED for the frame; CANDIDATE as the third click's)

- **On a group with no loop,** turning one band's sign equals turning one of its clocks' frames, so every frame-free
  reading is unchanged. A write that is a frame turn is unread, as a write that is a symmetry of the round is: the THIRD
  pair found turns.py's write a relabelling (both readers; the Boss's 6543768769 and 6543794891).
- **On a loop,** turning one band's sign multiplies that loop's parity by -1, which no frame turn reaches: the kernel
  goes or comes (2.3), and the rest read it. With quaternion links a write k -> k w on one band of a loop moves its
  holonomy likewise.
- **Its energy:** the band's square moves from w (g + u)^2 to w (g - u)^2, by -4 w g u. That is exactly 0 where
  g u = 0. So a write at the round its pair stands at 0, where 1 - lit(g u) = 1 by one carry (node-all's
  algebra/carry.py), moves no energy, and each clock's books stand with no passing. Anywhere else it moves 4 w g u,
  which a passing would have to carry (OPEN).
- **Its inverse:** turned twice the sign is back, so the write is its own inverse, a whole number in the state.
- **The fixed end** (a CANDIDATE reading of the owner's 12:28 and 13:0x words): each loop's parity at the end is its
  parity at the start times -1 for each write on it. So the third clicks' summed choices meet a fixed end exactly when
  each loop carries the right parity of writes, a condition on labels that no frame turn moves.
