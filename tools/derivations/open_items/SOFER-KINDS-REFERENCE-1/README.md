# Sofer's exact references for the 24 and the 48

Two scripts, written from the groups' own definitions and in no way from the build, for the stage that loads the kinds
(the ruling's stage 6): each is run with `venv314/bin/python`, uses whole numbers and exact fractions alone, and prints
its result (kinds_ref.txt, spinors_ref.txt).

- **kinds_ref.py: the 24.** The 24 rotations of the cube as the signed permutation matrices of three axes with
  determinant 1, their five classes by (order, trace, fixed faces) and the five kinds: A1 is 1, A2 the sign of the
  permutation each rotation gives the four body diagonals, T1 the trace, T2 the product of T1 and A2, E the faces'
  permutation character less A1 and T1. The five kinds are orthonormal over the 24 (all 25 sums), and the class sizes
  are 1, 6, 3, 8 and 6.
- **spinors_ref.py: the 48.** The binary octahedral group as the unit quaternions generated by (1 + i) over the root of
  2 and (1 + i + j + k) over 2, coordinates in the rationals with the root of 2 held exactly: 48 elements, each lifting
  one of the 24 rotations (found by the conjugation of i, j and k, whole numbers), eight classes, and the eight kinds:
  the five above through the rotation, E1/2 the trace of the lift, E5/2 the product of E1/2 and A2, G3/2 the product of
  E1/2 and T1 less E1/2. The eight kinds are orthonormal over the 48 (all 64 sums, exact).

**The five characters on the five classes** (class sizes 1, 8, 3, 6, 6: the identity, the third turns, the face half
turns, the quarter turns, the edge half turns):

| kind | identity | third turns | face half turns | quarter turns | edge half turns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| A2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | -1 | -1 |
| E | 2 | -1 | 2 | 0 | 0 |
| T1 | 3 | 0 | -1 | 1 | -1 |
| T2 | 3 | 0 | -1 | -1 | 1 |

The products that follow (T1 x T1 = A1 + E + T1 + T2, E x E = A1 + A2 + E, A2 x T1 = T2) are the characters'
products and hold by those values.

**The eight kinds of the 48** are written in spinors_ref.txt, class by class, each character as a pair (a, b) meaning
a + b times the root of 2: only E1/2 and E5/2 take the root, plus and minus, on the two classes of order 8 (six
elements each), and G3/2 is 0 there.

The numbers are for pinning a test, not for typing into code: the build's kind table is made at load from the shape,
and its characters are to equal these.
