Fourier's support bound on Tensor24's board: a place is a Node-clock, a motion a mode of the board; exact, whole numbers only, no draw

Part 1: the board's facts, on the engine at main (examples/tensor24)
  1. The Meshes: 64 Node-clocks on a board 4 by 4 by 4 that wraps; each has six neighbours,
     all different: True; each Spoke's opposite leads back: True; every neighbour one Mesh away, across the wrap too: True
  2. One gear laid on the example board: row 0 at Node-clock 0, its now slot 1 and its before slot 0.
     round 1: its own now slot moves, and the fine teeth of its six neighbours [1, 3, 4, 12, 16, 48] move, all six alike: True
     over 60 rounds: rounds where a now or before slot stands off the laid Node-clock: 0; rounds where a neighbour's fine tooth stands off 0: 40; the farthest Node-clock any slot or tooth reaches: 1 Mesh. So nothing travels on this example: the gear stays and its neighbours' teeth never carry. That a round reaches one Mesh at most is the act's own (step.py reads its six Meshes, seen(a, nb)), not this run's
  3. Two moments: every round's before slot is the last round's now slot: True; a gear's own rate is their difference (not part 2's motion, a mode of the board)
  4. The run back: 60 rounds run back to the laid start, slot for slot: True
  5. The state at round 6 is the laid start, slot for slot, and again at round 12: True; so the gear's beat repeats every 6 rounds exactly: its now slot [1, 0, -1, -1, 0, 1] (the example's teeth are six grips, so the laid gear, with its neighbours silent, steps a+ = a - b)
     on the ring of its 6 rounds: rounds held 4, beats held 2; one round alone holds every beat of the ring, 6 of 6 (a one-round wave's transform: an identity)
