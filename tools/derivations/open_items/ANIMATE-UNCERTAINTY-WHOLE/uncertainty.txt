Uncertainty on Tensor24's board: a place is a Node-clock, a motion a step along a Mesh; exact, whole numbers only, no draw

Part 1: the board's facts, on the engine at main (examples/tensor24)
  1. The Meshes: 64 Node-clocks on a board 4 by 4 by 4 that wraps; each reads six neighbours,
     all different: True; each Mesh read both ways: True; every neighbour one Mesh away, across the wrap too: True
  2. One gear laid on the example board: row 0 at Node-clock 0, its now slot 1 and its before slot 0.
     round 1 touches Node-clocks [0, 1, 3, 4, 12, 16, 48]: the gear's own and its six neighbours [1, 3, 4, 12, 16, 48];
     the six neighbours' fine teeth all one value: True (every direction alike)
     over 60 rounds, no Node-clock touched farther than one Mesh a round: True
  3. Two moments: every round's before slot is the last round's now slot: True; a motion is their difference
  4. The run back: 60 rounds run back to the laid start, slot for slot: True
  5. The laid gear's own beat, its now slot round by round: [1, 0, -1, -1, 0, 1], repeating every 6 rounds
     on the ring of its 6 rounds: rounds held 4, beats held 2;
     a click at one round: rounds held 1, beats held 6 of 6
