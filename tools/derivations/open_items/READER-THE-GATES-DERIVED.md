Lemma A (the slot is the direction). For every act of the interval but the click, F^-1 = R o F o R, equivalently F o R = R o F^-1.
Source: CA-S1 (docs/ALGEBRA.md:610): "Rule3's step at -1 is the step at +1 with the other slot exchanged and the remainder mirrored, w - 1 - r, one function ... the arrow is the click's alone". For one line: forward, w a' + r' = Y - w b + r with Y = S a + SUM_j R_ij m_j (docs/ALGEBRA.md:532). Feed R(post) = (a, a', w - 1 - r'): the total is Y - w a' + (w - 1 - r') = w b + (w - 1 - r), so the quotient is b and the remainder w - 1 - r: F(R(post)) = R(pre). The arrivals m_j are the neighbours' now slots and the paces read the now slot, both unchanged when every Node is exchanged. The inverse itself is The direction, Proof (docs/ALGEBRA.md:570-582). In the engine the direction is the slot order (clock.py:163) and the carry's mirror (core/acts.py:18).

Lemma B (the swap). A Node meeting the trigger in interval t ends it at R of the state it started from: S_i(t) = R(S_i(t-1)) (The turnover, docs/ALGEBRA.md:1586; The tick (v), docs/ALGEBRA.md:482; clock.py:252-268). If every Node meets it at t*, then S(t*) = R(S(t*-1)): a whole-board turn.

Lemma C (the retrace). If every Node turns at t* and no click stands in the intervals t*-k .. t*+k-1, then at every Node

    S(t*+j) = R(S(t*-1-j))   for j = 0 .. k-1,   and   X_i(t*+j) = X_i(t*-2-j)   for j = 0 .. k.
