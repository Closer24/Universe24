## 1. The algebra (one page)

The Node reads before it adds. Every reading of the engine is a function of arrays at every Node alike; what differs is the OBJECT read. There are two objects on the lattice and two off it:

(a) The Link's one read. A Link at a Port joins the Node i to its neighbour j; one array x read through core/ports.across gives the pair (x_i, x_j). Every reading of a Link in the engine is that pair combined one of two ways:
- linear, x_i + s x_j: at s = +1 the even reading (the tension's sum, readings.link_factors; the frame bits' sum whose parity is the frame sign, readings.frames); at s = -1 the difference (the Link's count n_ij = K_i - f_ij K_j with s = -f, readings.link_phases; the centred gradient as the two Ports' differences, links.gradient); at s = +1 times the Port's side the odd part (the shape-4 row's odd line, links.links_of; the flux line's fold, metric.fold_links);
- bilinear, F_ij = x_i y_j - y_i x_j, links.cross at the two ends: the current of a line (links.link_current with x = now, y = before), the row's current (summed over its lines), the net transverse current (two Ports' currents), the twist (the cross of the two net currents at the two ends, links.row_twist).
So the Link's record (the factor Q_ij, the frame f_ij, the phase pair at n_ij, the odd part, the fold triple at the record's rest rotation) and its turn of a row's lines (links.turned: the phase pair, then the odd part's tangent triple times the fold) are named readings of ONE function `read(x, port, y, sign)` with the direction in the sign and the Port. The reader: link_reader.py, 14 names, 80 function lines (74 code lines, 121 with the docstrings), against the engine's 160 (links.py 74, readings.py's four Link readings 31, metric.fold_links 11, node.turned_arrival 6, interval.readings' Link block 38).

(b) The row's one bilinear. A record of shape 2k holds k planes on one lattice with four matrices named by the law (gram.py): G the Gram, J the quarter turn, K the conjugation, Gamma_a the three axis matrices (the identity, the standard blocks, the im line's negation and none for a plane; the derived ones for a quartet, quartet.py). Every reading of the row is x.M.y, one bilinear form of two lists of lines, with M one of G, GJ, G Gamma_a, K: the charge B.GJ.N (the plane's Wronskian as the case G = I), the current dN_a.GJ.N with the neighbour's lines read as K x where the frame is -1, the spin density N.G Gamma_a.N, the Node term SUM_a coefficient Phi_a (Gamma_a x), the share's four quadratic parts n.G.n, b.G.b, n.G.b, n.G.(SUM_j R_j b_j), the period's ratio N.G.B / N.G.N, the kick's forms N.GJ.u and the emission F.GJ.N. The reader: row_reader.py, `bilinear(matrix(shape, name), xs, ys)` with the matrix chosen by name once, 10 names, 71 function lines (70 code), against gram.py's 77 reading lines (charge, conjugated, current, density, curl, term, share, weighted, form) and interval.readings' row block.

Is the row's gram a reading of the Link's record? Partly: the current and the curl read across a Link (the row reader's `current` reads `across` and `curl` is the Link reader's gradient), and the charge at G = I is links.cross at ONE place with the readings (re, im) and the places (now, before) exchanged (the same determinant). The quartet's G is not: it is the file's trace form, a constant derived once at the load. So two readers, one bilinear between them (the cross), and not one.

(c) Off the lattice, the readings that are neither the Link's nor the row's and stay as they are: the file's constants (core/rule3: the wall, the mass, the reaches, the factor's form, the unit, the held walls: 147 lines with node.rule_of and rows' walls), the carrier's lines (core/paces: the clock's table, 118; readings.paces_of, frozen_of, content, pair_angle, floor: 29), the seed's envelopes (seed.py 110, wave.py 14, gram.moving/rest 29), the click's readings (take.fall_of 43, draw.read_number 27, merger's walls and shares 74, close.list_of and meet 48, bodies.frame_of and bilinears_of 46), and quartet.py (348, the four matrices' derivation). These are 1,064 of the readings' 1,443 lines on this head; the two readers address the other 379.

The four roads: (1) the algebra: one bilinear where the letter writes nine (links.cross, links.wronskian, gram.charge, gram.form, gram.weighted, metric.stress_parts' d.G.d, metric.shear_parts' d_a.G.d_b, metric.net_flux's u.G.D_a N, bodies.bilinears_of's B.G Gamma_a.N: all x.M.y); (2) the matrix: the four matrices named once in `matrix`, the Link's record one object per Port per coupling built by `record` and read by `turn`; (3) the cube: the reader reads a Port, never an axis sign: the odd part takes the Port's side from PORT_SIDES (the cube's own table), the twist the cyclic order (a, a + 1, a - 1) from `wrap`, so every reading is covariant as the engine's is (the proofs run the same 48-tested code paths bit for bit); (4) Rule3: no branch on a row's kind in either reader: a plane's density is the empty list (no axis matrices), its conjugation the K the file's shape gives, the coupling 0's record the frames to the power 0 and the count times 0 (the identity by arithmetic, as the engine does; see the bugs: that identity costs six board-wide readings per interval).
