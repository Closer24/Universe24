## 1. The algebra (the law's letter)

1. **The sign is the rotation sense**: the charge density at a Node is W = re_now im_before - im_now re_before, the Wronskian of the plane's two lines, a bilinear of the four levels and of no remainder.
2. **The swap at the top** (the law's fifth act): at a turned Node every line's two levels are exchanged and "every plane record reading the holder is conjugated at the turned Node (im to -im, its remainder complemented at its wall) ... since the Wronskian is odd under the exchange of the two levels and odd again under the conjugation". The act on a plane z = (re, im) is C T_0: T_0 the exchange of the slots now and before, C the conjugation of the im line.
3. As built (node.exchange, node.py lines 292-304, this head): for every line `line.now = sgn * h.before`, `line.before = sgn * h.now`, sgn = -1 on an im line (is_im: the second line of a shape-2 row) and 1 otherwise; the remainder `mirrored(h.rem, h.wall, 1 + (sgn < 0))` (rule3.mirrored, line 122: the mirror w - 1 - r once per reversal of the line, the identity for an even count: the re line once, the im line twice, so the im line's rotation remainder is left); the write remainders mirrored once. The one rule of the remainder as FD_COMPARISON.md section 2 recommends (fd1's count), read at every site.
4. The derivation, exact in the integers: at a turned Node
   re_now' = re_before, re_before' = re_now, im_now' = -im_before, im_before' = -im_now, so
   W' = re_now' im_before' - im_now' re_before' = re_before (-im_now) - (-im_before)(re_now) = re_now im_before - im_now re_before = W.
   Step by step: T_0 alone gives W -> re_before im_now - im_before re_now = -W (odd); C alone gives W -> re_now (-im_before) - (-im_now) re_before = -W (odd); C T_0 gives (-1)(-1) W = W. The remainders do not enter W. So the law's letter and the build agree: **the swap keeps W at every turned Node, exactly, in integers, with no condition on the levels.** The same holds for the sum of W over the planes at the Node and for the board's W at the moment of the exchange.
5. What the swap does change on the board: the turned Node ends the interval at its exchanged pre-state, the step's output at that Node discarded. So across the interval the board's W changes at a turned Node by W_pre - W_post (the pre-state's W in place of the step's output's), while the exchange itself adds 0. This is the one place the fifth act touches the board's W, and it is not a flip.
6. What the law says of the step: the weighted total SUM_i W_i / p_i^2 is exactly invariant in the rationals "at paces fixed in time" and "the plain total drifts where the paces differ". cl1's "W per body" is the plain total. Where the paces move within the interval (the heavy packet digging its well), neither the plain nor the 1 / p_i^2-weighted total is the law's invariant.

Prediction before the runs (PREDICTIONS.md P1 to P4): kept at every turned site, negated 0, changed 0; the board's W2 - W1 at every interval equal to SUM over the turned sites of (W_pre - W_post); cl1's fall in the step's part and not in the swap's; the half masks summing to the board.
