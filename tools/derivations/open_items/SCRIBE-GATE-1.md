## How it decides: a ratchet, and no list to keep

A push may not raise the count of any hit above the same file's at its merge base (the revision named by CHECK_BASE, else origin/engine-5.0-test): what stands at the base stands, and the code departs from the law at the register's R5 until the Boss removes it. A hit is keyed by its rule, its file and its detail (the number, the name, the container), never by its line, so a moved or reformatted line is no new hit; a module git reads as renamed carries its counts; a new file has none. Replacing one hit by another of a different name is a new hit. A hit leaves the list by being removed and enters it only by the owner's word: tools/derivations/open_items/scribe_scan_allowed.json holds the entries the owner allowed (rule, file, detail, count, his word, the ledger's row), is written by Sofer alone and is read from origin/scribe-5, so that a push cannot allow itself by editing its own copy. It shouts at the session at the moment it types the push, which is the owner's "right there".
