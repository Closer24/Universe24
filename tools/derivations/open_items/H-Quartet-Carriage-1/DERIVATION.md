## 1. The algebra (one page): why the axis spreads, and the condition on the pair

The quartet's matrices (quartet.py, derived at the load; measured again here, out/one_node_test.json): G Gamma_a symmetric, J Gamma_a = Gamma_a J, J^T G J = G, Gamma_a^2 = I, Gamma_a Gamma_b + Gamma_b Gamma_a = 0 for a != b (every identity true, 3 of 3 axes, 6 of 6 pairs).

The axial as a difference of two norms. Gamma_a^2 = I splits the lattice into the eigenspaces at +1 and -1, P+- = (I +- Gamma_a) div 2. They are G-orthogonal: for v at +1 and u at -1, v.G u = v.G Gamma_a u (-1) = -(Gamma_a v).G u = -v.G u, so v.G u = 0 (G Gamma_a symmetric). Hence for any levels x, s_a(x) = x.G Gamma_a x = |P+ x|_G^2 - |P- x|_G^2 exactly (measured on 1,344 of 1,344 digit vectors of the lattice, three axes). A child laid in the eigenspace at e (qc1's v, J v in the same eigenspace since J commutes) has s_a = e |x|_G^2 and the sign e is kept by any step that stays inside the eigenspace; it flips only when the part that leaked into the other eigenspace outweighs the part kept, in G-norm.

Rule3's step on the row (core/rule3.py, the flux form): w (a_next - 2 a_now + a_before) + r' - r = SUM_j R_j (m_j - a_now) - D a_now per line, with the same R_j, D, w for the eight lines of the row, so the step is a scalar act on the 8-vector plus the per-line remainder: it commutes with Gamma_a and keeps the eigenspace exactly (qc1's statement, held).
