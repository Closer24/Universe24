## The act (whole numbers, products and comparisons)

It runs at the striking, before the bobs' chiming, for each twin pair (up and down, one winding W) at a clock.

1. **Split:** neither twin's wind reaches W, and their sum does. A counter per pair counts the ticks it stands so; the counter is in the state and the rewind note.
2. **When:** all three hold:

3. **The pick:** the vernier's draw (a carry c in [0, room)) against the sweeps at the tick's start: up where c (|W_up| + |W_down|) < room |W_up|, else down.
4. **The collapse:**
   - each of the picked twin's notches v goes to the largest n with n^2 |W_picked| <= v^2 (|W_up| + |W_down|), the sign kept;
   - the other twin's notches go to 0;
   - the window's wind, before less after, goes into the remainder (EX-S1, signed);
   - the notches before are logged, for the undo.
5. **The chime:** the pair strikes as one hand, one unwind of W from the light (the head's chime act), credited to the picked twin. If the unwind is refused, nothing of the act stands.
