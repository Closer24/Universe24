## 5. The mechanism, the derivation's sketch

X enters the state at one place, the turn of a record's arrivals by the Link's pair (`links.turned`, `turn_pair(re, im, cosine, sine, X)`: the pair (X cos n theta_0, X sin n theta_0) rounded to integers along the one orbit from the seed, `readings.pair_angle`, and the turned arrival its quotient by X), and the readings at one place, the angle's count by comparisons against p X (`gram.cosine_count`). The pair's rounding stays within 1.42 n + 2.3 Gamma levels of X over n acts (phase.amplitude's own docstring), so the turned arrival's quotient is exact once that error times the arrival is under one level, and the angle's count is exact once X resolves the orbit's step; above that X cancels from every quotient and the state is bit for bit the law's, as measured from k 100,000 (1,000 on the bang's world) to 10^14. Below it the turn's rounding moves a level: a ratio first (the summed quantities of rows 5, 8, 8b and 23 at k 10,000 and 30,000), a verdict next (rows 62, 63, 67 and 71 at k 1,000, with 29 fewer credits), the course at k 100 (on the bang's world the carrier's level doubles and more at every interval from 12, a runaway).
