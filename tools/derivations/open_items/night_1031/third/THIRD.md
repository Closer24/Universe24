row it lands on (hypothesis (d), stress), let A3 be the act with the third click of section 2. Then mirror(A3(mirror(
A3(s)))) = s, bit for bit, every gear, every click tooth and the third tooth.

1. **The mirrored state** m = mirror(s+) holds the now slot a, the before slot a+ (the impulse inside it), the teeth
   -1 - r+ and T_d - 1 - r_d+, the click teeth k+ and the third tooth T3 - 1 - z+.
2. **The trigger fires alike.** The backward round reads Y(a), the same source: Y + (T3 - 1 - z+) = Y + T3 - 1 - (z + Y
   - q3 T3) = q3 T3 + (T3 - 1 - z), so its quotient is q3 and its remainder T3 - 1 - z (the drive's lemma). The third
   click's quotient is the same both ways, not negated: the third click is even under the reversal where term 11's
   whole click is odd.
3. **The pointer reads alike.** The backward round's teeth in are -1 - r+, and its teeth out, as built, are -1 - r (the
   act's own run back, and Lemma 1: the impulse moves no tooth). With c(-1 - x) = -c(x), Q' = Q; T_f is read at the
   now slot, a, both ways; so u' = u and v' = v.
4. **The push reads alike.** The sizes, sigma and W(a) are read at the now slot: the cells, and so I' = I.
5. **The gear runs back.** N' = 2 D_out (a - a+) + 4 P' + 4 I' + (-1 - r+), and by the forward split 2 D_out (a+ - a)
   = N3 - r+, with N3 = 2 D_in (a - b) + 4 P + 4 I + r: N' = 2 D_in (b - a) + (-1 - r) + 4 (P' - P) + 4 (I' - I). The
   act as built gives P' = P; step 4 gives I' = I; so N' splits at 2 D_in into b - a and -1 - r: a' = b.
6. **The click teeth run back.** The backward window is (a+, a, b), the forward one reversed, the impulse inside a+ read
   as held both ways; X is antisymmetric, so S' = -S, and -S + k+ = -c key + k returns the tooth (COUNT5 M1).
7. **Mirrored back**, the state is s. QED.
