**1.3 Can the pick be made lawful? Not tonight (DERIVED; section 5 measures it).** A pick at weight p = source / D a
round, with no spurious gives, is a quotient lam in {0, 1} of the source and a draw. There are three ways to write it,

- **(a) The draw enters a carried tooth.** Take (lam, r+) = carry(source + d, D, r), its tooth kept. For lam to be
  independent of the past, (r + source + d) mod D must be independent of r, so d must spread over a full turn of the
  dial. The carry's identity, D SUM lam = SUM (source + d) + r(start) - r(end), then needs the mean of d to be 0 for
  the rate to be p. A full-turn draw with mean 0 takes r + source + d below 0 with positive chance: lam = -1, a taker

  order or spurious gives.
- **(b) The draw is the carry's remainder input, and no tooth is kept.** Take lam = q(carry(source, D, u)), u the draw
