## 1. The split take is the third click's weights (DERIVED)

**1.1 What is split.** G5's word (its section 1): "a spread quantum is clocks joined: one winding shared by hands at
several places, none holding it whole. The split click is where they come apart, and the winding goes whole with one of
them." In the engine's terms, with the owner's edge rule:
- a clock at a place is a train's gears at a Node-clock (schema 1.3: a clock is (site, train));
- the clocks a quantum can pass between are the ends of an edge: a pair of distinct trains joined by a monomial of
  the value legs' potential, the one plan the whole click's passing reads (step.passing reads J on the value legs
  alone; a monomial within one train passes nothing);
- a split take is a quantum leaving a clock that ends two edges or more at the place: one quantum toward two takers or
  more, the right triangle's legs.

The edges come from the plans, not from a file of roles (MEASURED on the board, edges.py, two runs identical):
- the click world (27 Node-clocks; trains wave, metric, held, giver, taker): three edges at every place, (held,
  giver) and (held, taker) of 12 monomials each, (giver, taker) of 6. Held, giver and taker each end two edges, so the
  place is the owner's triangle, and a third-click quantum leaving any vertex there has the other two as its takers:
  every take there is a split take. Wave and metric end none, and no monomial joins three trains;
- the mesh world (180 Node-clocks; trains wave, held, tied): one edge, (held, tied), of 12 monomials; each end has one
  edge, so every take there is whole.

**1.2 Its sizes are the takers' squares.** G5 left the parts' sizes OPEN ("the sizes (G5): the forward norm and the
backward share ... the sizes as the amplitudes squared", its section 7), and the architecture names the outcome as
drawn "by the hand's squares" (FULLPIC row 64). The right triangle gives them: each taker's weight is the base segment
next to its leg, s_i / L, with s_i = SUM over taker i's rows of a^2 at the now slot and L = SUM_j s_j, by Euclid's leg
theorem with no root (THIRD5 1.1). So G5's C_i / L and the third click's s_i / L are one quantity: the parting clocks
of a split take are the quantum's takers, and their sizes are the takers' present squares.

**1.3 One landing, one pick.** A split take lands its quantum on one taker. G5's pick and the third click's pick would
each name that taker.
- If the two picks agree, one is idle. If they disagree, either one overrides the other, and the other is idle again,
  or both land: the giver's -1 then meets two +1s, and the place's books open (C5).
- The law of the choice names one choice, the third click's, and scopes the act's one cell comparison to it. A pick of
  term 12's own, G5's half-open cells, would be a second choice the law does not name, and outside the third click its
  comparison is line 49's if.
- A pick of its own with no comparison would still read the hidden part a second time, with a dial or a draw of its own
  to choose, which the owner's 03:5x word forbids.

So the landing is the third click's draw, one per quantum, and term 12 is the weights it draws against.

**1.4 A whole take is the one-taker case.** Where the giving clock ends one edge (the mesh world), L = s_1, and the one
taker's share is 1: the third click's draw has one outcome, and the take is whole. So the split click is the third
click's draw where the giver ends two edges or more. The passing on each edge, carried at its pair of teeth, is term
11's (David's 6538584763; Big Pic 6538736766 item 2 (b)).

**1.5 Big Pic's three parts of item 19, placed.** The spread take is 1.1. Its draw (G5) is now the third click's
(1.3). The Born condition on the join of the rows' lattices was a condition on G5's pointer, which reads the squares'
residues, and it leaves term 12 with that pointer. The weights are read whole, not modulo a dial, so they need no such
condition. Whether the draw needs a like condition is the third click's question (section 2).

**1.6 What term 12 adds to the act.** Nothing of its own. The weights s_i and L are THIRD5's sums of squares inside the
third click's act, read at the now slot. The run back, the 24 and C5's books of the split are the third click's, and
