      E(z, z+) - E(z-, z) = (z+ - z-) [ W (z+ - 2 z + z-) + (s + 2 c z^2)(z+ + z-) ].

## 2. The coupled families: the polarized step is the coupled McMillan step

- **W is constant on both worlds.** The clock's read lands 0: the metric's 00 slot stays 0, with push 0, notch 0 and

- **The Mesh pull is 0.** The pair world is one site. The click world's 27 Node-clocks stay alike in every round

### 2.2 The step

- **HAT4's pairing gives**

      V_d(y, z) = s (|k(y)|^2 + |k(z)|^2) + c (|x(y)|^2 |k(z)|^2 + |x(z)|^2 |k(y)|^2) + tie (x_g(y).x_t(z) + x_g(z).x_t(y)).

  - It is quadratic in y for fixed z, with a diagonal second derivative: A_k(z) = s + c |x(z)|^2 on each held row,
    and A_x(z) = c |k(z)|^2 on each tied row.
  - Its linear part, b(z), comes from the hands' tie.
- **The energy's change:** with E(z, z+) = W |z+ - z|^2 + V_d(z, z+),

      E(z, z+) - E(z-, z) = SUM_g (z+_g - z-_g) [ W (z+_g - 2 z_g + z-_g) + A_g(z)(z+_g + z-_g) + b_g(z) ].

  So the step (W + A_g(z))(z+_g + z-_g) = 2 W z_g - b_g(z) keeps E exactly. These are the three lines of the
  verdict.
- **Each family's line is a Vieta step.** The sum of the two roots of E's quadratic in the row's next count is
  rational in the coefficients, and the coefficients are read at the other family's middle count. No root and no
  search.
  - It reads no next count, so the read is acyclic.
  - It is even under z+ <-> z-, so it runs back by the same act.
  - In rationals it needs no new element: its state is (now, before).
- **In the act's form:** both of the round's dials gain kappa_g(a) = 2 A_g(a), the row's curvature at the middle
  count:

      2 (D_out + kappa(a))(a+ - a) + s+ = 2 (D_in + kappa(a))(a - b) + s + 4 J(a).

  This is HAT2's dg and HAT4's candidate, exactly.

### 2.3 The difference quotient

- **The polarized form's own quotient.** Taken row by row over (z+_g - z-_g), [V_d(z+, z) - V_d(z-, z)] is the whole
  polynomial A_g(z)(z+_g + z-_g) + b_g(z), symmetric in z- and z+.
  - This is the Boss's (V(b) - V(a)) / (b - a), taken on the polarized potential's first slot with the middle
    frozen.
  - A quadratic's quotient is linear, which is why the next count enters linearly.
- **The quotient of V itself over the window**, W (z+ - 2z + z-) = -(V(z+) - V(z-)) / (z+ - z-) (1D):
  - it keeps E' = W (z+ - z)^2 + V(z+) + V(z) and is even;
  - but on the quartic legs it is cubic in z+, which needs a root (decision 41);
  - and in the coupled case each family's quotient reads the other family's next count: a cyclic read.
- **The coordinate-ordered quotient** (Itoh and Abe's) is explicit and keeps the energy, but it is not even, so the
  same act on the mirrored state does not invert it. Its even composition is HAT3's palindrome, with HAT3's new
  element.
- **So among these, the polarized step alone is explicit, acyclic, even and energy-keeping.** In rationals.

## 3. Theorem A: on (now, before, tooth) the act keeps only polynomials in its conserved momenta

### 3.1 The statement

- **The setting.** Let a step act on every gear's (a, b, s) by n = 2 d_in (a - b) + s + 4 J, and take
  (a+ - a, s+) from the split of n at d_out: n = 2 d_out (a+ - a) + s+, with -d_out <= s+ < d_out.
  - d_in, d_out and J are whole polynomials in the counts of any slot of any gear, and none of them reads a tooth.
  - The states are those whose teeth stand within their windows.
  - **Hypothesis (D): the drive's teeth are 1 wherever the plan's push lands.** Then the push is J itself and the
    drive's fine tooth stays 0, as on both shipped worlds (read and asserted in board.py).
    - A drive of teeth senses on a pushed row is one more carry, with a window of 2. node-all's square has one on its
      00 and ii rows. It is not treated here (OPEN).
- **The polynomial.** Let E be a polynomial in all the board's counts and teeth, of degree delta less than a third
  of the smallest half-window, with E(next) = E(state) on every state.
  - The board's state is (a, b, s). The click teeth are written by a carry and read by nothing that moves a gear
    (CONF1 section 3), so an energy of the board does not read them.
- **Hypothesis (H).** For each gear, the counts at which its tooth's window holds two neighbouring quotients, each on
  a run longer than delta, lie on no algebraic hypersurface.
  - On the pair world, at the engine's dial: at k = 1 every count of the tied rows is such a count for the held
    rows' teeth. There 4 J_k = -(8 10^6 + 48,000 |x|^2), and 8 10^6 + 48,000 m mod 2D = 2.4 10^7 stays at least
    16,000 from 0 for every whole m. The click world is alike, at least 160,000 from 0 mod 2.4 10^9.
  - On the seed: every a with 4 J(a) mod 2d farther than delta from 0.
- **Then, under (D) and (H), E is a polynomial in the conserved momenta.** These are the linear forms l(M) of the
  written momenta with l(J(b)) = 0 at every whole b.
  - Each such l(M) is kept exactly, since on the edge's own dial the momentum is carried exactly, M' = M + 4 J.
  - Where the forces at whole counts span every row, E is constant.
- **On the worlds:**
  - **The pair world and the seed:** their forces span every row (3.2), so the only kept polynomials are the
    constants.
  - **The click world:** the wave's 2 rows and the metric's 10 rows feel only the Mesh pull (no live monomial; push,
    notch and mesh 0), which sums to 0 over the 27 sites. So their 12 total momenta are kept, and every kept
    polynomial is a polynomial in them.
    - They are 0 on the shipped lay, where those rows are laid 0 and stay 0.
    - They read no held or tied count, so none of them is an energy.
  - **MATH1's bang world at strength 0:** the wave row moves, and its total momentum over the 64 sites is kept exactly
    (MATH1's mathematician, V5; MEASURED here too, rec/momentum_bang_*.txt). That is a kept degree-1 polynomial, as
    the theorem allows.

### 3.2 The proof

- **(1) The carry's exchange.**
  - Fix every count and every tooth but one gear's tooth s, and let s run over its window. Then n runs over 2d
    consecutive whole numbers, and by (H) the split's quotient takes two neighbouring values q and q + 1, each on a
    run longer than delta.
  - On each run, E(next) is a polynomial in s of degree at most delta that equals E(state). The other gears' next
    states do not read s, since no dial and no force reads a tooth. Two polynomials of degree delta alike on more
    than delta points are alike.
  - Equating the two runs: E(..., a + q, s+, ...) = E(..., a + q + 1, s+ - 2 d_out, ...) for every s+. With an edge
    dial that reads a+, the shift is s+ + 2 d(a, a+q) q - 2 d(a, a+q+1)(q+1), still a polynomial in the counts.
  - So E is unchanged by the carry's own exchange of a count for a dial's worth of tooth. It reads the gear's new
    count and tooth only through the momentum the round wrote, M' = 2 d_out (a+ - a) + s+.
  - Over all gears, E = G(the before counts, the written momenta M).
- **(2) The standing branch.**
  - On the states where every count stood still on its last edge (a = b), the written momentum is the tooth alone,
    and it fills a box of side 2d.
  - The round adds 4 J(a) to every momentum. So G(a, M + 4 J(a)) = G(a, M) on the box, and therefore for every M.
  - So G(a, .) is unchanged by the translation 4 J(a): constant along it.
- **(3) The moving branches, for a dial that is the edge's own** (the engine's act, D_in = W(a) + W(b) and
  D_out = W(a) + W(a+), and road B's edge dial).
  - Here the momentum is read on the dial it was written on, so it is carried exactly, M' = M + 4 J(a): CONF1's
    momentum books.
  - The states with a = b + q give G(b + q, M + 4 J(b + q)) = G(b, M).
  - With G~(b, U) = G(b, U + 4 J(b)) this reads G~(b + q, M) = G~(b, M - 4 J(b)), which by (2) is G~(b, M), for
    every whole q.
  - So G~ is periodic in the counts, hence does not read them: G~ = g(U). Then (2) gives g(U + 4 J(b)) = g(U) for
    every whole b.
  - So g is unchanged along every force J(b). Hence g, and with it E = g(M - 4 J(b)), is a polynomial in the linear
    forms l(M) with l(J(b)) = 0 for every whole b.
  - On the pair world and the seed the forces at whole counts span every row (the spring alone gives -2 s k, the
    ties the rest), and E is constant.
  - Where a row's forces are the Mesh pull alone, the sum over the sites is such an l: that row's total momentum is
    kept.
- **(3') The moving branches, for the round's dial** (HAT2, HAT4: d = D + kappa(a) at the middle count).
  - The moving branch reads G~(b + q, U + 2 (d(b+q) - d(b)) o q) = G~(b, U), where o is row by row.
  - Taking q and then -q gives G~(b, U + 4 (d(b+q) - d(b)) o q) = G~(b, U): more translations.
  - On the seed, kappa(b) = 2s + 4cb^2 makes them nonzero for q != 0 and b != -q/2. So G~(b, .) is constant, then
    periodic in b, so constant.
  - On the shipped couplings (kappa_k = 2s + 2c|x|^2, kappa_x = 2c|k|^2):
    - q = e_k + e_j and q = e_k + 2 e_j, for a held row k and a tied row j, give the translations
      (8c (2 x_j + 1), 8c (2k + 1)) and (32 c (x_j + 1), 16 c (2k + 1)) in the plane of those two rows;
    - their determinant is -128 c^2 (2k + 1), never 0, so they are independent at every count;
    - over all pairs they span every held and tied row;
    - the rows they do not reach have no curvature and the dial D (the click world's wave and metric rows), and on
      those rows the momentum is carried exactly, as in (3).
  - So G~(b, .) reads only the conserved momenta, and the moving branches make it periodic in b, hence free of b. E
    is a polynomial in the conserved momenta, and it is constant on the pair world and the seed.

- **Two escapes that are not escapes:**
  - **An invariant read along one orbit only** is not a law of the act. A bounded run visits finitely many states,
    and on a finite set any function is a polynomial. The theorem is about a polynomial kept on every state, as an
    energy law must be.
  - **Rounding the exact rational back to a fixed dial** now and then is a carry whose quotient the force then reads,
    so Theorem A's step (1) applies to that carry, under (D) and (H). Keeping its remainder exactly is Theorem B again.
- **What the one tooth does hold exactly.** On the edge's own dial it holds the momentum, M_out = M_in + 4 J. That is
  why the engine's run back is exact: the act is a bijection on (now, before, tooth) wherever every tooth stands in
  its window. A bijection needs no polynomial invariant.

## 4. Theorem B: the exact rational keeps the invariant and runs away

### 4.1 The exact form

- **The form.** Let the force and the dial read each slot's exact rational, z = a + t / Q: its count, its tooth, and
  the tooth's own dial, with carry(numerator, Q) = (a, t).
  - Then the act is the exact map of section 2. It keeps E4 exactly, and its run back by the same act on the
    mirrored state is exact, since the map is even.
- **MEASURED** (board.py exact). The engine's own sources, weights and plans were run on exact rationals, reduced by
  Euclid's carries in the probe, with the split replaced by the exact quotient:
  - 4E was kept exactly in every round, on the pair world over 6 rounds and on the click world over 5;
  - the run back from the end state alone was exact over those rounds;
  - the 24 were alike in 24 of 24 turns, over 4 rounds on the pair world and over 2 on the click world;
  - the click world's 27 Node-clocks stayed alike.

### 4.2 Its width

- **The dial is a new element.** The dial is the exact rational's denominator. It is not a function of the counts
  (the engine's dials are), so it must be held.
- **Unreduced, on the seed** (as the act would hold it, since the act has no loop and cannot reduce a fraction):
  - z+ = 2WZQ / e - Z- / Q-, with e = (W + s) Q^2 + 2 c Z^2, so Q+ = e Q-;
  - so log Q+ = log Q- + 2 log Q + log(W + s), and the digits grow by 1 + sqrt 2 per round;
  - MEASURED: 24, 70, 185, 461, 1129, 2741, 6634 and 16032 bits; past 128 at round 3.
- **Reduced, on the seed** (the least any exact form holds): the bits grow as the square of the round.
  - The McMillan map is a translation P -> P + Q on the elliptic curve E4 = constant, the composition of its two
    Vieta involutions.
  - The canonical height of P + nQ is h(P) + 2n <P, Q> + n^2 h(Q). It grows as n^2 unless Q is torsion.
  - MEASURED: the second difference of the bits is constant, mean 8.6 at lay 14 and 10.7 at lay 1. Past 128 bits at
    round 5 on both lays.
- **Coupled, reduced:** the coupled step is not integrable, which its heights show (section 2.4).
  - Each family's step reads its own counts once and the other family's twice, so a height can triple in a round
    when nothing cancels. That is a heuristic count, not a proof.
  - MEASURED, pair world: 11, 44, 128, 394, 1176 and 3539 bits at rounds 1 to 6; past 128 at round 4.
  - MEASURED, click world: 19, 50, 153, 462 and 1398 bits; past 128 at round 3.
  - About threefold per round in both cases. Past round 3752 that is some 3^3750 times the lay's digits.

### 4.3 What state it needs, exactly

- **The counting, for the coordinator's question.** The one tooth can hold one of two things, and neither works:
  - **The edge's momentum fraction** (the engine's own convention). Then the positions are the counts, the force
    reads the counts. Theorem A applies (under (D) and (H)): the step keeps no polynomial but those in
    its conserved momenta, so no energy.
  - **One slot's position fraction.** After one round the before slot is the old now, fraction and all. So the exact
    two-step state has two fractions, and one tooth holds one. A second tooth is needed, and each fraction's dial
    compounds (4.2).
- **Each gear's two slots, each an exact rational:** a count, a tooth and the tooth's own dial.
  - Beside the engine's (a, b, s), that is the before slot's own fraction (a second tooth), plus a dial per slot,
    with no bound.
  - The engine's one tooth then holds the now slot's fraction and no longer the edge's momentum.
- **A lower bound for any fixed act.**
  - An act of L operations (+, x and carries) on numbers of at most w bits writes numbers of at most 2^L w + L bits.
  - So a state from which a fixed act computes z_n exactly holds at least (h(z_n) - L) / 2^L bits, and it grows as
    the height does.
  - No fixed new element suffices, the third count and the second tooth included.

### 4.4 No free exact orbit has a bounded width

- **The QRT orbit.**
  - An exact rational orbit of bounded height is a finite set (Northcott), so the translation Q is torsion.
  - Over the rationals a torsion point has order 1 to 10 or 12 (Mazur). So the orbit is periodic with period at most
    12.
  - The seed's exact counts at lay 14 read 14, 3, -8, -16, -12, -1, 10, 15, 8, -3, ..., with highs at rounds 0, 7,
    14, 22 and 29: a beat of about 7.3 rounds at that swing. A small swing beats in about 11.6 rounds, since
    cos theta = mu = 6/7.
  - Its heights grow (MEASURED), so its Q is not torsion.
  - A lay whose orbit were torsion would be a lay chosen so that a turn is exact, which the owner's law forbids. The
    files' lays are not torsion.
  - The coupled step keeps no fibration, so Mazur does not apply to it. Its finite orbits are its periodic points,
    and the shipped lays' heights grow (MEASURED).
- **The linear oscillator.**
  - An integer 2 x 2 matrix keeping a positive definite form has order 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6 (the crystallographic
    restriction).
  - The files' spring alone, polarized, gives z+ + z- = (12/7) z. Its exact orbit from whole counts has denominators
    7^n: about 2.8 bits a round, past 128 near round 46 (DERIVED, not run).
- **The exact form outside the act: finite fields** (named, refused). Modulo a prime p, the QRT map is exact and
  bounded, and the invariant is kept modulo p.
  - But the counts become residues, with no magnitude. The force read on a residue is not the gear's force, and the
    clicks would read wrap-arounds.
  - The inverse also needs Fermat's power or Euclid's loop. It is not the board's motion.

## 7. VARY3's residual (the Boss's question of 07:5x UTC)

- **What Theorem A says about it** (under (D) and (H)). The residual would break a kept quantity only if
  one existed. Theorem A proves that no kept polynomial on the board's (now, before, tooth) is an energy: the kept
  ones are polynomials in the conserved momenta. So the books cannot be made to close against an exactly kept energy
  in whole numbers, by this step or by any step of the act's kind.
  - Precisely: suppose every clock's energy, a polynomial in its counts and teeth, changed in every state by exactly
    its booked total.
  - The books' zero sum would then make the clocks' total energy a polynomial kept on every state. Theorem A
    excludes that: a kept polynomial is a polynomial in the conserved momenta, and an energy is not.
  - So the residual's road cannot run through an exact energy.
- **What road A gives: the residual itself is 0, exactly, on whole counts, for the polarized step's own work form**
  (DERIVED, a polynomial identity).
  - The residual is not the energy's failure but the work form's. It is the part of a monomial's work over the
    window (b, a+) that is not the drop of a function of the edge.
  - For the polarized step, the force on the counts is -grad^pol, with grad^pol_g = A_g(a)(a+_g + b_g) + b_g(a)
    (sections 2.2 and 2.3; its rounding sits in the tooth). Its own work form is
    W_g = -grad^pol_g (a+_g - b_g).
  - For every whole a, b and a+, each monomial's work over the window is exactly the drop of its polarized edge
    potential:

        SUM over the monomial's rows of grad^pol_g (a+_g - b_g) = V_d,m(a+, a) - V_d,m(a, b).

    - The tie, f x^2 k^2: f k^2 (x+^2 - x-^2) + f x^2 (k+^2 - k-^2) = f (x+^2 k^2 + x^2 k+^2) - f (x-^2 k^2 + x^2 k-^2),
      with V_d,m(z-, z) = V_d,m(z, z-).
    - The spring: s (r+^2 - r-^2). The hands' tie: f (x+ y + x y+) - f (x- y + x y-).
  - Summed over the rounds it telescopes, and nothing is left over.
  - The explicit work form, J_g(a)(a+_g - b_g) (node-all's), leaves R = f [e1(b; a) - e1(a; b)] on the four-leg tie,
    because e1 is not symmetric there (CONF1 4.1). V_d is symmetric by construction.
- **With node-all's edging and the polarized work form, what each clock leaves unbooked is the change of a function
  of its edge, round by round.**
  - The edging is the one CONF1 section 3 and VARY3 B1 describe: each edge books (kappa / L)(W_i - W_j), and a train
    keeps (|A| / L) of each monomial's total work unbooked.
  - Each clock's own work then equals its booked passing plus the change of its leg share of the polarized edge
    potential, (|A| / L) V_d,m, which is a function of its edge's two slots. Nothing accumulates that is not a
    function of the state.
  - The residual is 0 as a polynomial identity, not as a reading. A check of it cannot fail, so none is offered as a
    measurement.
  - It holds whatever the rounding does, because it is an identity in (b, a, a+).
  - It does not contradict Theorem A: what it books is the work, not the kinetic energy. The step's teeth move the
    kinetic energy off the work, so the energy is held, not kept.
