## 1. What a mass is in the click world

### 1.1 Nature's reading, which a lawful reading must meet

A body at rest carries a clock. Its phase runs at its Compton rate m c^2 / h, so its own count between two events is
its mass times its proper time (de Broglie's clock). Two bodies at rest side by side count, between the same two light
signals, in the ratio of their masses. A body that travels counts less between two meetings (the twins), and a deeper
one counts less (the redshift). So a mass is a clock's own rate at rest, and a mass ratio is a ratio of two clocks' own
counts between the same two light links.
