## 2. The residual MEASURED on node-all

### 2.1 What is read

probe_vary4.py and probe_split.py, built from both readers' probes. Whole numbers only; Rule3's carry the one division.
- **A clock** is a train t at a Node-clock (the Boss's answer 3). Its sides are (t, u) at its own Node-clock and
  (t, u) across each Spoke.
- **Its booked total,** B_t = SUM_u P(t,u), running, is what its sides carry.
  - The carry books it exactly: key_e N_t = B_t + (its teeth at the start - its teeth now), N_t its net quanta.
  - This is David's identity at a clock: Rule3's identity, which holds while no turn moves a tooth.
- **Its own work on its edged terms,** O_t, running, is kappa times the sum of W_i = J_i(a)(a+ - b) over the terms of
  J's plan with a nonzero edge weight whose leg is in t. W_i is step.passing's own work, before the weights.
  - An own monomial weighs 0 on every edge, so it is no edged term.
- **The part no edge carries** is M_t = O_t - B_t (David's middle).
- **Its teeth:**
  - Of a clock's 35 sides only the 4 sides (t, u), u != t, at its own Node-clock can move. The diagonal's P is 0 by
    antisymmetry, and the 30 across sides' P is 0 by the Mesh couplings at 0 (MEASURED, 0 off). So its teeth hold less
    than 4 edge keys.
  - On this world only 2 of those 4 carry an edge weight, and on L1 only the (held, giver) edge moves. So 4 is a
    generous bound, and counting against it understates every failure below.
- **The reading that can fail:** a clock holds its books only where |M_t| < 4 key_e.

### 2.2 DERIVED: the share by legs, and the split in two

**The share by legs.**
- plans.edging: "a leg's weight kappa (L - l) / L, L the monomial's legs and l its own train's". A held train's
  positions are one leg together.
- Take a monomial on two trains t and u: legs L = l_t + l_u, work W_t on t's legs and W_u on u's, total R = W_t + W_u.
  - The side (t, u) carries w_t W_t - w_u W_u of it.
  - So the clock t keeps kappa W_t - (w_t W_t - w_u W_u) = kappa (l_t / L) R, since kappa - w_t = w_u = kappa l_t / L.
- **Each clock keeps its legs' share of every edged monomial's total work, and no side books it:**
  M_t = SUM over the monomials on t of kappa (l_t / L) R.
  - The tie (the held hand's two positions one leg, the tied pair two): kappa R / 3 at the held clock and 2 kappa R / 3
    at the tied one.
  - A two-leg monomial: kappa R / 2 at each end.
  - Summed over a Node-clock's trains, SUM_t M_t = kappa SUM R, since B summed over the trains is 0. This is work no
    antisymmetric edge can carry.
- On L1 the taker's counts stay 0 (the physicist's; M_taker is 0 throughout, MEASURED), so only the held-giver ties
  work, and 2 M_held = M_giver exactly at every place.

**The split in two.**
- Per term, write T_i(x; y) = J_i(y) x_target. Then:
  - W_i = T_i(a+; a) - T_i(b; a);
  - 2 G_i(n) = T_i(a+; a) + T_i(a; a+), the polarized energy at the window's top;
  - D_i = [T_i(a+; a) - T_i(a; a+)] + [T_i(a; b) - T_i(b; a)], the antisymmetric part over the two windows (a, b) and
    (a+, a).
  - So 2 W_i = 2 G_i(n) - 2 G_i(n - 1) + D_i, exactly.
- Through the clocks' shares, 2 M_t = St_t + Res_t:
  - St_t, the state part: the clock's shares of 2 G now less 2 G at the start, a function of the board's counts alone;
  - Res_t, the residual's share: the running sum of the clock's shares of D.
- **For a two-leg monomial T is symmetric, so D = 0.** Its work is the change of its staggered energy, which is Big
  Pic's f (x_next y_now + x_now y_next) up to J's sign, and it telescopes.
- **For the four-leg tie, D is not 0.** It is the antisymmetric part over the same two windows that the head's NC29
  residual reads, -f (e1 - e3) (VARY3 section 2). So the residual at a place is expected to be the head's miss.
