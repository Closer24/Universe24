## 1. The reference is a choice for the tie (math B1)

### 1.1 The identity holds by definition

### 1.2 VARY4's reference, and the potential-normalized G'

- **Euler at rest.** Take a monomial of k legs (positions), V = f x_1 ... x_k, with the act's push J_i = -d_i V and
  T_i(x; y) = J_i(y) x_i. Then SUM_i T_i(y; y) = -k V(y). So VARY4's per-term 2G = T(a+; a) + T(a; a+), summed over
  the monomial at rest, is -2kV: the form is counted k/2 times against the work.
- **First order** in the counts' step v (b = x - v, a+ = x + v; DERIVED, the mathematician's expansion, re-derived
  here):
  - 2W = -4 v . grad V;
  - 2 dG = -2k v . grad V, since SUM_i x_i d_j d_i V = (k - 1) d_j V;
  - so D = 2W - 2 dG = 2(k - 2) v . grad V.
- **What follows.**
  - For two legs D is 0 at first order.
  - For the tie (k = 4), D = -dG at first order: a difference of a function of the counts, not "the part that is no
    difference".
  - So VARY4's reference counts the tie's form twice, and most of what VARY4 called the residual's share at its peak
    is the coupling's form (1.4).
- **The potential-normalized reference,** G' = (2/k) G, the mathematician's:
  - it is -2V at rest for every monomial;

  - D' = 2W - 2 dG' is second order in v.
  - It is a reading's choice and puts no number into the act. It is read in whole numbers doubled: 4M = St' + Res',
    with St' the clocks' shares of (4/k) 2G now less at the start (probe_ref.py).

### 1.3 D's class (DERIVED)

- **(i) Two legs.** D summed over the monomial is 0, since T is symmetric there, and the reference that makes it 0 is
  unique up to a constant (the mathematician).
  - It is not 0 per term: a two-leg term's own D_i is -f (a+_p a_q - a_p a+_q + a_p b_q - b_p a_q) (math m2;
    MEASURED, 1.4).
  - The clocks' shares see only the monomial's total: the clocks' map gives each clock kappa (l_t / L) of it (VARY4
    2.2). So VARY4's conclusion on two legs stands.
- **(ii) Three legs or more.** No function of the window's counts makes a monomial's work an exact difference on
  today's act (the mathematician's (ii), re-derived here).
  - The work over the window is W = g(a+, a) - g(b, a), with g(c, a) = SUM_i J_i(a) c_i = -f e1(c; a).
  - Suppose W = F(a+, a) - F(a, b) for every b, a and a+.
    - Setting a+ = b gives F(b, a) = F(a, b): F is symmetric.
    - Then g(x, a) - F(x, a) is a function c(a) alone, so g(x, a) - g(a, x) = c(a) - c(x).
  - With every leg row at one value this reads -f k (x a^(k-1) - a x^(k-1)) = c(a) - c(x).
    - Setting x = 0 makes c constant.
    - Then x a^(k-1) = a x^(k-1) for all x and a, which is false for k >= 3 (x = 1, a = 2, k = 3: 4 against 2).
  - The tie's repeated leg changes nothing.
- **So the residual is D's class:** the act's work modulo differences of functions of the window's counts.
