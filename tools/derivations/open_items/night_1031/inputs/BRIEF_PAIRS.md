the same act on its gears (a gear: the count now a, the count before b, a tooth), with addition, multiplication and
Rule3's carry and nothing else (carry(n, d, r) -> (q, r') with n + r = q d + r'). The run back is the same act on the
mirrored state; the cube's 24 turns act on everything. A click is the click tooth's quotient at one Node-clock (term
11: q = +1 a quantum taken, -1 given), written to the record.
