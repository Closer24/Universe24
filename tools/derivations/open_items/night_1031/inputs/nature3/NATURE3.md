**The words, as ruled.**

- A **meeting** is read from what passes: one side passing quanta (q not 0) with its mate, joining two clocks at one

    to the owner's batch (08:4x, 08:5x). NATURE3 counts an across meeting, as both readers did.
- **Causal order** is the order of chains of meetings that share a clock (the 07:5x rule). Its pieces:
  - the **islands** are the clocks joined by meetings in any round;
  - the **reach** follows chains from the record's first round.
  - A clock's first click either is reached or is not. The reading that asks only whether a clock's first partner
    clicked earlier is named **one-step** and stands beside it.
- **Per Node-clock:** on the mesh world a place, a round and a Node-clock, pools clocks of different islands. So every
  per-place count there is per Node-clock, a board site (08:5x).
- A **physics reading** reads the record's lines and sides alone (q only; shadow.py reads q alone). Its parts are the
  meetings and their order (the round as an index of order, never a duration) and each clock's own lines. Light's lines
  come once LIGHT3 gives them.
- A **board check** sets the record against a board field: a tooth, a dial, the lay, the board's passing or the end
  board. A passing recovered through the teeth is a board check (08:5x). A **code check** reads the record's
  faithfulness to the act or the runner's determinism.

  and the teeth's ranges. It does not mean faithful to the act, since the guard reads neither the plan nor the header
  (F1).

  serve as a reading's no (08:5x).
