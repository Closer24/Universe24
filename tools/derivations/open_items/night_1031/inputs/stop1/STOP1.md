## 3. HAT2's dg covers a row held four times (DERIVED; a correction to CONF1 7 (iii) and HAT2 1)

- **HAT2's polarization** pairs each monomial's legs as the plans lay them, (legs 1, 2)(legs 3, 4):
  V_d(z+, z) = SUM_m f_m [P1_m(z+) P2_m(z) + P1_m(z) P2_m(z+)], with V_d(z, z) = 2V.
  - For a pair holding a row twice, P(a+) - P(b) = (a+_r - b_r)(a+_r + b_r).

- **The difference over the window** (b, a+) is V_d(a+, a) - V_d(a, b) = SUM_r (a+_r - b_r) G_r. G_r sums, over
  every pair holding r twice, f times the other pair at a times (a+_r + b_r), and, over every pair holding r once with
  the unit, f times the other pair at a.
- **The stop's x_r^4 is (x_r x_r)(x_r x_r).** The row stands twice in each of its two pairs, so G_r gains
  2 f a_r^2 (a+_r + b_r): linear in the row's own next count. Its V_d is 2 f a+_r^2 a_r^2, and its difference
  factors as (a+_r - b_r) 2 f a_r^2 (a+_r + b_r).
- **The step** W_r (a+_r - 2 a_r + b_r) = -G_r is HAT2's (W + kappa / 2)(a+ - 2a + b) = J(a). kappa_r sums 2 f times
  the other pair at a over every pair holding r twice, so a row held four times gains 4 f a_r^2. On a tied train
  under the stop, kappa_r = 4 lam c_T(a) on every row (the entries 1).
- **It keeps E_n = SUM_r W_r (a+_r - a_r)^2 + V_d(a+, a) in exact rationals.** The difference is
