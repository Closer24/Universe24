## 1. The mass reading in clicks

### 1.1 Nature's reading, in the FACE2 frame

- **In nature** a body at rest carries a clock whose phase runs at its Compton rate m c^2 / h. That phase is never
  observed alone: what is observed is a difference of phases, two clocks compared.
- **In the FACE2 frame** that comparison is two clocks' own counts between the same two light links:
  - two bodies at rest side by side count in the ratio of their masses;
  - a body that travels counts less between two meetings (the twins);
  - a deeper one counts less (the redshift).
- So a mass ratio is read as a ratio of own counts, never as one clock's count alone, and only where depth and motion
  are the same for both clocks.
