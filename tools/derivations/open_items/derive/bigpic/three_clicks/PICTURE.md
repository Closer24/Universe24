## 7. The law of the three clicks, and its proof

**The law** (for Sofer to enter): at every Node-clock the structure's edges and triangles make three clicks. The first click: each edge carries its own share of the passing by Rule3's carry on its one pair of teeth, born complementary, the far end clicking the opposite quotient; it draws nothing. The split click: where three clocks are pairwise joined, the passing splits by the structure, 3 x = (3 x - C C^T x) + C C^T x, with no division and no condition; an edge on no triangle keeps its whole share, as the first click. The third click: each triangle's own tooth, turned with its hidden twin by the palindrome at C^T C between the carries, carries the triangle's circulating passing C^T x, and each quantum it lands books one whole circulation around the triangle in one round. So every clock's line is the carry of its own passing, every clock's books hold within its teeth, the third click is never seen alone in any clock's line, and its choice is the path. The click teeth run back by the same act on the mirrored state, the label rule holds draw by draw, and the whole has no choice (the law of the path).

**The setting.** At one Node-clock: clocks V; edges E, each a pair of clocks a term joins; triangles F, three clocks pairwise joined, each edge on at most one (so C^T C = 3 I). B (V x E) is each clock's sign on each edge, +1 at its lower end and -1 at its higher; C (E x F) is each edge's sign on a triangle's boundary, [j, k] - [i, k] + [i, j]. D is the edge dial, odd (D = 3 K, the key odd), x in Z^E the round's passings, y = C^T x. One round: (i) eta += 3 tau, then tau += 3 eta, about the birth on the dial 3D; (ii) (Q_e, g_e) = carry(3 x_e - (C y)_e, 3D, g_e) and (W_f, tau_f) = carry(y_f, 3D, tau_f); (iii) step (i) in reverse order; (iv) book q = Q + C W, each edge's two ends q_e and -q_e.

- **L0, B C = 0.** At a triangle's vertex, its two edges there carry boundary signs whose products with that vertex's incidence signs cancel: the boundary of a boundary is 0. Checked exactly on the click world (kturn.py).
- **L1, each edge's books close:** its ends book q_e and -q_e in the same round, by (iv).
- **L2, each clock's line is its own:** B q = B Q + B C W = B Q. The line reads the edge teeth and the passings alone, never tau or eta.
- **L3, each clock's books hold within its teeth:** summing the carry's law 3D Q_e = g_e(before) + p_e - g_e(after) over the rounds and applying B, with B p = 3 B x - B C y = 3 B x, gives D (sum of the clock's line) - (sum of its swept passing) = (B (g at the start - g at the end)) / 3, less than D times the clock's number of edges.
- **L4, never seen alone:** by L2, a change in the hidden pair changes no clock's line, only C W, a whole circulation on the sides.
- **L5, the first click is the structure's own case:** an edge on no triangle has (C y)_e = 0 and carries 3 x_e at 3D from the birth 3B + 1 (B = (D - 1) div 2), whose quotients are floor((3B + 1 + 3s) / 3D) = floor((B + s) / D), the first click's at D, for every whole s. No condition separates the cases.
- **L6, an idle triangle lands nothing:** y_f = 0 gives carry(0, 3D, tau) = (0, tau), as 0 <= tau < 3D.
- **L7, the run back:** (i) and (iii) are shears between tau, kept by the mirror, and eta, flipped; for each, mirror, shear, mirror is the shear's inverse, and the carries keep the sign law (carrying -x undoes carrying x, the quotient negated). So the same act on the mirrored state with every passing negated runs the click teeth back exactly; the board runs back by its own untick, and nothing else is kept.
- **L8, the label rule:** relabelling clocks alike in kinds moves B and C by signed permutations and leaves the round's form unchanged; with D odd the birth is its own complement, so a tooth read from its other end is its centred negation, which the linear shears commute with. The relabelled run is the original relabelled, draw by draw.
- **L9, the whole has no choice:** by L7 the act on board and teeth is one-to-one, so the end fixes the start; two runs one hidden unit apart end on one board (the board reads no tooth) and apart only in the teeth.
- **Measured, not proved:** the circulation's landings carry no memory (1,000,000 rounds, within one standard error) and their lives follow the geometric law; one hidden unit moves sides only at its own Node-clock; the 24 hold, lines and sides.
