## 1. In the clocks (the owner's order: the clocks first)

1. **A quantum is one winding of a train's hand.** Two equal quanta are two hands of one train and one sense at one tick (E's reading of line 1562's open question).
2. **Every act of the board moves one hand alone**: the one formula, the hop across a Mesh, the turnover at the top, the rewind. Each act is the same on every hand, so no act can tell a pair from the pair exchanged. Exchanging two equal quanta and then ticking is the same as ticking and then exchanging. So the board never chooses whether a pair is added or subtracted. The pair's sign can only be read where two hands are read together, and that is a click.
3. **A Node-clock's own reading has no direction.** Every act commutes with the 24, so a Node-clock reading two hands with no detector reads a directionless (A1) number of the two.
4. **The click adds the two ways' amplitudes** (the owner's step 1, 11:1x UTC). So the joint reading of two quanta is linear in each quantum's hand: the pair's dial is a bilinear form of the two hands.
5. **The 24 leave exactly one such dial for each kind** (Schur): B(psi1, psi2) = <T psi1, psi2>, one quantum's hand against the other's phase reversal.
   - On the twin hands it is det[psi1, psi2]; on a hand, the product; on vectors, the dot.
   - In the law's own picture (lines 1005 and 1021) the click is the meeting of a hand run forward with a hand read back, and on the board a hand read back is the hand turned over.
6. **Exchanging the two quanta turns that dial by T T**, the turnover twice, which is the full turn of one quantum: B(psi2, psi1) = (T T) B(psi1, psi2) = B(psi1, z psi2).
   - On the twin hands T T = -1. This is the ratchet's carry of two teeth: two turnovers reverse a twin hand and four return it.
   - On the 24's kinds T T = +1.
7. **The one clock fact the board lacks: the pair dial.** Every train's two equal quanta at one Node-clock can click as one directionless event.
   - The two ways must then be added with the sign that keeps the dial, eps = T T.
   - With the other sign the dial is 0 for every pair of that train, and no Node-clock would ever see two equal quanta of it as one event.
8. **What follows is draft E's,** under the same sign at every pair of parts:
   - at one part the amplitude is (1 + eps) times one product, so two equal twin quanta never click in one state (Pauli) and light bunches;
   - a third twin quantum has no state at one Node-clock;
   - the books close, and no sign is ever written on the board.
9. **Rule3 in it:** the click is Rule3's carry on the pair's wind, c (sigma) = t room + r_t, as in law 6. Where the dial is 0, the quotient is 0 and nothing is booked. The two windings stay in their own hands and the remainder stays on the dial (Sofer's two checks, E's part 6). Every bound is a carry (law 11): the zero is the arithmetic's (1 + eps) = 0, never a refusal.

## 6. The proofs (protocol step 2)

- **P1, superselection.** Let S be any act linear and the same on each quantum's hand (the step, a hop, the rewind). On the labelled pair, (S x S)(u x v) = S u x S v, and the exchange P gives P (S x S)(u x v) = S v x S u = (S x S) P (u x v). By linearity this holds on every pair state.
  - The turnover tau x tau is antilinear and still the same on each hand, so it too commutes with P.
  - So both eigenspaces of P are kept by every act, and no act selects one. CHECKED (j1 part 5).
- **P2, the turnover at parts and on the dial.** T is antiunitary, so <T x, T y> = conj <x, y>.
  - Hence M_aj becomes conj M_aj, and the reversed-order sum is eps times the forward sum. Its norm is unchanged for eps = +-1.
  - B(T u, T v) = <T T u, T v> = conj <T u, v> = conj B(u, v).
  - B(v, u) = <T v, u> = <T u, T T v> = (T T) <T u, v> = (T T) B(u, v), using <T x, T y> = <y, x> and T T = +-1 real.
  - Then B(T v, T u) = (T T) B(T u, T v) = (T T) conj B(u, v).
  - CHECKED (j1 parts 1 and 2).
- **P3, the run back on the A1 part.** With Q_eps(u, v) = B(u, v) + eps B(v, u) = (1 + eps T T) B(u, v):
  - eps conj Q_eps(T v, T u) = eps conj[(1 + eps T T)(T T) conj B(u, v)] = (eps T T) Q_eps(u, v).
  - So the run back keeps a present A1 part iff eps T T = 1. For eps = -T T, Q is identically 0.
  - With the order kept, conj Q_eps(T u, T v) = Q_eps(u, v) for either eps, so no selection. CHECKED (j1 part 3, mutant M3).
- **P4, the hop.** The lifts of the quarter turns about a are generated by Q_a = 1 + H_a, which is diagonal with two distinct entries (1 - i, 1 + i) in the basis of sigma_a. Its commutant is the diagonal algebra, span{1, H_a}.
  - Units commuting with T: diag(a, conj a) with a a Gaussian unit, that is +-1 and +-H_a.
  - Covariance: Q_a H_b Q_a^-1 = H_(R_a b) and Q_a 1 Q_a^-1 = 1.
  - **Staggering:** with g(x) = H_x^x1 H_y^x2 H_z^x3, take g(x + e_a)^-1 H_a g(x).
    - The powers of the axes after a cancel directly.
    - The new H_a meets the powers of H_a once it is moved past the powers H_b^(x_b) of the axes before a. Each H_b there anticommutes with H_a and contributes (-1)^(x_b).
    - The powers of H_a then cancel to H_a^0.
    - So the transport is (-1)^(x_1 + ... + x_(a-1)) = eta_a(x), and the plaquette product of the eta's is -1.
