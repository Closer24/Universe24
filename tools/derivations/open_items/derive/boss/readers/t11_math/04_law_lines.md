# Section 6's law lines checked against the derivation and the measurements

Item 1 (line 35 (4), new text):
- "D = now^2 - next x before summed over its gears": the law as written sums "over the Node-clock and its six
  neighbours"; the six neighbours are dropped without a word, and "next" enters where the law had "now and before".
  Measured consequence (probe_e): a free train over five Node-clocks clicks at every one it reaches.
- "the lay's held wind is the one quantum per Node-clock of decision 40 (g)": 40 (g) is the coupling family's
  steady count, one per Node-clock; D_lay is one per TRAIN per Node-clock. Misread, now in a law line.
- "the click is where n moves between two rounds, the wind given or taken, read alike in both runs": the COUNT n is
  alike; the click is -q on the backward run (probe_b2 c: 5,999 of 5,999). Say "the count alike, the click
  reversed: a take read as a give".
- "a clock that keeps its beat does not click": fails for this count at A = 10^3 (free hand 4,731 clicks) and for
  a travelling train (probe_e). Holds for the count only at large swing on one Node-clock.
- "two clocks connecting or coming apart by one winding": |q| up to 10 and 95 at smaller keys (probe_b2 a).
- "plus the half key": the text's k_0 = (key - 1) div 2 is not the law's (i) birth key div 2 at an even key;
  equal at odd keys. Name (i), or say (key - 1) div 2 and why.
- "the carry is the hand's wind less its lay's held wind plus the half key = n x whole winding + remainder": the
  remainder is recomputed each round and kept nowhere; calling it a remainder/tooth suggests a dial that is not there.
Item 2 (line 35 (5)): the clock's click, section 1, not in my brief.
Items 3 to 6: handedness and the 48, not in my brief (section 2's claim that the wind count keeps the 48 is an
identity: D is a sum of each gear's products with itself; the "measured 24 of 24 (TERM11 Measured 4)" is the AREA
click on the draft act, not this count).
