 Reading: the floor at an odd key and the half-open split have both the books and 'one key, one click'; the centre-0 tie-even
 split has the books at every key and, at a tooth on its edge, counts a whole key as 0 or 2 clicks (the lost shift covariance is
 not harmless for a count); CLICK25's half-tooth birth at u = key has 'one key, one click' and misses the books at even keys once
 per key of area. No odd tie rule is shift covariant (f(1 - q0) = not f(q0) forbids f(q0 + k) = f(q0)), so this is a theorem,
 not a choice: a count's tooth cannot be born on the mirror's fixed point and be shift covariant at a dial with a middle tooth.
