## The books fix the form and the strength
Total energy H = sum_rows H_r (each depends on the slots through W and the grips) + the slot gears' own H. A slot gear
pushed by P gains 4 P of momentum a round; its energy changes by P x its velocity (d(M^2 / 32 W) = M dM / 16 W = P q).
The rows' energies change by (dH_r / d slot) x the slot's velocity. So dH / dt = sum_slots (P_slot + dH / d slot) q_slot,
and the books close iff P_slot = - dH / d slot for every slot, at every site, every round:
  P_00(x)  = + k0 (1/2) sum_{rows r reading W} v_r,in v_r,out (x)            (the kinetic energy's W-dependence)
  P_ii(x)  = - k1 (1/2) sum_rows [ (Delta+_i a_r)^2 + (Delta-_i a_r)^2 ](x)  (the two Meshes along i that end at x)
  P_jk = P_0i = 0                                                             (no reader: a push there is energy from nothing)
