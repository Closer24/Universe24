## 1. The sign and gamma (P-1, M-5), probe_g.py: out_g.txt

**Clock.** The 00 slot is counted in the teeth: one count of c00 is 1/rest of a clock's dial. An ii slot is counted
in the grips, and a Mesh reads it at both its ends: one count of a uniform ii is 2/grip_rest of a Mesh's grip. The
teeth are -g^00 (the dial a clock divides by), the grips are g^ii (what light divides by): lifting the index flips
h's sign, so the two kinds of slot must be pushed with opposite signs by one source. As built: +S J00 on the 00 slot,
-J on the others.

**Algebra: -1/12 is -c^2/2 per slot, derived, not CHOSEN.** For dust J00 = J_ii = T00. One coupling pushes the 00

delta grip / grip = -2 J / grip (both ends). Equal and opposite: W = rest (1 + u), grip = grip_rest (1 - u) at first
