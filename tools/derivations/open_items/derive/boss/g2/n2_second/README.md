## 4. Algebra: the theorem

**Statement (M5).** Let every round be the first-order form above, with (a) every r_d within [0, T_d); (b) every
monomial of the plan with an even count of odd legs counting its target as a leg (a stride plane and a 0i row are odd;
a count of another row and the unit are even); (c) the clock's read and the grips' read landing even rows only; (d)
pass 2 reading only the strides of rows it does not push; and the clock's reads acyclic (no row's teeth reading its
own count, directly or through other rows). Then the mirrored state is R_P's after the drift undone, and R, the tick,
R returns the state before, exact at every W of at least 1 from every such r_d, the clock rising or falling.

**Proof sketch.** The tick is a drift D after a shear K: K changes p and r_d at fixed a, D changes a at fixed p and
reads W from a. (1) D is inverted by carries in the order of the reads: a row reading no row comes back at the first
carry, a row reading only such rows at the second, and so on; a cycle has no inverse (M4: the 00 row reading itself
sends two counts to one image; a cycle of two even rows sends 1600 states to 1599 images). (2) R_P leaves an even
row's push as it is and negates an odd row's, by (b): every reading of a monomial is a count at the now slot (the
pivot) or a stride, and under R_P the strides map (sigma_b, sigma_a) to (-sigma_a, -sigma_b) because (W - 1 - p) div W
= -(p div W) at every W of at least 1, while a 0i row's count negates; the monomial's sign is its target's. (3) On an
even row, (W - 1 - p) + push = W - 1 - (p - push), and the drive's flipped tooth gives the quotient's negative with the
flipped remainder (the carry's sign law), by (a). (4) sigma_a as pass 2 reads it is the stride the row actually takes
only for a row pass 2 does not push, by (d); W and the grips are read from even rows, by (c), so the mirrored state
reads the same teeth and grips. Hence R_P K R_P is K's inverse, and no tooth is read at another round's teeth: the
rate p carries its own remainder inside it. The hypotheses are each load-bearing: every broken case departs (M1).

**rho and the pressure (M3).** With J_mu nu = 2 T_mu nu - eta_mu nu T: 4 rho = J00 + sum_i J_ii and 4 pr = J00 - (sum_i
J_ii) / 3 (the trace, since the gradient's pressure is anisotropic). S00 = sigma_b^2 + sigma_a^2 is never below 0; J00
= S00 - 2V is below 0 wherever the potential rules, as rho + 3 pr is for a held potential (rho = V, pr = -V).

**The 00 source in the act's energy units (P3).** A gear's energy at teeth W is E = (W / 2) sigma^2 + (g / 2) a^2
(W sigma^2 + g a a+ is kept by the act up to the carries), so rho + 3 pr = 4K - 2V = W (sigma_b^2 + sigma_a^2) - g a^2:
the stride terms carry W = rest + c00 as a leg (even under the reversal, the pivot's count), and the potential's
strength over the kinetic's is g / W, the act's own, no ratio typed. Its mean over a swing is E (measured 1.019). The
overall scale is the coupling's (G3).
