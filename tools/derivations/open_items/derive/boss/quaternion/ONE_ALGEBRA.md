## Clock

- A hand is four gears at one Node-clock, counted together: a scalar gear and three along the Spokes. Turning the
  board by one of the cube's 24 turns moves the three Spoke gears among themselves, with signs; the scalar gear stays.
  Every such turn is a whole quaternion g held on both sides of the hand, g h conj(g), divided by g's own norm, 1 or 2;
  g and -g are one turn. The six Spokes are the six pure units +-i, +-j, +-k.
- Every round every gear of the hand is carried once at the teeth W of its Node-clock, the same W for the four gears
  (the 00 slot's count plus the rest), so the hand is carried as one: four quotients leave, four fine teeth stay.
  Nothing in the round multiplies two hands, except where the coupling's plan reads a product of two readings: there
  the product is Hamilton's (the dot and the cross of two Spoke hands are its real and vector parts).
- A half-speed hand (the families' spinor) is turned from one side alone, g psi, and a full turn of the board sends
  it to -psi. For its four (or eight) gears to be carried together so that the turned hand is carried as the hand
  turned, the fine teeth must be kept in a dial the turns move into itself. A dial of W teeth per gear is such a dial
  only when W has no factor 2 or 3: the full turn (-1) pins a nonzero tooth whenever W is even (the tooth at W / 2),
  and a third turn pins one whenever 3 divides W; a pinned nonzero tooth would have to be a point the turn fixes, and a
  one-sided turn fixes no point but 0. At such W the hand's teeth cannot be kept in any dial that turns.
- The clock's own now and before are a second kind of turn: a hand with a spring k at teeth W moves in its (now,
  before) plane by [[2 - k/W, -1], [1, 0]]; when W divides k that is an exact whole turn of order 6, 4 or 3 (k = W,
  2 W, 3 W) and the fine teeth never move; at k = 2 W the pair (now, before) is a Gaussian integer turned by i every
  round. Otherwise the teeth move and the round is no turn of any ring.
- The metric clock's slots are the four-vector's square: the act reads its 00 slot as every gear's teeth and its ii
  slots as the Meshes' grips, both as plain whole counts; its 0i and jk slots are read by no gear as built.
