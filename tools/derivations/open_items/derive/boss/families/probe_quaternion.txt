The families from the quaternion (probe_quaternion.py)
1. The eight kinds from the ring's 48 units, each checked against the engine's kinds on the turn it covers.
   T1, the sandwich, is the engine's turn on 48 of 48 units (two to a turn); A1 the real part, kept by every sandwich;
   A2 = sigma(g) / g is +-1 on 48 of 48, equal to the engine's A2 on 48 of 48: -1 exactly on the 24 units whose
   coordinates carry sqrt 2 (the odd turns' lifts); T2 = sigma(g) v conj(g) is the engine's T2 on 48 of 48;
   |T1| on the three axes has the character 1 + chi_E on 48 of 48: E rides with A1 there (the square's ii slots).
   E1/2 = g psi, the left turn of Lambda (probe_spinor 1, eight gears, -1 = -I); E5/2 = sigma(g) psi, the same matrices
   in another order, one whole kind with E1/2 (the quarter turns' traces sqrt 2 and -sqrt 2 summing to 0);
   G3/2 = E1/2 x E (probe_spinor 5, sixteen gears, two copies of the eight-gear least form of probe_induced 1).

2. Each whole-speed kind's train on the act, J through a term pairing it with another train by the engine's own
   tables, W = 9, 4 rounds (the norm alone, a1 (k . k), holds under every signed permutation of the gears, 48 of 48 for
   the random control too: the anchor's tie tells no kind; the kinds are told by the tables that are not the norm).
   A1, the real part, in k (t1 . t1): 1 gear, -1 acts as +I; 3 pushes; the 48 lifts hold 48 of 48; random signed permutations in the kind's place 16 of 48; the table with one entry raised 16 of 48
   A2, the field's second sign, in k (t1 . t2): 1 gear, -1 acts as +I; 3 pushes; the 48 lifts hold 48 of 48; random signed permutations in the kind's place 24 of 48; the table with one entry raised 16 of 48
   T1, the sandwich, in a1 (k . t1): 3 gears, -1 acts as +I; 3 pushes; the 48 lifts hold 48 of 48; random signed permutations in the kind's place 0 of 48; the table with one entry raised 16 of 48
   T2, the sign times the sandwich, in a2 (t1 . k): 3 gears, -1 acts as +I; 3 pushes; the 48 lifts hold 48 of 48; random signed permutations in the kind's place 0 of 48; the table with one entry raised 16 of 48
   A1 + E, the axes permuted, in a1 times its own table: 3 gears, -1 acts as +I; 9 pushes; the 48 lifts hold 48 of 48; random signed permutations in the kind's place 8 of 48; the table with one entry raised 16 of 48
   (the half-speed kinds, G3/2 on eight gears and spin 1/2 on twenty-four, with their T1 tables: probe_induced 4.)

3. The vertex's two tables as one product: (0, v)(0, w) = (-v . w, v x w) against the engine's tables.
   the A1 table is +(v . w) = - the product's real part on 20 of 20 pairs; the epsilon table is +(v x w), its vector part, on 20 of 20:
   one quaternion product of two vector hands gives the dot and the cross, the two tables the plan lays (SYM24 plan.txt).

4. A hand's before times its now on the ring: conj(b) a read at once as the dot and three windings; the norm's four squares.
   N(conj(b) a) = N(b) N(a) on 20 of 20 pairs of hands of Lambda (the Gaussian basis): the norm multiplies;
   conj(b) a = (the dot, the i winding) + j (the determinant's two parts) on 20 of 20: one product reads the hand's
   in-step overlap (its energy's and anchor's part, the norm when b = a) and its three windings (the charges).
   The anchor's tie, the own spring f (h . h), is f times the real part of N(h): one tie for every kind.
