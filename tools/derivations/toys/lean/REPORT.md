# The lean Node toy (tools/derivations/toys/lean), David's report of 2026-10-25

A generic Node of 119 integers (95 with the metric's six zero lines apart), every integer within 128 bits, the same acts at every Node, built from the engine's exact primitives (core.rule3, core.paces, features.phase, loader.circle, features.volume) with the architecture the toy's; its decoder reads the run's log alone, in integers; the run back is exact. The toy is a derivation tool, not the engine: it names what the law does on a lean Node and what it does not do, with numbers.

Files: `lean_full.py` (the Node and the board, built from the two files and nothing else), `decoder_full.py` (the gates read from the record alone, every verdict from the record's numbers), `runback_test.py`, `cube48_test.py` (the 48 images), `mirror_diag.py` (where one mirror breaks), `residuals.py` (the one-click floor), `freeze_diag.py`, `records/` (the gates' tables), `universe_toy.json` (the integers den, node_clock, quantum_action, link_unit; the rows by shape and pair: the light at (den, den) and the three lifts) and `world_toy.json` (the shape, the intervals, the seed as lays: the sea with its hole, one quantum more at (1,0,0)). No number of a universe stands in the toy's code: the two held rows are the law's (the volume at (den - den div Gamma, den), the sign at (den, den)), the phase pair's amplitude is derived from half the law's width 128 less one, every root is the law's search `largest_below`. The probes that read the 4.0 record are under `../probes`.

Run: `PYTHONPATH=src python tools/derivations/toys/lean/lean_full.py <world.json> <out.json> [back N]` (the Link close by the light's current, the law's; `back N` runs N intervals back at the end and writes the result on the record; the former modes `link` and `node` and every environment switch were struck on 2026-10-25 with the fold: the two files decide everything), then `python tools/derivations/toys/lean/decoder_full.py <out.json>`. The runs below are at the den 15162856759112389 (54 bits, three lifts), the cube 5, Gamma 1000, T 36000, G 16, as `universe_toy.json` has them; the runs before the rebuild took the same numbers on the command line.

## The acts in the two shapes (the owner's law of 2026-10-25)

act(row), atomic to the row (one function, applied to every row by one loop over the list):

1. Rule3's step of a line: inputs the line's three integers at the Node, the same line's now at the six neighbours, the row's pair, the Node's paces, the six Link factors; one carried division at the wall w. For a plane the arrival is read in the Node's frame (im negated where the two ends' frame bits differ), turned by the Link's pair (cos, sin) over X with one rounding and by the odd line's tangent triple (W^2 - n^2, 2 W n, W^2 + n^2), W = 4 Gamma, with one rounding (`step_line`, `step_plane`).
2. The share of a row at a Node: the frame's term 2 [w (now^2 + before^2) - S now before - now SUM_j R_j before_j] - SUM_j R_j (before - b_j)(now + n_j), one division by 4 p^2 G^2 (`share`, the engine's share.share with the halves).
3. The standing and the beat of a record: the share at or above the row's unit over one period of the re line's upward crossings (`bodies`).
4. The tension's part of a row on each axis: weight x (now(i+a) - now(i-a))^2 (The stress is the Links'; `stress_parts`, one act on every line alike).

7. The Link's count: K = Phi_next - Phi_before = 2 (Phi_now - Phi_before) + c at each end, n_ij = K_i - f_ij K_j, f the frame sign (`centred_count`, `link_phases`, `frames`).
8. The swap at the top: the weak maximum X0 <= X1 >= X2 with one strict on the volume's time line (the held before, the held now, the now after the writes), or the floor X0 < X_f or X2 < X_f with X_f = volume_of(Gamma, -Gamma); the turned Node ends the interval at the state it started from with every line's two levels exchanged and every remainder complemented at its wall, the im lines conjugated, the write remainders complemented from the held ones, the step's output discarded (`swap`, `exchange`).

## The Node as four functions, the Link as one, the shapes as functions (the owner's words of 2026-10-25: one function steps a line; the Link's act generic and atomic in one place; fold everything that can be folded)

- `across(a, port)`: the one read across a Link (the array's value at the Node beyond the Port, the torus wrapped); `link(port, even, odd, frame_bits)`: the Link's numbers at one Port read the same from both ends (the symmetric sums, the antisymmetric sums signed by the side, the frame sign). Every Link read of the toy passes here.
- the shapes: `gradient`, `wronskian`, `plane_current`, `stress_parts`, `tensor_parts`, and the Node's `link_current`.
- `rotate(lines, r, turn, direction, flux)`: Rule3 on a row's lines at the Node's paces, the arrivals turned as the row's shape turns them (`turned_arrival`), one carried division at the wall w per line, the phase row's write inside its division (its flux); direction -1 the same function with the division's direction -1.
- `write(line, numerator, direction)`: the one write act on a line at the line's write wall, the remainder carried in the line; direction -1 the quotient taken back.
- `act(lines, r, turn, numerators, direction)`: the Node's act on one row, rotate then write (forward), the writes undone then the rotate inverted (backward). `run(direction)` calls it on every row in the rows' order (the phase row, the planes, the massless row, the sign's row, the volume's row), the numerators the law's writes: the volume's time line from every form less the pressure, its axis lines from the traceless stress, the sign's time line from every plane's Wronskian, its odd lines from every plane's current, the massless line from the Wronskian's change (W-S1).
- `swap` and `exchange`: the trigger on the gapped time line, one exchange over every row alike; backward the same exchange returns the turned Nodes' pre-state first.
- `close`: the Link close by the current, the bodies' closing, the detectors' light credits; `unclose` from the log.
