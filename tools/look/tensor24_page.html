Two views of one run, each named as what it is. The board holds the clocks: the Node-clocks and their trains, a clock fact. The click world holds the clicks: each click line a quantum passing between two clocks at one place, in the record's order. The decoder's readings stand beside them, each with its verdict as the decoder gives it. The round is the record's order alone, never a duration.
