# What changed

One entry per version, the newest first; what the engine does differently, with the files, and what the documents say. The law is [docs/ALGEBRA.md](docs/ALGEBRA.md), the engine's document [docs/ENGINE.md](docs/ENGINE.md), the decisions in force [docs/HIGHLIGHTS.md](docs/HIGHLIGHTS.md).

## Version 2.0.0 (2026-10-11), the tag v2.0.0, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23289515

The engine V2, on main: Rule3 only. Against 1.1.0, by theme.

- **The rule and its acts.** Every act on a level is Rule3's one carried division with the remainder kept; everything else is a coefficient's one division with the remainder discarded or a search by comparing integer products. No shear, no rotation act, no sine or cosine, no root, no float, no table: `features/rotation` is deleted, `division_fixed_point` is deleted, and the one search is `core/rule3.largest_below`, the largest integer whose square is at most a count, found by bisection on the share reading (`src/event_universe/core/rule3.py`, `resonance.py`, `emission.py`, `loader/derived.py`, `tools/pixel_mode.py`).
- **The families' phase and gravity, the fold form.** The sign's Link phase and gravity's odd lines ride one fold: each Link's read is a pair (X^c, X^s) in the Link unit with one rounding per part. The time turn is replaced by the phase line, a Rule3 pair at one fixed angle iterated by a level (`features/phase`, the NodeState's `phases`, `node.phased`, `plane.link_pairs`, `plane.sign_pairs`), and the Link's count carries each end's clock as one coefficient rounding (`core/paces.turn_factor`). The amplitude bounds under the fold: `examples/events/turning.json` 7,591 (5,961 at the tag), `pair_atom` 29,482 (23,302 at the tag), `charged.json` 9,266 unchanged.
- **The click and its momentum.** Rule3 conserves a lattice momentum exactly in the rationals at uniform paces, to a bound each interval on the integers, a theorem of the law (ALGEBRA.md, The lattice momentum). The piece's momentum is read at the detector and booked, and at a click the taker is given the piece's momentum as far as it can hold it, the rest booked, by a twist of its lines found by bisection on the phase line to the quarter turn's cap, the inverse through the click exact; the click has two receivers, the taker's part folded by +p_a and the piece's source's part by -p_a at the click's tick by one twist, the source drawn among the emitters by the quanta each still holds, the file's lay an entry in that draw by its laid count (`twist.py`, the click's momentum and its items, `meeting.py`, `credit.py`, `node_detector.py`, `bookings.py`, `features/click`, `features/currents`).
- **The window's turn.** A record declared a NodeDetector at one Node reads its window's turn by carried divisions on the labels, with the hop pair from the phase line (`resonance.window_pair`, `resonance.hopped`); no root at the close.
- **The write and the wiping.** The null window's re-lay keeps each Node's own direction, so the fold survives a body's null close; a region that takes folds no source and books "P lost to the region" and "unpaid by the region"; a click's write at a count above 1 lays the entered part in one direction and wipes the part's earlier gradient, an open item by name (`meeting.py`, `lattice.py`, `node.py`).
- **The remainder's carry.** Every act on a level keeps its remainder between intervals: Rule3's step, the phase pair, the window pair, the held write and the write carried forward and back, the twist. The acts table names every carried act, the step's and the ones outside it, and every division of `src/event_universe` with its kind: 202 call sites under 129 keys, R carried on levels (19), D a coefficient's division once (86), S a search by comparison (24); a new division anywhere fails the table until it is listed (`tests/test_the_acts_table.py`).
- **The report's record and the credit line.** The credit line carries six new keys, momentum, fan, twist and lost from the write, source and recoil from the two receivers; the two slits' output equals the tag's oracle bit for bit with the six keys stripped (`credit.py`, `reports.py`, `records.py`, `features/read`).
- **The modules.** The engine is nineteen modules: the click's momentum and its items stand in `twist.py` (the twist, the fold, the two receivers, the re-lay) beside `meeting.py`, the readings of a record's lines in `readings.py` beside `node.py`, which re-exports them, the rotation unit K = omega_0 Gamma in `features/phase` (`phase.rotation_unit`, a feature importing core alone) and the turned plane's room in `plane.py` (`plane.turned_room`); every module within the shape's limits or at its count of the tag.
- **The tests and the gates.** The suite grows from 150 tests at the tag to 212, the new files `tests/test_part_a_fold.py` to `tests/test_part_h_fix.py`, `tests/test_the_phase_line.py`, `tests/test_the_acts_table.py` and the gates' additions in `tests/test_the_gates.py` and `tests/test_rule3.py`. Three gates are the contributor's law and run on every test run: no root anywhere in src, no shear, no rotation act and no trigonometric call, and the acts table. The documents' three gates on the law and the engine's document (the history clauses, the numbers table, the derivation scripts) run unchanged. The tests' oracles stand under `tests/oracle/`: the two slits' look file of the tag's engine (`tools/look/record.py` over 130 intervals, compressed), the Coulomb run's record before the phase line and the one photon's output before the two receivers, each read bit for bit by its test.
- **The documents.** ENGINE.md describes the engine as main holds it, its section 10 the known issues of the tag (the shelved ion at z = 7 with its refusal at 18,099, the Zeno worlds not re-run at the frozen hash, the holders' swing under a body laid off the step's fixed point) and the engine V2's open items by name: the two-clock closure's integer, emitters whose light leaves the board, the fan's remainder off-centre, the per-source share, the one-Node write, the dipole, the front's delay, the region's +p, a click's write at a count above 1, and `examples/events/shelved_ion` refused by the amplitude bound. The law carries the click's algebra on V2 (The click's algebra beyond the law, The lattice momentum) and the open items 46, 49 and 50 of What is open as the series wrote them. CONTRIBUTING.md states the gates as the rule for the engine.
- **The papers' folder.** The papers stand in `papers/`, one folder each: the first paper moved from `paper/` to `papers/one_rule/`, its content unchanged byte for byte (a three-line `paper/README.md` points to it; at the tag v1.1.0 the old path stands forever), and the time paper, "A discrete spacetime of events in whole numbers: why clocks slow, why nothing outruns light, and why time has a direction only where something was measured", enters as `papers/spacetime_events/` with its run, its records and the checks its `build.sh` runs; every path in the tests, the tools and the documents follows.
