# Local complexity mechanisms: bounded first investigation

This investigation follows the six-part audit on main
`7d238e84a2167a4d3fe7bc7942da0cbf97bd29b2`. Its scope is six complementary
mechanisms, not a promise that complexity already supplies Born statistics
or recurrent cosmological bursts. No production law is modified.

The existing `experiments/cosmology/PROTOCOL.md` remains the acceptance rule
for the complete recurrent-burst goal. New toy-sector successes are reported
as separate subgoals and cannot replace its failed gates.

## Hypotheses to examine

1. Retained finite local histories can distinguish novelty from repetition,
   and a declared invariant alphabet can support an exact local predicate.
2. A difference in retained structural memory can change a later local signal
   even when the current carrier and its amplitude are the same.
3. Local requests propagated from that memory can causally alter concentration
   and reassembly under one fixed law.
4. Macroscopic cycles can leave different retained, behaviorally useful
   microscopic organization while preserving the complete inverse.
5. Operational phase-resolved histories or correlation measures can be compared
   with coherent Born weights without defining complexity to be those weights.
6. Independent controls can distinguish these mechanisms from arithmetic
   growth, a scheduled cycle, noise, a hidden observer and selected outcomes.

Each owner declares its tensor/seed family and bounded test grid before its
candidate runs. The first runs normally use L=3,5 or 7, at most 160 updates,
and a 128/256-bit resource stop after a complete update. Longer established
controls may reproduce the existing 600-step baseline. A resource stop is
recorded as a stop, not clipped dynamics. All completed steps must rewind.

## Model and evidence requirements

- Preserve the exact fixed-six recurrence, old-snapshot evaluation, degree
  at most three, single-edge support and acyclic old-a dependencies.
- Build the tensor and complete preparation before evolution. No runtime
  injections, reseeding, host-triggered bursts, dynamic coefficients or
  measurement-derived branch selection is allowed in an Engine candidate.
- A restricted alphabet or preparation is an explicit model domain. Prove
  or test its invariance; do not silently apply its finite polynomial predicate
  to arbitrary integers. State finite capacity and the fate of old records.
- Save the actual law, preparation and final complete state or an exact
  reproducible encoding, full-state hashes, source hashes and inverse result.
- Separate the retained mechanism from passive analysis. A statistical metric
  computed by Python is not a local sensor until implemented in the law.
- Report all declared cases and failures. New ideas after a failed run must
  be labelled follow-up hypotheses rather than replacements for old evidence.

## Discriminating controls

Use matched current signals with differing retained histories; disconnect
only the proposed feedback; vary local motifs without merely changing elapsed
time; and check cone-respecting intervention effects. Include constant,
periodic, amplitude-scaled, shuffled/noisy controls where the interpretation
requires them. Random diversity alone is not useful predictive organization.

The complete goal still requires four separated reference reassemblies,
componentwise nondecreasing predeclared structural proxies with strict growth
and persistence, causal feedback/stagnation attribution, and exact recovery.
A helpful local memory or sensor may pass while this complete goal fails.

For quantum comparisons, distinguish a declared physical change to the state
before measurement from post-weighting outcomes of a fixed state. A formula
that assigns Born weights by definition is a reference, not their derivation.
Keep all specified trials and state the Bell-local assumptions explicitly.

The independent acceptance audit records pass/fail/unknown separately for
each subgoal. The synthesis will prioritize the smallest demonstrated causal
mechanism and the next missing connection, preserving negative controls.
