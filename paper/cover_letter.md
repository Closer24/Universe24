# Cover letter to Foundations of Physics

Alon Gonen, independent researcher, Haifa, Israel. ORCID 0009-0008-2701-4599.
[date]

To the Editor-in-Chief, Foundations of Physics,

I submit the manuscript "Universe24: an integer cellular automaton read through
clicks, the formulas it shares with nature and the ones it adds" for consideration
as an original research article. It is a new submission: the work has not been
published and is not under consideration elsewhere, and this is the project's
first paper.

The paper builds one world, Universe24, from a single line of integer arithmetic
stepped reversibly on a cubic lattice and read only through clicks, the reports of
declared counters that the model calls NodeDetectors. The main text derives from
that line the forms the world shares with nature (the conserved form and its
current, the bands and the Klein-Gordon equation with its Schroedinger limit, the
weak-field forms of gravity under one named assumption, the moving clock, Bell's
and Tsirelson's values under a declared credit) and names the forms it adds, each
with its status: derived, computed, declared, fitted or an assumption. One run of
the implementation is reported, the two slits, against a prediction written before
the run. The paper does not claim that nature is such a lattice; it states what the
world computes, how the method builds it, and which calculations a world closer to
nature would need.

I believe the paper fits the journal's scope: its subject is the conceptual basis of
measurement, a click as the meeting of a record run forward from its source with the
transition read backward from the click, and the status of every formula a lattice
model can and cannot claim.
