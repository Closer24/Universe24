## 2. The compile recipe

Engine: pdfTeX 3.141592653-2.6-1.40.25 (TeX Live 2023/Debian); `pdftoppm`
24.02.0 for the page renders. Three passes, from the paper's directory, into a
build directory outside the repository:

```
cd paper/general_formula
B=/path/to/build; mkdir -p "$B"
for i in 1 2 3; do
  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -output-directory="$B" main.tex > /dev/null
done
grep -o "([0-9]* pages" "$B/main.log"
grep -c "Overfull \\\\vbox" "$B/main.log"; grep -c "Overfull \\\\hbox" "$B/main.log"
grep -c "undefined" "$B/main.log"
```

The page count is the "(N pages" of the log's "Output written" line (the line
wraps when the build path is long), the only count the paper reports
(NUMBERS.md). The delivered state: 30 pages, the body
through page 25, the references 26 to 27, the appendices 28 to 30; no overfull
vertical box; no undefined reference. Overfull horizontal boxes are the tables'
and are read page by page:

```
pdftoppm -r 60 -png "$B/main.pdf" "$B/page"
```

## 3. Regenerating the manuscript

1. Edit `cut30/part1.py` to `part4.py`, never `main.tex`.
2. `python paper/general_formula/cut30/assemble.py` from the repository root.
   The assembler reads the base with `git show b684ba0b:paper/general_formula/main.tex`;
   the commit must be in the clone (a shallow clone of depth 50 from a later
   head may lack it; `git fetch --deepen=200 origin main` or an unshallow fetch
   brings it in).
3. An `AssertionError` from `sub` or `cutp` means an anchor no longer matches
   the base's text (a count other than the one asserted): loosen or move the
   anchor in the part; do not edit the output.
4. Compile (Section 2), read the page count and the overfull count, look at
   the pages where a table changed.
5. Run the gates (Section 4). Then commit the parts and `main.tex` together.

## 5. The rules in force for the paper

- Only a detector's reading is a measurement; nobody measures the GameBoard
  (docs/HIGHLIGHTS.md 5.4, records 277, 281, 562, 564, 575). The paper carries
  no probe reading that samples the board. A GameBoard reading is a diagnostic
  and is labelled so where it appears; it never enters the checks (Tables 2
  and 3) and never confirms a formula.
- The status words are the owner's: "derived from the update rules implemented
  in the simulator", "numerical finding", "hypothesis", "definition or input".
- The forcing ledger (Table 1) is the spine: for each result, the rules it
  starts from, the assumptions added with their kind and the freedom left, the
  ground (the three rungs: exact on the GameBoard; a limit of the grain, on
  paper, with its rate; a limit under one of the three admissible averages,
  over the shell's Nodes, over a fan's directions, over an orbit's sweep) and
  the section. A detector reading never lifts a rung-3 claim to rung 1.
- Every number has one source in NUMBERS.md; a number is not moved by rounding
  in prose; a compiled count (pages) is read from the log.
- A record of the log is cited by number only once it is on the tree
  (`docs/LOG_<date>.md`); until then by date, "its record to follow".
- The conditional derivations are declared hypotheses (docs/HYPOTHESES.md,
  entry 27) with their exit rule; the paper states them under that status.
- Newton's and Poisson's laws count as measured only after a detector; those
  runs are not made; the paper says "not made".
- The owner's guiding statement (PLAN.md, the thirty-page plan): only closed
  results with a clear claim and proof; the central claim is several phenomena
  from one discrete update law and few assumptions; the decisive question is
  how much the law forces; the paper is built around the precise distance
  between the explicit model and the description of nature (Table 4).
