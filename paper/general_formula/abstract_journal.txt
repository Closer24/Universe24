Universe24 is computed by an integer cellular automaton: a cubic lattice of Nodes with two integer levels per field, stepped by one integer recurrence, Rule3, from a Node's present, past and six neighbours, reversibly and covariantly under the cube's group of 48. The lattice is read only through declared counting regions, NodeDetectors, whose clicks are the only measurements, each the meeting of the field propagated forward from its source with the transition read backward from the click. From it follow, in lattice form, the Klein-Gordon equation with Schroedinger's slow limit, Planck's relation in the band's form and, under one named assumption fixed by two measurements, the exponential weak-field metric, gamma = beta = 1. A field's rational pair [num, den], cos omega_0 = num / den, fixes its inertial mass m^* = 3 tan omega_0 and the post-Newtonian parameters gamma_K = den / num, beta_K = (den + num) / (2num), closing to 1 with the gap. The lattice's own terms are predictions under their conditions: a black-hole shadow radius 2e m, light's gravitational source at E / c^2 under the static kernel, the metric's second order. Bell's and Greenberger-Horne-Zeilinger (GHZ) correlations follow from a declared counting rule, not from the lattice. The misses are named: the preferred-frame parameters alpha_1 = -8 and, for a static source, alpha_2 = -1, excluded by observation, no frame dragging, a decelerating cosmology. Universe24 is one world of a method that can build others; the paper claims that the model helps compute things in nature, not that nature is such a lattice.
