Universe24 is an integer cellular automaton: a cubic lattice of Nodes stepped by one recurrence taking a Node's present, its past and six neighbours through one integer division, reversibly. The lattice is read only by NodeReaders, declared regions of Nodes writing the clicks; Universe24 is those clicks. Measurement is a meeting at a NodeReader of the record run forward with its transition read backward from the click, two quanta and never one; the NodeReader draws the click, the lattice draws nothing. One line, Rule3, gives the lattice Klein-Gordon equation, Schr\"odinger's as its slow limit, the quantum of action and, under one named assumption fixed by one click, the metric of gravity. A family's pair $[\mathrm{num}, \mathrm{den}]$, $\cos\omega_0 = \mathrm{num} / \mathrm{den}$, gives its inertial mass $m^* = 3\tan\omega_0$ and the post-Newtonian $\gamma_K = \mathrm{den} / \mathrm{num}$ and $\beta_K = (\mathrm{den} + \mathrm{num}) / (2\mathrm{num})$, closing to $1$ with the gap; $\alpha$ and $G$ are two declared weights read from nature. The exponential metric gives $\gamma = 1$ exactly in the clock's potential; the lattice's frame gives $\alpha_1 = -8$ and, under the static read, $\alpha_2 = -1$, both excluded, the mend a named hypothesis. A bound body is a record standing in a holder's well, a cluster bodies in one another's wells. Three predictions that no click has read: the shadow's radius $2e\,m$, light's pull at $E / c^2$, the metric's second order. The paper's claim is that Universe24 helps compute things in nature: each computed quantity stands against measurement with its status and number, misses included; what it does not compute is named, the two-body correlation among it, the law holding no two-position amplitude.
