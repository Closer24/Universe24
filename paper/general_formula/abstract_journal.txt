We present a method, Universe24: an integer cellular automaton, a cubic lattice of Nodes stepped by one recurrence taking a Node's present, its past and its six neighbours through one integer division, reversibly and conserving a quadratic form exactly. The lattice is read only by NodeReaders, declared regions of Nodes that write the clicks; Universe24 is those clicks. Measurement is a meeting at a NodeReader: the record run forward meets the NodeReader's own transition read backward from the click, two quanta and never one; the NodeReader draws the click, the lattice draws nothing, that draw the one non-local element. The wave equation, the quantum of action and the metric of gravity are three readings of one line, the metric under one named assumption fitted to two clicks. A family's pair $[p, q]$, $\cos\omega_0 = p / q$, gives its inertial mass $m^* = 3\tan\omega_0$, its light speed $c_m^2 = \omega_0 / (3\tan\omega_0)$ and the post-Newtonian $\gamma_K = q / p$ and $\beta_K = (q + p) / (2p)$, which go to $1$ as the gap closes; $\alpha$ and $G$ enter as two declared weights read from nature. The exponential metric gives $\gamma = 1$ exactly; the lattice's frame gives $\alpha_1 = -8$ and $\alpha_2 = -1$, both excluded by nature, the mend a named hypothesis. A bound body is a record standing in a holder's well, and what it gives is its clicks, derived; a cluster is bodies standing in one another's wells. The paper claims the method and not nature: every comparison a supporting finding; what it does not give is listed, the two-body correlation among it, since the law holds no amplitude over two positions.
