Universe24 is an integer cellular automaton: a cubic lattice of Nodes stepped by one recurrence taking a Node's present, its past and six neighbours through one integer division, reversibly. The lattice is read only by NodeReaders, declared regions of Nodes writing the clicks; Universe24 is those clicks. Measurement is a meeting at a NodeReader of the record run forward with its transition read backward from the click, two quanta and never one; the NodeReader draws the click, the lattice draws nothing. The wave equation, the quantum of action and the metric of gravity are three readings of one line, the metric under one named assumption fitted to two clicks. A family's pair $[p, q]$, $\cos\omega_0 = p / q$, gives its inertial mass $m^* = 3\tan\omega_0$, its light speed $c_m^2 = \omega_0 / (3\tan\omega_0)$ and the post-Newtonian $\gamma_K = q / p$ and $\beta_K = (q + p) / (2p)$, closing to $1$ with the gap; $\alpha$ and $G$ are two declared weights read from nature. The exponential metric gives $\gamma = 1$ exactly in the clock's potential; the lattice's frame gives $\alpha_1 = -8$ and, under the static read, $\alpha_2 = -1$, both excluded, the mend a named hypothesis. A bound body is a record standing in a holder's well, its clicks derived; a cluster is bodies in one another's wells. The paper's claim is that Universe24 helps compute things in nature: each quantity it computes stands against measurement with its status and number, the misses with theirs; what it does not compute is named, the two-body correlation among it, since the law holds no amplitude over two positions; the method is how it computes.
