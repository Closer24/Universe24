We present a method: an integer cellular automaton, a cubic lattice of Nodes stepped by one recurrence taking a Node's present, past and six neighbours through one integer division, reversibly, conserving a quadratic form exactly in the rationals. Measurement is the meeting at a detector of the record run forward from its root with the detector's own transition read backward from the realised click, two quanta and never one; the detector draws the click, the lattice draws nothing, that draw the one non-local element. On it the wave equation, the quantum of action and the metric of gravity are three readings of one line. From the line alone, its own formulas with no number of nature: a family's pair $[p, q]$, $\cos\omega_0 = p / q$, gives its inertial mass $m^* = 3\tan\omega_0$ and its light speed $c_m^2 = \omega_0 / (3\tan\omega_0)$, with $E = m^* c_m^2$ exactly and Lorentz's factor at $c_m$ as the gap closes; the matter pair's post-Newtonian $\gamma_K = q / p$ and $\beta_K = (q + p) / (2p)$, closing to $1$ with the gap; charge universality and the forces' ratio $(m_p / m_e)^2$ across families.. Newton's gravitation is reflected, $G$ a reading, and Einstein's weak field under one named assumption fitted to two clicks: the exponential metric, $\gamma = 1$ exactly and $\beta = 1$ as the gap closes. The paper claims the method and not nature: every result a supporting finding for the reader's test, what it does not give listed.
