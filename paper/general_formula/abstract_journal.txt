We present a method, Universe24: an integer cellular automaton, a cubic lattice of Nodes stepped by one recurrence taking a Node's present, past and six neighbours through one integer division, reversibly, conserving a quadratic form exactly in the rationals at paces fixed in time. The lattice is read only by NodeReaders, declared regions of Nodes that write the clicks that happened; Universe24, the world of the model, is those clicks. Measurement is a meeting at a NodeReader of the record run forward from its root with the NodeReader's own transition read backward from the click that happened, two quanta and never one; the NodeReader draws the click, the lattice draws nothing, that draw the one non-local element. On it the wave equation, the quantum of action and the metric of gravity are three readings of one line. From the line alone: a family's pair $[p, q]$, $\cos\omega_0 = p / q$, gives its inertial mass $m^* = 3\tan\omega_0$ and its light speed $c_m^2 = \omega_0 / (3\tan\omega_0)$, $E = m^* c_m^2 = \omega_0$ by definition of $c_m$, and, with the one assumption below, the post-Newtonian $\gamma_K = q / p$, $\beta_K = (q + p) / (2p)$, closing to $1$ with the gap. Charge universality and the forces' ratio $(m_p / m_e)^2$ are identities of its definitions, no test; $\alpha$ and $G$ enter as two declared weights read from nature. Einstein's weak field follows under one named assumption fitted to two clicks: the exponential metric, $\gamma = 1$ exactly in the clock's potential, $\beta = 1$ as the gap closes, the shadow $4.6$ percent over Schwarzschild's, conditional on the interior; the lattice's frame gives $\alpha_1 = -8$ and, under the static read, $\alpha_2 = -1$, both excluded, the mend a named hypothesis. The paper claims the method and not nature: every comparison a supporting finding, and what it does not give is listed, the two-body correlation among it, since a Node holds no joint amplitude of two records.
