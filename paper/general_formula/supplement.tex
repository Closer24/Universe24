\section*{The model (Section 3 of the paper)}

\begin{derivation}[the band at a pace, Eq.~(4) of the paper]
\emph{Inputs:} Rule3's line, $w\,\anext = \sum_a R_a(\mathrm{arr}_{+a} + \mathrm{arr}_{-a}) + S\,\anow - w\,\abefore$, with the remainders dropped (they are bounded by $w$ and do not move a plane wave's rotation). \emph{Steps:} for a plane wave $a_t(\mathbf x) = A\cos(\mathbf k \cdot \mathbf x - \omega t)$ the two arrivals of the axis $a$ sum to $2\cos k_a\,\anow$ and $\anext + \abefore = 2\cos\omega\,\anow$, so
\[
2\cos\omega = \frac{2\sum_a R_a\cos k_a + S}{w}
= \frac{4\,\num\sum_a p_a^2\cos k_a + 12\,\den\,\Gamma^2 - 12(\den - \num)\,p_0^2 - 4\,\num\sum_a p_a^2}{6\,\den\,\Gamma^2},
\]
that is $\cos\omega = 1 - g\,(p_0 / \Gamma)^2 - \frac{\num}{3\,\den}\sum_a (p_a / \Gamma)^2(1 - \cos k_a)$, which is Eq.~(4). At the vacuum's pace, $p_0 = p_a = \Gamma$, this is $\cos\omega = \num / \den - \frac{\num}{3\,\den}\sum_a(1 - \cos k_a) = \frac{\num}{3\,\den}\sum_a\cos k_a$; at $k = 0$, $\cos\omega_0 = \num / \den$. \emph{Light's speed and dispersion:} for $[1, 1]$ along one axis $\cos\omega = (2 + \cos k) / 3$; expanding, $1 - \omega^2 / 2 + \omega^4 / 24 = 1 - k^2 / 6 + k^4 / 72$, so $\omega = (k / \sqrt 3)(1 - k^2 / 36 + \dots)$ and the group velocity $d\omega / dk = \sin k / (3\sin\omega) = (1 / \sqrt 3)(1 - k^2 / 12 + \dots)$: the speed $1 / \sqrt 3$ at long wavelength and the group speed falling as $1 - 0.083\,k^2$. \emph{Check:} at $k = 0.05$, $0.1$ and $0.2$ the exact $\sqrt 3\,d\omega / dk - 1$ over $k^2$ is $-0.0833$, $-0.0834$ and $-0.0835$.
\end{derivation}

\begin{derivation}[light's index at a content, Section 3.4]
\emph{Inputs:} Eq.~(4) with the paces of Eq.~(3), $p_0^2 = \Gamma^2(1 - 2x + 2x^2)$ and $p_a = \Gamma(1 - 2x)$ for a reader at the weight $1$ and no tension. \emph{Steps:} for light, $g = 0$, so $\cos\omega = 1 - \frac{1}{3}(1 - 2x)^2\sum_a(1 - \cos k_a)$, and at long wavelength $\omega = (1 - 2x)\,|\mathbf k| / \sqrt 3$. Light's speed at the content $x$ is $(1 - 2x)$ times its vacuum speed, so its index is $n = 1 / (1 - 2x)$ and $n - 1 = 2x + O(x^2)$: the Link's pace reads the content twice, and so does light.
\end{derivation}

\begin{derivation}[the guard's edge and the horizon, Section 3.4]
\emph{Inputs:} Eq.~(4). \emph{Steps:} the lowest rotation of the band is at $k_a = \pi$ on every axis, where $2\cos\omega = 2 - 2g\,(p_0 / \Gamma)^2 - \frac{4\,\num}{3\,\den}\sum_a(p_a / \Gamma)^2$; the recurrence $\anext + \abefore = 2\cos\omega\,\anow$ has no exponentially growing solution iff $|2\cos\omega| \le 2$; at the edges $\pm 2$ a linearly growing solution exists ($u_t = \pm t$ from $u_0 = 0$, $u_1 = 1$), which the guard admits, light's checkerboard mode sitting at $-2$; the bound below is stated for $\num \ge 0$, since for a negative numerator the lowest rotation is at $k = 0$. With $p_0 = p_a = p$ the condition $2\cos\omega \ge -2$ reads $(p / \Gamma)^2\,(2g + 4\,\num / \den) \le 4$, that is $(p / \Gamma)^2 \le 2\,\den / (\den + \num)$, so the edge is $P^2 = 2\,\den\,\Gamma^2 \ddiv (\den + \num)$, which is $\Gamma^2$ for light. \emph{The horizon:} $R_a = 2\,\num\,p_a^2$ vanishes where $p_a = \Gamma(1 - 2x) = 0$, at $x = 1 / 2$. The clock's pace there is $(p_0 / \Gamma)^2 = (1 - x)^2 + x^2$, whose minimum over $x$ is $1 / 2$ at $x = 1 / 2$: the clock never stops, and at the horizon it runs at $1 / \sqrt 2$ of the vacuum's rate. \emph{The frozen Node:} with all three $p_a = 0$ the line is $\anext + \abefore = (S / w)\,\anow$ with $S = 12\,\den\,\Gamma^2 - 12(\den - \num)\,\Gamma^2 / 2 = 6\,\Gamma^2(\den + \num)$, so $2\cos\omega = (\den + \num) / \den = 1 + \cos\omega_0$; $5 / 3$ at $[2, 3]$, a period of $10.7$ intervals.
\end{derivation}

\begin{derivation}[the cube's group of 48 and its action, Section 3.1]
\emph{Inputs:} Rule3's line, whose six arrivals enter through the three axis coefficients $R_a = 2\,\num\,p_a^2$ with no coefficient naming an axis beyond its own pace; the readings $D$, $F$ and $W$ of Eq.~(1). \emph{The group:} a map of the six Ports onto themselves that keeps opposite Ports opposite is a signed permutation of the three axes, $\mathbf x \mapsto \mathbf P\mathbf x$ with $\mathbf P$ a permutation matrix with signs; there are $3! \cdot 2^3 = 48$ of them, the group $O_h$ of the cube, of which the $24$ with $\det\mathbf P = +1$ are the rotations and the other $24$ reflections and rotatory reflections; with the translations of the lattice they form the space group of the simple cubic lattice. \emph{Covariance:} let $\sigma$ be such a map and let the turned world carry the levels $a^\sigma_{\sigma(i)} = a_i$ and the paces $p^\sigma_{\sigma(a)}(\sigma(i)) = p_a(i)$. The arrivals of the axis $\sigma(a)$ at $\sigma(i)$ in the turned world are the arrivals of the axis $a$ at $i$ in the original, in either order, and $R_a$ is even in the axis's sign; so the sum $\sum_a R_a(\mathrm{arr}_{+a} + \mathrm{arr}_{-a})$ is the same number at $\sigma(i)$ as at $i$, $S$ and $w$ are the same, and the division gives the same quotient and remainder: the step commutes with $\sigma$, and a world turned by $\sigma$ runs as the turned world. A translation commutes for the same reason. \emph{The readings' kinds:} $D_i$ is one number at a Node with no axis in it, carried to $\sigma(i)$ unchanged, a scalar; the currents $F_{ij}$ are attached to the Links and carried with them, and the momentum density $P_a(i) = (F_{i, i+a} - F_{i, i-a}) / \num$ changes sign where $\sigma$ reverses the axis $a$ and is carried to the image axis otherwise, a vector; $W_i$ carries no axis and is carried unchanged, and it changes sign under the reflection of a plane record's sense, $\mathrm{im} \mapsto -\mathrm{im}$, a pseudoscalar in that sense; the click $(R, t)$ names a region and an interval and is carried to the turned region, a scalar. \emph{The time reversal:} exchanging $\mathrm{now}$ and $\mathrm{before}$ leaves $D_i = \mathrm{now}_i^2 - \mathrm{next}_i\,\mathrm{before}_i$ unchanged once $\mathrm{next}$ and $\mathrm{before}$ exchange their roles, since $D$ is symmetric in the pair $(\mathrm{next}, \mathrm{before})$; it reverses $F_{ij}$ and $W_i$, which are antisymmetric in $(\mathrm{now}, \mathrm{before})$; and it carries the step to its inverse by Theorem 1. \emph{The rational rotation:} the two parts of a plane record obey the same linear line with the same coefficients, so any linear combination of them does; the combination $(\mathrm{re}', \mathrm{im}') = ((p\,\mathrm{re} - q\,\mathrm{im}) / r, (q\,\mathrm{re} + p\,\mathrm{im}) / r)$ with $p^2 + q^2 = r^2$ carries solutions to solutions, and its Wronskian is $(p^2 + q^2) / r^2$ times the old one, unchanged. \emph{What is not in the group:} a boost changes the record's wave number and rotation together, which no permutation of Ports does, and a rotation by an angle other than the cube's carries a Node off the lattice; so neither is a symmetry, and Lorentz's factor, where the paper finds it, is a property of the clicks (Derivation S.21).
\end{derivation}

\section*{Exact properties (Section 4)}

\begin{derivation}[the share's change is the currents, Eq.~(10)]
\emph{Inputs:} the share at the vacuum's paces, $e_i = 3\,\den\,(\mathrm{now}_i^2 + \mathrm{before}_i^2) - \num\,\mathrm{now}_i\,S_6(\mathrm{before})_i$, with $S_6$ the six-neighbour sum; Rule3 at the vacuum's paces, where $S = 0$, $R_a = 2\,\num\,\Gamma^2$ and $w = 6\,\den\,\Gamma^2$, so that $3\,\den\,(\mathrm{next}_i + \mathrm{before}_i) = \num\,S_6(\mathrm{now})_i + \rho_i$ with $\rho_i = (r_i - r_i') / (2\Gamma^2)$ the remainders' term (from $6\,\den\,\Gamma^2(\mathrm{next} + \mathrm{before}) = 2\,\num\,\Gamma^2 S_6(\mathrm{now}) + r - r'$). \emph{Steps:} after the step the pair $(\mathrm{now}, \mathrm{before})$ becomes $(\mathrm{next}, \mathrm{now})$, so
\[
e_i' - e_i = 3\,\den\,(\mathrm{next}_i^2 - \mathrm{before}_i^2) - \num\,[\mathrm{next}_i\,S_6(\mathrm{now})_i - \mathrm{now}_i\,S_6(\mathrm{before})_i].
\]
With $3\,\den\,(\mathrm{next}_i + \mathrm{before}_i) = \num\,S_6(\mathrm{now})_i + \rho_i$ the first term is $(\mathrm{next}_i - \mathrm{before}_i)(\num\,S_6(\mathrm{now})_i + \rho_i)$, and the bracket cancels its $\mathrm{next}_i$ part:
\begin{align*}
e_i' - e_i &= \num\,[\mathrm{now}_i\,S_6(\mathrm{before})_i - \mathrm{before}_i\,S_6(\mathrm{now})_i] + (\mathrm{next}_i - \mathrm{before}_i)\,\rho_i\\
&= \sum_{j \sim i} \num\,(\mathrm{now}_i\,\mathrm{before}_j - \mathrm{before}_i\,\mathrm{now}_j) + (\mathrm{next}_i - \mathrm{before}_i)\,\rho_i,
\end{align*}
the six currents $F_{ij}$ into the Node plus the remainder term. Where the three Link paces are equal but not $\Gamma$ the Node term carries the weight $3\Gamma^2 / \sum_a p_a^2$ and the same substitution goes through with $S \ne 0$, the $S$ term cancelling between $\mathrm{next}_i^2 - \mathrm{before}_i^2$ and the bracket as in the proof of Theorem 3; where they differ, the term that does not cancel is the anisotropy term of Eq.~(10), $\num\,(\mathrm{next}_i - \mathrm{before}_i)\sum_a (3p_a^2 - P^2)\,\mathrm{arr}_a(\mathrm{now})_i / P^2$ with $P^2 = \sum_a p_a^2$.
\end{derivation}

\begin{derivation}[the total share is non-negative, Section 4.4]
\emph{Inputs:} $e_i$ as above on a closed or periodic GameBoard; $S_6$ symmetric with eigenvalues $2\sum_a\cos k_a \in [-6, 6]$. \emph{Steps:} $\sum_i e_i = 3\,\den\,(|\mathbf n|^2 + |\mathbf b|^2) - \num\,\mathbf n \cdot S_6\mathbf b$, and $|\mathbf n \cdot S_6\mathbf b| \le \|S_6\|\,|\mathbf n|\,|\mathbf b| \le 6\,|\mathbf n|\,|\mathbf b| \le 3(|\mathbf n|^2 + |\mathbf b|^2)$, so $\sum_i e_i \ge 3(\den - |\num|)(|\mathbf n|^2 + |\mathbf b|^2) \ge 0$ for $|\num| \le \den$. Equality needs $|\num| = \den$ and $\mathbf b$ an eigenvector of $S_6$ at $\pm 6$ with $\mathbf n = \pm\mathbf b$: the static uniform level of light, or on an even periodic board its checkerboard, $\mathrm{now} = (-1)^{x + y + z}$ with $\mathrm{before} = -\mathrm{now}$, whose shares are $0$. \emph{A Node's share:} at fixed $\mathrm{before}_i$ and $S_6(\mathrm{before})_i = s$, $e_i$ is a quadratic in $\mathrm{now}_i$ with the minimum $3\,\den\,\mathrm{before}_i^2 - \num^2 s^2 / (12\,\den)$, negative iff $|s| > 6(\den / \num)\,|\mathrm{before}_i|$; a plane record has $|s| / |\mathrm{before}_i| = 2\sum_a\cos k_a \le 6$ and is never negative, a lay's edge can be. \emph{A region:} pairing each boundary Link's cross term and bounding $2|\mathrm{now}_i\,\mathrm{before}_j| \le \mathrm{now}_i^2 + \mathrm{before}_j^2$, $\sum_{i \in R} e_i \ge 3(\den - \num)\sum_R(\mathrm{now}_i^2 + \mathrm{before}_i^2) - \frac{\num}{2}\sum_{\text{boundary Ports}}(\mathrm{now}_j^2 + \mathrm{before}_j^2)$ over the outside neighbours $j$: a region's share is negative at most by half a level-square per boundary Port in the current's units.
\end{derivation}

\begin{derivation}[the tension of a plane wave, Section 4.3]
\emph{Inputs:} the stress reading $G_{aa}(i) = \mathrm{now}_i(\mathrm{now}_{i+2a} - \mathrm{now}_i) - \mathrm{now}_{i+a}(\mathrm{now}_{i+a} - \mathrm{now}_{i-a})$. \emph{Steps:} for $\mathrm{now}_i = b\cos(kx_i + \phi)$ along the axis, with $c_n = \cos(kx_i + nk + \phi)$, $G = b^2[c_0 c_2 - c_0^2 - c_1^2 + c_1 c_{-1}]$; the identities $c_0 c_2 = \frac{1}{2}[\cos(2kx + 2k + 2\phi) + \cos 2k]$, $c_1 c_{-1} = \frac{1}{2}[\cos(2kx + 2\phi) + \cos 2k]$, $c_0^2 = \frac{1}{2}[1 + \cos(2kx + 2\phi)]$ and $c_1^2 = \frac{1}{2}[1 + \cos(2kx + 2k + 2\phi)]$ cancel the phase-dependent parts, leaving $G = b^2(\cos 2k - 1) = -2\,b^2\sin^2 k$ at every Node and interval; so $T_{aa} = -\num\,(G(i) + G(i - a)) \ddiv 2 = 2\,\num\,b^2\sin^2 k$, and for a wave with the components $k_a$, $k_b$ the cross reading is $G_{ab} = -2\,b^2\sin k_a\sin k_b$.
\end{derivation}

\section*{Measurement (Section 5)}

\begin{derivation}[the region's reading factor and the draw's scatter, Section 5.3]
\emph{Steps:} a region of $n$ consecutive rows sums a fringe $\cos(2\pi y / \Lambda)$ to $\mathrm{Re}\sum_{y = 0}^{n - 1} e^{2\pi i y / \Lambda} = \mathrm{Re}\,\frac{1 - e^{2\pi i n / \Lambda}}{1 - e^{2\pi i / \Lambda}}$, whose modulus is $\sin(\pi n / \Lambda) / \sin(\pi / \Lambda)$; over $n$ rows the fringe's modulation is kept with the factor $\sin(\pi n / \Lambda) / (n\sin(\pi / \Lambda))$, $0.90$ at $n = 4$, $\Lambda = 16$, and $0$ at $\Lambda = n$. A frequency read as a click rate over $N$ intervals resolves $\Delta\omega \ge 2\pi / N$, the window's own resolution. $N$ quanta drawn independently by the shares $p_i$ give each region a binomial count, $Np_i \pm \sqrt{Np_i(1 - p_i)}$.
\end{derivation}

\begin{derivation}[the Wronskian's conservation and its current, Section 5.5]
\emph{Inputs:} the two parts of a plane record step by the same symmetric read matrix $\mathbf M$ (the paper's Theorem 3), $\mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{next}} = \mathbf M\,\mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{now}} - \mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{before}}$ and the same for $\mathbf{im}$, with the weights $\mathbf D$ of the conserved form, $\mathbf D\mathbf M$ symmetric. \emph{Steps:} let $W = \mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{now}}^\top\mathbf D\,\mathbf{im}_{\mathrm{before}} - \mathbf{im}_{\mathrm{now}}^\top\mathbf D\,\mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{before}}$. After the step it is $\mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{next}}^\top\mathbf D\,\mathbf{im}_{\mathrm{now}} - \mathbf{im}_{\mathrm{next}}^\top\mathbf D\,\mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{now}} = (\mathbf M\mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{now}} - \mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{before}})^\top\mathbf D\,\mathbf{im}_{\mathrm{now}} - (\mathbf M\mathbf{im}_{\mathrm{now}} - \mathbf{im}_{\mathrm{before}})^\top\mathbf D\,\mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{now}}$; the $\mathbf M$ terms are $\mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{now}}^\top\mathbf M^\top\mathbf D\,\mathbf{im}_{\mathrm{now}} - \mathbf{im}_{\mathrm{now}}^\top\mathbf M^\top\mathbf D\,\mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{now}}$, which vanish since $\mathbf M^\top\mathbf D = \mathbf D\mathbf M$ is symmetric, and the rest is $W$ again. Per Node the same cancellation leaves the Link terms, $\num\,(\mathrm{im}_i\,\mathrm{re}_j - \mathrm{re}_i\,\mathrm{im}_j)$, the Wronskian's current. For the circular record $\mathrm{re} = A\cos\omega t$, $\mathrm{im} = eA\sin\omega t$ at one Node, $W = A\cos\omega t\cdot eA\sin\omega(t - 1) - eA\sin\omega t\cdot A\cos\omega(t - 1) = -eA^2\sin\omega$, constant. \emph{Domain:} the identity is the line's, $\mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{next}} = \mathbf M\,\mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{now}} - \mathbf{re}_{\mathrm{before}}$ exactly; the integer step's division adds $\epsilon = (r - r') / w$ to each part, and the remainder term $\epsilon_{\mathrm{re}}\,\mathrm{im}_{\mathrm{now}} - \epsilon_{\mathrm{im}}\,\mathrm{re}_{\mathrm{now}}$ is not zero in general: at the vacuum's pace $S = 0$ and $w\,\anext = 12\,\num\,\Gamma^2\anow - w\,\abefore$ for a uniform level, so at $[2, 3]$ the parts $(1, 3)$ with zeros before step to $(\lfloor 4 / 3\rfloor, \lfloor 4\rfloor) = (1, 4)$ and $W$ goes from $0$ to $1\cdot 3 - 4\cdot 1 = -1$; an exactly conserved integer charge is open.
\end{derivation}

\begin{derivation}[Bell's correlation under the declared credit, Section 5.7 and Section 9.4]
\emph{Inputs:} two parts with the products $M_1$, $M_2$ of their amplitudes at the two sides' ports; the port coefficients $e(+) = (\cos a, \sin a)$, $e(-) = (-\sin a, \cos a)$ on side A and the same with $b$ on side B; the joint share $J(p, q) = \bigl(\sum_k e_k(p)\,e_k(q)\,M_k\bigr)^2$; $E = \sum_{p, q} s(p)s(q)\,J(p, q) / \sum_{p, q} J(p, q)$ with $s(\pm) = \pm 1$. \emph{Steps:} write $c_a = \cos a$, $s_a = \sin a$ and the same for $b$. The four sums are $M_1 c_a c_b + M_2 s_a s_b$, $M_1 s_a s_b + M_2 c_a c_b$, $-M_1 c_a s_b + M_2 s_a c_b$ and $-M_1 s_a c_b + M_2 c_a s_b$ for $(+, +)$, $(-, -)$, $(+, -)$ and $(-, +)$. Their squares sum to $(M_1^2 + M_2^2)(c_a^2 c_b^2 + s_a^2 s_b^2 + c_a^2 s_b^2 + s_a^2 c_b^2) = M_1^2 + M_2^2$, the cross terms cancelling in pairs. The signed sum is $(M_1^2 + M_2^2)(c_a^2 - s_a^2)(c_b^2 - s_b^2) + 8M_1M_2\,c_a s_a c_b s_b$, so
\[
E(a, b) = \cos 2a\cos 2b + \rho\sin 2a\sin 2b, \qquad \rho = \frac{2M_1M_2}{M_1^2 + M_2^2},
\]
and the marginal $P(A+) = [(M_1 c_a c_b + M_2 s_a s_b)^2 + (-M_1 c_a s_b + M_2 s_a c_b)^2] / (M_1^2 + M_2^2) = (M_1^2 c_a^2 + M_2^2 s_a^2) / (M_1^2 + M_2^2)$, independent of $b$. For equal parts $\rho = 1$ and $E = \cos 2(a - b)$. \emph{The integer settings:} with $e(+) = (p, q)$ and $e(-) = (-q, p)$ unnormalised and equal parts, $J(+, +) = J(-, -) = (p_Ap_B + q_Aq_B)^2$ and $J(+, -) = J(-, +) = (p_Aq_B - q_Ap_B)^2$, whose sum is $2(p_A^2 + q_A^2)(p_B^2 + q_B^2)$ by Lagrange's identity, so $E = [(p_Ap_B + q_Aq_B)^2 - (p_Aq_B - q_Ap_B)^2] / [(p_A^2 + q_A^2)(p_B^2 + q_B^2)]$, a rational number. At $a = (1, 0)$, $a' = (1, 1)$, $b = (12, 5)$, $b' = (5, 12)$: $E(a, b) = (144 - 25) / 169 = 119 / 169$, $E(a, b') = (25 - 144) / 169 = -119 / 169$, $E(a', b) = (17^2 - 7^2) / 338 = 120 / 169$, $E(a', b') = 120 / 169$, and $S = E(a, b) - E(a, b') + E(a', b) + E(a', b') = 478 / 169 = 2.8284$. \emph{Inputs:} a circular record of the plane at the vacuum's paces, $z = Ae^{i\omega_0 t}$, whose two parts' shares sum to the count, $2A^2\sin^2\omega_0 = T$ for one quantum (Section 4.4, Section 5.5), and whose Wronskian is $|W| = A^2\sin\omega_0$ (S.9); the holder of the sign's rest far from a static source of $s$ per interval, $3s / (4\pi r)$ at the divisor $1$ (Derivation S.25's line at $E_s = 1$); the rotation's angle $\theta = \mathrm{level} / \Gamma$ (Section 5.5); de Broglie's $k = p / \hbar$ (S.22) and light's $c = 1 / \sqrt 3$ (S.1). \emph{Steps:} one quantum writes $s = W / T = A^2\sin\omega_0 / (2A^2\sin^2\omega_0) = 1 / (2\sin\omega_0)$ per interval; the angle at $r$ is $\theta(r) = 3s / (4\pi r\,\Gamma)$; a reader of unit sense gains the angle per interval, so its wave number changes by the gradient, $dk / dt = -\nabla\theta = 3s / (4\pi r^2\Gamma)$; Coulomb's law in nature's units, $dk / dt = \alpha c / r^2$, gives $\alpha = r^2 (dk / dt) / c = \sqrt 3\,r^2\,dk / dt = 3\sqrt 3\,s / (4\pi\Gamma) = (3\sqrt 3 / 8\pi) / (\Gamma\sin\omega_0)$; the level weight $LW$ of the holder enters the source and the read alike and cancels. \emph{Check:} $3\sqrt 3 / (8\pi) = 0.20675$; $137.036 \times 0.20675 = 28.33$. With the pair $[\den - 1, \den]$, $\sin^2\omega_0 = 1 - (1 - 1 / \den)^2 = 2 / \den - 1 / \den^2$, so $\den = 2 / \omega_0^2$ to the first order and $\den = 2\Gamma^2 / 28.33^2 = \Gamma^2 / 401.3$.
\end{derivation}

\begin{derivation}[the units, the highest photon energy and the matter pair's gap, Section 10.1]
\emph{Inputs:} the electron as the free quantum of matter, rotating at $\omega_0$ per interval, and nature's electron at $m_e c^2 / \hbar$ per second; light's band $\cos\omega = 1 - \frac{1}{3}\sum_a(1 - \cos k_a)$ (S.1); the highest photon observed, $1.4$ PeV. \emph{Steps:} one interval is $\omega_0\hbar / (m_e c^2) = \omega_0 \times 1.288 \times 10^{-21}$ s, and one Link, light moving $1 / \sqrt 3$ Link per interval at $c$, is $\sqrt 3\,\omega_0\,\bar\lambda_e = \omega_0 \times 6.69 \times 10^{-13}$ m with $\bar\lambda_e = \hbar / (m_e c) = 3.862 \times 10^{-13}$ m. The band's top along the body diagonal, $k_a = \pi$ for all $a$, is $\cos\omega = -1$, $\omega = \pi$, and along an axis, $k = \pi$ alone, $\cos\omega = 1 / 3$, $\omega = 1.231$; at both the group velocity vanishes; so the highest energy a light record carries is $\pi m_e c^2 / \omega_0 = 1.605\,\mathrm{MeV} / \omega_0$ along the diagonal and $1.231\,m_e c^2 / \omega_0 = 0.629\,\mathrm{MeV} / \omega_0$ along an axis. A photon of $1.4$ PeV that arrived needs $0.629\,\mathrm{MeV} / \omega_0 > 1.4 \times 10^9$ MeV, $\omega_0 < 4.5 \times 10^{-10}$; then $\den = 2 / \omega_0^2 > 10^{19}$, $\Gamma = 28.33 / \omega_0 > 6.3 \times 10^{10}$ (Derivation S.26), and $\gamma_K - 1 = 1 / \cos\omega_0 - 1 \approx \omega_0^2 / 2 < 1.0 \times 10^{-19}$ (S.19). \emph{Check:} $\hbar / (m_e c^2) = 1.0546 \times 10^{-34} / 8.187 \times 10^{-14} = 1.288 \times 10^{-21}$ s; $\sqrt 3 \times 3.862 \times 10^{-13} = 6.69 \times 10^{-13}$ m; $\pi \times 0.511 = 1.605$; $1.231 \times 0.511 = 0.629$.
\end{derivation}
