\title[Universe24: an integer cellular automaton read through clicks]{Universe24: an integer cellular automaton read through clicks, the formulas it shares with nature and the ones it adds}
\author*{\fnm{Alon} \sur{Gonen}\,\orcidlink{0009-0008-2701-4599}}\email{alon@defounder.ai}
\affil{\orgname{Independent researcher}, \city{Haifa}, \country{Israel}}

\abstract{Universe24 is one line of integer arithmetic: a cubic lattice of Nodes, two integer levels per field, stepped by one recurrence, Rule3, from a Node's present, past and six neighbours, reversibly and covariantly under the cube's 48. The lattice is read only through declared counting regions, NodeDetectors, its clicks the only measurements, each the meeting of the forward field with the transition read backward. From it follow, in lattice form, the Klein-Gordon equation with Schr\"odinger's slow limit, Planck's relation in the band's form and, under one named assumption fixed by two measurements, the exponential weak-field metric, $\gamma = \beta = 1$, and, with no number put in, frame dragging's form, $\alpha_1 = \alpha_2 = 0$ and $\eta_N = 0$ at nature's gap. A field's rational pair $[\num, \den]$, $\cos\omega_0 = \num / \den$, fixes its inertial mass $m^* = 3\tan\omega_0$ and the post-Newtonian parameters $\gamma_K = \den / \num$ and $\beta_K$, closing to $1$ with the gap. The lattice's own terms are predictions under their conditions: a black-hole shadow radius $2e\,m$, light's gravitational source at $E / c^2$, the metric's second order, light undragged by a rotating mass. Bell's and Greenberger-Horne-Zeilinger correlations follow from a declared counting rule. Its misses are named, a decelerating cosmology among them. Universe24 is one world of a method that can build others; the paper claims that it helps compute things in nature, not that nature is such a lattice.}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{The question and the claim}\label{sec:question}

This paper is about one method of computing physics. A cubic lattice of Nodes is stepped by one local rule in whole numbers, Rule3, which is exactly reversible, and it is read only through clicks, the reports of declared counting regions that the model calls NodeDetectors. In the words of the title: the integer cellular automaton is the lattice stepped by Rule3; Universe24 is its clicks, written by the NodeDetectors from the fields that its configuration files declare; the formulas it shares with nature are the known formulas reached from the one line in lattice form, the Klein-Gordon equation with Schr\"odinger's slow limit, Planck's relation in the band's form (the adiabatic invariant \cite{ehrenfest}) and, under one named assumption, the exponential weak-field metric of gravity (Section~\ref{sec:shared}); and the ones it adds are the lattice's own terms, which depart from the continuum or have no counterpart there (Section~\ref{sec:added}). The paper has one claim: that Universe24 helps compute things in nature. Its content is what the model computes of nature set against measurement, each quantity with its status and its number, the hits and the misses alike; the method is how it computes and not the claim. The paper does not claim that nature is such a lattice.

The criterion is the same throughout: a derived, click-fenced formula that disagrees with a measurement beyond its stated order refutes the model at that formula, and Section~\ref{sec:limits} lists the refutations found. Universe24 is one world built by the paper's method: one declared configuration file, one run read by its NodeDetectors (the two slits, Section~\ref{sec:slits}), and the forms of Sections~\ref{sec:builds} and~\ref{sec:formulas} derived from the law's lines and computed from no run. Other worlds closer to nature can be built by the same method, and building one needs the calculations named in Section~\ref{sec:closer}, which this work does not do.

The paper starts from one statement and never contradicts it (assumption, the paper's first statement; clicks): Universe24 is the meeting of the past with the future; a click is that meeting, the one measurement there is in the model; and in it only clicks are seen. A Node holds a field's present and its past, two integer levels, and the rule makes the field's future from these two and from the six neighbouring Nodes. Run forward from the source Node of its initial data, the split events are the future, every path at once; read backward from a click that happened, by the same rule, they are the past, and through two slits both of them pass through both gaps. A click is where the two meet at a declared NodeDetector, and which meeting happens is the one thing the model does not determine, the one non-local element of the description. What looks like a wave is the future; what looks like a particle is the present, the click; the track is the past.

\label{sec:whyclicks}In the model, whoever sees is itself a cluster of bound states on the lattice, a Node reads its six Ports and nothing else, and beyond the cluster's tail nothing reaches it except what a NodeDetector reports, one quantum credited at a meeting; what stands on the lattice between clicks is not a fact of the world but its possibilities, and a fact is a realised event, a quantum credited at a click. So the paper has three words and keeps them apart. The lattice is the lattice stepped by Rule3; it computes. The NodeDetector is the gate between the lattice and Universe24: it reads the lattice and writes the clicks that happened. Universe24 is those clicks, the world of the model. A lattice reading, a level, a support, a count at a Node, is a diagnostic of the computation and is labelled so, and only a click pattern is compared with a measured pattern of nature. A deterministic, reversible integer lattice read only through clicks gives the statistics of one quantum computed here, one quantum per click, the two slits' pattern and Bell's values, from a wave on the lattice and a declared counting rule; Born's rule is put in, and the Hilbert space beyond one quantum, spin and the many-body atom are not reached (derived for the wave, exclusivity and interference; an assumption, fitted, for the counting rule, the draw and the conditional; the misses by name, Section~\ref{sec:limits}); the backward reading fixes no number that the forward counting rule does not, the joint energy being computed from the forward configurations alone (Section~\ref{sec:click}).

\section{The model and the rule}\label{sec:model}

The physical location is a \emph{Node}, and its complete local information is its NodeState: for every field and every part (a part one level pair of a field: a potential field's time and axis parts, a bound state's own levels, a pair field's two lines), two integer levels, the present and the past, and one remainder, the potential fields' levels being the potential level a Node holds for the other fields to read; nothing else is kept at a Node, and every count, current, charge or stress is a reading computed from these numbers when it is needed. Nodes are joined by \emph{Links} through six directional \emph{Ports} per Node, and the cubic array of Nodes is the lattice. A \emph{field} (the long version's family) is one kind of physical value with its rational pair and its shape, and its \emph{configuration} is its levels over the lattice. A \emph{pace} is the step factor of a Node (its clock) or of a Link (its ruler). A \emph{NodeDetector} is the model's counter, a connected region of Nodes declared in the configuration file, and its \emph{click} is one quantum credited to it, the one measurement.

\subsection{The lattice and its group of 48}\label{sec:lattice}

The lattice is a box of $N_x \times N_y \times N_z$ Nodes, each joined to its six neighbours by Links through its Ports $\pm x$, $\pm y$, $\pm z$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:lattice}(a)). An axis is declared periodic, open (beyond an open face a field's rest is read, $0$ for a field without one) or folded (one layer, whose two Ports return the Node itself); an inner face is a plane of Nodes declared closed except for declared gaps, a wall with slits; and a face may be declared receding (an assumption), the box growing by a layer of zeros whenever a nonzero level reaches the Node next to it, so that a run on such a box is a run on the unbounded lattice, with no cosmological redshift since nothing stretches.

The symmetry group of the cube has $48$ elements, the $24$ rotations and, with the reflections, the signed permutations of the three axes: $O_h$, the group of the octahedron of the Nodes one interval away (Fig.~\ref{fig:lattice}(b)), with the translations the space group of the simple cubic lattice. It acts by permuting the Nodes and their Ports and carries every level pair with its Node, and Rule3 commutes with it, since its line reads the six Ports symmetrically and names no axis. Under the group the form $D$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:law} is a scalar, even under the time reversal, which exchanges a field's present and its past and carries the step to its inverse; the current $F$ is a vector, odd under it; the Wronskian $W$ is a scalar under the cube's $48$, odd under the time reversal and under the plane's reflection; and the click is a scalar, naming a region and an interval, never a Node or an axis. No boost, no rotation beyond the cube's $24$ and no scaling is in the group, so Lorentz's group and the continuum's rotations are no symmetries of the lattice, and what the paper finds of the moving clock it finds as a property of the clicks (theorem; lattice; S.4).

\subsection{Rule3 and the vacuum band}\label{sec:rule3}

The paper follows from one line of integer arithmetic, Rule3, and the inputs named beside it: one write, three readings, the one assumption of the forms (Section~\ref{sec:paces}), the counting rule of the click (the long version's credit) and the declarations named where they stand. The line holds at every Node, in every interval and for every field:
{\small\begin{equation}\label{eq:law}
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
&\text{Rule3:} && w\,\anext + r' = \textstyle\sum_{j \sim i} R_{ij}\,\mathrm{arr}_j + S\,\anow - w\,\abefore + r,\\
&&& 0 \le r' < w = 6\,\den\,\Gamma^2, \quad R_{ij} = 2\,\num\,p_a(i, j)^2 = 2\,\num\,p_i^2 q_{ij}^2 / \Gamma^2,\\
&&& S = 12\,\den\,\Gamma^2 - 12\,(\den - \num)\,p_0^2 - \textstyle\sum_{j \sim i} R_{ij};\\
&\text{the one write:} && \text{a potential field's part at a Node gains } \Bigl(\textstyle\sum_f w_f\,q_f(\text{Node}) + r\Bigr) \ddiv E,\\
&&& \text{the remainder } r \text{ kept there};\\
&\text{the readings:} && D_i = \mathrm{now}_i^2 - \mathrm{next}_i\,\mathrm{before}_i,\\
&&& W_i = \mathrm{re}_{\mathrm{now}}\,\mathrm{im}_{\mathrm{before}} - \mathrm{im}_{\mathrm{now}}\,\mathrm{re}_{\mathrm{before}},\\
&&& F_{ij} = \num\,(\mathrm{now}_i\,\mathrm{before}_j - \mathrm{before}_i\,\mathrm{now}_j).
\end{aligned}}
\end{equation}}
In the first line $\anow$, $\abefore$ and $\anext$ are the field's level at the Node now, one interval before and one interval later; $\mathrm{arr}_j$ is the level of the neighbour $j$, the six Ports summed; $r$ and $r'$ are the remainder kept at the Node before and after the step; $\Gamma$ is the Node clock; $[\num, \den]$ is the field's pair, $\cos\omega_0 = \num / \den$ its rotation per interval at wave number zero; $p_0$ is the Node's clock pace, $p_i$ its own pace and $p_a(i, j)$ the pace of the Link through the Port, the Node's pace times the Link's factor $q_{ij} / \Gamma$; at the vacuum's paces the two Links of an axis share one $R_a = 2\,\num\,\Gamma^2$ and $S = 0$, and $\ddiv$ is floor division. One whole number is formed and divided once by the wall $w$; the quotient is the next level, the remainder stays at the Node, nothing is rounded away, and the step back is the same division (Theorem~\ref{th:direction}). The second line is the only way a potential field's level (the long version's holder: a field that holds a value for others to read) is ever written, the sourcing fields' quantities $q_f$ at the Node, each a form $D_f$, a Wronskian $W_f$ or a stress $T_{aa,f}$, read in the Node's own units (Section~\ref{sec:paces}; S.34), weighted by $w_f$, summed with the part's one remainder and divided by the part's wall $E$ (a divisor, not the form $E$ of Section~\ref{sec:form}), $E_s T$ for a time part and $E_s \Wc$ for an axis part. The third line is what is read from a configuration and never written: its form $D_i$, which sources the potential fields; its Wronskian $W_i$, its charge (for a plane field $\mathrm{re}$ and $\mathrm{im}$ are its two level pairs); and its current $F_{ij}$ into the Node $i$ from its neighbour $j$. A NodeDetector reads that current through its boundary in quanta of the field's unit $\Wc = 3\,\den\,T$, $T$ the quantum's norm, one quantum's inflow being $\Wc\sin\omega_0$ in the current's unit and its energy $T\sin\omega_0$ in the quantum's, and a massless field's quantum $\Wc$ itself.

At the vacuum's paces the line reads $\anext + \abefore = \frac{\num}{3\,\den}\sum_a (\mathrm{arr}_{+a} + \mathrm{arr}_{-a})$, whose rotation at the wave vector $\mathbf k$ is $\cos\omega = \frac{\num}{3\,\den}\sum_a \cos k_a$, $\cos\omega_0 = \num / \den$ at wave number zero. For the massless pair $[1, 1]$ along one axis $\cos\omega = (2 + \cos k) / 3$, so $\omega = |k| / \sqrt 3$ as $k \to 0$, and along the cube's diagonal $\cos\omega = \cos k$ exactly, with no dispersion (S.1): light's speed at long wavelength is $1 / \sqrt 3$ Link per interval (derived; lattice; S.1), below the causal bound of one Link per interval (assumption, postulate 4 of the long version's list, the step's reach; lattice), and its group speed falls at shorter wavelengths as $1 - 0.083\,k^2$. In nature's names the vacuum's line is the discrete Klein-Gordon equation (a quantum walk's lattice form of it in \cite{arrighi2013}), exact but for the remainder term, with $c_K^2 = \num / (3\,\den)$ and $m^2 = 2(1 - \num / \den)$, the rest rotation the mass, and its slow limit at small wave number is Schr\"odinger's equation with the inertia $m^* = 3\tan\omega_0$ under three named conditions (S.53); $m^* c_m^2 = \omega_0$ by construction for every pair, with $c_m^2 = \omega_0 / (3\tan\omega_0)$ the band's kinetic scale, the model's $E = mc^2$ read in three click numbers, nature's its small-gap limit, the departure $\omega_0 / \tan\omega_0$, $1 - \omega_0^2 / 3$ to the order $\omega_0^2$ (S.18, S.21). $c_K$ is the discrete equation's coefficient at every wave number and $c_m$ the band's kinetic scale at rest; they differ at a finite gap and agree at nature's (derived; lattice; S.1, S.18, S.21, S.34 (c)). At the vacuum's pace Rule3 is the leapfrog scheme of the Klein-Gordon equation, exact in the variable $s = 2\sin(\omega / 2)$; what the law adds is one rational $[\num, \den]$ that fixes mass and speed together, departures that are predictions with bounds (Section~\ref{sec:shared}), the integers with the remainder, and the paces.

\subsection{The paces and the one assumption}\label{sec:paces}

A potential field enters a field's step by changing the field's paces at a Node, the coefficients of Rule3 there, $c_i$ the potential level the field reads at the Node (the long version's content), $U = c / \Gamma$ the same in the potential's unit, and $t_a(i, j)$ the Link's stress (the long version's tension, the level the Link holds), one number per Link from the parts at its two ends (S.7):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:paces}
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
p_0 = \Gamma\,(1 - 1 / \Gamma)^{c}, \qquad p_i = \frac{p_0(c_i)^2}{\Gamma}, \qquad p_a(i, j) = \frac{p_i\,q_{ij}}{\Gamma}, \quad q_{ij} = \Gamma - t_a(i, j), \\ c = \sum_{\text{reads}} \text{weight} \times \text{the read field's time level}.
\end{aligned}}
\end{equation}
A positive weight makes a hollow, an attraction, and a negative weight a hill; every field reads the potential fields its declaration names at the weights it names, a plane field also the charge potential, and sources what it reads, so every pair of fields that read each other is reciprocal.

Nothing in Rule3 fixes how a level enters the paces. Beside the line, the model holds that a potential level is a count of units and that every act is measured in the Node's own units, $hN = 1$ in its units form: the one assumption of the forms (assumption, fitted; the composition derived from it; lattice; S.34). From it the clock composes and a uniform potential level $U$ changes only the units, time by the lapse $N = p_0 / \Gamma$ and length by $h = p_0 / p_a$ per Link (S.34). One sentence is not in Rule3: a Link is clocked twice by its reading Node's own clock, $p_i = p_0^2 / \Gamma$, the ruler going with the clock. The lattice says why twice, since a Link has two ends; it does not say why the reading Node's clock stands at both ends rather than the product of the two ends, which gives $\beta_K = -1$ (the post-Newtonian $\beta$ as the orbits read it, Section~\ref{sec:shared}). The sentence is one discrete choice: the one assumption, $hN = 1$, is fixed by the bending's measurement, $4U_b$ for ``twice'' ($\gamma = 1$), and the perihelion's $43$ arcseconds choose the Node's own reading of the Link's pace over the two ends' product ($\beta_K = -1$, $72$ arcseconds) and then stand as the test of the choice, $\beta = 1$ as the gap closes, $\beta_K = 1.25$ at the computed pair, where the band's $k^4$ term adds $13$ to $15$ percent to the advance for an orbit in a coordinate plane (S.19); it is the first of three choices made with a known result in view (Section~\ref{sec:inputs}); every gravitational form of Section~\ref{sec:shared} is conditional on it, the bending's measurement its calibration and no test of it, the perihelion the test of the end's choice, Shapiro's delay and the redshift's factor $f(\omega_0)$ its consequences, read against Cassini and Pound and Rebka, and the shadow's $2e\,m$ bounded by measurement and not confirmed. The same assumption holds for a source that moves: its content's flux sources gravity's three odd lines as its content sources the time line, in the count's unit, $V_{ij} = -V_{ji}$, and the Port turns an arriving plane configuration by the phase $\theta_{ij} = -\theta_{ji} = 4V_{ij}\,\omega_0$ of its family, the $4$ the clock's and the ruler's exponents at $\gamma = 1$ and no number put in, paid inside Rule3's one division, the odd Link part of the metric, the clock family's three odd parts; a one-part configuration, light among them, carries no phase (assumption, the one assumption for a moving source; the coefficient derived under it; lattice; S.56). Under it the model's static metric for light is $ds^2 = -e^{-2U}dt^2 + e^{2U}d\mathbf x^2$, to $O(U / \Gamma)$ and in the ray limit, the exponential metric \cite{yilmaz}, Einstein's to the first post-Newtonian order for a static source, $\gamma = \beta = 1$, the frame's parameters derived under the same assumption for a moving source (Section~\ref{sec:shared}); the clock's rate is $N$, exactly in the lattice's variable $2\sin(\omega / 2)$ and in $\omega$ up to the finite-rotation correction of order $\omega_0^2 / 12$, $6.3$ percent at the computed pair as the exact $f(\omega_0) - 1$ and below $1.1 \times 10^{-20}$ at nature's gap under the photon bounds of Section~\ref{sec:shared} (S.31).

From the coefficients, the rotation of a configuration at a Node with clock pace $p_0$ and Link paces $p_a$ is (S.1)
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bandpace}
\boxed{\cos\omega = 1 - \Bigl(1 - \frac{\num}{\den}\Bigr)\Bigl(\frac{p_0}{\Gamma}\Bigr)^2 - \frac{\num}{3\,\den}\sum_a \Bigl(\frac{p_a}{\Gamma}\Bigr)^2 (1 - \cos k_a)}.

\section{Exact properties}\label{sec:theorems}

This section states what holds exactly on every lattice, each item proved in S.45: the reversibility of the rule, the kind of function its acts can form, the conserved form with its current, the count as a reading of the configuration (S.5, S.6), the Wronskian as the charge (S.9) and the three exact bands.

\subsection{Reversibility}\label{sec:direction}

Let $\sigma$ be the direction, $+1$ forward and $-1$ backward, $\ddiv$ and $\dmod$ the floor division and its remainder in $[0, w)$, and $\rho$ the remainder carried in. With $u = \sigma(\sum_j R_{ij}\,\mathrm{arr}_j + S\,x - w\,y) + \rho$, $z = \sigma\,(u \ddiv w)$ and $\rho' = u \dmod w$, the forward call with $(x, y, \rho) = (\anow, \abefore, r)$ gives $(z, \rho') = (\anext, r')$, and the backward call with $(x, y, \rho) = (\anow, \anext, r')$ gives $(z, \rho') = (\abefore, r)$.

\begin{theorem}[the direction]\label{th:direction}
The backward call returns $(\abefore, r)$ bit for bit; the reversibility of every step needs no second function, the odd lines' source recomputed by the backward step as the paces are (S.56 (b)). (theorem for the step and where the writes change no level; the write an assumption; lattice; S.45)
\end{theorem}

A NodeDetector's write is outside the reversal, as the initial data are; the whole run goes back, $N$ intervals forward and $N$ back returning every array of every field bit for bit, provided every number an act read at an interval's start is one the state after the interval still holds. On the two slits' file the run forward and back returns every array bit for bit over $40$ intervals, the counting window opening at $45$, so the check contains no click (computed, a run, the archive's \texttt{blind\_and\_reading.md} at 1fe3790a; lattice). The condition rules out one read: a coefficient read from a Node's own state across a step cannot be recovered at the step back, so a configuration's rotation sense is never read at a Node, and the charge potential is read by the configuration's shape, a plane or a line (Section~\ref{sec:sign}).

\subsection{What the acts can form}\label{sec:acts}

Rule3's line is one act, and declared coefficients make four special cases of it, which the law also calls acts: the read, the line with its six arrivals; the one-Node step, the arrivals declared $0$, which rotates a level by Chebyshev's recurrence; the division, $R = S = 0$, Euclid's division with the remainder kept; and the load, a declared integer in the numerator, which is how a count enters a level.

\begin{theorem}[what the acts can form, on the infinite lattice, for one field at fixed coefficients]\label{th:acts}
Every fixed finite composition of the line's four acts with fixed coefficients is an affine form of one field's levels plus a term bounded by a constant of the composition, since a floor is its argument less a fraction in $[0, 1)$; so no product of two of its levels is written by the step on the whole lattice. The products across fields enter through the coefficients the read turns from the held levels: the read multiplies. On a bounded domain every integer function is a finite sum of floors of affine forms, and the theorem says nothing there. (theorem; lattice; S.45, S.56 (c))
\end{theorem}

So every product of two levels is a reading and never a write by the step, and the readings of Eq.~\eqref{eq:law}, the stress and the share are the only products the law takes. A line enters the law only if it passes three tests, generic (one primitive with declared integers, no field's name), vector (an act on the whole state, with no root and no floating-point number) and local (its own configuration and the six neighbours); a line that needs more is a hypothesis under its own name.

\subsection{The conserved form}\label{sec:form}

The read coefficient $R_a(i \to j) = 2\,\num\,p_i^2 q_{ij}^2 / \Gamma^2$ carries the Node's factor and the Link's; where the paces differ the Node's factor is not symmetric and the Link's is, so the conserved form carries the weight $1 / p_i^2$ on its Node terms and the Link's factor on its Link terms:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:form}
\boxed{E = \sum_i \frac{w\,(\mathrm{now}_i^2 + \mathrm{before}_i^2) - S_i\,\mathrm{now}_i\,\mathrm{before}_i}{p_i^2} - 2\,\num\sum_i \sum_{j \sim i} \frac{q_{ij}^2}{\Gamma^2}\,\mathrm{now}_i\,\mathrm{before}_j}.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[the conserved form]\label{th:form}
At constant paces for which a positive Node weight $d_i$ with $d_i R_a(i \to j) = d_j R_a(j \to i)$ on every Link exists, $E$ with the weights $d_i$ is exact under the line without the rounding. Under the law's read coefficient, with $q_{ij} = q_{ji}$ and $p_i = p_0(c_i)^2 / \Gamma$, the weight $d_i = 1 / p_i^2$ cancels the Node's factor on both sides of every Link. So the weighted form, with $\bM$ the matrix of Rule3's step, $\mathbf a_{\mathrm{next}} + \mathbf a_{\mathrm{before}} = \bM\,\mathbf a_{\mathrm{now}}$, $\bD = \mathrm{diag}(1 / p_i^2)$ and $\bD\bM$ symmetric at every interval, is exact for any static potential levels and static Link stresses, and for a plane configuration under the Port's phase, the weighted coupling Hermitian, the same form exactly (S.56 (a)). Its Link term carries the Link's factor, so the current of $E$ through the Link $ij$ is $2\,\num\,(q_{ij}^2 / \Gamma^2)\,(\mathrm{now}_i\,\mathrm{before}_j - \mathrm{before}_i\,\mathrm{now}_j)$, twice the quantum share's current of Theorem~\ref{th:share}, the plain current $F_{ij}$ per Link's factor being the share's where no stress stands; the form with the plain current holds only where no stress stands, and with a stress it is not conserved (computed, two witnesses; lattice; S.32). (theorem at static paces, weighted; lattice; S.5, S.7, S.32, S.45)
\end{theorem}

With the rounding, each Node's remainder adds the term $(r - r')(\mathrm{next} - \mathrm{before}) / (w\,p_i^2)$ to the walk of the step's own two-level form $Q$, the form of now and before without the wall (derived, exact in integers, two derivations; S.5). $E$ is a diagnostic; where the paces move it changes exactly in the rationals by $E(t + 1) - E(t) = \mathbf a_{\mathrm{next}}^{\top}\bD\,(\bM(t) - \bM(t + 1))\,\mathbf a_{\mathrm{now}}$, $0$ at paces fixed in time and negative where a well deepens under a standing configuration (a theorem by substitution). The momentum density along an axis is the current per axis, and its change is a stress reading on the Links, the momentum flux at uniform coefficients the wave's own stress (a theorem for the line; S.7), a plane wave of amplitude $A$ at the wave number $k$ having the stress $T_{aa} = \num\,A^2\sin^2 k \ge 0$, as a wave's pressure is, so that light gravitates by its pressure too, written into the Link's factor and not the clock.

\subsection{The count is the configuration's quantum share}\label{sec:share}

Three things are called a count: the register's count $N$, an integer moved by clicks alone; the quantum share (the long version's share: the field's count in a region, Theorem~\ref{th:form}'s form over one quantum's norm), $\sum_i e_i / \Wc$, a lattice reading that drifts between clicks by the currents and the remainders within the floors; and the quantum $W_{\mathrm{rec}}$, the configuration's energy per quantum read once at its initial data, constant along its passage by the adiabatic invariant (S.14). \begin{equation}\label{eq:share}
\boxed{e_i = \Bigl[w\,(\mathrm{now}_i^2 + \mathrm{before}_i^2) - S_i\,\mathrm{now}_i\,\mathrm{before}_i - \mathrm{now}_i \sum_{j \sim i} R_{ij}\,\mathrm{before}_j\Bigr] \ddiv (2p_i^2)},
\end{equation}
the floor of the rational term $\tilde e_i$ of the conserved form at the Node with its weights; the counting rule reads $\sum e_i$ in quanta over $\Wc$, rounded half up to the nearest whole. At the vacuum's paces it is exactly $3\,\den\,(\mathrm{now}_i^2 + \mathrm{before}_i^2) - \num\,\mathrm{now}_i\,S_6(\mathrm{before})_i$, with $S_6$ the six-neighbour sum.

\begin{theorem}[the count is the configuration's quantum share]\label{th:share}
Over one step of Rule3 the rational quantum share at a Node changes by exactly the six currents into the Node, each weighted by its Link's factor, plus Rule3's own rounding: $\tilde e_i(\mathrm{next}, \mathrm{now}) - \tilde e_i(\mathrm{now}, \mathrm{before}) = \sum_{j \sim i} (q_{ij}^2 / \Gamma^2)\,F_{ij} - (\mathrm{next}_i - \mathrm{before}_i)(r'_i - r_i) / (2p_i^2)$ (S.5). So over any region the total energy changes only by the weighted currents through the region's boundary, the remainder terms and the NodeDetectors' floors, the floors each a residual in $[0, 1)$ per Node and level pair. The total quantum share of any lattice whose Links read the same from both ends and whose every beyond reads $0$ (the archived files, whose massless potential rests at $60$ beyond the open faces, lie outside this condition) is non-negative for $|\num| \le \den$ at the vacuum's coefficients, with equality for a static uniform level of light and, on an even periodic lattice, for its checkerboard (S.6); one Node's energy can be negative at the initial data's edge, and a region's by at most half a level-square per boundary Port. (theorem under the two conditions; lattice; S.5, S.6)
\end{theorem}

Over the whole passage of one forward wave packet the inflow accumulated at a region's boundary equals the region's final energy plus what left through its back, each bounded below by the theorem, so the inflow is non-negative up to the rounding and the counting rule by the energies is well defined; where a counting window is cut inside a passage the NodeDetector credits $\max(s_R, 0)$, the floor at $0$ the counting rule's declared rule (an assumption).

\subsection{The Wronskian as the charge}\label{sec:sign}

A field of quanta laid as a plane carries its configuration as two level pairs, $(\mathrm{re}, \mathrm{im})$, stepped by one call of Rule3 with one set of coefficients, each with its own remainder. The charge density at a Node is the reading
\begin{equation}\label{eq:wronskian}
\boxed{W_i = \mathrm{re}_{\mathrm{now}}\,\mathrm{im}_{\mathrm{before}} - \mathrm{im}_{\mathrm{now}}\,\mathrm{re}_{\mathrm{before}}},
\end{equation}
the Wronskian of the two pairs.
