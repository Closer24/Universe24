\begin{abstract}
Can one local rule in whole numbers, on a cubic lattice read only through detectors' clicks, carry the quantum and the relativistic experiments? The law is one line, Rule3: at every Node, each interval, a second-order step of a family's level from its two levels and its six neighbours' arrivals, with integer coefficients from the family's pair and the Node's paces, and one Euclidean division whose remainder stays at the Node. A quantum moves whole when a remainder crosses the quantum's action: the count's line, Rule3 read on its own conserved current, whose division is the click, the one non-local act. Exact on the lattice: reversibility bit for bit, a conserved quadratic form, the count's conservation, one quantum per click, Born's rule, no signalling through the click, and for a pair record the correlation $\cos 2(a - b)$ with the Clauser--Horne--Shimony--Holt sum $2\sqrt 2$. The families are bands of the rule: three exact rotations of periods $6$, $4$ and $3$, three tensor ranks from the three axes, one free number per rank. A universe whose every number is the rule's is stated as a hypothesis: pairs from $1$, $2$ and $3$, divisors $1$, zero free numbers; there every bound body is one Node between the edge $0.2255\,\Gamma$ and the horizon $\Gamma \ddiv 2$, the observer is a cluster of such Nodes, and the Node clock grows by $6$ each interval. Eighteen experiments are stated as blind expectations from the algebra, beside the forms of nature they are compared with. None is run in this paper.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} integer lattice model; one local rule; the click as the measurement; Bell correlations; discrete relativity; pre-registered simulation

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\paragraph{The question.} The law of this paper is one line of integer arithmetic applied at every Node of a cubic lattice at every interval. Nothing else acts: no field equation beside it, no law of motion for a body, no rate, no draw, no real number. The paper states that line, proves what is exact on the lattice, derives what follows from the line alone, names what is assumed beside it, and writes down, before any run, what eighteen experiments read in clicks. The forms of Newton, Einstein, Lorentz, de Broglie, Born, Malus and Bell appear only on the comparison side, beside the law's own expectation; none is an input.

\paragraph{The law in one line.} At every Node, each interval, for every family:
{\small\begin{equation}\label{eq:law}
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
&\text{Rule3:} && w\,\anext + r' = \textstyle\sum_{a \in \{x, y, z\}} R_a\,(\mathrm{arr}_{+a} + \mathrm{arr}_{-a}) + S\,\anow - w\,\abefore + r, \quad 0 \le r' < w,\\
&&& w = 6\,\den\,\Gamma^2, \quad R_a = 2\,\num\,p_a^2,\\
&&& S = 12\,\den\,\Gamma^2 - 12\,(\den - \num)\,p_0^2 - 4\,\num\,(p_x^2 + p_y^2 + p_z^2);\\
&\text{the count's line:} && T\,c_{\mathrm{next}} + r' = T\,c_{\mathrm{now}} + \textstyle\sum_{j} F_{ij} + r, \quad 0 \le r' < T,\\
&&& F_{ij} = \text{weight} \times (\mathrm{now}_i\,\mathrm{before}_j - \mathrm{before}_i\,\mathrm{now}_j);\\
&\text{the one write:} && \text{a level at a Node gains } (w\,q(\text{Node}) + r) \ddiv E, \text{ the remainder } r \text{ kept there}.
\end{aligned}}
\end{equation}}
In the first line $\anow$, $\abefore$ and $\anext$ are the family's level at the Node now, one interval before and one interval on; $\mathrm{arr}_{\pm a}$ is the arrival through the Port $\pm a$, the neighbour's level or its rotation by the Link's angle; $r$ and $r'$ are the remainder kept at the Node before and after the step; $\Gamma$ is the Node clock, the pace of an empty Node; $[\num, \den]$ is the family's pair, with $\cos\omega_0 = \num/\den$ its rotation per interval at wave number zero; $p_0$ is the Node's pace and $p_a$ its pace along the axis $a$, both the Node clock lowered by the levels the family reads there; and $\ddiv$ is the floor division. The second line is the same rule for the family of clicks: $c$ is the count, the number of quanta at the Node; $T$ is the quantum's action, the one unit of every family; $F_{ij}$ is the current into the Node $i$ from its neighbour $j$ over the interval, the bilinear form of the record's two levels at the two ends; and the division is the click, one quantum moving whole exactly when the remainder crosses $T$. The third line is every write of a body or a record into a level: $q(\text{Node})$ is the writer's own quantity there, $w$ the one weight with which the writer's family reads the written family, and $E$ the written row's divisor. Everything else is a number in a file. Section~\ref{sec:rule} states the first line with its proofs, Section~\ref{sec:click} the second and Section~\ref{sec:write} the third; the paper cites the law's document \cite{algebra} by the name of the section each formula stands in.

\section{The GameBoard and Rule3}\label{sec:rule}

This section states the object and proves what is exact on it: the lattice, the families, the one line with its coefficients, its reversibility, what its acts can and cannot form, the paces and their guard, the transport and the conserved form. Every formula is the law's \cite[the GameBoard and the families; Rule3]{algebra}.

\subsection{The objects}\label{sec:objects}

A \emph{Node} is a physical location. The \emph{GameBoard} is all the Nodes together, a box of $\mathrm{shape} = (N_x, N_y, N_z)$ Nodes; each axis is periodic (the box a torus on that axis) or open (a face beyond which no Node lies), as the world declares. Two Nodes that differ by one step along one axis are joined by a \emph{Link}; each Node has six Links, one through each of its six \emph{Ports}, named by their outward direction $+x$, $-x$, $+y$, $-y$, $+z$, $-z$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:lattice}). An axis of one layer folds: its two Ports return the Node itself. The local information of a Node is its \emph{NodeState}: the levels and remainders of the families at that Node, and nothing else. A change of a NodeState is an \emph{Event}; the rule that makes it is a \emph{LocalRule}, and there is one, Rule3.

A \emph{family} is one kind of physical value with its own declaration. A family's \emph{record} is a set of levels over the GameBoard, one integer per Node per level, with a remainder per Node; a family with two levels per Node holds a pair $(\mathrm{re}, \mathrm{im})$ at each Node. A \emph{body} is a set of Nodes on which a family's \emph{count} stands, with the values the body's writer declares (its content, its momentum, its spin, its moment); a body is one connected region.

\paragraph{The universe's integers.} Every physical number comes from the world's files: the universe file (the families and the universe's integers) and the world file (the bodies, the detectors, the readings). The engine holds no number of its own \cite[a family's declaration]{algebra}. The universe's integers are the Node clock $\Gamma$, the pace of an empty Node and every family's clock unit; the quantum's action $T$, the action of one quantum of any family, a unit as $\Gamma$ is; the momentum unit $Q_{\mathrm{unit}}$; the twist table (Section~\ref{sec:transport}); and the least number of remainder values a giving family's pair must leave at its giving Node, so that the residue of the ladder (Section~\ref{sec:ladder}) is spread over them. A family's row declares its pair $[\num, \den]$, written as one integer $m$ over the GameBoard's one denominator $\Gamma$ (Section~\ref{sec:mass}), its parts ($1$, $3$ or $6$ components, the tensor rank of what writes it), its phase ($2$ where it carries quanta, $1$ where it never clicks), the families it reads, and where it holds a body's count, the row's divisor $E_s$. The weights of every read are $1$, the sign is the body's and the action of one quantum is $T$ for every family, so the files hold $\Gamma$, $T$, the matter pair and the two divisors, and nothing else of physics.

\subsection{The line}\label{sec:line}

At every Node, each interval, for every family, with the wall $w$, the read coefficient $R_a$ on each axis $a$, the self coefficient $S$, the level $\anow$ at the interval's start, $\abefore$ one interval earlier and the remainder $r$ kept at the Node,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rule3}
w\,\anext + r' = \sum_{a} R_a\,(\mathrm{arr}_{+a} + \mathrm{arr}_{-a}) + S\,\anow - w\,\abefore + r, \qquad 0 \le r' < w,
\end{equation}
$\mathrm{arr}_{\pm a}$ the arrival through the Port $\pm a$: the neighbour's level, or its rotation by the Link's angle (Section~\ref{sec:transport}); $0$ beyond an open face; the Node's own level on a folded axis. The remainder is the Node's own and never travels. The coefficients at a Node, from its paces (Section~\ref{sec:paces}) and its family's pair, are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:coefficients}
w = 6\,\den\,\Gamma^2, \qquad R_a = 2\,\num\,p_a^2, \qquad S = 12\,\den\,\Gamma^2 - 12\,(\den - \num)\,p_0^2 - 4\,\num\,(p_x^2 + p_y^2 + p_z^2).
\end{equation}
One floor division per Node per interval; the remainder stays at the Node. A held family's field (a real field of phase $1$) steps at its row's pair with the pace $1$ and the wall $3\,\den$, at first order \cite[the line]{algebra}.

\begin{proposition}[the vacuum's wave rule and the band]\label{prop:band}
At the vacuum's pace, $p_0 = p_a = \Gamma$, the self coefficient vanishes and Eq.~\eqref{eq:rule3} is, up to the remainder, $\anext + \abefore = \frac{\num}{3\,\den}\,S_6(\anow)$ with $S_6$ the sum over the six neighbours. A plane wave of wave number $k$ along an axis rotates by $\omega$ per interval with
\[
\cos\omega = \frac{\num}{3\,\den}\,(2 + \cos k), \qquad \cos\omega_0 = \frac{\num}{\den} \text{ at } k = 0,
\]
and off an axis $\cos k_x + \cos k_y + \cos k_z$ stands in place of $2 + \cos k$. The family's band of rotations is $[\num/(3\,\den), \num/\den]$ in the cosine; light, the pair $[1, 1]$, has $\cos\omega = (2 + \cos k)/3$, its group velocity at long wavelength $1/\sqrt 3$ Links per interval, and its band reaches $\cos\omega = 1$; a pair with $\den > \num$ has the gap $\cos\omega_0 = \num/\den$ below $1$, a rest rotation, a mass.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
With $p_0 = p_a = \Gamma$, $S = 12\,\den\,\Gamma^2 - 12\,\den\,\Gamma^2 + 12\,\num\,\Gamma^2 - 12\,\num\,\Gamma^2 = 0$ and $R_a / w = \num / (3\,\den)$. For $\anow = A\cos(k x - \omega t)$ along the axis $x$, $S_6(\anow) = (4 + 2\cos k)\,\anow$ and $\anext + \abefore = 2\cos\omega\,\anow$, so $2\cos\omega = \frac{\num}{3\,\den}(4 + 2\cos k)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[the pace is conformal]\label{prop:conformal}
At a Node whose pace is $p$ on every axis, every family's rotation at every wave number obeys
\[
\sin^2\frac{\omega}{2} = \Bigl(\frac{p}{\Gamma}\Bigr)^2 \sin^2\frac{\omega_{\mathrm{vac}}(k)}{2},
\]
$\omega_{\mathrm{vac}}(k)$ the rotation of Proposition~\ref{prop:band}: the pace multiplies the clock of every wave and every bound mode alike.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
At an isotropic pace $S = 12\,\den\,(\Gamma^2 - p^2)$ and $R_a = 2\,\num\,p^2$, so $2\cos\omega = \frac{\num\,p^2}{3\,\den\,\Gamma^2}(4 + 2\cos k) + 2\Bigl(1 - \frac{p^2}{\Gamma^2}\Bigr) = 2 - 2\Bigl(\frac{p}{\Gamma}\Bigr)^2\Bigl(1 - \frac{\num}{3\,\den}(2 + \cos k)\Bigr) = 2 - 2\Bigl(\frac{p}{\Gamma}\Bigr)^2 (1 - \cos\omega_{\mathrm{vac}})$, and $1 - \cos\omega = 2\sin^2(\omega/2)$.
\end{proof}

Two clocks at one level shift alike, $d\ln\omega / dc = 2\tan(\omega/2) / (\omega\,p)$ per unit of the held level $c$, and a packet's fall in a pace gradient does not depend on its family or its shape beyond its band's own \cite[the line]{algebra}: this is the paper's equivalence principle, a reading of the line and no postulate. The clock's slowing is this pace: a record at a Node of pace $p$ rotates and moves as a record at $\Gamma$ does, with its intervals $p / \Gamma$ as long.

\subsection{The direction}\label{sec:direction}

\begin{theorem}[the step is reversible bit for bit]\label{th:direction}
Let $\sigma$ be the direction, $+1$ forward and $-1$ backward, $\ddiv$ and $\dmod$ the floor division and its remainder in $[0, w)$, and $\rho$ the remainder carried in. With
\[
u = \sigma\Bigl(\sum_a R_a\,\mathrm{arr}_a + S\,x - w\,y\Bigr) + \rho, \qquad z = \sigma\,(u \ddiv w), \qquad \rho' = u \dmod w,
\]
forward $(x, y, \rho) = (\anow, \abefore, r)$ gives $(z, \rho') = (\anext, r')$, and backward $(x, y, \rho) = (\anow, \anext, r')$ gives $(z, \rho') = (\abefore, r)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Forward, $\sigma = 1$, the line is the definition of $\ddiv$ and $\dmod$. Backward, $\sigma = -1$: write $Y$ for the right side's first two terms, $\sum_a R_a\,\mathrm{arr}_a + S\,\anow$. Then $u = w\,\anext - Y + r'$ and $z = -\lfloor u / w \rfloor = \lceil (Y - w\,\anext - r') / w \rceil$. From the forward line, $Y - w\,\anext - r' = w\,\abefore - r$ with $0 \le r < w$, whose ceiling divided by $w$ is $\abefore$ exactly; and $\rho' = u - w\lfloor u / w \rfloor = w\,\abefore - (Y - w\,\anext - r') = r$. So the backward call returns $(\abefore, r)$ bit for bit, and the reversibility of every run needs no second function \cite[the direction]{algebra}.
\end{proof}

The whole interval is a bijection but for the click: the places of the interval (Section~\ref{sec:interval}) run backward in reverse order, each primitive by its own inverse, and the count's line by the same line with the current reversed (Section~\ref{sec:count}).

\subsection{The four acts and what they can form}\label{sec:acts}

Rule3's line is one act; declared coefficients make it four \cite[the four acts]{algebra}: (a) \emph{the read}, the line over the GameBoard with the six arrivals; (b) \emph{the one-Node step}, the line with the neighbours' reads declared $0$, $\anext + \abefore = (a / b)\,\anow$, the rotation by the angle whose doubled cosine is $a / b$ (Chebyshev's recurrence); with $a / b = 1$ and the coefficient on $\abefore$ declared $0$ it keeps a level, and with $a / b = 2$ from $(0, 1)$ it counts, $a_t = t$; (c) \emph{the division}, the line with $R = S = 0$, $w\,\anext + r' = \text{the numerator} + r$, Euclid's division with the remainder kept; (d) \emph{the load}, a declared integer in the numerator, how a count enters a level. A composition of the acts is a finite sequence of them, each with its coefficients (a pair, a weight, a wall, a load) declared in the files, applied to declared levels.

\begin{theorem}[what the acts can form]\label{th:acts}
Every composition of the four acts is a piecewise-linear function of the levels with integer slopes: a sum of linear forms and of floors of linear forms, composed. No such function is quadratic in the levels.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Each act is a linear form of the levels or the floor of one; a floor of a linear form is piecewise linear with integer slopes; sums and compositions of piecewise-linear functions with integer slopes are of the same kind. The second difference of such a function in any one level is $0$ almost everywhere, while the second difference of a product $\mathrm{now}_i\,\mathrm{before}_j$ in the pair $(\mathrm{now}_i, \mathrm{before}_j)$ is $1$.
\end{proof}

What the theorem separates: every product of two levels in the engine is a \emph{booking}, a reading of a bilinear form with declared coefficients and no write (Section~\ref{sec:booking}); the one thing written from a product is the count, by the count's line, and that product is Rule3's own conserved current (Theorem~\ref{th:form}). \emph{The three tests of every rule:} a line enters the law only if it is generic (one primitive with declared integers, no family's name and no kind), vector (one of the acts on the state, no root and no float) and local (its own record and the six neighbours, nothing kept at a Node beyond the law's numbers). A hypothesis that needs more is stated under its own name, outside the law.

\subsection{The paces and the guard}\label{sec:paces}

The \emph{pace} of a Node for a family is what its reads make of the Node clock \cite[the paces]{algebra}:
\begin{align}
p_0 &= \Gamma - \sum_{\text{reads}} \bigl(\text{weight} \times \text{by} \times \text{the read family's time level}\bigr), \label{eq:pace}\\
p_a &= p_0 - \sum_{\text{reads}} \bigl(\text{the same}\bigr) - \sum_{\text{reads}} \bigl(\text{weight} \times \text{by} \times \text{its } aa \text{ component} \ddiv 2\bigr), \quad a = x, y, z, \notag
\end{align}
one division per read per axis, rounded at the read: the pace is a coefficient of the interval and no level, so no remainder is kept for it. A positive weight is a hollow (the read level slows the clock, an attraction); a negative weight is a hill; by ``$q$'' the weight is multiplied by the reading record's charge sign. A family with no reads steps at $p_0 = p_a = \Gamma$, the plain rule. The time level lowers the clock's pace $p_0$ once and each axis's pace $p_a$ once more, so a wave's speed along a Link falls by the level twice (the Link as well as the clock, as nature's light past a body bends by twice the clock's share) while a mode's rest rotation, from $S$ alone, falls by the level once.

\begin{proposition}[the guard]\label{prop:guard}
The step is stable at a Node exactly while $0 < p \le P$ with the edge
\[
P = \isqrt\bigl(2\,\den\,\Gamma^2 \ddiv (\den + \num)\bigr),
\]
$\Gamma$ for light and above $\Gamma$ where $\num < \den$; every pace at or below $\Gamma$ is inside it.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The mode at wave number $\pi$ on every axis has $S_6(\anow) = -6\,\anow$, so its factor is $(S - 6R) / w = 2 - 2\,(1 + \num/\den)\,(p / \Gamma)^2$, which is $-2\,\num/\den$ at $p = \Gamma$ and falls below $-2$, the edge of Chebyshev's recurrence, once $p^2\,(\den + \num)$ exceeds $2\,\den\,\Gamma^2$; there the record grows without bound. $P$ is the largest integer pace at which $p^2\,(\den + \num) \le 2\,\den\,\Gamma^2$ holds. Below $0$ the pace is no clock.
\end{proof}

A hill lessens a hollow and never exceeds it; a pace outside the guard refuses the run, naming the Node. \emph{The band at a pace}, a reading of the line and no new rule: at a Node of pace $p$ on every axis a record's rotation at wave number $k$ along an axis is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bandpace}
\cos\omega = 1 - \Bigl(\frac{p}{\Gamma}\Bigr)^2\Bigl(1 - \frac{\num}{3\,\den}\,(2 + \cos k)\Bigr),
\end{equation}
so the band of rotations a body's Nodes carry narrows with $p^2$: a record whose rotation lies outside the band there is evanescent inside (for $[1, 1]$, $\cosh\kappa = (\Gamma / p)^2 (1 - \cos k) - 1$ per Node) and is reflected whole, and one whose rotation lies inside is slowed to the band's group velocity. Light meets a total mirror exactly where $p < \Gamma\sin(k / 2)$, that is where the held count $c$ exceeds $\Gamma\,(1 - \sin(k / 2))$ ($0.2929\,\Gamma$ at the wavelength $4$ Links), and a window otherwise, with the step's partial share at each face. A cluster of clusters is the same structure one level up, Nodes each a bound body clicking at one another, with no number that bounds the depth. \emph{The click is the pointer:} the click is the only thing that passes between a cluster and its neighbour and between a level and the next. \emph{The levels at a Node are the nesting:} the three held levels of a Node, the matter's tail, the charge's well and gravity's well, are the three kinds of binding by rank, and the size of a level is the depth in its kind, gravity's well at a Node being the sum of the tails of every body above it (each $c_i\,t_i^{2 d_i}$), so a GameBoard reading names the clusters a Node belongs to; a level $n + 1$ is bound by the same inequality as the level $1$ with the cluster's total count over its members' spacing in place of a pixel's count over one Link, and the one bound on the depth is the horizon at a Node, the content read there under $\Gamma$, at which a cluster's centre is the horizon pixel. \emph{Three kinds of binding, not three levels:} quanta into a pixel by the matter's tail (the step in count the pixel's $c$, in ticks its period), pixels into a white cluster by the charge's well (the step $E_s$ over $q\,M$, $37$ at $\Gamma = 3{,}672$, the same number as the coupling's gap to $\alpha$), and white clusters into a gravitational cluster by gravity's well ($e^{\kappa d}$ for tail-touching pixels, the sum's well beyond); the third kind nests without bound, so the count of gravitational levels is the universe's history and no number of the law.

\paragraph{The ladder of the bound states.} The bound states of a Node at a given $\Gamma$ are one rung per integer amplitude $b$ from the edge to the horizon, each carrying the count $c = b^2\,(2 - 2\cos\omega_b(c))$ at its self-consistent rotation: at $\Gamma = 24$ there are two rungs ($b = 5$ and $b = 8$) and at $\Gamma = 6{,}000$ about $67$ on the law ($b = 46$ to $112$, $c = 1{,}396$ to $2{,}700$, the period $7.5$ to $13.4$ intervals), the ratio of neighbouring rungs $1 + 2 / b$ and the whole ladder spanning $2.22$ (COMPUTATION), a near continuum that singles out no ratio: neither $1{,}836$ nor the hadrons' $1.31$ is predicted, the mass ratio to the electron fixing $\Gamma$ and nothing more.

\paragraph{The two readings of $\Gamma$ are two levels.} Every level has its own $\Gamma$ and its own interval, a level's interval being the period of the record one level below it, so the proton to electron ratio reads the atomic level's $\Gamma$ ($3{,}672$ to $8{,}142$) while the universe's age reads the top level's $\Gamma = 6\,t$ (below); the tick ratio of one level is the cluster's period over the pixel's, $e^{\kappa d}$, $7.6$ for two pixels of $2{,}000$ at two Links at $\Gamma = 6{,}000$, and about $66$ such levels lie between the atomic $\Gamma$ and the cosmic one (COMPUTATION). The chain from the universe's age to the atomic count is not closed: the step per level is a law's number only if the law fixes the stable cluster, and the count of levels and the law tying a level's $\Gamma$ to its parent's are open by name.

\subsection{The Node clock is not constant}\label{sec:gamma}

\begin{hypothesis}[the Node clock grows]\label{hyp:gamma}
$\Gamma_{t+1} = \Gamma_t + 6$, the step the six Ports of a Node and no free number.
\end{hypothesis}

The rule's physics depends on the ratios $c / \Gamma$ and $m / \Gamma$ alone, so every pair steps with $\Gamma$ ($[m_0\,\Gamma_t \ddiv \Gamma_0,\ \Gamma_t]$, the matter $+4$ and light and the charge $+6$ per interval) and the wall, $A$ and the twist's unit are derived from $\Gamma_t$ each interval, the file holding the Node clock as $[\Gamma_0, 6]$ and the engine no number. A count is the integer its Node holds and stays, so a pixel that does not gather falls under the rising edge $0.2255\,\Gamma_t$ and dissolves after $(c / 0.2255 - \Gamma_0) / 6$ intervals, the small giving to the large being the way a body keeps its $c / \Gamma$; the distinct bound counts between the edge and the horizon number $0.2745\,\Gamma$, one and two thirds more kinds each interval ($7$ at $24$, $1{,}647$ at $6{,}000$). Light on $[\Gamma, \Gamma]$ does not drift; the clocks of pixels do, a pixel of a fixed count slowing as $c / \Gamma$ falls, with the rate
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hubble}
H = \frac{6}{\Gamma}\,\Bigl|\frac{d\ln\omega_b}{d\ln(c / \Gamma)}\Bigr|,
\end{equation}
$5 \times 10^{-4}$ to $1.3 \times 10^{-3}$ per interval at $\Gamma = 6{,}000$ (COMPUTATION), the rule's Hubble rate, and $\Gamma = 6\,t$ reads the universe's age in intervals; a look reads it as the takers' clock ratio growing by $(6 / \Gamma)(s_{\mathrm{taker}} - s_{\mathrm{giver}})$ per interval. A GameBoard closed on every axis has no rest of the held field under a net count other than $0$, so a bound universe closed on itself carries a mean of the held field that drifts by the net count over the Nodes each interval: the hypothesis that the universe expands, under its own name and no look of today.

\subsection{The well is the count and no field; the generator is Rule3}\label{sec:nofield}

The threshold is $\kappa = 0$, the bottom of the vacuum's band $[1, 1]$ at $k = 0$ and the self-binding edge alike, where a tail is infinite and a static field on $[1, 1]$ is Laplace's, no decay and no rest on a closed GameBoard; no computation of the law and none of the generator stands there: every bound body sits inside its band at $\kappa > 0$ and light inside $[1, 1]$ at $k > 0$. So in the rule's own universe a held family has no record of its own that steps and no rest that is solved: its level at a Node is the count laid there and nothing else, every interval; the well at $d$ Links is the matter record's own count there, $c\,t^{2d}$, laid from its form over its period, and gravity's well at a Node is the sum of those tails. Under a divisor above $1$ the held field on $[1, 1]$ is the universe of record's.

\emph{The generator is Rule3:} a body's bound record is what Rule3 makes of its count at its Node in the engine's own integers, seeded at the Node with the count's well held, run on its GameBoard over many periods until the record stands (its levels repeating over a whole period), and the record itself is the mode file; its period, its amplitude, its clock pair, its tail and its wavelength are readings of the standing record and never inputs, and a clock pair, a tail or a twist computed in floating point is a tool's defect. At $\Gamma = 24$ Rule3 in integers gives the periods $9.6$, $11$, $12$ and $14$ at the counts $8$ to $11$ on the law (the Green's function: $9.55$, $10.8$, $12.2$, $13.8$), and the pixels the integers hold are $b = 5$ carrying $8$ to $10$ quanta and $b = 8$ carrying $12$ to $15$ (COMPUTATION). \emph{The count is the record's form over its period:} an integer record's form $D$ at a Node moves between $0$ and $2c$ within one period, so the count is $(\sum_{\text{period}} D) \ddiv (P\,T)$, read once per period on the record's own clock, never the form of one interval; a declared count is a reading checked against the loaded record's form at the gate $|c - D \ddiv T| \le 2\,\isqrt(c) + 1$, refused by name beyond it; a count moves only whole and never below zero, a Node at $0$ gives nothing, and a clamp that sets a negative result to $0$ creates quanta and is no rule. \emph{The generator has no sink:} Rule3 is reversible and nothing leaves a GameBoard but a quantum by a click at a face; a fill of $0$ beyond an open face is a wall, so the unbound part of a seed never leaves; a seed of one Node carries the mode's share $1 / (1 + 6 t^2 + 18 t^4 + 38 t^6 + 66 t^8)$ and the rest is debris spread over the GameBoard's Nodes, so the record is read at the Node over many periods and purified by re-seeding from the record within three Links of the Node at its peak's phase, integers alone. The generator of the rule's universe writes bound bodies alone: a body is its count at its Node, its record the bound state of that count and nothing else, and a world file holds counts, their bound records and the band $[1, 1]$, and nothing more.

The root is the law in one line. From it alone: the exact bands, the colours, the tube's linear cost, the edge $P$, entanglement as the click and the observer as a cluster. With the named assumption of the pairs from $1$, $2$ and $3$: the matter pair $[2, 3]$ and the crack $[1, 3]$. \section{What the law says of nature}\label{sec:nature}

\subsection{The postulates}\label{sec:postulates}

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] The world is Nodes and Events. Space is the GameBoard; an Event is a wall crossed by a remainder at a Node: a level stepped, a count moved, a click; nothing else happens.
\item[2.] A Node holds bounded local information: its NodeState, fixed in size, read from itself and its six neighbours; no read beyond them, no list that grows with the world, nothing of the past kept. Every physical update uses its own record and the six causally available neighbours with fixed work and storage for a fixed set of families; a self-field estimated or subtracted from anything global is forbidden, whatever the size of its final answer.
\item[3.] Consistency is local and causal: an Event first changes its own Node and travels Link by Link, one Link per interval, each Node updating on receipt under the same rule. The one exception is the click: a record ends at once, whole, at the detector.
\item[4.] The causal speed is one Link per interval, built in; every other speed is a rational, a count of Links over a count of intervals.
\item[5.] Every physical calculation is on bounded integers; there is no float, no root and no draw in the law; a Node keeps only the law's own numbers: each record's two levels and its remainder, the family's pair and the Node clock from the content there.
\item[6.] A measurement is a detector's click, an action of the law on the state: the record ends at the detector and the detector's own record changes. Only a click is compared with nature or pinned as an expectation; displays and the host's readings read state and write nothing.
\item[7.] The Inside is the GameBoard, where no one measures; the Outside is the detectors and their clicks, the only thing claimed to represent nature.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The constants}\label{sec:constants}

The speed of light is one Link per interval. The quantum of action is one click, its energy the quantum's action $T$. Newton's constant is not declared: a body of energy count $s$ makes the level $c(r) = s \times \text{the reference flux} / E_s / r$ at the distance $r$ in Links (the hold's row: the count enters the field's line over the row's divisor $E_s$), and the level slows the clock by the potential $\Phi = -c / \Gamma$, so
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G}
G = \frac{\text{the reference flux}}{\Gamma\,E_s} \text{ per unit of energy count},
\end{equation}
with the reference flux a number of the GameBoard's geometry (about $1.4$ for a cube of side $3$) and the divisor $E_s$ the energy unit setting the mass of one unit of level \cite[the constants]{algebra}. A world at a smaller $\Gamma$ has stronger gravity per unit of content; the ratios between rows carry $G$ once and cancel it. The quantum of distance is the Link, of time the interval; nothing between two Nodes or two intervals is observed. The potential at a body's Nodes changes by $G$ per unit of energy count and never by less: the quantum of gravity is the click.

\paragraph{The three free numbers and their bounds.} The rule's own universe (Section~\ref{sec:own}) carries none of the three; the universe of record carries all three, and its three are these. $E_s$ of gravity is fixed by no line of the law, as nature's $G$ is fixed by none (Rule3 is linear and its form's scale is free); its bound is the law's: at every Node of every body the pace stays positive, $\text{count} \times (1 + K / E_s) < \Gamma$ with $K$ the hold's kernel summed over the body (about $51$ for a body wider than the reach), so $E_s > K \times \text{count} / (\Gamma - \text{count})$ at the world's heaviest Node, and the reading's resolution bounds it above. The charge's $E_s$ is free as $\alpha$ is, bounded below by the same pace at the bound charge's Nodes and by the train (one quantum at least one period). The matter pair is free as the mass is, bounded by the band: $\den > \num$, and the reach $\cosh\kappa = 3\,\den / \num - 2$ fitting the body's width.

\subsection{How the forms of nature are reached, not assumed}\label{sec:reached}

The law holds none of the forms it is compared with. The causal speed is one Link per interval by construction, and light's group velocity $1 / \sqrt 3$ at long wavelength is Proposition~\ref{prop:band}'s, not a constant declared. Planck's constant is the click's unit $T$, and $E = h\nu$ is the record's click rate against its rotation. \section{The engine}\label{sec:engine}

The engine is an integer simulator of the GameBoard, and the code holds nothing physical: the families, their attributes and their integers come from the run's files, and every primitive is one folder found by its name \cite{engine}. Three things happen on the GameBoard and nothing else: Rule3 at every Node for every family, in one function that is the only place holding the rule's arithmetic; the one write in its five instances; and the click. A run reads three files, the world (the GameBoard's shape and boundaries, the intervals to run, the bodies by their Nodes and counts, the detectors and the readings), the universe (the families' rows and the universe's integers) and the start file, steps the GameBoard a declared number of intervals, and writes one output file: the verdict (lawful, refused by name, or a leak of a family with no source), every click with its detector, interval, record, giver and taker, the count per detector, the comparison per registered pin, and the declared readings, each labelled DETECTOR, GAMEBOARD or HOST. Every run is headless; the engine never draws.

\paragraph{The folders and the step file.} Every primitive is one folder with its declaration (its name, its place in the interval, what it reads, what it writes, its line in the law) and its function, $\mathrm{apply}(\text{term}, \text{start}, \text{own})$, whose term is one line of the files, whose start is the interval's read-only state and whose own holds the remainders of its divisions; the register finds the folders at load and refuses, by name, a folder without a card, a name declared twice, a term naming a primitive that is not built, or a call at a place other than the declared one. The interval's order lives in one data file shared by every world, an ordered list of acts, its digest in every run's output. At run time every array of the interval's start is read-only for the whole walk, and an act writes only into the arrays its card grants; a write elsewhere is refused under the act's name. The step is a bijection but for the click: the backward step returns the state to the interval before, bit for bit, by Theorem~\ref{th:direction}.

\paragraph{The gates.} Every pull request runs the check: the linters and the typing, the tests of the changed files and their consumers, and the gates \cite{engine}. Genericity: a seeded generator draws one to twenty families with random English names and random admitted attributes; every draw loads and runs, and five properties hold, that renaming the families leaves the run bit for bit, that reordering them leaves it bit for bit, that a family with no source stays exactly zero, that Rule3's conserved form holds where no click and no load acts, and that the backward run returns the start. Code shape: no Rule3 arithmetic outside its one function, no shift of a level across Nodes outside the transport, every docstring one line. Engine gates: no new number, no family's name and no unapproved module in the source. Language, ownership and documents: one language, one owner per area, the three documents and nothing else. The acceptance tests state what the finished engine must satisfy: no family name, no integer of the universe, no default, no flag and no version in the code, the rule as one function.

\paragraph{Reproduction.} The code, the law's document, the engine's document and the decisions are archived \cite{zenodo}. A world is run headless by one command naming its file; the output file carries the verdict, the clicks and the readings, and the input's digest. A pin is written in a pins file before the run, and the output then says match or miss per pin; a run is compared with a pin only on the owner's word. No world is recorded or replayed in the checks; a world's reference is the blind expectation beside it.

\section{What is open, and what is not claimed}\label{sec:open}

The law's document keeps its open points by name \cite[what is open]{algebra}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] Whether a body at rest jitters when its current's swing reaches $T / 2$.
\item[2.] The self-source's cubic term: not in the engine, the line is the squares' sum alone.
\item[3.] The twist table's small angles: a triple with $d$ at most $10^9$ reaches no angle below $6.3 \times 10^{-5}$ radians.
\item[4.] The equivalence principle holds to the band's own shape: $\Delta \times 2\tan(\omega_0 / 2)$ differs by a tenth across the bound bodies of one table; the fall with two light bodies reads the rest.
\item[5.] The two thirds' charges: a third with the sign $q$ reads a third, and the composite read modulo a turn identifies $-1/3$ with $+2/3$, but no line gives the up third $2/3$ and the down third $1/3$.
\item[6.] The three generations: a hypothesis under its own name, the three ranks of the exact band $[-1, 2]$; no line gives a rank a mass.
\item[7.] The exact band $[0, 1]$ of the period $4$: a family that never clicks and carries no colour, unnamed in nature here.
\end{enumerate}
Beside them stand the three distances of Section~\ref{sec:tree}, $\alpha$, $m_e / m_P$ and $m_p / m_e$, of which the last fixes the atomic level's $\Gamma$ and is not predicted, and the spin $1/2$.

Not claimed: any agreement with nature, since no row of Table~\ref{tab:rows} is run here; the Lorentz group, which is no symmetry of the lattice; a controlled phase of order one between two records, which Theorem~\ref{th:acts} excludes from the acts alone; the count of gravitational levels, which is the universe's history; and the reach of the divisor $1$ in the universe of record. Every hypothesis of the paper is named as one, and the tree of Section~\ref{sec:tree} says what each claim needs beyond the line.

\section{Where this model stands among discrete models}\label{app:related}

Deterministic lattice models of physics are old: the lattice gases of Hardy, de Pazzis and Pomeau \cite{hpp1976} and of Frisch, Hasslacher and Pomeau \cite{fhp1986} recover hydrodynamics from Boolean collisions; the quantum cellular automata of Bialynicki-Birula \cite{bialynicki1994}, Meyer \cite{meyer1996} and Arrighi \cite{arrighi2019} step a unitary state on a lattice. This model differs from both in its state and in its measurement: the state is integer levels with a remainder per Node and no complex amplitude, and the measurement is a division of the law and no projection. It differs from the pilot-wave account \cite{bohm1952, dgz1992} in that no particle position is kept beside the wave: the count follows the record's current by the count's line, and a body's position is its count's centroid. It differs from spontaneous-collapse models \cite{grw1980} in that the click's residue is the rule's own remainder and no stochastic term. In the ontological-models language \cite{spekkens2007, hs2010} the record is the ontic state and the click its one read; the correlation $2\sqrt 2$ is reached, and Popescu and Rohrlich's $4$ \cite{pr1994} is not, since the second label's shares are a rotation's squares.
