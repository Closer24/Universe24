# The worlds

Every world the repository ships, one row each, from the files alone: the world file, the universe it runs (the world's own `universe` key), the board (`shape` and `boundary`), what one sees in one sentence (the folder's `design.json` and `expectation.json`, the blind's one claim), the run's `intervals`, and the status. The status words are the decisions' (docs/HIGHLIGHTS.md): "approved experiment" (the two slits), "gate" (Bell, GHZ, Zeno and the pulsed Zeno), "cancelled" (the shelved ion's two worlds), "lattice reading, no blind" (a world with no NodeDetector, no click and no blind row, read on the lattice through its look page) and "world" for the rest. A world stays in the repository only if it is in the paper or on a test. Every number in a blind is the design's, written before the first run; the readings beside the blinds stand in each folder's `blind_and_reading.md`.

Beside each world file stands its mode file (`<world>.mode.json`, written by `tools/pixel_mode.py`, never by hand), and in each folder `design.json` (every number with its reason), `expectation.json` (the blind row, written before the first run), `build_world.py` and `blind_and_reading.md`. The two folders body_motion and two_bodies hold instead the scripts that write and run their worlds through `tools/look/record.py` (`run_moving.py` and `run_viz.py`; `run_fall2.py` and `run_fall_long.py` with the three checks `gradient_check.py`, `wall_check.py` and `tension_check.py`), their results (`results.json`, `fall_results_49x17.json`, the checks' JSON), the look page with the recorded run embedded, and a `design.md` stating the world, what one sees, the one fact and what a reader must not conclude; the recorded runs are the 2.0.0 engine's. The two slits' folder holds beside its files its look page too, `two_slits.html`, built from the shipped world by the README's commands (the look and the page, the fourth and the fifth) with the run's own output file beside the look (`tools/run_inputs.py`'s file, the page tool's `--output`), as the which-way pages are, over the 130 intervals and linked from the site's front page: the bars are the credit lines' clicks per region, the row [22, 21, 27, 16, 9, 36, 56, 11, 12, 26, 24, 25], 285 clicks, the folder's reading at the clean engine bit for bit, the window's shares beside them and the dashed curve the blind row of 273; unlike the two bodies' pages it draws its board with three.js and its fonts from the web, so it needs a connection; the board and the graphs on it are lattice readings, the bars the clicks, the one measurement. The which-way folder holds three look pages, `which_way.html`, `one_gap.html` and `two_gaps.html`, one per world (the page tool draws one run per page), built the same way with the run's own output file beside the look (`tools/run_inputs.py`'s file, the page tool's `--output`), so that the bars are the credit lines' clicks per region with the window's shares beside them: the two gaps' row [22, 21, 27, 16, 9, 36, 56, 11, 12, 26, 24, 25], 285 clicks, the one gap's [0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 19, 32, 15, 26, 17, 16, 17], 142, and the which-way world's [0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 18, 35, 12, 22, 23, 14, 13] with the channel's 149, the folder's reading at the clean engine bit for bit. The anticoincidence folder holds `anticoincidence.html` (`one_photon.json`: one light record laid at the chain's centre toward two atoms, the one credit line at the interval 48, body 0 brought to e by the photon and body 1 left in g) and `two_photons.html` (`two_photons.json`, the control: one photon per atom, both atoms brought to e at the interval 48). The packet emission folder holds `packet_emission.html` (`packet_emission.json`: the atom laid in e emits one packet of the family `pulse` at the interval 61, its one credit line, the packet's Nodes the `lay` lines drawn as wire cubes; the region `reach` at the far end reads no click, the folder's finding by name). The resonance folder holds `resonance.html` (`resonant.json`) and `detuned.html` (`detuned.json`), one run each at the design's seed 1, one of the 35 of 100 at which the tuned taker absorbs (the shipped world files unchanged but for the two records' seeds, set as `read_world.py` sets them for its seed 1, body 0 at 2 and body 1 at 3): the giver emits its pulse at the interval 27 in both worlds, the emission's credit line; the tuned taker takes it, its credit line at the interval 96, g to e by the pulse; the detuned world at the same seed, one of its 100 with no absorption, its taker left in g; the reading over all seeds in `blind_and_reading.md`. Every one of these pages is built as the two slits' page is, dark, with three.js from the web. The Bell folder holds `singlet_a_b.html` (the (a, b) world under the family's sign, the one draw through the root) and `singlet_back_a_b.html`, and the GHZ folder `ghz_back_x_x_x.html`: the click that runs back, the sides drawing in the time's order and the record's own light cone read back to its birth and forward to the next side between the draws, the rest of the board untouched, the cone shaded on the board frame by frame, the frames labelled by the board's tick and the interval the record is read at, three passes on the chain and five on the square.
