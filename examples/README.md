# The worlds

Every world the repository ships, one row each, from the files alone: the world file, the universe it runs (the world's own `universe` key), the board (`shape` and `boundary`), what one sees in one sentence (the folder's `design.json` and `expectation.json`, the blind's one claim), the run's `intervals`, and the status. The status words are the decisions' (docs/HIGHLIGHTS.md): "approved experiment" (the two slits), "gate" (Bell, GHZ, Zeno and the pulsed Zeno), "toy" (a world its design calls so), "cancelled" (the shelved ion's two worlds), "lattice reading, no blind" (a world with no NodeDetector, no click and no blind row, read on the lattice through its look page) and "world" for the rest. A world stays in the repository only if it is in the paper or on a test. Every number in a blind is the design's, written before the first run; the readings beside the blinds stand in each folder's `blind_and_reading.md`.

Beside each world file stands its mode file (`<world>.mode.json`, written by `tools/pixel_mode.py`, never by hand), and in each folder `design.json` (every number with its reason), `expectation.json` (the blind row, written before the first run), `build_world.py` and `blind_and_reading.md`. The two folders body_motion and two_bodies hold instead the scripts that write and run their worlds through `tools/look/record.py` (`run_moving.py` and `run_viz.py`; `run_fall2.py` and `run_fall_long.py` with the three checks `gradient_check.py`, `wall_check.py` and `tension_check.py`), their results (`results.json`, `fall_results_49x17.json`, the checks' JSON), the look page with the recorded run embedded, and a `design.md` stating the world, what one sees, the one fact and what a reader must not conclude; the recorded runs are the 2.0.0 engine's. The two slits' folder holds beside its files its look page too, `two_slits.html`, built once from the shipped world by the README's commands (the look and the page, the fourth and the fifth) on the engine at version 2.1.0 (130 intervals, the detectors' clicks per region beside the blind row) and linked from the site's front page; unlike the two bodies' pages it draws its board with three.js and its fonts from the web, so it needs a connection; the board and the graphs on it are lattice readings, the bars the detectors' clicks, the one measurement.

## The universe files

The files at `examples/events/*.json` and the universe files the folders carry. Every universe file declares the world's integers (`node_clock` Gamma, `quantum_action` T, `width`, `link_unit` G) and its families, each a pair [num, den] and, for a held row, its sources and its level weight; `engine_start.json` declares the start's mode. Unless a row says otherwise, Gamma = 6,000, T = 32,768, the width 63 and G = 16.
