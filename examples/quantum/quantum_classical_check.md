# Quantum-to-classical claim: bounded numerical investigation

The existing engine reproduces suppression and recovery of interference, and
classical probability evolution under supplied dephasing. These checks do **not**
establish an emerging Newtonian trajectory or a new solution to measurement.
The spatial contact profile produces localized records whose initial immobility
is explicitly configured. No simulator source or physical law was changed.

## Reproduce

Use the project Python environment with `PYTHONPATH=src`:

```sh
python examples/quantum/quantum_classical_check.py --output artifacts/quantum-classical-result
```

The harness uses existing configuration preflight, the primary headless runner,
and the existing quantum owner. It saves ten complete native runs plus one
aggregate `summary.json`. The aggregate includes the finite quantum controls,
three spatial contact controls, and the unsupported moving-capture validation
result. Inputs and outputs generated by the harness have 24-hour retention;
this authoring code, its source JSON dependencies and this report are durable.
Choose a fresh output directory. There is no simulator build or visualization.

## Fixed-clock interference sweep

The authoring function reuses [interference.json](interference.json) and the
unchanged channel from [partial_dephasing.json](partial_dephasing.json):

`D(rho) = (9 rho + 16 diag(rho)) / 25`.

The channel leaves populations unchanged and multiplies off-diagonal terms by
`9/25`. All runs start in level zero, apply H at tick 1, have four operation slots
at ticks 2 through 5, and recombine at tick 6. Exactly `n` slots use the channel;
the remaining slots use identity. Geometry, carrier laws, budget, preparation,
readout instrument, seed and contact tick 8 remain fixed. The two analyzer
settings differ by phase zero or pi. There are no prescribed random tickets.

Independent prediction: `V(n) = (9/25)^n` and the bright/dark level-zero
probabilities are `(1 + V)/2` and `(1 - V)/2`. The measured values are exact
pre-selection probabilities from native decision weights, not frequencies from
a sampled ensemble. For this real channel the two phases are the extrema.

| Applied channel encounters | Exact measured visibility | Visibility percent |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 1 | 100 |
| 1 | 9/25 | 36 |
| 2 | 81/625 | 12.96 |
| 3 | 729/15625 | 4.6656 |
| 4 | 6561/390625 | 1.679616 |

All ten native worlds matched the independent predictions. Their 120 completed
ticks preserved declared mass 2 and net momentum zero. Every model cost matched
the event ledger and both carriers met at tick 8. These carriers already have
configured momentum and movement before readout; this is quantum control of
classical collision outcomes, not a wavepacket acquiring classical mechanics.

## Coherent environment and probability controls

[environment_coherence_check.py](environment_coherence_check.py) uses explicitly
named, co-located internal registers and supplied unitary interactions. A fresh
environment register undergoes rotation `[[3,-4],[4,3]]/5` conditioned on the
system path. Independent environmental overlaps give `V(n)=(3/5)^n`:
`1, 3/5, 9/25, 27/125` for zero through three registers. All values match.

Reversing every retained interaction restores visibility one. Interacting twice
with the same environment by CNOT also restores interference, whereas one
CNOT gives equal readout probabilities. Explicitly dephasing that environment
between the two CNOTs prevents recovery. All sixteen controls pass without
sampling a measurement outcome. Merely counting interactions does not guarantee
irreversible decoherence; their information transfer and retention matter.

The existing `markov_experiment()` in [run_physics_checks.py](run_physics_checks.py)
also passes five exact cycles. After rotation and full dephasing, probabilities
follow the independently calculated transition matrix `[[9,16],[16,9]]/25`.
No outcome is selected. This is classical probability dynamics for a supplied
channel, not Newton's equation or proof of a unique macroscopic outcome.

## Spatial contact support and missing trajectory

[trajectory_support_check.py](trajectory_support_check.py) runs the existing
[recurrent input](repeated_contacts.json) for 96 ticks with each of three
prescribed ticket tapes. Physical laws and geometry remain identical; the tapes
exercise supported outcomes and are not typical-frequency evidence.

| Tape | Result | Later localized observations | Final ordinary field |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `[0]` | Localized at event tick 3 | 93 at `(2,1,1)` | 0 |
| Existing eight tickets | Localized at event tick 24 after repeated contacts | 72 at `(2,1,1)` | 0 |
| `[25] * 40` | 31 continued contacts, inventory remains quantum | 0 | 0 |

All 288 ticks preserve mass 1 and charge -1 and balance ordinary field accounting.
Final field injections equal dissipation: -18, -36 and -42 respectively. Localized
records retain `momentum_known=0` at every sampled tick. The existing validator
rejects configuring their immediate transport as moving:

> localized capture must retain undefined momentum and hold until a local rule acts

This initial hold is a profile constraint. The experiment does not establish
that all possible later local interactions are unable to induce movement; it
shows that a stable held record alone is insufficient evidence of an emerging
classical trajectory. This support control and the internal-register sweep are
different experiments, not a continuous wavepacket-to-trajectory derivation.

## Interpretation and remaining acceptance

Environment-induced decoherence and the emergence of effective classical
behavior are established research subjects; see
[Zurek, Rev. Mod. Phys. 75, 715 (2003)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0105127).
The distinction between decoherence and the definite-outcome problem is reviewed
by [Schlosshauer, Rev. Mod. Phys. 76, 1267 (2004)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0312059).
Our numerical results reproduce finite examples of these known mechanisms.

A stronger claim requires the same spatial dynamics to produce a moving,
localized state with independently verified position/momentum evolution as
environmental coupling changes. It also requires environment recoil and full
matter/field energy accounting. The current controls have no calibrated photon
cross section, dephasing rate in physical time or SI trajectory error, so there
is no quantitative comparison with a laboratory experiment here. Exact quantum
evaluation retains the explicit Q-ORACLE-1 exception; these checks do not prove
a fully local microscopic implementation or general no-signalling.
