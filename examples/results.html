Bell's gate

Two parts of one record are laid equal and sent to two sides, where each side reads them at its own setting, a pair of whole numbers. Nature's pairs show the correlation E = cos 2a cos 2b + rho sin 2a sin 2b with rho = 1, and at the settings a = (1, 0), a' = (1, 1), b = (12, 5), b' = (5, 12) the CHSH sum S reaches 478/169 = 2.8284, below Tsirelson's bound 2 sqrt 2 by 2.5 x 10^-5 and above the bound 2 of every local product form. Under the declared counting rule the correlation follows by Lagrange's identity at equal parts, and the board's four worlds read E = 119/169, -119/169, 120/169, 120/169 and S = 478/169 = 2.8284, the blind's fractions exactly, while the three local credits read beside them stay at or under 2. Bell is a gate of the engine and not an experiment: no reading of nature is claimed by it.

The GHZ gate

One record of four parts is read at three sides, each side's pattern saying how its parts read its setting, x = (1, 0) or y = (1, 1). Nature's three-particle state shows E_3 = cos 2(a + b + c), that is -1 at the settings x y y, y x y and y y x and +1 at x x x, Mermin's M = -4, where every local product form holds |M| at most 2, and the three local credits land at M = -1 at these settings. Under the declared counting rule the three-sided credit follows, and the board's four worlds read E_3 = -1, -1, -1, 1 and M = -4, the blind's exactly, the three local credits at M = -1, -1 and -1. GHZ is a gate of the engine and not an experiment.

The quantum Zeno gate

One record of two parts, g and e, stands at one Node under a drive whose turn over 48 intervals is a pi pulse, so left alone it ends in e; the record reads its own parts n times over the run, and each reading closes a window with a write. Nature (Itano, Heinzen, Bollinger and Wineland, 1990) shows the transition held back by the readings: P(e at T_pi) = (1 - cos^n(pi / n)) / 2, 1 at n = 1 and 0.1334 at n = 16. The board's 480 trials per world read 1.0000, 0.5062, 0.3917, 0.2292, 0.1417 at n = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, every row within one standard error of the formula (the folder's criterion), the largest deviation +0.75 standard errors at n = 4; without the window's write the law would give 1 at every n.

The pulsed Zeno gate

The same body at the Node clock 96,000, the pi pulse lengthened to 768 intervals, probed by one quantum laid at its Node n times over the pi time, Itano's protocol: the body's window is bounded by the probe's lays, no window declared, and at each probe the body's one draw by its labels' squares. Nature shows the same formula down to 0.0371 at n = 64. The board's 480 trials per world read 1.0000, 0.5021, 0.3625, 0.2104, 0.1292, 0.0646, 0.0292 at n = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, every row within two standard errors of the formula (the folder's criterion), the largest deviation -1.27 standard errors at n = 8; the windows closed n times exactly in every trial.
