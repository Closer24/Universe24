E11 under the law of the bit

The field of one thing at rest, read shell by shell and by test things; work in progress, 2026-09-18, main at ffa4a56, source 6fef7cc0

What this page is

The first confrontation run of E11 under the law of the bit (Highlights 5.4, 2026-09-18), on the engine of main at ffa4a56 (features 15 to 18 and 16d part 1), work in progress: eight of eleven open-board worlds recorded, the (111) probes, the closed-board world and the shell replays still to come. The register's entry is "E11 repeated under the law of the bit (2026-09-18)" in docs/EXPERIMENTS.md; the worlds, analyzer and records are under examples/nature/e11_law/.

The worlds

  • One thing at rest: an external body of the proton family, content 2^28, whole charge 3 x 2^28, at the centre of a 33^3 open board; its shadow set given with the board by initial_field fill 12 with release [1, 512]: 6 x 2^19 x 12 = 37748736 quanta, the largest fill the prefill admits.
  • N = 64, K 1, wait_per_quantum 1, the dense mode, 40 ticks.
  • Test things: one per world, a real ray of the electron family of content 1 standing at (r, 0, 0), (m, m, 0) or (m, m, m), reading the body's shadows by content, so its momentum line is the pushed amount in quanta; it heads inward, and it waits a tick per whole quantum read, so it never moves again.
  • pulse: one release of 2^19 on each heading on a 41^3 board, for the front and the fraction off the planes (the replay that reads it is pending).

What came out so far

  • The books per bit balanced at every tick of every world.
  • On the open board the field is not a standing set: half of the shadow set has left the 33^3 board by tick 40. The closed board (the model owner's decision during the runs) is where the standing set is to be read next.
  • The cumulative push at tick 40 falls as r^-2.6 on the axis and r^-2.4 on (110); the axis reads about a third of the (110) fit at the same r.
  • The push per interval changes sign from interval to interval: the probe's own returned shares come back to it through the mixing.
  • The recoil: within 40 ticks nothing of the push came home at r = 8 and 12, 1.4 % at r = 4 and 3.5 % on (110) at r = 4.24; the rest stays in flight on the shadows.
