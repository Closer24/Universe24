# Series I: the nucleus

Eight worlds of one base, written by `make_worlds.py`; the register entry is
[I, the nucleus (2026-09-20)](../../../docs/EXPERIMENTS.md#i-the-nucleus-2026-09-20)
and the evidence is in [validation](../../../docs/VALIDATION.md). The
question, in the model owner's words (Highlights 5.4, 2026-09-20): one
mechanism for all the laws on the GameBoard, the strong force a column with
a sign and a range that is a lifetime, "let there be a nucleus". What was
added on the law's side, all of it generic ([BEAM_LAW note 31](../../../docs/BEAM_LAW.md#10-implementation-notes-2026-09-19-the-implementation)):
the columns of the one coupling (`columns` per family; the push the signed
inner product over gravity, charge and the declared columns), the lifetime
of a family (`lifetime`; a ray whose age reaches it clicks on the border
`lifetime`, booked as a face books an escape), the content a measured event
holds of several families (`held`), and the contact through the table (a
body's refused step onto another body is read by the occupant's table,
`measure` by the keys: the momentum component is handed over). A research
run, made once, never a test; the expectations below were written before
the runs (the physicist's design of the strong force, DESIGN sections 2 to
4 and 6, with its integers); a reading outside its expectation is reported
with its numbers, never moved. Every number is one of two kinds ([the
register](../../../docs/EXPERIMENTS.md), "Two kinds of readings"): a
DETECTOR reading (the bodies' own `read` and `contact` records, the border's
clicks: the only kind reality has) or a GAMEBOARD reading (the bodies'
steps and separations, the design: the host's view of the mechanism). No
detector is declared on these GameBoards: a nucleon is an external thing,
and what it reads is the reading.

## The base

An open cube of 21^3 Nodes, `"law": "beam"`, K 2^20, N 64, `suspension` 0
(the push alone moves a body), `release` [1, 1] (one row of the held
content per direction per interval), `width` 2^28 (W = 64 x 2^28 x M = 3.2
x 10^13 label units against a push of 3.1 x 10^11 per interval: a body
steps at about p / W Links per interval, the slow regime), 3000 intervals.
Three free families without a phase circle: `p` (charge per unit of
content 4), `n` (no charge) and `nuclear` (the column `strong` with the
value G = 10000 per unit of content and the sign minus; `lifetime` 3: its
rays reach the six neighbours at the age 1, the twelve face diagonals at 2,
the eight cube diagonals and the second Link of a heading at 3, and click on
the border `lifetime` at the age 3, so a Node three Links away never reads
them). A proton is a free body of `p`, content 1836, holding one unit of
`nuclear` (`held`): the charges M 1837 (gravity), Q 7344 (charge), G
10000 (strong); a neutron 1839 of `n` holding one unit: 1840, 0, 10000.
Every body releases on the fan of the 290 primitive directions with
|a| + |b| + |c| <= 6 (the six headings among them). The push per interval
between two bodies at mirror Nodes within the reach is `(Q_A Q_B - G_A G_B
- M_A M_B) x U(r)` per unit per direction, U(1) = 3008 on the axis (the
x components of the 57 unit vectors whose first step is along the axis),
U(sqrt 2) = (910, 1041), U(2) = 1048, U(3) = 316: two protons at one Link
bind because `G^2 + M^2 > Q^2` (103 374 569 > 53 934 336; the least G that
binds is 7111), a proton and a neutron by `G^2 + M_p M_n`, and beyond the
reach only `Q^2 - M_A x 1836` remains (Coulomb's repulsion less gravity).
Under the contact through the table every refused step of a body onto its
neighbour hands the momentum component to the neighbour (`measure`, the
keys' rule for a paid arrival: a body's momentum is its own label), so a
bound pair's labels are bounded by one inter-attempt accumulation and the
books of the momentum close; until 2026-09-20 they grew without bound.

## The worlds

`<name>.json` for the eight names above (the model ids
`beam-nucleus-<name>-space-v1`). Run them in parallel and read the records:

```bash
python examples/events/nucleus/make_worlds.py
PYTHONPATH=src python tools/run_series.py --jobs 3 --out artifacts/nucleus examples/events/nucleus/*.json
PYTHONPATH=src python tools/nucleus_readings.py artifacts/nucleus
```

Each run takes 4 to 90 s (the bound worlds keep their 290 x 2 rows per
body per interval on the GameBoard for the whole run; the separating
worlds empty as the bodies leave); the records are a few hundred megabytes
per bound world.
