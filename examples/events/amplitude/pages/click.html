The Click of One Record

One record of the registered two-slit world, from the lamp to the screen: the rows spreading with their phases, the offers and the ladder growing at the detectors, the click at one pixel and the deletion of the record.

1. The GameBoard and what is on it

The registered world examples/events/amplitude/slits_low.json of series L2 (the two slits at a low rate), a plane of 60 x 121 x 1 with z periodic and x, y open, K = 2^30, N = 64, 230 intervals. On it, by the world file:

  • the lamp (yellow), a measured event of the family light at (2, 60), rate [1, 1]: one record per interval, each of 5 rows of amount 1 on its five directions (multiplicity 5);
  • the wall (grey), wall Nodes at x = 8, freed within 6 Links of each opening; the two openings (green) at (8, 55) and (8, 65), wall Nodes with a rerelease table that re-emits an arriving row into the fan of 91 directions by integer weights;
  • the three absorbers (white) at (7, 58), (7, 60), (7, 62), wall Nodes that end the lamp's three rows that miss the openings;
  • the screen (dark cyan), 121 pixels at x = 52, each a detector of one Node reading sum (screen_0 .. screen_120);
  • the open faces in y (purple), detectors too: a row leaving the board clicks there.

The lamp births one record per interval; this page follows one of them and draws only its rows. The other records are their own: the merge adds rows of one record only, and no rule of the GameBoard reads another record, so the picture of this record is the same with or without them.

2. Why it was shown

The model owner, 2026-09-21 (translated): "show me an HTML with a GIF of how a click looks: I want to see the amplitudes spreading, and a click on the other side, and how the amplitudes in it spread". This is a demonstration of the registered world, not a new reading: every number below is the run's, as the register L2 has it.

3. The moving picture: one record from its birth to its click

spreading

Born at tick 43 with the birth phase u = 41, the record's 5 rows fly toward the wall; three end at the absorbers (ticks 51, 52) and two pass the openings, where at tick 56 the split re-creates them as 182 units over the fan, which spreads behind the wall with its phases and ends, row by row, at the screen's pixels (from tick 131) and at the faces, each end adding its pointer to the record's offer at that set.

the click

At tick 192 the last row ends and the record is complete: the layer builds the ladder over its 80 cells (the Gram weight of each set, coherent within a Node and incoherent across Nodes, normalised by the total), and u = 41 falls in the cell screen_60 (its rungs 41 to 42 of 64): the click is at the pixel (52, 60), with the content 4.

the deletion

In the same interval the record leaves the layer's table: its 126 offers (the pointers of 185 units that ended at 80 sets) are erased, and nothing of the record remains anywhere; the world kept one bit, the cell that clicked.

5. What the picture shows

The amplitudes that spread are the rows of one record with their phases: the GameBoard carries them locally, one Link per interval at most, and never reads the layer. The click happens once, at the completion, when the whole record has ended: the ladder of the Gram weights and the one birth phase u decide the cell, the content goes to that Node, and the record's vector f (its offers) is erased everywhere in the same interval. This is the reading of DERIVATIONS_BEAM section 14: the click reads a few bits of u and erases the rest; the rows on the GameBoard were never deleted at the click because none remained.
