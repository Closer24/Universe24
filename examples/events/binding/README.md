# Series N: the binding that costs content

Three worlds of one base, written by `make_worlds.py`; the register entry is
[N, the binding that costs content (2026-09-20)](../../../docs/EXPERIMENTS.md#n-the-binding-that-costs-content-2026-09-20)
and the evidence is in [validation](../../../docs/VALIDATION.md). The
question, in the model owner's words (record 115 of
[the day's log](../../../docs/LOG_2026-09-20.md), the decision of record 137):
a binding that costs content, `binding-v1`, with its own identity, so that
the released binding energy is content in flight that a detector at the
border reads as clicks. What was added on the law's side, all of it
generic ([BEAM_LAW note 40](../../../docs/BEAM_LAW.md#10-implementation-notes-2026-09-19-the-implementation);
the physicist's design [DESIGN.md](../../../docs/designs/binding_v1/DESIGN.md)
sections 1 to 6): one condition on one verb, GIVE at the contact. At a
contact under `measure` (the momentum hand-over of note 31 (ix)) the refused
body gives its held content of every paid family it carries (`held` of a
paid family other than its own) to the flight: per such family with the
quantum h, `held // h` units of content h as one row on the heading opposite
to the refused step, away from the occupant; the recoil on the giver;
`held mod h` kept. The rows then have the law's existing fates: the border
`lifetime` clicks them two Links from their birth with their content (the
released binding energy, measurable as clicks); a body on their line takes
them under the keys' `measure`. No new key: the paid family's `quantum` and
`lifetime` and a body's `held` are the existing keys; `run.json` carries
`binding-v1` under `hypotheses` when a body holds a paid family; the
`contact` record gains `given`. A research run, made once, never a test;
the expectations below were written before the run (DESIGN section 4 and
the integers of its section 2); a reading outside its expectation is
reported with its numbers, never moved. Every number is one of two kinds
([the register](../../../docs/EXPERIMENTS.md), "Two kinds of readings"): a
DETECTOR reading (the bodies' own `read` and `contact` records, the border's
clicks, a set's gathers: the only kind reality has) or a GAMEBOARD reading
(the bodies' steps, their held content, the host's view of the mechanism).

## The base

Series I's ([the nucleus](../nucleus/README.md), `nucleus/make_worlds.py`,
loaded by this generator from its file): an open cube of 21^3 Nodes, K 2^20,
N 64, `suspension` 0, `release` [1, 1], `width` 2^28, the fan of 290
primitive directions, 3000 intervals; the free families `p` (charge 4),
`n` and `nuclear` (the column `strong` 10000 with the sign minus, `lifetime`
3). Added: the paid family `bond` (`quantum` 1, `lifetime` 3, no column, no
phase circle, as `nuclear`). A nucleon carries `bond` 2 with its free total
kept: the proton 1834 of `p` + 1 `nuclear` + 2 `bond` = 1837, the neutron
1837 of `n` + 1 `nuclear` + 2 `bond` = 1840, the sum 3677 as in series I. A
held paid family is never released (only a lamp releases paid content, note
31 (viii)); it leaves a body only by the give. The size of the give is one
declared width, the held `bond` per nucleon (2 at h = 1): an input, as
record 106 makes every content an input; the law derives no binding energy.

Nature (CODATA 2018, AME; the design's section 2): E_B(d) = 2.2246 MeV =
4.3534 m_e on m_p + m_n = 3674.836 m_e, 0.1185 %; E_B(alpha) = 28.296 MeV =
55.373 m_e on 7349.673, 0.7534 %; the ratio of the energies 12.72.
