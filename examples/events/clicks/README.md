# The clicks' world (2026-10-21)

The world `clicks_chain.json` with its mode file, the universe `nucleons_light.json`, the click log `clicks_chain.clicks.json` (the engine's credit lines and conversion line of the run, verbatim, one line per entry), the blind `blind.md` and the page `clicks_chain.html`: one run of 132 intervals on a closed chain of 101 Nodes in which three kinds of clicks of the one law occur, each a declaration of the world file and each written by the one act at one Node. (1) A region's click: the region `screen` credits a photon packet at the close 24 by the count's rounding of its window's inflow, the hole at its one front Node 88 and no front at the count 1. (2) An instrument body's click: the one-Node body of the `absorber` family with its `parts` g and e absorbs the first photon packet by its transition at the declared scalar weight [23, 2] (the quarter turn's weight for the one-quantum lay: the quarter turn 9424.8 units of 1 / Gamma over the window's sum of the envelope at the group pace, 110 / 2 x 8 / 0.5469 = 804.5, gives 11.71, and the pair stands at 11.5) at its derived window's close 32, the one-Node exchange, the photon's line to 0 at 62, the erasing front from 62 and the count down by one; no `dipole`, the chain having one axis and no direction to select. (4) A conversion: the neutron packet credited by the region `target` at the close 48 converts at its front Node 20 into a proton and an electron at the senses 1 and -1 by the table derived from the pairs, the Wronskians 5450 + 5458 + 5476 = 16384 against -16384, the sum 0. The blind, written before the run, stands whole: every DERIVED line held (the three closes, the three Nodes, the lays' split, the books) and the one OPEN line, the absorption's draw against 6.4 parts in a million, was read. The taker body's click (kind 3, the world key `takers`) is absent by name: it leaves the engine for further research as a fourth mechanism beside the one law's two rules of the when, so this world declares no `takers` key, lays no pixel giver and holds no frame. The world is neutral: no sign holder stands in its universe, every family reading the two holders of the content at 1. The test `tests/test_the_clicks_world.py` runs the world to 50 intervals in under 4 seconds and asserts the three kinds' lines, the holes, the fronts, the lays and the books.

The commands, from the repository root with `PYTHONPATH=src`: `python tools/pixel_mode.py --input examples/events/clicks/clicks_chain.json` (the mode file); `python tools/run_inputs.py --out <folder> examples/events/clicks/clicks_chain.json` (the log); `python tools/look/record.py examples/events/clicks/clicks_chain.json --out <folder>/clicks_chain.look.json --output <folder>/clicks_chain.output.json` (the look, 133 frames, and the same log) and `python tools/look/page.py <folder>/clicks_chain.look.json --output <folder>/clicks_chain.output.json --out examples/events/clicks/clicks_chain.html` (the page in the dark tokens of the slit and atom pages, the clicks in its records table and its run). The look and the output files are not shipped, as in the other folders. The runner's output file keeps the `conversion` line beside the credits' (its line kinds since 2026-10-21: `click`, `density`, `parts`, `credit`, `conversion`, `erasure`, `lay`, `face`), so the page's embedded output holds the conversion line with the three credit lines and the null window's line; the page's records table lists the credit lines as rows and the conversion stands in the output block and in the eight lay lines at 48; the shipped click log holds the engine's five lines as written in-process, and the test reads them so.
