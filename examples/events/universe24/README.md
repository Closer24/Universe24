## What the clicks change

The reading is derived before it is run: a region NodeDetector without a `draw` reports its click, the net current through its front Ports, and writes nothing on the lattice (the click's write is the draw's act, **The click's read of the board**), so the course with the detector is the course without it bit for bit, and the clicks are a reading of the course and not an act on it; the test's clause (7) holds the same on the small world at every one of 70 intervals, the fires alike.
