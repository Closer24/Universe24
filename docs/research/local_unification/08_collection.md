# 08 — One reproducible collection, explicit proof boundaries

The command below runs the construction, supporting controls and independent
readers, then writes a compact collection manifest.

~~~sh
python -m validation.local_unification.run_all --out runs/local-unification
~~~

The output must be a new or empty directory. An unsuccessful audit exits with an
error and preserves its partial evidence plus a failure report. A successful
collection saves the actual tensors, complete seeds, every retained transition,
canonical click streams, supporting trial archive and source hashes. The command
with `--quick` uses the labelled nine-case organization subset; it cannot be
reported as the full panel.

## What is joined

The main evidence identity is the twelve-channel geometry run, using the default
19-cube, six microticks and positive finite causal chart. Its first eight
channels implement the record/carrier collision; four retained channels carry
the source profiles. The following readers consume this same saved history:

- A literal scalar monomial forward/inverse oracle reconstructs the complete
  arithmetic and click stream independently of the vectorized engine.
- A record reader verifies the whole saved runtime before inspecting the exact
  native eight-channel prefix.
- Local balance ledgers check every packet energy, momentum and species recoil
  residual, with both pipeline banks counted.
- Functional readouts retain every receiver opportunity and distinguish the two
  collision cohorts.
- An independent expanded recurrence checks all twelve retained channels,
  including the nonphysical seams. Its separate spline and metric derivations
  anchor the finite chart to the original profiles and establish a positive
  lower bound throughout its declared continuous domain.

The join binds tensor, bundle, complete seed, horizon, trace, clicks and current
source files. A matching digest is identity evidence, not a grant of physical
acceptance. Main-run evidence from another law, seed or horizon is rejected.

The pure native-eight audits, organization panel and limiting counterexamples
remain supporting reports with their own identities. Their results are not
relabeled as twelve-channel execution evidence. Comparison counts overlap and
are not summed.

## Complete controls

The default organization panel has 324 runs: two sizes, three random seeds, six
directions, three memory preparations and three law variants. Its compressed
archive contains every complete 25-snapshot history. The collector checks the
full Cartesian product, unique trial identities, expected variant tensor, seed
and trace hashes, current implementation hashes, exact state allocation, legal
carries and complete horizons. Missing negative controls fail collection.

The separate 48-case prepared repeat-use comparison retains shared interaction,
record transport disabled, collision disabled and same-law late supply removed.
The last control changes preparation while preserving the interaction law.
Frozen, random and periodic metric controls remain explicit non-Engine
surrogates. Neither a high old motif score nor a successful prepared correction
is renamed sustained complexity growth.

## Claims and limits

| Classification | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Algebraic proof | The native Boolean collision is reversible and preserves exact packet balances. |
| Restricted geometric proof | The spline/source identity gives the stated null-stream metric on its positive finite causal chart. |
| Finite execution evidence | Complete saved arithmetic, retained records, local balances and prepared repeat use are checked on the same actual run. |
| Failed extension | A nonzero everywhere nonnegative periodic source cannot equal minus a periodic second difference. |
| Unproved or unimplemented | General Einstein behavior, massive bound bodies, operational proper clocks, sustained complexity growth and a Born/Bell detector model. |

The same register system connects local selection, a retained record, recoil and
a restricted source geometry. The evidence does not establish a universal rule
that the most complex path wins. Reversible closed dynamics alone cannot imply
monotone growth of every proposed complexity function; the limiting proofs and
counterexamples are retained in `limits.json`.

## Verification provenance

The collection tests cover missing/duplicate trials, changed horizons, mixed
laws, stale sources, an altered archive even after its checksum is refreshed,
omitted same-law supply controls and an actual two-tick twelve-channel join.
The shorter join deliberately fails the repeated-use gate instead of turning
one correction per cohort into a repeat-use success.

The collector, its tests and independent proof additions were reconstructed as
immutable repository objects after the local execution service became
unavailable. Earlier runs do not validate these reconstructed bytes. The
repository's local-unification CI workflow is responsible for fresh execution;
its artifacts and commit identify the version actually checked.

Coordination and scientific scope are tracked in
[issue #63](https://github.com/Closer24/Universe24/issues/63). Generic runtime
acceptance gates and production physics behavior remain unchanged.
