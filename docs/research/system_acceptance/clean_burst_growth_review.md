## What prevents a stronger claim

The selector's exact complete-state cycles repeat its state-defined structural
score. Nine independent rings give a full Boolean cube period dividing
1,670,760. This recurrence excludes indefinite strict growth on that prepared
finite domain, not on every possible unbounded-integer Rule3 construction.

The local source construction has a different finite projection: its eight
Boolean packet channels, fixed carries and inverse-prepared history form an
autonomous permutation with at most `2^(8N)` states on a board of `N` nodes.
Its four additional geometric profile channels are unbounded. Therefore a
finite-state theorem for the entire twelve-channel state would be incorrect.
The packet projection receives no geometric feedback, so enlarging or drifting
the profile registers cannot cure packet-organization recurrence by itself.

The collision preserves total plus/minus occupancy. At two-tick macro times,
those source densities simply translate in opposite directions. On finite
unwrapped support, the centered energy-spread polynomial has quadratic
coefficient `E^2-P^2 = 4 E_plus E_minus`. A positive two-stream source with
`E>|P|` therefore spreads without bound in this sector. Periodic refocusing
does not establish a localized massive body, a bounce or a new cosmological
epoch.

The same complete packet histories can coexist with shifted geometric profiles
and different proper-time rates. Packet-only records consequently do not yet
supply a universal material clock. This is an explicit counterexample within
the selected model, not a claim about the equivalence principle.

## Structural-score ceiling

The inherited score counts distinct persistent, nonuniform, three-site axial
motifs of two Boolean carrier values. Each site has four carrier labels, hence
there are `4^3 - 4 = 60` possible nonuniform motifs. A counted motif must occur
at least twice, giving

`score <= min(60, floor(N/2))`.

This bound follows directly from `axial_motifs` and `structure_trace`. Growing
the board alone cannot make this particular score increase indefinitely.
Frozen random structure also reaches score 38 in an existing negative control.
Thus a high score is not sufficient evidence of useful organization. The
current failed tests should remain unchanged; a future scalable metric must
be declared as a new protocol rather than retroactively making these trials
pass.

## Concrete next experiment

1. Specify one counted local interaction that changes total directional source
   flow and responds to the same geometric degrees of freedom. Reprove its
   complete-history invariant domain, source identities and strict locality;
   simply concatenating the successful blocks does not supply this interaction.
2. Supply a retained material-clock/record mechanism and an autonomous record
   resource account. Keep depletion, transport-off, disconnected coupling,
   matched complete-history and changed-source controls.
3. Declare a finite-horizon growth target or a justified sector with expanding
   accessible state. Measure bit cost. A finite autonomous Boolean permutation
   cannot support indefinite strict state-score growth.
4. Predeclare a scalable multiscale structural measure and a separate functional
   persistence/reuse measure. Retain frozen, translated, periodic, random and
   amplitude-only controls; retain every preparation and insufficient-history
   outcome.
5. Define a physical burst by source/geometry observables and verify several
   episodes on the same saved tensor, complete seed and calibrated protocol.
   Arithmetic peaks and periodic routing remain activity diagnostics until
   that physical relation is established.
