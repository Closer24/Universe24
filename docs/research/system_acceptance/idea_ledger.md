# Integrated ideas and physical acceptance

There is one execution architecture: complete prepared inputs, one immutable
local tensor, fixed Rule3, the existing Engine, and passive observations.
Research packages share that architecture; they do not thereby share one
physical tensor. A six-channel example and a 45-channel example cannot pool
their successes, channels, calibration, or physical interpretation.

Here **mechanical** means exact execution, retained-state integrity and replay;
**limited physical** means only the stated mathematical domain; **rejected as
unified** means the proposed combined interpretation fails its stated gate;
**blocked** identifies the missing construction or evidence. These statuses
can coexist. A passing negative-control test retains a failed physics gate.
