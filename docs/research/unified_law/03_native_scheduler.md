## Physical pair and polynomial force

A counted sign channel with u=0 and prepared s_a=s_b=1 rotates by
(s_a,s_b)→(s_b,−s_a), preserving every legal remainder. For a fixed positive
integer preparation amplitude A, decode

\[
y_n=s_a b/A,\qquad y_{n-1}=-s_b a/A.
\]

Every data channel also has u=0 except at one copy pulse. An odd number of free
rotations exchanges these physical slices.

Write F=F2+F1+F0 by polynomial degree and let L clear its static rational
coefficients. At the force-evaluation phase,

\[
W(b,s_a)=L\{F_2(b)+A s_a F_1(b)+A^2 F_0\}=L A^2 F(y_n).
\]

W has retained degree at most two. Multiplication by a counted pulse stays
cubic. Neither A nor L is a runtime divisor.

## Compute, copy and uncompute

The certified scheduler has period nine and quotient depth k=1 or k=2.
Each field has k workspace channels, prepared with zero a/b and a declared
legal remainder. Work otherwise follows the free u=0 rotation.

At phase j−1, workspace j computes a quotient of its predecessor; workspace1
reads W. The independent arithmetic description is

\[
q_1=\lfloor(2W+r_1)/6\rfloor,\qquad
q_j=\lfloor(2q_{j-1}+r_j)/6\rfloor.
\]

These are exactly the Engine divmods, not runtime calls to the macro oracle.

At copy phase4, the last work gear holds q_k in a known signed a or b register.
The cubic input copy_pulse*s_b*signed_work transfers that value to the data.
Coefficient3 copies it exactly. Coefficient1 performs one additional quotient,
with the data remainder retained and updated.

Workspace j uncomputes at phase 4k−3(j−1). The input is the negative of the same
force/predecessor that it previously used. At that phase its (a,b)=(-q_j,0).
If r_new=2w+r_old−6q_j, then

\[
\lfloor(-2w+r_{new})/6\rfloor=-q_j.
\]

The next workspace triple is therefore exactly (0,0,r_old), for signed inputs
and all remainder classes. For k=2 the schedule is compute0/1, copy4,
uncompute5/8. For k=1 it is compute0 and copy/uncompute4.

Copying to data at a multiple-of-four phase leaves its b register equal to the
original b at subsequent multiples of four before the final odd macro boundary.
Thus the first workspace uncomputes from the original force, without a hidden
copy bank or past history.

The exact macro relation is

\[
y_{n+1}=y_{n-1}+s_b\ell/A,\quad
\ell=\lfloor(2c s_b q_k+r_{\rm data})/6\rfloor,
\]

with c=3 or c=1. Every work and pulse triple restores; the counted sign makes its
prescribed rotation. The independent oracle retains and updates the data carry.

## Precision and refinement boundary

For exact copy put d=k; for the extra quotient put d=k+1. The corresponding
physical centered timestep is

\[
h=L A/(2\,3^d).
\]

Each quotient has error at most2/3 because remainder parity is invariant and
legal remainders of the same parity differ by at most four. Summing the geometric
division chain gives absolute raw macro error less than one, hence physical
forcing error less than1/A per field. No statistical cancellation is assumed.

On a bounded smooth ODE domain with a fixed Lipschitz constant, the two-slice
error recurrence gives a finite-time envelope of the form

\[
\max\|e_n\|\le C_T(e_{\rm prepared}+h^2+T/(hA)).
\]

This requires staying in that domain; it is not global nonlinear Einstein
stability. At fixed certified depth d≤3, h→0 and A→infinity cannot both occur:
the exact coefficient identity forces h to grow with A. Deeper division or a
reusable-workspace scheduler remains necessary for controlled refinement.

A period27 base clock was explored, but the straightforward quadratic pulse

inserted into the integer tensor. This is not an impossibility theorem for
another period27 construction. No unverified depth is exposed by the builder.
The cubic spinor force also requires a further counted factorization; this
input interface deliberately rejects degree three.
