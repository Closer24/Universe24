# Constraint certificates belong to the complete retained state

Reproduce with `python -m validation.generic_structure.constraint_certificate`.
Evidence: `runs/generic-structure/constraints.json`. This adds source-only
validation, not a supported physical model or a change to Rule3.

The reusable result is a **conditional complete-state certification criterion**.
It is instantiated by two exact integer examples, including a cubic nonlinear
map preserving an indefinite quadratic form. The existing five-channel reduced
Einstein attempt is reproduced and still fails at tick two. The positive
examples do not repair that physical failure.

## 1. What must be invariant

Let `s=(a,b,r)` be the entire retained lattice state, `T` the actual fixed-six
Engine map, and `D` a declared domain including legal carries, integer support,
nonzero readout denominators, signature conditions and any prepared-history
relations. Let `H(s)=0` list **all** constraints defining the intended sector,
including history and workspace constraints, not just the physical constraint
evaluated on `b`.

A sufficient forward certificate is:

1. `T(D) ⊆ D`.
2. The exact map identity `H(T(s)) = A(s) H(s)` holds for every `s∈D`.
3. The initial complete state belongs to `D` and obeys `H=0`.

Induction proves `H(T^n(s))=0`. For polynomial `T,H`, item 2 can be certified by
membership of every component of `H∘T` in the ideal generated by `H`. An exact
zero Groebner remainder is one executable certificate. Ideal membership is a
**sufficient** algebraic criterion; ordinary ideal membership is not claimed
necessary for preservation of a real or integer zero set. Those converse
questions involve the appropriate vanishing ideal and domain.

For a rational readout or map, clear denominators only on a domain where those
denominators are proved nonzero. Algebraically one works in the corresponding
localized ring. A cross-multiplied identity at a zero denominator does not define
a physical state. Rule3's quotient also cannot be treated as one unrestricted
polynomial: certify all reachable carry/congruence sectors, or supply an exact
integer identity valid on the whole declared domain.

To certify preservation in **both** directions, also prove `T(D)=D` and either
an inverse constraint certificate or an invertible square cofactor `A(s)` on
`D`. Then `H(T(s))=0` iff `H(s)=0`. Exact inversion of the Engine on its larger
mechanical state space does not by itself establish this physical-domain result.

For a composed map, each *actual* factor must preserve the declared sector and
its domain. Composition of tangent continuum fields or individually consistent
approximations does not provide that certificate.

## 2. The commuting macrostep obligation

Suppose a proposed target is `z_next=Phi(z)`. An encoding `E(z,eta)` must include
all retained history, carries, clocks and workspace; `eta` denotes any explicitly
counted auxiliary degrees of freedom. At physical macrostep boundaries require

```
T^m(E(z,eta)) = E(Phi(z), H_aux(z,eta)),
pi(E(z,eta)) = z.
```

Both equalities must hold for the declared domain, not only for the first seed.
The auxiliary update must keep the encoded domain closed and the complete
evolution reversible. If physical constraints are claimed at intermediate
microticks too, certify their phase-dependent encoding as well. A macrostep
certificate alone says nothing about those intermediate readouts.

The first equality proves `pi∘T^m∘E=Phi`; a separate exact identity such as
`C(Phi(z))=B(z)C(z)` proves physical constraint propagation. Matching a Taylor
coefficient, numerator or initial step does not establish either equality.

## 3. Why a continuum identity is insufficient

The actual homogeneous Einstein/matter reduction from
[`07_constraint_closure.md`](../../completion/07_constraint_closure.md) uses
`z=(k,p,phi,d)` and `J=diag(6,-1,-mu,-2)`, with `C=z^T J z`. Its vector field
satisfies `Cdot=6kC`. Along an exact well-defined flow this preserves `C=0`.
For the explicit Euler map, however,

```
C(z+hF(z)) = (1+6hk) C(z) + h^2 F(z)^T J F(z).
```

At the constrained massive seed `(-1,0,2,1), mu=1`, the last coefficient is
`-7`; at the massless constrained seed `(-1,2,0,1), mu=0`, it is `-3`.
The audit rechecks the entire polynomial identity and its nonzero remainder
modulo `C`, not just these examples. No finite floating-point tolerance changes
the conclusion that the discrete zero set is not invariant.

## 4. A known embedding, now with constraint obligations made explicit

The repository already uses the `3(F+F^-1)` construction in
[`TRANSPORTED_PROBE_CLOCK.md`](../../TRANSPORTED_PROBE_CLOCK.md), and its linear
permutation case in [`LINEAR_SPIN2.md`](../../LINEAR_SPIN2.md), section 8.
The embedding itself is not a new discovery here. The following sufficient
criterion exposes its useful generality and its missing obligations for a
physical compiler.

Let `Phi` be a bijection of an integer configuration domain with an explicit
inverse. Suppose the componentwise sum

`K(b)=Phi(b)+Phi^-1(b)`

has integer polynomial coefficients, degree at most three, and each monomial
has the complete support of the target and at most one physical face edge.
The entire retained allocation, including everything used to implement these
maps, must fit 48 channels. Locality of `Phi` alone says nothing sufficient
about locality of its inverse or the sum; both expressions must be audited.

Set the fixed tensor input `u=3K(b)` and prepare

`E(z,r)=(Phi^-1(z), z, r)`, with arbitrary legal `0<=r<6` componentwise.

Then the exact arithmetic gives

```
divmod(2u+r,6) = (K(b),r),
b_next = Phi(b)+Phi^-1(b)-a = Phi(b),
a_next = b = Phi^-1(b_next),
r_next = r.
```

Consequently `T E = E Phi` and the history graph is invariant in both
directions. If `Phi` preserves a physical constraint, that constraint is
preserved on this complete-state graph. No runtime projection, solve, reset or
discarded old bank occurs. This theorem does **not** say that an arbitrary
rational constrained target has such a bijection, integer encoding, degree,
support or channel count.

## 5. Constructive exact positive oracles

### Integer Lorentz-form map: two channels

```
Phi(x,y)    = (2x+y, 3x+2y)
Phi^-1(x,y) = (2x-y,-3x+2y)
Q(x,y)     = 3x^2-y^2
u          = (12 b_x,12 b_y).
```

Direct multiplication gives `B^T diag(3,-1) B=diag(3,-1)`. The encoded
trajectory is nontrivial and hyperbolic; this is not a Euclidean oscillator.
For example `(1,0)→(2,3)→(7,12)` preserves `Q=3`. The invariant sector includes
`a=B^-1 b`, not merely `Q(a)=Q(b)=3`.

### Polynomial null-form map: three channels, degree three

```
Phi(x,y,z)    = (x, y+2x^2, z-2xy-2x^3)
Phi^-1(x,y,z) = (x, y-2x^2, z+2xy-2x^3)
Q(x,y,z)     = 2xz+y^2
u            = (6 b_x, 6 b_y, 6 b_z-12 b_x^3).
```

Both inverse compositions are polynomial identities and `Q∘Phi=Q` exactly.
One null-cone orbit is `(1,2n,-2n^2)`, starting at `(1,0,0)`. The complete
history ideal generated by `a-Phi^-1(b)` is invariant. The audit reduces its
updated generators and `Q(b_next)-Q(b)` modulo that ideal, obtaining zero
exactly. This is an explicit nonlinear constraint-preserving integer Rule3
fixture with no rational division at runtime. It is onsite and is not a
discretization of the Einstein source equations.

For **each** positive example the actual Engine is checked over 27 distinct
node preparations, 24 steps, and six uniform carry choices: 3,888 node
transitions. Each step agrees with independent target formulas and an
independent scalar `divmod` oracle; every immediate inverse and full rewind
passes. Observed maximum retained bit lengths are 48 and 15 respectively.

## 6. Two separately constrained histories are insufficient

For the two-channel tensor above choose `a=b=(1,0)`. Both have `Q=3`, but
the actual Rule3 recurrence is `b_next=4b-a=(3,0)`, with `Q=27`.
The exact constraint defect is 24, for all six carries, while inversion
still succeeds. The missing condition is `a=Phi^-1(b)`.

The physical reduced target exhibits the same obstruction less visibly.
The original five-channel tensor is rerun unchanged, fingerprint
`e33be737ff30660efc4293c73e2ef4c715fdc08f22f73f288d4adbe2eecabb4c`.
Its first decoded constraint is zero. At tick two it is exactly

```
-211805381883956703960796807492009984 /
6648074056234057895988554997906461158237804299242833919931086162572083201
```

The value is about `-3.185966e-38`, but it is not zero. Rewind, all-six-carry
coverage and the independent scalar oracle still pass. Tick three also fails.

For its actual numerator recurrence `P_next=M N(P,D)-P_old`, the identity is

```
C(P_next) = M^2 delta_hom^2 C(P) + C(P_old)
            -2M N(P,D)^T J P_old.
```

Even when both separate quadratic terms vanish, the bilinear cross-history
term need not. Removing `P_old` would change the permitted dynamics.

## 7. Rational constructive target versus implemented integer law

The existing reduced field has a decomposition `F(z)=3kz+A(z)z`, with
`A^T J+JA=0`. Wherever the required inverses exist, its frozen Cayley map

```
Phi_h(z) = (I-hA(z)/2)^-1 (I+hA(z)/2) z / (1-3hk)
```

satisfies `C(Phi_h(z))=C(z)/(1-3hk)^2`. This is an exact sufficient one-step
constraint identity on its nonzero-denominator domain and is first-order
consistent with the reduced Einstein vector field. Its numerator and common
denominator are cubic, as the source audit verifies.

It is not a proof of an invariant nonzero-denominator domain, global
invertibility, a local polynomial inverse, or a counted Rule3 implementation.
Freezing a state-dependent matrix in a Cayley formula does not automatically
make the nonlinear map globally invertible. Copying its numerator into a
second-order history recurrence fails the commuting-encoding obligation, as
the actual tick-two result shows. A fixed step or an offline symbolic inverse
does not license a state-dependent division during evolution.

The constructive next target is therefore precise: supply a domain-preserving
discrete Einstein map **and** an exact complete-state encoding certificate,
with all support, degree, allocation and inverse obligations counted. The
positive integer fixtures prove that history-compatible quadratic constraints
are possible under Rule3. They do not prove this for Einstein's constraints.
