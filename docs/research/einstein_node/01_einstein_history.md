# 1. What Einstein actually connected

This review uses the original 1911, November 25 1915 and 1916 papers, with
Sauer's archival research for the development between them. It does not claim
access to Einstein's private reasoning beyond the surviving record.

With signature (-+++), `c² dτ²=-gμν dxμ dxν`. Stationarity of the free massive
particle action `-mc² ∫dτ` gives the geodesic equation. This is a variational
statement about an entire path, not a greedy next-node maximization. Tidal
relative acceleration measures Riemann curvature. Separately, the metric obeys
`Gμν=8πG Tμν/c⁴` (with a cosmological term if the model declares one).
The contracted Bianchi identity requires compatible covariant matter balance;
it is not a statement that arbitrary raw register sums must remain constant.

The original 1911 clock argument and the later full spatial metric provide a
particularly useful historical falsifier: redshift and slow attraction alone
do not establish general relativity. In 1916 Einstein obtained approximately
1.7 arcsec solar bending, twice his 1911 result. The difference is physical,
not a clock-unit recalibration.
