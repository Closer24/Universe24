# Replications: every cited run re-run by a second agent, checked from every direction

The Replicator's register, one writer (the Replicator, a standing role on
a machine of its own, created by the Boss on 2026-09-21). The model
owner's rule (2026-09-21, about 11:22Z, record 353 of the day's log,
translated): "Every experiment you rely on, make sure that two different
ones did it and checked it from every direction. Let there be no
experiment that does not pass a check, always." This document is the
second agent's record for every run the paper relies on: nothing here is
a new experiment, nothing is tuned, and a difference is reported, never
fixed (a FAIL is the register's or the engine's to answer, not this
document's). Only a detector's reading is compared ([the readings by
type](ENGINE.md#the-detectors-readings-by-type)): the gathers' chosen
cells, the click lines' ages and phases; the books and the layer's counts
are GameBoard diagnostics and are labelled so.

## The method

For each cited run, on the Replicator's own machine at `origin/main`'s
head, the register's world file(s) are run through the shipped runner
(`python -m event_universe --init <world> --output <dir>`, headless, one
world at a time, as the run block of the register says), and every
registered reading is compared with the Replicator's reading, bit-exact
(the counts, the rungs, the phases, the digests) or inside the pin where
the register pins an interval. The host's wall time is reported apart
from the model's cost. Then the four directions, each a separate reading
with its own numbers:

- **(a) The 48 signed axis permutations** of the world (the transform of
  `tests/test_hand.py` with `full = True`: the positions about the
  centre, every direction and vector by the permutation g, the hand by
  det(g), a per-axis boundary by g; the record mapped back by g^-1, the
  Nodes, the vectors, a face's name and a row's arrival index through the
  world's direction table): under how many of the 48 the detector reading
  equals the base's, and under how many the whole record (every line of
  `events.jsonl`, sorted) equals the base's. The digital line's tie (an
  equal deficit on two axes breaks x before y before z; the auditor's
  round 2, record 349) is expected to break the 40 axis permutations that
  move a tied direction off its plane's order: on a world with a fan the
  8 / 40 split is reported with the tied directions named, not as a FAIL.
- **(b) The renaming of the entities**: every family, every detector and
  the model identity relabelled consistently in the expanded world
  document (the JSON string tokens replaced wherever they stand, keys and
  values alike); the reading mapped back must equal.
- **(c) Another order** where the register allows one: the families'
  declarations, the measured events, the detectors and every table's
  entries reversed together; the counts must equal. The ladder's
  cumulative rungs follow the declared order of the sets (the register:
  "the cells in the layer's order"), so they are reported apart.
- **(d) The pin against the reading**: the expectation the register's
  `derivations` field names, derived again on this machine from the
  design's formula and the engine's own tables (the generator's `bell.py`
  for the pair; the offers and the ladder for the Mach-Zehnder; the
  half-angle tables of 2N for Malus), against what was read: met or not,
  by how much.

The verdict per run is one of three: **REPLICATED** (every registered
reading equal, every check passed or explained by a declared tie),
**FAIL** (a reading that differs, with the numbers), **INCONCLUSIVE**
(with what is missing). The rounds are the Boss's: round 1 the four PASS
rows of NATURE (1a, 2b, 2c, 11b), the Bell run of DERIVATIONS_BEAM 24.4
at N = 512 and 4096, and Malus A12; round 2 the cone, the two slits and
the click's gate set; then every other cited run.

Notation ([the workflow's rule](../skills/workflow.md#notation-every-symbol-named-its-kind-shown-the-model-owner-2026-09-21-record-184)):
N the phase circle, W the birth wheel's width, u the birth phase, E x N
the correlation at one pair of settings times N, S the CHSH sum, g a
signed axis permutation, det(g) its determinant.

## The declared ties and what the rounds saw of them

The law's two declared ties (BEAM_LAW section 4 and note 39; TERMINOLOGY's cube-group entry): the digital line's axis order (an equal deficit on two axes breaks x before y before z; the auditor's round 2, record 349: 8 of 48 equal on a fan with tied directions) and the collision's Port order (the auditor's round 3: on two singles meeting head-on, 16 of 48 equal). No world of round 1 exercises either: every row of these worlds flies on a heading (the bars of the pair worlds, the plane of the Mach-Zehnder with its mirrors, the bar of Malus), so no line is tied, and no two singles meet head-on (a record's rows meet at a splitter or a counter, not in flight). The whole record therefore equals the base under all 48 on every world of round 1, which is what the tables report. The cone (round 2) carries the diagonal (1, 1, 0), a tied direction, and the 8 / 40 split was expected there.

Round 2 saw the ties where the record carries a line that depends on them, and not where it does not. The cone's diagonal (1, 1, 0) is tied, yet the reading and the whole record equal the base under all 48: the record holds no line of the row in flight (its births, its clicks at the counter and its gathers only), and the tie moves the row's Nodes inside a period without moving its arrival, so nothing the record carries can differ. The gate set's fan worlds (`j3_deuteron`, `j3_deuteron_crowd`, `r2`, `alpha_square`) are the ones whose record can see the line's tie and the collision's; the tables give their splits. Two gate worlds without a fan split under the 48 by a third declared tie of the frame, the apportioning of what is released or re-released over the directions whole, "ties in table order from age mod len(directions)" (BEAM_LAW, the frame's step 5, `apportion_whole`; the genericity probe of record 295 lists it as `age mod n` over the default headings): `1b_m16` (a body's `read` and `step` lines differ by the unit the source's release puts on one heading rather than another, the reading equal under 32, the record under 4) and `grouped_12_nodes` (a `pass` line of the home ray's re-creation present under 16 and absent under 32). The reversed declarations are not an allowed order on `grouped_12_nodes`: its `in_transit` rows name the measured event `number` 1, which is the declaration order, so reversing the declarations changes which Node is the rays' own source (the home ray becomes a click); the register declares that order and the check is reported as not applicable, not as a difference.

Round 2 part 2 saw the frame's numbering tie again (BEAM_LAW note 31 (ix), record 295's G1: the bodies move and hand over in number order), on the two-stars worlds under the reversed declarations: the windows' readings before the contact are equal, and after the contact the given amounts and the totals differ by a click or a unit, the same tie that moved `alpha_square` in part 1. The clock-like worlds' fans (the sources' nine directions per lamp, eight of them tied by the line's rule) carry the tie into the record (the record equal under 16 of 48 on the still crowd worlds and the cluster, 8 of 48 on the reader worlds) and, where the lamp moves, into the detector's reading: on `cluster_moving`, `moving_08`, `moving_3`, `moving_1` and `alike_receding` the reading is equal under the 16 images that keep the lamp's axis of motion (the 8 sign changes and the exchange of the two transverse axes) and moves in the third decimal under the 32 that carry the motion to another axis (`cluster_moving`'s m1 2.2132 for 2.2147, m06 1.8059 for 1.8092, m03 1.5052 for 1.5066; every image inside its pin), because the lamp steps on the digital line of its momentum's direction and its sources' rows land by the same line's rule, so which axis carries the motion changes the crowd the lamp counts: the declared tie, the 8 / 40 split of record 349 in its 16 / 32 form on a thrown lamp, not a FAIL. `j3_deuteron_crowd` at its full 700 intervals splits alike (the reading under 16, the record under 8; at the gate set's cap of one interval in part 1 both were 48 of 48), and the two J1 worlds and `j3_neutron_free` keep their reading under 48 of 48 with the record under 2 and 8 (the 212 tied directions of the 290-direction fan).
