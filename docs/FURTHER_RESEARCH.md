# Further research

This file is the project's further research: every open thing the project has named, in plain words, for a researcher to take; each entry gives what nature shows, what the law has in its own status words, what is missing, where to start, and, where a local study exists, what was checked locally on 2026-10-17 with its numbers. It holds only what is open: what the law states and has closed stands as the bold lines of [the law](ALGEBRA.md) and nowhere else, a thing closed by a theorem is removed from here and named by its law line, and where the local studies of 2026-10-17 closed a part of an entry, the entry says closed by the law's bold line for that part and keeps the open remainder. The law's words are defined in the README's section [The words, from the law](../README.md#the-words-from-the-law); a pointer is a bold line name of [the law](ALGEBRA.md) with its section on first use, "item N" an item of [what is open](ALGEBRA.md#what-is-open), and B.9 the claims list of Online Resource 1. The status words are the law's, open, a hypothesis under its own name, closed as a route, a finding by name, no line, built and parked unmerged, and two of this file's: checked locally, no world, a derivation or a run outside the repository, on the law's line or on a local variant of the engine, with no world of the repository and no click of the engine's own; and a declaration, not made, a line the owner could add, nothing derived. A local variant is a plain copy of main with named functions or lines replaced, and never the engine; each local check below says which instrument its numbers rest on, so a researcher knows what the engine itself does not do.
