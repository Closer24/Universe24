# Research status after the fixed-six migration

The active engine has one scalar update with divisor six. The default is the
48-channel group-reference construction. The earlier 382-channel field and
cosmic assembly, its variable-divisor calibrations and associated physical
presets are retired. This change does not preserve their old trajectories.

## What the mathematical construction supplies

- Exact integer state, a fixed local polynomial tensor, and an explicit seed.
- Synchronous old-snapshot reads and an exact inverse under the dependency
  and remainder conditions in [MODEL](MODEL.md).
- Cube-group matrices and representation readouts whose transformation rules
  can be checked independently of physical interpretation.
- Passive official events; no event callback changes the board.

These statements do not establish physical spacetime, energy, particles,
Born probabilities, quantum entanglement or a preferred complexity measure.

## Constructive examples retained

The [bounded routing example](BOUNDED_CIRCUITS.md) has a proved prepared
Boolean sector. The [integer reciprocal constructions](INTEGER_RECIPROCAL_GEOMETRY.md)
include interacting periodic clocks and exact growth controls. The
[curl-wave example](LINEAR_SPIN2.md) has constrained travelling sectors but
also a general three-dimensional instability control.

The [local pp-wave construction](PPWAVE_PALATINI_LOCAL.md) transports a known
vacuum geometry and satisfies its declared centered residuals inside a
coordinate chart. It fails at transverse periodic seams. The [geodesic
extension](PPWAVE_OPERATIONAL_ROUTE.md) encodes selected exact worldlines in
one carrier node; its calibrated proper-time relation is not a universal
clock or moving signal implementation.

Every example is a chosen tensor and preparation within the common engine.
Their success does not show that Rule3 uniquely selects those structures.

## Physical targets still unresolved

A useful next stage must jointly establish stable local structures, a common
operational metric measured by different clocks and signals, propagation of
geometric constraints, and matter backreaction. Quantum claims additionally
need a precise event-probability rule, observable interference and contextual
or Bell-test consistency under explicit assumptions.

A reversible map retains information. It does not implement deletion of
alternative complete histories. A proposed route-selection or complexity
mechanism requires an explicit reversible construction and a justified
observation map. No such physical law follows merely from the click label.
