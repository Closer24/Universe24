## From timing to geometry

The operational goal is to infer geometry from exchanges timed by retained
clocks. Defining an effective length `c * transit_time` is a calibration;
it does not derive the metric or gravitational field equations. Site storage
and actual edge flight must be measured separately. Busy traffic can alter
dwell time without changing the propagation step between adjacent sites.

For example, with zero shift a Lorentzian metric can be written

\[
 ds^2=-N^2c^2dt^2+\gamma_{ij}dx^i dx^j.
\]

Null travel times constrain the optical combination gamma/N squared. They do
not independently determine spatial geometry and clock lapse. Six axial
samples do not determine all mixed components. General geometry requires
additional directional/loop information, a time orientation and a shared
calibration for material clocks. Lattice cube covariance is not Lorentz
invariance or a continuum convergence proof.

Regge calculus offers a precise discrete comparison: edge lengths on a
specified simplicial complex determine piecewise-flat metric data, and
varying a discrete curvature action yields gravitational equations. Merely
assigning integer delays to the six edges of a cubic stencil does not supply
that construction or its equations. A discretization must state its domain,
constraints, source conservation and refinement behavior.

A common-law acceptance test must measure the same geometry using freely
propagating signals and material clocks, account for source backreaction,
and check all independent constraints/evolution equations under refinement.
Calling frozen clock phases 'matter' or 'dark matter' cannot substitute for
moving bodies, stress-energy and operationally distinct interactions.
Homogeneous FLRW equations are useful targets, but passing them under another
tensor would not certify the active spatial clock law.

## Clock, structure and light

The global Engine index is bookkeeping. The retained increasing counter is
explicitly prepared; synchronized initial values do not derive synchronization
or proper time. The collision's bounded Boolean states permute on a finite
periodic lattice, so they recur. Appending the increasing counter removes full
state recurrence but does not force the Boolean spatial structure to grow.
Structural complexity must be measured independently of amplitude and clock
labels; it can decrease.

In the transverse readout, E and B propagate together with B=n cross E.
Neither component acts as a returning acknowledgment. Such a protocol would
require a separately specified, counted mechanism and tests. C2 is a remainder
class, and the present tensor never emits it; no quantum inference follows.
