occupation-dependent transport and face turning with exact scalar continuity

solve Einstein's equations. The following criteria apply to this one tensor;

## What the local load rule establishes

All channels use the same rule without light, matter or dark-matter branches.
The scalar occupation is

\[
 Q(x)=\sum_{g=0}^{47}z_g(x),\qquad z_g=b_g-1\in\{0,1\}.
\]

A quartet has two slots at each endpoint of a physical adjacency. Its edge
stage holds words of occupation three and otherwise rotates their values.
The subsequent onsite turn changes faces but preserves each node's Q. These
operations conserve total occupation exactly, and an endpoint's current
depends on its neighbor's occupation. A four-run causal intervention detects
that dependence in aggregate node load, beyond a change in channel phase.

Local feedback is not a global optimization theorem. In two prescribed
comparisons with the same unconditional transport and turning, concentration
improved in 13 of 16 sparse cases at update 8 but in only 5 of 16 denser cases
when integrated over updates 1..30. The unfavorable cases remain part of the
result. A full central-pair-symmetric burst never activates the three-slot
gate, even while it visibly spreads. The default asymmetric boundary halo is
prepared to exercise the gate; it is not an emergent material species.
[LOAD_DISTRIBUTION.md](LOAD_DISTRIBUTION.md) gives the domains and controls.

The fixed symbolic work schedule is identical at every node. Within this
proved domain a,b remain 1 or 2 and carries remain in 1..5. Occupied capacity
therefore does not imply growing integer arithmetic cost. A bit-length or
queue-load measure is not automatically physical energy.

## Light and the missing stress-energy account

For an isolated occupied slot in channel g, each update follows one face and
turns the channel to gT. Over three updates its displacement is
\(R_g(1,1,1)\). Its graph-hop speed is one per update; its unwrapped Euclidean
speed over that block is \(1/\sqrt{3}\). The nearest-neighbor causal bound does
not make these zigzag carriers a Lorentz-invariant light cone. The former
straight-axis tensor's transverse E/B result does not apply, and the current
model exposes no E/B field API.

A natural coarse momentum candidate based on isolated motion is

\[
 \mathbf P=\sum_{x,g}R_g(1,1,1)\,z_g(x).
\]

This quantity is constant for an isolated carrier but is not conserved by the
capacity collision. For the edge quartet rooted at channel 0 with word
(1,1,1,0), one exact Engine update changes P from (1,3,1) to (-1,3,-1), while
Q_total remains 3 and the entire retained state still rewinds exactly. Thus
conservation of Q cannot stand in for a complete relativistic stress-energy
account. This counterexample rejects that natural momentum interpretation;
it does not prove that no different conserved account can exist.

General relativity requires a stress-energy tensor that includes energy,
momentum flow, pressure and stress, with covariant conservation in the same
metric. Software label blindness is not by itself the equivalence principle:
different physical probes and material clocks would have to recover the same
operational geometry. Massive bodies need stable dynamics and inertial
properties. A dark-matter identification additionally needs gravitational
sourcing and the appropriate absence of optical interactions under this law.
A held or quiet occupied slot establishes none of those properties.

## From timing to geometry

The goal is to infer geometry from signal exchanges and retained material
clocks. Defining an effective communication length as c times a passage time
is a calibration, not a derivation of a metric or gravitational field equation.
The present gate can change local holding and routing; it has no independently
retained scalar edge length. Waiting inside a node and flight across an edge
must be measured separately. Anonymous Boolean occupancy also does not supply
identities for tracking every individual carrier through a full quartet.

With zero shift, a Lorentzian metric can be written

\[
 ds^2=-N^2c^2dt^2+\gamma_{ij}dx^i dx^j.
\]

Null travel times constrain the optical combination \(\gamma_{ij}/N^2\), not
spatial geometry and clock lapse separately. For example,

\[
 ds_A^2=-c^2dt^2+n(x)^2dx^2,\qquad
 ds_B^2=-c^2dt^2/n(x)^2+dx^2
\]

have the same null travel times for positive n(x), while stationary clocks
read dτ_A=dt and dτ_B=dt/n(x). Six axial timing samples also do not determine
all mixed metric components. General geometry requires additional directional
or loop information, time orientation and material clock calibration. Exact
cube covariance does not establish Lorentz invariance or convergence to GR.

Regge calculus makes a precise discrete comparison: lengths on a specified
simplicial complex determine piecewise-flat metric data, and varying a
curvature action yields discrete gravitational equations. Integer delays on
six cubic neighbors alone do not supply that construction. Jacobson's
thermodynamic derivation instead requires local causal horizons, an area
entropy, Unruh temperature and an energy/entropy relation for every local null
direction. Neither result identifies gravity with CPU-load equalization.

Acceptance requires a common metric and source account from the same retained
construction, backreaction, operational clocks, and independent gravitational
constraint/evolution tests under refinement. A timing diagram, a homogeneous
expansion example or a colored lattice does not certify those conditions.

## Clocks, clicks and complexity

The global Engine index orders computation. The counted internal states cycle;
they are not an added monotonic timestamp or a calibrated proper clock. On a
finite periodic lattice, the complete prepared sector is finite and evolves
bijectively, so every orbit recurs. Concentration and structural complexity
can increase or decrease. No indefinite strict monotonic growth is possible
in this closed bounded sector. Useful finite-time spreading remains a testable
claim with a specified preparation and window.

The passive board reads clicks plus explicitly public initial calibration,
not an inferred hidden event geometry. Fixed carries give only C0 arithmetic
clicks. C1 and C2 denote other remainder classes in the general decoder, not
second or third handshakes; this tensor does not emit them. No component of an
unproved E/B representation can be assigned a returning quantum acknowledgment
without a separately specified, counted mechanism and operational tests.
Click counts are not photon counts or quantum probabilities.
