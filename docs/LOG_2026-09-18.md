### 01. PASS and RETURN (the section's text before 2026-09-17, superseded thrice)

**PASS and RETURN.** Only a Node whose Detector bit is set may draw, and replay never redraws or re-emits. 1 = PASS is the marked Node behaving as an ordinary Node for that arrival, the ray continuing on its line or interacting; 0 = RETURN is the same wave ray reversed on its line, unchanged, walking back the number of steps it has made since its event. One draw per arriving transfer, independently for up to six arrivals in one interval; every ray is a wave ray, so the kind of ray makes no difference. PASS/RETURN at a marked Node is the quantum measurement itself. Only PASS is a measurement; RETURN is none: nothing is measured, nothing is recorded as an outcome, the Node waits. The action distribution is not assumed to be 50/50. The return content is the arriving content unchanged; the return-content/phase laws, LOCK lifecycle, clock mapping and cancellation handling of the former shared resource lapsed with section 3.18. Superseded on 2026-09-18 by the law of the bit below: a mark draws nothing (point 14), it absorbs a thing and returns a shadow (point 6), and a return is a field, not a walk back by a step count (point 3). Superseded again on 2026-09-18 (the evening) by the law of the shadow (below): no thing and no shadow to tell apart, no PASS and no RETURN; a mark is held content with a table that counts, and a measurement is the absorption of a whole quantum there. (Under the law of events, 2026-09-19: read as section 5.4's law of events says, there being no ray, thing, shadow or bit, no draw and no return; what this describes is an event, in transit or measured, handled by the Node's one computation, and a Node keeps nothing.)

### 02. A pair

**A pair.** A returning ray retraces its own trajectory by its step count, reaches its birth interaction with certainty and there performs the inverse split of its share: it transmits what happened at the event, with its bit, to the same places the event sent to, the partner ray's line among them, so the partner's Detector is not missing it: the first Detector saw only its own value, and the second Detector receives that value on the ray that reaches it. With two Detectors, Alice's and Bob's, whichever returns first sends its value through the birth event and the other receives it; sometimes it is Alice's information, sometimes Bob's. To Alice and Bob the correlation feels as if it were decided at time zero, but nothing happened at time zero: the value was carried through the birth event in event spacetime, one Link per interval. Every Detector behaves the same: the second Detector draws its own bit on that arrival like on any other and reads nothing from the ray; the received value is information on the ray, not an input to the draw (superseded on 2026-09-17 for rays that carry a bit: see the next paragraph, a Detector reads the bit). The information of the last event, its Ports and shares, stays on the ray as a hidden variable: no Detector and no ordinary coupling reads it, it comes from no ordinary physics, and nothing on the board feels it. The Detector's bit is different since 2026-09-17: it is a visible property of the ray (next paragraph). (2026-09-18: for a shadow the return is a field and retraces nothing, point 3; what a mark that misses a thing does is point 6.) (2026-09-18, the evening, the law of the shadow, below: nothing retraces and nothing is carried through a birth event; Bell is two events of one field at two places, each born of the local field and the local setting, at most 2, reading (vii).) (Under the law of events, 2026-09-19: read as section 5.4's law of events says, there being no ray, thing, shadow or bit, no draw and no return; what this describes is an event, in transit or measured, handled by the Node's one computation, and a Node keeps nothing.)

### 03. The Detector's bit is a property of the ray

**The Detector's bit is a property of the ray (model owner, 2026-09-17).** Whoever meets a ray sees that it came from a Detector: the bit a marked Node set on a ray, 1 for PASS and 0 for RETURN, travels with the ray as a property like charge, visible to every meeting, to the record and to the rendering, and it propagates: the outputs of any event a marked ray takes part in carry it, so the descendants of a realized ray are known to be realized and the descendants of a transmission are known to carry a return; where the inputs of one event carry different bits, the declared coupling says which bit the outputs carry, and the default written in the catalog is that 1 outranks 0 and 0 outranks none. A coupling in the catalog may read the bit as it reads charge, so an apparatus that behaves differently for realized content is a catalog entry, not an engine mechanism (section 3.26). A Detector reads the bit (model owner, 2026-09-17, closing the open decision of that morning): a ray carrying 1 is already realized and passes a later Detector without a draw, as a measurement repeated in the same basis repeats its result; a ray carrying 0 is a transmission and is never drawn, it passes the marked Node and does what a transmission does; only a ray carrying no bit is drawn. How a marked Node meets each bit is its declared coupling in the catalog, with this as the default and the draw on every arrival, as before, the declarable alternative, so the rule is a table entry and not an engine mechanism (section 3.26); feature 2b implements both. This supersedes, for rays that carry a bit, the sentence of the pair paragraph that the second Detector draws on every arrival and reads nothing from the ray; the price of section 5.4 is to be re-derived under this rule by hypothesis 11 and experiment A13 before it is quoted again. The engine already carries the bit and stamps it on the outputs of the inverse split (features 1 to 4); the inheritance at every meeting, the catalog read and the visible mark in the record and the rendering are feature 2b, after feature 10. Under the law of the bit (2026-09-18) there is no ray without a bit and no draw: 1 is a thing, 0 its shadow, a mark absorbs the one and returns the other (points 6, 14), and a mark reads the bit, it does not draw on it. Superseded on 2026-09-18 (the evening) by the law of the shadow (below): there is no bit on any ray; a mark absorbs whole quanta by its table and counts them. (Under the law of events, 2026-09-19: read as section 5.4's law of events says, there being no ray, thing, shadow or bit, no draw and no return; what this describes is an event, in transit or measured, handled by the Node's one computation, and a Node keeps nothing.)

### 04. A click is an absorption

**A click is an absorption (model owner, 2026-09-18).** The field quantum a marked Node realizes ends there: on a draw of 1 the arriving field content is absorbed into the mark's exact counter, per family, booked on the audit as absorbed by marks with its momentum on the mark's line, and nothing of it spreads on; a photon is detected by being absorbed, and every measurement is paid (section 3.14). A draw of 0 stays the return of section 3.19, the ray sent back on its line unchanged, a perfect mirror; a ray carrying 1 still passes without a draw. Matter that draws 1 passes with the bit 1 as before, so the electron seen carries its mark; how a mark meets each family on a click, absorb or pass, is its declared coupling in the catalog, with absorb the default for a field family and pass the default for matter (section 3.26), so a screen that stops electrons and a counter that lets light through are catalog entries. The bit is therefore the mark of content that passed a Detector without being absorbed, and the field a Node re-releases never carries the bit of what was realized, because what was realized is no longer there to spread. Decided on the finding of the loop screen (E9, 2026-09-17): with the clicked quantum spreading on, its bit left the marks along the screen and back toward the source and the screen stopped clicking at tick 72, a screen that remembers; a screen that absorbs counts, and its count grows as an intensity, which the two-slit run A1 needs. Feature 2c implements it; E9 is repeated under it. The same day (points 6, 12 and 14): no draw and no field family; a mark absorbs a thing, or passes it if its declared coupling for the family says so, returns a shadow, and re-releases nothing. Under the law of the shadow (2026-09-18, the evening, below) this stands and is the whole of measurement: a click is the absorption of a whole quantum at the mark's held content; the return on a draw of 0 is gone, since nothing returns, and what a mark's table says per family is absorb, hold or release again. (Under the law of events, 2026-09-19: read as section 5.4's law of events says, there being no ray, thing, shadow or bit, no draw and no return; what this describes is an event, in transit or measured, handled by the Node's one computation, and a Node keeps nothing.)

### 05. Definitions for the law of the bit

**Definitions for the law of the bit (fixed terms, model owner, 2026-09-18; one name per thing, one thing per name).** *Node*: a point of the lattice; it is its six *Ports*, one per heading, and holds nothing else (point 22). *Link*: what joins two Nodes; it carries rays both ways. *Lane*: one direction of a Port, in or out; two per Port, twelve per Node; a lane carries per interval at most one real and one shadow per owner (point 25). *Interval*: one tick; every ray moves one Link per interval (point 21), a thing that waits excepted (point 23). *Ray*: content in motion on a line, with an amount, a phase, a heading and a bit, and, for a thing, a momentum. *Amount*: whole quanta; it is never negative and never destroyed. *Phase*: an angle on a circle of *N* steps; every ray advances it by its content / *K* per interval, light included, whose content is its amount (model owner, 2026-09-18, the evening: "the phase rotates like a ray"; so light's frequency is its amount, Planck's relation as a consequence); a shadow carries its owner's phase as it was at its release ("a release stamps": every release, the prefill's and the re-release at home alike, stamps the owner's phase of that interval, so a source with a clock makes a wave and a source without one a still field). *Heading*: the Port a ray leaves by. *Bit*: the one property that says what a ray is; its two values are *real* (1) and *shadow* (0). *Family*: the declared kind of a thing (electron, proton, light); it declares the charge of a thing of it, one whole number whatever the thing's content (point 16 as amended), its tables and its phase width *N* (points 12, 17, 19), and its quantum, of which every thing of it is a whole multiple (point 25). *Thing*: a real ray (an electron, a photon, a piece of a split); it has mass and a clock, one path, and its bit never changes at a meeting (points 9, 10). *Shadow*: a ray with bit 0, the field of a thing, of the same family as its thing (point 12); it carries its owner's identity, charge and content as a message, has no mass and no clock, and is absorbed where it reaches its owner (point 3). *Owner*: the thing a shadow belongs to; one number, given to a thing at its birth and carried by its shadows, the name of the coupling between a thing and its field (model owner, 2026-09-18: "a shadow and its source have the same label, and a shadow always has one label"). The shadow does nothing with it; the Node reads it in two places: shadows with one number are one field and mix as amplitudes, shadows with different numbers are different fields and are counted (point 24); a shadow that meets a real with its own number is home, absorbed and released again, and with any other number it pushes and is turned back (point 3). A shadow never knows whom to return to (model owner, 2026-09-18: "it is simply turned back at a meeting with a 1"); the number is not a route, a memory or a step count, it is a property like the sign of the charge. Why the number is needed (the orchestrator's elaboration, 2026-09-18): without it the mixing makes one wave of every source, so the force of two things at rest on a third ripples with the period N; the reflection of a thing's own field (four ninths of every share, back within two intervals) pushes the thing and its returns land on a stranger with a momentum proportional to the square of its content; and a return is absorbed by the first real it meets, so a push's recoil never reaches the source. What physics does with the same number: the self-field of a charge does not push it, and the fields of different sources add, they do not interfere. A merged thing (point 25) carries the numbers of all it merged. Under the emission of the same evening ("A thing emits"): every thing gets its number at its birth and stamps it on what it emits, and nothing inherits, so a thing born at an event, emitted or a piece of a split, has its own number and its own field from birth (a lamp's field pushes the light it emitted, a star's its own light), and the field of a thing that ended streams off the board; the guidance of a thing through two slits is then the ordinary push of its source's field (point 16), and home stays what point 3 says, a zero (the orchestrator's reading, adopted by the model owner, 2026-09-18, "closed"). *Shadow set*: all the shadows of one thing, its size proportional to the thing's content (point 18). *Standing set*: the shadows around a thing at rest, a steady state a formula can give (points 11, 13). *Content*: the amount of a thing; it is its mass and its clock (point 19). *Momentum*: a property of a thing, a vector it carries like its amount and its phase; every push adds to it, and when its component on an axis reaches the thing's content the thing steps to that axis and the momentum drops by that content (point 21). *Meeting*: two rays at one Node in one interval. *Event*: a change of trajectory at a Node that holds a real ray (point 13); a push is not one (point 15). *Push*: the field's arithmetic on a thing's momentum, what a thing takes from a shadow it meets (point 15). *Reading*: what a thing multiplies a shadow's message by, gravity by content and electricity by charge (point 16). *Return*: a ray turned back at a meeting, and only at a meeting (model owner, 2026-09-18: "returns are a meeting of a shadow with a real, or a real with a real"): a shadow meeting a thing is pushed on and turned back, the same shadow with its heading reversed and its momentum inverted, a field like any other from then on (point 3); a thing meeting a thing turns by the pair's table (point 4: steering by the phase difference within a family, point 17; the table of section 5.2 between families; a mirror is the table "reversed heading"). A mark is a thing with a table (point 22): it absorbs things and it is pushed by shadows and turns them back as any thing does, so a mark's return is a meeting like every other and carries the push inverted (point 6 as amended); the engine's exception, a mark returning a shadow with nothing carried, is retired (feature 16h). *Remainder*: a shadow parked at a Node, an amount below one quantum waiting to become one (section 3.17, point 22). *Mixing*: the Node's step on the shadows of one owner that arrive in an interval, the coherent sum shared among the six Ports (point 24); it is how a shadow spreads. *Steering*: two things of one family meeting, the share leaving each Port set by the table of their phase difference (section 5.2); the table is computed from *N* (point 17). *Wait*: the tick a thing pays for every whole quantum it reads (point 23). *Mark*: a Node whose Detector bit is set; it absorbs things into a resident thing, its counter, and returns shadows (points 6, 22). *Source*: a thing that spends its own content as things of another family by an emission table (point 22). *Prefill*: the shadows given with the board at the start; a thing does not emit them. *Books*: the ledger, kept per bit; things conserve amount, momentum and charge among themselves exactly, shadows are initial content (point 7). *K*: the content per phase step per interval, one for the world. *N*: the number of steps of the phase circle, the one input behind interference; one for the world, like *K*, declared once and 64 (model owner, 2026-09-18): a phase difference between any two rays, of one family or of two, is read on the one circle, so a width per family means nothing; at 64 steps the mixing's rounding costs nothing (DERIVATIONS.md round 4), and a wider circle adds no measured precision. The per-family `phase_bits` of the engine is retired in the cleanup. (Under the law of events, 2026-09-19: one name, event; a Node is its six Ports and keeps nothing, neither a share below one nor a count; no ray is excepted from point 21 by a wait, the exit of a Port being suspended by what is present in the interval.)

### 06. Definitions for the law of the shadow

**Definitions for the law of the shadow (the orchestrator's draft from the model owner's words, 2026-09-18, flagged).** *Shadow*: the only ray ("No real and shadow. There is only shadow."): a whole quantum of a family in flight, moving one Link per interval and spreading by the Node's mixing (point 24) from the event that released it, its phase rotating by its content over *K*, light's content being its amount; it carries its family, its amount, its phase, its heading and its number, and it never disappears. *Event*: a whole quantum at a Node that holds content, where it is absorbed, held or released again by the family's table; from the event the shadows spread ("There are events, which are a whole quantum."). "Events are created only at a detector" and "what the eye sees is events", the model owner's words. *Held content*: matter: content held at a Node, the only place events happen; the external body and the mark's counter are its forms. It releases, absorbs, holds and releases again by its family's table, and it moves by its momentum. *Release*: what held content does every interval: it emits shadows in proportion to its content ("A thing emits"; "a real emits shadow forever"), each stamped with the holder's phase of that interval and its number. *Absorption*: the event: a whole quantum taken in at held content by the family's table. The push is an absorption: the quantum's momentum, with the family's sign, gravity's toward the source, times the holder's cross-section, its content for gravity and its whole charge for electricity (charge per thing, point 16 as amended), so the equivalence and Coulomb stay; there is no return and no home. *Re-release*: a whole quantum absorbed at held content and released again by the family's table, which is what a mirror is; it leaves with the holder's number and phase stamped. *Hold*: an absorption whose quantum stays as the holder's content; light, a paid family, is held, and its hold at a mark is a click (that gravity and electricity are free and light paid is what round 8 must show). *Step*: how held content moves: when a component of its momentum reaches its content (point 21), a release toward the neighbour and an absorption there; so nothing outruns the field, and light and matter share one c, the field's. *Wait*: the tick paid where the size of the field, the whole units of the coherent sum of the shadows at the Node, is read; paid by every quantum, held or in flight: a held content's release slows in a potential (the clock, the redshift), and a quantum of light in flight steps slower where the size is larger (an index, n = 1 + 2GM/r). *Number*: the label a release stamps on a quantum, the last emitter's; a quantum absorbed and released again carries the holder's number and phase, so coherence is the last emitter, with no inheritance and no orphan, and shadows of one number mix as one field (point 24). *Mark*: held content with a table that counts; the whole quanta it absorbs are its clicks and its counter is its content. *Lamp*: held content that releases light, a paid family, and spends its own content in doing so (the source of point 22 in this law's words). *Edge*: the boundary of an open board, standing for infinity as it does in physics ("an open board, then"); a shadow that leaves through it is booked as escaped and never comes back, since a shadow never disappears and nothing returns. *Books*: one line for held content and one for the shadows; the shadows' line carries the emission, the escaped and the absorbed, and momentum held, in flight and escaped is constant at every interval. *N*, *K*: as in the glossary above, one for the world each: *N* the steps of the phase circle, 64, and *K* the content per phase step per interval; every ray's phase, a shadow's included, rotates by its content over *K*. *ρ*: the rate of release, the shadows a held content emits per unit of content per interval (the orchestrator's name for the ratio of point 18 that "A thing emits" made a rate); its value is the model owner's (section 5.5). (Under the law of events, 2026-09-19: read as section 5.4's law of events says: no thing or shadow, no register, remainder or parked share, no return; an event's exit is one of seven, by the shares and its momentum, its suspension is carried, its clock is the count of its self-creations, and matter is a measured event.)

### 07. The law of the bit: the thing or its shadow

**The law of the bit: the thing or its shadow (model owner, 2026-09-18).** Every ray carries one bit, and the bit says what the ray is: 1, the thing itself; 0, its shadow, the field. Superseded the evening of 2026-09-18 by the law of the shadow (the paragraph below): there is no thing ray, every ray is a shadow, and an event is a whole quantum at held content.
