# The engine

This document describes the engine as the code holds it on `main`, beside [ALGEBRA.md](ALGEBRA.md) (the law) and [HIGHLIGHTS.md](HIGHLIGHTS.md) (the decisions in force). The engine is the law's implementation and nothing else (the owner's decision (c) of 2026-09-29 on #1495): a GameBoard of Nodes, every family's NodeState at every Node, and one interval of acts in the law's order, each a call of Rule3 (`src/event_universe/core/rule3.py`) on whole-board arrays, every neighbour read through a Port (`src/event_universe/core/ports.py`). The engine holds no number, no formula, no family's name and no flag: every physical value comes from the run's files. No act of the interval takes a square root and no act reads a Node to decide anything: the guard reads the whole initial state once at load, as squares.

## 1. The words

- **Node**: one place of the GameBoard. Its whole local information is its **NodeState**, the law's own numbers and nothing else (section 2).
- **Port** and **Link**: a Node has six Ports, +x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z; a Link is the pair of Ports between two neighbours. A level reaches a neighbour only through a Port (`arrival`). An axis is periodic (it wraps), closed (a wall, read as 0) or open (a wall read as 0 whose outer layer of `face_depth` Nodes is told to report); an axis of one layer folds, its two Ports returning the Node itself.
- **GameBoard**: the box of Nodes, `shape` [X, Y, Z]; in code `GameBoard` (`src/event_universe/game_board.py`).
- **Family**: one kind of physical value, a row of the universe file: its name, its pair [num, den] and what it holds (`held`: the content or the sign, at the divisor E_s). The rule derives the rest ([ALGEBRA.md, the families from the rule](ALGEBRA.md#a-familys-declaration), `src/event_universe/loader/derived.py`): a family that does not hold the content carries quanta (two level pairs, the real one and the second, the rotation sense; a count, a sense and their currents); a held family has its parts, the time part alone or with the three axis tensions (1 or 1 + 3 components, the parts Rule3 reads and no other), and is stepped where its pair has no gap (num = den) and laid as the well each interval where it has one (`FamilyRule.gap`); a family of quanta reads every holder of the content plainly and every other holder of the sign by its own q, and sources what it reads at the same weight; no family gives another anything.
- **Record**: a line of the books, never a level of its own: a family's level at a Node is one number. The holder of the sign is one record: its time part is its real level pair, the light's own record.
- **Body**: the initial state the world file declares and no ledger: its family, its Nodes with their counts. Its Nodes at a later interval are where its family's count stands about its declared Nodes, and its shell the Nodes among them with a Port to a count 0, derived when a report needs them (`reports.standing`) and never kept; its quanta that leave it stay its family.
- **Detector**: a Node declared in the file to report. Its **click**, the rise of a family's count across its Nodes, is the one measurement; every other number (a level, a count, the books) is a GameBoard reading, a diagnostic.
- **Interval**: one step of the whole GameBoard; `ticks` intervals make a run.

## 2. The NodeState

The engine is six modules under 400 lines each: `node.py` (the NodeState and the Node's acts: the read, Rule3 on the levels, the lines, the well and the hold), `lay.py` (the lay of every count and sense at the first act), `flow.py` (the flows into a held row's tensions), `reports.py` (the detectors' clicks, a body's Nodes derived for a report, the books), `game_board.py` (the GameBoard that walks the interval forward and back) and `world_files.py` with `loader/` (the files), on `core/` (Rule3, the Ports, the working bound) and the folders of `features/`.

`src/event_universe/node.py`, `NodeState`, one per family, every array over the GameBoard in 64-bit integers:

| Part | Held by | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `levels` (`now`, `before`, `remainder`) | a family of quanta | its real level pair and Rule3's remainder; for the holder of the sign the time part itself (`parts[0] is levels`) |
| `second` (`now`, `before`, `remainder`) | a family of quanta | its second level pair, the rotation sense (0 on a real record), stepped by the same rule |
| `count`, `count_remainder` | a family of quanta | its count and the count's line's remainder, laid at the first act |
| `sense`, `sense_remainder` | a family of quanta | its sense (the booked Wronskian) and the sense's line's remainder, laid at the first act |
| `well_remainder`, `wronskian_remainder` | a family of quanta | the remainders of its well, (D_i + r) div T, and of its Wronskian's quanta, (W_i + r) div T |
| `parts` | a held family | each component's two levels and remainder (the time part; the three tensions xx, yy, zz where its rank has them) |
| `carry` | a held family | the hold's carry at its time part |
| `flows` | a held family | per sourcing family, the carries of its three tensions' writes, at half their divisor from the first act |

## 3. The interval

Before the first interval every held family's time part stands at its sources' rest, the sources each body's form, the vacuum's share of its two level pairs laid at the count's wall (`lay.share` at the content 0, `lay.laid`) at its Nodes at the weight with which its family sources the held family by plain, and its Wronskian's quanta at the written moment, W div T, at the weight by sign (the holder of the sign's rest, sources of either sign): a row without a gap at the rest of its line (`features/start`, the division act iterated from nothing until the levels repeat, 6 den b = num S_6(b) + 3 den sigma at a fine unit from the file's width, the sources of either sign or both where the board has a sink; the same iteration serves the generator), the remainder at the half wall of the rule the row steps by, a row with a gap laid, the sources through its divisor with the remainder's origin at half of it; the tensions start at 0. Then the guard, once: every family of quanta's read of the initial state is checked as squares, 0 < p_a and p^2 (den + num) <= 2 den Gamma^2 at every Node on the clock's square and the three Link paces (`node.guarded`, `features/signed_read`), refused by name outside; no act of the interval reads a pace to refuse. `GameBoard.step()` walks the acts in order, each one loop over the families (or the detectors) with no case for any of them (ALGEBRA.md #the-interval):

1. **The signed read and Rule3** (`GameBoard.stepped`, `node.signed_read`, `features/signed_read`, `node.step`, `node.rule_of`): for every family the content c = SUM over its reads of (weight x by x the read family's time part, for a held row with a gap the well it laid) and the axis contents t_a from the tensions, (weight x by x the aa part + 1) div 2, by sign the reader's q the sign of its own sense at the Node (`node.sense_sign`); no floor and no clamp. Every record of the family steps: a family of quanta's two level pairs by `coefficients(num, den, Gamma, c, t)` at its reads' paces (the holder of the sign's time part and its second pair the same), a holder of the content's parts by the plain rule at the pace 1 and the wall 3 den, a row with a gap not at all; every read is of the interval's start, the stepped records assigned after every family has read; a level beyond the amplitude bound A refuses the run by name. The well of every family of quanta is formed here from both pairs' forms, D_i = now^2 - next x before, (D_i + r) div T, and its Wronskian's quanta, W_i = re_now im_before - im_now re_before as the step left them, (W_i + r) div T (`node.form`, `node.wronskian`, `node.well`).
2. **The lay** at the first act (`GameBoard.lay`, `lay.lay`, `lay.lay_sense`): every family's count from the weighted share of its two level pairs at the paces of the read, e_i = 3 [w (now^2 + before^2) - S now before] div (2 SUM_a p_a^2) - num now S_6(before), W_c c + r = e_i + W_c div 2, W_c = 3 den T (`lay.share`, exact in integers of any size), and its sense from w W_i div (2 p^2) the same way (`lay.sense_share`); a held family's count starts at 0 where its record is 0; the quanta a body `holds` of other families added at its declared Nodes; each body's declared count checked within the rounding of the count laid at its Nodes, ((|c - laid| - 1) div 2)^2 <= c, refused by name beyond; the holds' tension carries set at half their divisor.
3. **The count's and the sense's lines** (`GameBoard.lines`, `node.count_line`, `node.sense_line`, `features/counts_line`): the six currents num (now_i before_j - before_i now_j) of both level pairs move each family's count, and the Wronskian's currents num (im_i re_j - re_i im_j) at the levels the step started from move its sense, no block and no clamp (a hole is conserved and filled); beside the count the line books the tension on each axis, T_aa(i) = num (G_aa(i) + G_aa(i - a)) div 2 with G_aa(i) = now_i (now_(i+2a) - now_i) - now_(i+a) (now_(i+a) - now_(i-a)), the neighbour's difference brought through the Port (`node.axis_differences`), read and never written.
4. **The clicks** (`GameBoard.report`, `reports.clicks`): every detector (a detector's declared Nodes, the Nodes of the body it names derived now, the open faces' layer) reports per family the quanta the count's line's currents carried into its Nodes this interval: at every Port of its Nodes leading in from a Node outside them, (r + F_p) div W_c where that is above 0, r the Node's remainder before the line, one division per Port in the order [+X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z], one `click` line per quantum with the Node it entered and the Port's axis and side; nothing is handed over.
5. **The hold** (`GameBoard.hold`, `node.held_step`, `flow.flow_hold`, `features/hold`): a held family with a gap is not stepped: its time part is laid, (w x n_i + r) div E_s with its own remainder, the level before kept beside it (backward the level before returns, so its inverse is exact over one interval and over more where its level stands still); every held family without a gap has its time part gain (w x n_i + r) div E_s by the write's carried division (`features/write`), n_i the wells of the families that source it by plain and the Wronskian's quanta of those that source it by sign (light is born by this write: a breathing or moving rotating body writes a varying source at its Nodes and the row carries it away as a wave), and each tension aa gain (w x T_aa + r_aa) div (E_s W_c), one act per axis with the remainder carried, no count in the divisor and no branch (`flow.Flow`).

`GameBoard.step_inverse()` runs the acts back with Rule3's direction -1: a held family's hold back once every family that sources it is booked back, a family of quanta booked back (its lines back from the levels as the step left them, its records back, its wells and its Wronskian's quanta back) once its own hold is off, so the holder of the sign goes before the rows it sources, then every held row of the content stepped back, the records assigned after every read of the interval's start (`GameBoard.booked_back`). Every interval returns every level, remainder, count, sense and carry bit for bit, and the lay is not undone; a held row with a gap keeps one interval of its past, so its level before returns over one interval and over more only where its level stands still. The back-in-time gate, `tools/back_in_time.py` (`PYTHONPATH=src python tools/back_in_time.py --intervals 40 <world>.json`), runs a world N intervals forward after its first act and N back and compares every array of every family and the interval counter with the state at the same interval on the way forward: MATCH, or MISS naming the first interval, the array and the first Node that differ; `tests/test_the_node.py` runs it on the closed cube. The readings: `books()` per family of quanta (`reports.book`) (the count's sum, the remainders' sum, SUM (W_c c + r) against its laid value, the clicks, the sense's sum and its total against its laid value, and the least Link pace of the final state, a diagnostic) and `contents()` per body (each family's count at the body's Nodes derived now).

## 4. The loader and the files

`src/event_universe/world_files.py` (`load_world`) reads the world file, the universe and engine start files it names by their repository paths and the generator's mode file beside it, and `src/event_universe/loader/world.py` (`parse_world`) checks every key and refuses every other key as unknown by name; no default is written.

| File | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| world | `shape` [X, Y, Z]; `boundary` {`x`, `y`, `z`: `open`, `periodic` or `closed`}; `face_depth` (from 1, required with an open axis); `ticks`; `universe` and `engine` (repository paths); `measured`, a list of bodies, each `family` (a family of quanta), `nodes` [{`node` [x, y, z], `count` from 1}] (no Node shared) and optionally `holds` {family name: count}, the quanta of other families of quanta laid over its Nodes in proportion to its counts by the carried division; `detectors`, each `name` (not `face`) with `positions` (its Nodes) or `block` (the number of a body, whose Nodes derived each interval report) |
| universe | `integers`: `node_clock` (Gamma), `quantum_action` (T) and `width` (the integers' bits, at most the host's); `families`: each `name`, `pair` [num, den] (den from 1, |num| below den or num = den) and optionally `held` {`count`: `content` or `sign`, `divisor` from 1} |
| engine start | `mode` |
| mode (`<world>.mode.json`, `tools/pixel_mode.py`) | `world_digest` (the world's digest, `input_digest`) and `bodies`, one per body: `family`, `pair` and `moving` {`now`, `before`, and for a body laid with a sense `im_now`, `im_before`, both or neither} (the level pairs, one integer per Node in x-major order; a second pair on real levels at 0 is refused by name), with the generator's readings `profile`, `count`, `seed`, `carried`, `period`, `amplitude`, `clock` |

The amplitude bound A is derived from the file's width, never written (`derived.amplitude_bound`): the largest level at which Rule3's total 6 A R + A |S| + w (A + 1) at the levels 0, Gamma div 2 and Gamma - 1 of every pair and the count's line's total 6 x 2 x 2 |num| A^2 + W_c (most + 2) of every family of quanta (two products on each of two level pairs) stay inside the width, the largest A whose square fits found as the fixed point of the division act (`rule3.division_fixed_point`, Newton's integer iteration x <- (x + n div x) div 2 from above until it repeats) and never by a root; the generator reads its scales the same way. The shipped files are `examples/events/rule.json` (the rule's own universe, the universe the tests run on: Gamma 6,000, T 32,768, every divisor 1) and `examples/events/engine_start.json`; `examples/events/universe.json`, `planck.json` (Gamma 24) and `planck_6000.json` (Gamma 6,000) are not the law's and stay this round, read by no test, until the owner decides on the numbers.

## 5. The output

The observer handed to `GameBoard` receives one dictionary per line: `click` (`tick`, `family`, `detector`, `node` the Node the quantum entered, `axis` [axis, side] the Port it crossed, `count` the detector's count after, `body` the body a detector names or None; the measurement, one line per quantum). `tools/run_inputs.py` writes one `<name>.output.json` per world with the verdict (`LAWFUL`, or `REFUSED` with the reason, the guard's at load among them), the intervals run, the `click` lines and the books (a GameBoard diagnostic, the least Link pace of the final state among them).

## 6. How to run a world

1. Write a world file with its bodies declared on their Nodes, naming a universe file and `examples/events/engine_start.json`.
2. Lay its bodies: `PYTHONPATH=src python tools/pixel_mode.py --input <world>.json [--sense +1 -1 ...]` finds every body's fixed point in the rows its family reads (its sources the vacuum's share of its record at the written moment), rewrites the bodies' Nodes and counts (the weighted share the engine lays) and writes `<world>.mode.json` beside it; a sense, one per body in the world's order, lays the body rotating (its second level pair the record a quarter period on), two bodies or more in two passes; the search's memory is bounded by a digest per state.
3. Run it headless: `PYTHONPATH=src python tools/run_inputs.py --out runs/first --jobs 4 <world>.json`, one output file per world, one summary line printed per world.
4. Read the verdict first: `REFUSED` names the key, the value or the guard that refused; `LAWFUL` carries the lines and the books.
5. See it, a diagnostic: `PYTHONPATH=src python tools/look/record.py <world>.json --ticks N` is the host's reader, which steps the world by the engine's own step and writes every family's arrays per interval, the lines, the world's declared numbers and the books to `<world>.look.json`, labelled "GameBoard reading" and holding no number of its own.
6. `PYTHONPATH=src python tools/look/page.py <world>.look.json [--blind <world>.blind.json]` writes the one page that shows it: the board as cubes, one frame per interval, the counts as dots, the levels as the cubes' glow, a field as mist, the detectors' report as the bar chart with the dashed blind curve behind it (the one measurement), the graphs over the intervals beside it, every family's role from the file's row; every number on the page is the files' or the engine's, and only the detectors' report is drawn as a measurement.

## 7. How to add a family

A family is a row of the universe file, never a line of the engine: its `name`, its `pair` and, for a held family, `held` {`count`, `divisor`}. The rule derives its parts, its reads and the rows it sources; the engine steps it, counts it, holds it and reads it with the code it already has. A new act of the law starts from its line in ALGEBRA.md (CONTRIBUTING.md), is a pure function of arrays that calls Rule3 alone, and comes with its test.

## 8. The gates

`python tools/check.py` selects the changed files and their consumers and runs `ruff`, `ruff format --check`, `mypy` (strict) and `pytest` on them with the tests of `tools/every_pull_request.txt`; `--full` runs everything, and CI runs the suite in shards. `tests/test_rule3.py` holds the rule's arithmetic to `core/rule3.py`, every shift of a level across a Link to `core/ports.py`, every integer literal of the engine and the generator to the law's own, and no root imported or called anywhere in `src/` or `tools/`; `tests/test_integer_algebra.py` holds every module of the package to integers alone; `tests/test_the_node.py` checks the acts against Rule3 by hand, the interval on a closed cube (the count conserved, the 48 symmetries in every part, the back-in-time gate MATCH over twelve intervals), the tension as Rule3's own conservation of the current, light born by the write on the chain with the click at the end detector, the tension of a moving record and of a standing tail, and the sense booked by its line; `tests/test_documents.py` holds every heading of the documents in sentence case and no run of three capital words in their prose or in the code's docstrings, comments and strings; `tests/test_repository_language.py` holds every text file to ASCII letters; both run on every selection (`tools/every_pull_request.txt`).
