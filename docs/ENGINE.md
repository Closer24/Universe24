# The engine

This document describes the engine as the code holds it on `main`, beside [ALGEBRA.md](ALGEBRA.md) (the law) and [HIGHLIGHTS.md](HIGHLIGHTS.md) (the decisions in force). The engine is the law's implementation and nothing else (the owner's decision (c) of 2026-09-29 on #1495): a GameBoard of Nodes, every family's NodeState at every Node, and one interval of acts in the law's order, each a call of Rule3 (`src/event_universe/core/rule3.py`) on whole-board arrays, every neighbour read through a Port (`src/event_universe/core/ports.py`). The engine holds no number, no formula, no family's name and no flag: every physical value comes from the run's files. No act of the interval takes a square root and no act reads a Node to decide anything: the guard reads the whole initial state once at load, as squares.

## 1. The words

- **Node**: one place of the GameBoard. Its whole local information is its **NodeState**, the law's own numbers and nothing else (section 2).
- **Port** and **Link**: a Node has six Ports, +x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z; a Link is the pair of Ports between two neighbours. A level reaches a neighbour only through a Port (`arrival`). An axis is periodic (it wraps), closed (a wall, read as 0) or open (a wall read as 0 whose outer layer of `face_depth` Nodes is told to report); an axis of one layer folds, its two Ports returning the Node itself. An inner face is the same rule declared inside the board (`faces` in the world file, section 4): a plane of Nodes across an axis, beyond the board but for its gaps; a Node beyond the board is read as 0 through every Port and reads 0 through its own (`core/ports.py`, `Wrap.beyond`), so no level, current or source crosses its Links, nothing is laid or sourced there, and no body, message or detector may stand there (refused by name). A **receding face** is a face of an open or closed axis the world declares receding (`receding`, section 4) with the axis's largest size and the layers per growth, both the file's numbers: beyond it the GameBoard grows by that many layers of zeros whenever a level, a count or a sense other than 0 of any family stands on the layer before it, so no wave meets it (`src/event_universe/growth.py`; ALGEBRA.md, **The unbounded board**); with the axis at its largest size the run ends there, named, rather than reflecting; a face before the origin (the low side) grows by prepending, and every declared coordinate (a body's Nodes, a message's envelope, a detector's positions, an inner face) stays the file's by the offset of the layers grown before the origin, held once per axis (`GameBoard.offset`, read by `mask` and written into every `click` line's Node).
- **GameBoard**: the box of Nodes, `shape` [X, Y, Z] as declared, grown beyond its receding faces as the run goes (`GameBoard.shape` the shape now); in code `GameBoard` (`src/event_universe/game_board.py`).
- **Family**: one kind of physical value, a row of the universe file: its name, its pair [num, den] and what it holds (`held`: the content or the sign, at the divisor E_s). The rule derives the rest ([ALGEBRA.md, the families from the rule](ALGEBRA.md#a-familys-declaration), `src/event_universe/loader/derived.py`): a family that does not hold the content carries quanta (two level pairs, the real one and the second, the rotation sense; a count, a sense and their currents); a held family has its parts, the time part alone or with the three axis tensions (1 or 1 + 3 components, the parts Rule3 reads and no other), every part a record stepped by Rule3 at the row's own pair, with or without a gap; a family of quanta reads every holder of the content plainly and every other holder of the sign by its own q, and sources what it reads at the same weight; no family gives another anything.
- **Record**: a line of the books, never a level of its own: a family's level at a Node is one number. The holder of the sign is one record: its time part is its real level pair, the light's own record.
- **Body**: the initial state the world file declares and no ledger: its family, its Nodes with their counts. Its Nodes at a later interval are where its family's count stands about its declared Nodes, and its shell the Nodes among them with a Port to a count 0, derived when a report needs them (`reports.standing`) and never kept; its quanta that leave it stay its family.
- **Message**: a record the world file lays and no body (`messages`): a packet of a family of quanta along an axis, its wave number a fraction of pi per Link, its amplitude and its envelope declared, its levels the generator's (`tools/pixel_mode.py`, the message lay).
- **Detector**: a group of Nodes declared in the file to report (its `positions`; a detector of one Node a group of one; the Nodes of the body it names; the open faces' layer). Its **click**, a whole quantum's entry into the group through one of its boundary Ports, is the one measurement (the advisor's ruling, #1515 comment 5909238819): a hop between two of its Nodes is no click, and the rise of its summed count is a reading of its book and no click; a group may declare its count remainders at the start (`remainder`), the warm screen the file declares; every other number (a level, a count, the books) is a GameBoard reading, a diagnostic.
- **Interval**: one step of the whole GameBoard; `ticks` intervals make a run.

## 2. The NodeState

The engine is seven modules under 400 lines each: `node.py` (the NodeState and the Node's acts: the read, Rule3 on the levels, the lines, the well and the hold), `lay.py` (the lay of every count and sense at the first act), `flow.py` (the flows into a held row's tensions), `reports.py` (the detectors' clicks, a body's Nodes derived for a report, the books), `growth.py` (the receding face: the GameBoard grown by layers of zeros where the front reaches it, the layers taken off on the way back), `game_board.py` (the GameBoard that walks the interval forward and back) and `world_files.py` with `loader/` (the files), on `core/` (Rule3, the Ports, the working bound) and the folders of `features/`.

`src/event_universe/node.py`, `NodeState`, one per family, every array over the GameBoard in 64-bit integers:

| Part | Held by | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `levels` (`now`, `before`, `remainder`) | a family of quanta | its real level pair and Rule3's remainder; for the holder of the sign the time part itself (`parts[0] is levels`) |
| `second` (`now`, `before`, `remainder`) | a family of quanta | its second level pair, the rotation sense (0 on a real record), stepped by the same rule |
| `count`, `count_remainder` | a family of quanta | its count and the count's line's remainder, laid at the first act |
| `sense`, `sense_remainder` | a family of quanta | its sense (the booked Wronskian) and the sense's line's remainder, laid at the first act |
| `well_remainder`, `wronskian_remainder` | a family of quanta | the remainders of its well, (D_i + r) div T, and of its Wronskian's quanta, (W_i + r) div T |
| `parts` | a held family | each component's two levels and remainder (the time part; the three tensions xx, yy, zz where its rank has them), a row with a gap as a row without one |
| `carry` | a held family | the hold's carry at its time part |
| `flows` | a held family | per sourcing family, the carries of its three tensions' writes, at half their divisor from the first act |

## 3. The interval

Before the first interval every held family's time part stands at its sources' rest, the sources each body's form at its Nodes and each message's form over the whole GameBoard, the vacuum's share of its two level pairs laid at the count's wall (`lay.share` at the content 0, `lay.laid`) at the weight with which its family sources the held family by plain, and its Wronskian's quanta at the written moment, W div T, at the weight by sign (the holder of the sign's rest, sources of either sign): every row at the rest of its own line, with or without a gap (`features/start`, the division act iterated from nothing until the levels repeat, 6 den b = num S_6(b) + 3 den sigma at a fine unit from the file's width, the sources of either sign or both where the board has a sink or the pair a gap; the same iteration serves the generator), the remainder at the half wall of the rule the row steps by; the tensions start at 0. Then the guard, once: every family of quanta's read of the initial state is checked as squares, 0 < p_a and p^2 (den + num) <= 2 den Gamma^2 at every Node on the clock's square and the three Link paces (`node.guarded`, `features/signed_read`), refused by name outside; no act of the interval reads a pace to refuse. `GameBoard.step()` walks the acts in order, each one loop over the families (or the detectors) with no case for any of them (ALGEBRA.md #the-interval), after the receding faces are read (`growth.grow`): where a level, a count or a sense other than 0 of any family (every record's two levels, a held row's tensions among them, the count, the sense) stands on the layer before a receding face, the GameBoard grows by the face's `layers` layers of zeros on that side (`growth.resize`: every NodeState, the detectors' declared Nodes, the inner faces' Nodes, the shape, the offset of the layers before the origin and the laid totals), every grown Node at the NodeState the first act gives a Node with no level (every level, count and sense 0, the count's and the sense's remainders at the lay's origin W_c div 2, a held row's time part at the remainder the start gave the row, the flows' carries at their origin (E_s W_c) div 2, every other remainder and carry 0), so that the run is the run of the larger GameBoard bit for bit; with the axis at its largest size the run ends, `GameBoard.ended` naming the interval, the axis, the side and the size, no act is taken and a further step is refused; the growth copies every array once per `layers` intervals per face and keeps nothing else, one ledger line per growth (the interval, the axis, the side, the layers):

1. **The signed read and Rule3** (`GameBoard.stepped`, `node.signed_read`, `features/signed_read`, `node.step`, `node.rule_of`): for every family the content c = SUM over its reads of (weight x by x the read family's time part) and the axis contents t_a from the tensions, (weight x by x the aa part + 1) div 2, by sign the reader's q the sign of its own sense at the Node (`node.sense_sign`); no floor and no clamp. Every record of the family steps: a family of quanta's two level pairs by `coefficients(num, den, Gamma, c, t)` at its reads' paces (the holder of the sign's time part and its second pair the same), a holder of the content's parts by the plain rule of its own pair at the pace 1 and the wall 3 den, with or without a gap (the reads num, the self coefficient 0); every read is of the interval's start, the stepped records assigned after every family has read; a level beyond the amplitude bound A refuses the run by name. The well of every family of quanta is formed here from both pairs' forms, D_i = now^2 - next x before, (D_i + r) div T, and its Wronskian's quanta, W_i = re_now im_before - im_now re_before as the step left them, (W_i + r) div T (`node.form`, `node.wronskian`, `node.well`).
2. **The lay** at the first act (`GameBoard.lay`, `lay.lay`, `lay.lay_sense`): every family's count from the weighted share of its two level pairs at the paces of the read, e_i = 3 [w (now^2 + before^2) - S now before] div (2 SUM_a p_a^2) - num now S_6(before), W_c c + r = e_i + W_c div 2, W_c = 3 den T (`lay.share`, exact in integers of any size), and its sense from w W_i div (2 p^2) the same way (`lay.sense_share`); a held family's count starts at 0 where its record is 0; the quanta a body `holds` of other families added at its declared Nodes; each body's declared count checked within the rounding of the count laid at its Nodes, ((|c - laid| - 1) div 2)^2 <= c, refused by name beyond; the holds' tension carries set at half their divisor; every detector's declared remainders (`remainder`) laid at its Nodes, one value per Node, as the named family's count remainder, replacing the lay's half wall there, before the laid totals are booked (the books balance against them).
3. **The count's and the sense's lines** (`GameBoard.lines`, `node.count_line`, `node.sense_line`, `features/counts_line`): the six currents num (now_i before_j - before_i now_j) of both level pairs move each family's count, and the Wronskian's currents num (im_i re_j - re_i im_j) at the levels the step started from move its sense, no block and no clamp (a hole is conserved and filled); beside the count the line books the tension on each axis, T_aa(i) = num (G_aa(i) + G_aa(i - a)) div 2 with G_aa(i) = now_i (now_(i+2a) - now_i) - now_(i+a) (now_(i+a) - now_(i-a)), the neighbour's difference brought through the Port (`node.axis_differences`), read and never written.
4. **The clicks** (`GameBoard.report`, `reports.clicks`): every detector (a detector's declared Nodes, the Nodes of the body it names derived now, the open faces' layer), each one group, reports per family of quanta the whole quanta the count's line's currents carried into it this interval through its boundary Ports: at every Port of its Nodes leading in from a Node outside them, (r + F_p) div W_c where that is above 0, r the Node's remainder before the line, one division per Port in the order [+X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z], one `click` line per quantum with the Node it entered and the Port's axis and side; a hop between two of its Nodes is no click, and the rise of its summed count is a reading and no click; nothing is handed over.
5. **The hold** (`GameBoard.hold`, `node.held_step`, `flow.flow_hold`, `features/hold`): every held family has its time part gain (w x n_i + r) div E_s by the write's carried division (`features/write`), n_i the wells of the families that source it by plain and the Wronskian's quanta of those that source it by sign (light is born by this write: a breathing or moving rotating body writes a varying source at its Nodes and the row carries it away as a wave), and each tension aa gain (w x T_aa + r_aa) div (E_s W_c), one act per axis with the remainder carried, no count in the divisor and no branch (`flow.Flow`).

`GameBoard.step_inverse()` runs the acts back with Rule3's direction -1: a held family's hold back once every family that sources it is booked back, a family of quanta booked back (its lines back from the levels as the step left them, its records back, its wells and its Wronskian's quanta back) once its own hold is off, so the holder of the sign goes before the rows it sources, then every held row of the content stepped back, the records assigned after every read of the interval's start (`GameBoard.booked_back`); then the layers the same interval grew beyond a receding face are taken off (`growth.resize` with the direction -1), the grown Nodes having returned to the state they were laid in, bit for bit, so the GameBoard's shape returns with its state. Every interval returns every level, remainder, count, sense and carry bit for bit, since every level a family read at the interval's start is one the state after the interval still holds (a held row's `before`, the hold booking the same interval's well into the row after its step), and the lay is not undone; so the gate says MATCH over the whole run (ALGEBRA.md #what-is-open, item 21, decided). The back-in-time gate, `tools/back_in_time.py` (`PYTHONPATH=src python tools/back_in_time.py --intervals 40 <world>.json`), runs a world N intervals forward after its first act (fewer where the run ends at a receding face's largest size, the end named in the verdict) and as many back and compares every array of every family, at the shape of that interval, and the interval counter with the state at the same interval on the way forward: MATCH, or MISS naming the first interval, the array (a shape that differs among them) and the first Node that differ; `tests/test_the_node.py` runs it on the closed cube over twelve intervals, on the chain with and without light born over 400 and on the chain of light alone with both faces receding over its run to the largest size, `tests/test_pixel_mode.py` on the slit world over its run; on the two slits' worlds of `examples/events/two_slits/` it says MATCH over their 130 intervals, and on the bright world with its far face declared receding (the largest size 160, 8 layers per growth) MATCH over the 130 too, the screen's rises within the window 252 against 258 on the fixed board (the blind 273), the far face's returns through the screen gone. The readings: `books()` per family of quanta (`reports.book`) (the count's sum, the remainders' sum, SUM (W_c c + r) against its laid value, the clicks, the sense's sum and its total against its laid value, and the least Link pace of the final state, a diagnostic) and `contents()` per body (each family's count at the body's Nodes derived now).

## 5. The output

The observer handed to `GameBoard` receives one dictionary per line: `click` (`tick`, `family`, `detector`, `node` the Node the quantum entered, `axis` [axis, side] the Port it crossed, `count` the group's count after, `body` the body a detector names or None; the measurement, one line per quantum). `tools/run_inputs.py` writes one `<name>.output.json` per world with the verdict (`LAWFUL`, or `REFUSED` with the reason, the guard's at load among them), the intervals run, `ended` (None, or the lawful end at a receding face's largest size: the interval, the axis, the side and the size; the run stops there, LAWFUL, with the intervals run), the `click` lines (their Nodes at the file's coordinates whatever the GameBoard grew) and the books (a GameBoard diagnostic, the least Link pace of the final state among them). `tools/click_counts.py` reads the rises per reporter over a window of intervals from an output file against an expectation file (the detector or the detectors, a list, the family, the window, the axis across the detector, the pattern's range and the blind counts, every number the file's): a detector whose Nodes share one coordinate on that axis is one reporter placed at it (a group across the beam), and one whose Nodes spread along it reports per Node; the rises beside the blind counts, the totals, the local maxima and minima and the visibility as integers, DETECTOR. `tools/train_clicks.py` reads a train of single quanta against its expectation file (the family, the axes across and along the beam, the pace [Links, intervals], per quantum the interval its peak reaches the slab's first Node and its window for reference, the halves of the screen as ranges on the axis, and the blind row): the groups are the world's detectors whose Nodes share one coordinate on the axis across, a click line belongs to the quantum whose peak passes its Node nearest in time (a rise comes while the packet's mass climbs at the Node, before the peak passes it), and the reading gives per quantum the groups that rose, its rises and the halves that rose, then the mean rises per quantum, the both-halves, neither and exactly-one rates and the rises per group beside the blind row, DETECTOR.

## 6. How to run a world

1. Write a world file with its bodies declared on their Nodes and its messages by their keys, naming a universe file and `examples/events/engine_start.json`; `examples/events/two_slits/` holds the two slits' worlds (the wall an inner face, the emitter a message, the screen a detector of 48 Nodes) with their blind expectation; `examples/events/two_slits_train/` holds the train world (the advisor, #1515 comment 5907540901): the two slits' geometry shifted along x by the train's length on a board of 3,664 x 48 x 1, a train of 300 single-quantum packets 12 Links apart behind the wall, the screen a slab 12 Nodes thick, one detector group per column of y = 4 to 43 across the beam, with its count remainders declared per Node by the advisor's blind rule, (n + 1/2) / N of W_c, written by `examples/events/two_slits_train/build_world.py` from its design file `design.json` (every number of the world: the geometry, the train, the slab, the columns, the far face's distance beyond the slab, the remainders' rule (even per Node with a fraction's offset, or golden per group or per Node), the pace) and the loader's W_c, its cold twin without the remainders (the lay's half wall everywhere), and their expectation file. `examples/events/two_slits_wholes/` holds the train under the whole line (the advisor, #1515 comment 5910272948 B): one packet carrying N wholes and no train, on a flat board of 400 x 48 x 1, its envelope a flat top of one Node with raised-cosine edges of the half-widths 47 along x and 33 across y (the advisor's Gaussian of sigma 20 and 14 matched at the half maximum, the mass within one percent), its amplitude per N the design's (measured by laying: the count laid is not the amplitude's square at this size), the wall an inner face with two gaps of five Nodes (d = 16), the screen a slab 12 deep 32 past the wall with one detector per four rows (twelve groups) and no declared remainders (under the whole line the click is the whole's entry), the far face receding, 400 intervals, written by `build_world.py` from `design.json` as one world per N (3,000 and 300) with its blind expectation file (the pattern's shares per group, the tolerances, one entry per whole, the deviation's two lines), not yet run; `tools/click_counts.py` reads it, each named whole's first entry alone with the wholes summed up (none twice, none in two groups) and the summed absolute deviation of the groups' entries from the blind row's shares over the total. The present train world stands beside it unchanged. `examples/events/bell/` holds Bell's first world under the whole line (the advisor, #1515 comment 5910272948 C, ALGEBRA.md row (h)) the same way (`design.json`, `build_world.py`, which also writes its expectation file): the same flat board with the source at its centre column, one packet per side carrying 3,000 wholes at mirror quantiles, narrow across the beam as the two gaps' aperture, centred 60 Links on either side of the source and moving apart at one angle (theta = 0; the angle spread is the second world, later, through the message key `transverse`), the two-gap walls 120 from the source and the screens 152, one detector per screen over every row with no declared remainders, both far faces receding, 400 intervals; the pair's mirror lay is the whole line's round's, and `--mirror` writes its declaration (the key `mirror` on each message naming the other), which the loader refuses until that round names it; not yet run. `tools/bell_clicks.py` reads its run by the comb: a landing is a click line on a screen naming its whole, a pair the left packet's whole n with the right packet's whole n, each landing's outcome at a setting +1 where cos(Phi(y) - a) > 0 and -1 under (Phi(y) the fringe phase of its row, a the setting's position on the screen in Links), E(a, b) the mean product over the pairs landing on both screens, S at the CHSH settings, E as a curve in a + b over every position, the coincidence map over Phi_A + Phi_B with its ridge's visibility, each screen's single-side pattern with its maxima, and the GameBoard check (two landings per pair, mirror rows), beside the blind rows, DETECTOR.
2. Lay its bodies and messages: `PYTHONPATH=src python tools/pixel_mode.py --input <world>.json [--sense +1 -1 ...]` finds every body's fixed point in the rows its family reads (its sources the vacuum's share of its record at the written moment), rewrites the bodies' Nodes and counts (the weighted share the engine lays), lays every message as the wave under its envelope with its level one interval earlier (ALGEBRA.md #the-generator (h)) and writes `<world>.mode.json` beside it; a sense, one per body in the world's order, lays the body rotating (its second level pair the record a quarter period on), two bodies or more in two passes; the search's memory is bounded by a digest per state.
3. Run it headless: `PYTHONPATH=src python tools/run_inputs.py --out runs/first --jobs 4 <world>.json`, one output file per world, one summary line printed per world.
4. Read the verdict first: `REFUSED` names the key, the value or the guard that refused; `LAWFUL` carries the lines and the books. A look runs only after `tools/back_in_time.py` says MATCH on its own world over its intervals; `tools/click_counts.py` then reads the detector's rises against the expectation file, and `tools/train_clicks.py` a train's per quantum.

## 7. How to add a family

A family is a row of the universe file, never a line of the engine: its `name`, its `pair` and, for a held family, `held` {`count`, `divisor`}. The rule derives its parts, its reads and the rows it sources; the engine steps it, counts it, holds it and reads it with the code it already has. A new act of the law starts from its line in ALGEBRA.md (CONTRIBUTING.md), is a pure function of arrays that calls Rule3 alone, and comes with its test.
