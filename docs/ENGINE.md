# The engine

This document describes the engine as the code holds it on `main`, beside [ALGEBRA.md](ALGEBRA.md) (the law) and [HIGHLIGHTS.md](HIGHLIGHTS.md) (the decisions in force). The engine is the law's implementation and nothing else (the owner's decision (c) of 2026-09-29 on #1495): a GameBoard of Nodes, every family's NodeState at every Node, and one interval of acts in the law's order, each a call of Rule3 (`src/event_universe/core/rule3.py`; Rule3, named for the three directions of the vector it works with, the Link's paces: one read coefficient per Port, R_ij = 2 num p_i^2 Q_ij on the Link to that neighbour, the product of the Node's pace squared, p_i = p_0^2 / Gamma from the Node's own content, the composed clock p_0 = Gamma (1 - 1 / Gamma)^c twice (`src/event_universe/core/paces.py`, the law's two functions of the content), and the Link's factor Q_ij, the Link's tension booked squared as one integer per Link in the unit G^2 of the run's Link unit G, read the same from both ends (ALGEBRA.md #the-paces, The clock is the Node's, the tension is the Link's); the six Ports the three directions twice; three levels in time) on whole-board arrays, every neighbour read through a Port (`src/event_universe/core/ports.py`). The engine holds no number, no formula, no family's name and no flag: every physical value comes from the run's files. No act of the interval takes a square root and no act reads a Node to decide anything: the guard reads the whole initial state once at load, as squares. Beside Rule3's acts the loop takes the instrument's, from outside the Node as the lay and the receding face are: where the world declares the `instrument` the detectors draw inside the run, window after window, and write their click on the GameBoard at one Node (`src/event_universe/credit.py`, `features/click`; HIGHLIGHTS.md, the owner's decision of 2026-10-02, after a click the paths are cancelled on the GameBoard, keeping Rule3); the Node knows nothing of it, and a world without the key runs as before bit for bit. The repository holds the engine, the tools that lay, run, gate and read a world, and the worlds that gate the engine, the two slits and Bell, with the GHZ gate beside Bell's, the same reader over three sides (HIGHLIGHTS.md **One experiment and one gate**); sections 4 to 6 are the input and the output explained with the two slits and Bell as the examples, and section 9 is the recipe for a new experiment as the shipped folders have it.

## 1. The words

- **Node**: one place of the GameBoard. Its whole local information is its **NodeState**, the law's own numbers and nothing else (section 2).
- **Port** and **Link**: a Node has six Ports, +x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z; a Link is the pair of Ports between two neighbours. A level reaches a neighbour only through a Port (`arrival`). An axis is periodic (it wraps), closed (a wall, read as 0) or open (a wall read as 0 whose outer layer of `face_depth` Nodes is told to report); an axis of one layer folds, its two Ports returning the Node itself. An inner face is the same rule declared inside the board (`faces` in the world file, section 4): a plane of Nodes across an axis, beyond the board but for its gaps; a Node beyond the board is read as 0 through every Port and reads 0 through its own (`core/ports.py`, `Wrap.beyond`), so no level, current or source crosses its Links, nothing is laid or sourced there, and no body, message or detector may stand there (refused by name). A **receding face** is a face of an open or closed axis the world declares receding (`receding`, section 4) with the axis's largest size and the layers per growth, both the file's numbers: beyond it the GameBoard grows by that many layers of zeros whenever a level other than 0 of any family stands on the layer before it, so no wave meets it (`src/event_universe/growth.py`; ALGEBRA.md, **The unbounded board**); with the axis at its largest size the run ends there, named, rather than reflecting; a face before the origin (the low side) grows by prepending, and every declared coordinate (a body's Nodes, a message's envelope, a detector's positions, an inner face) stays the file's by the offset of the layers grown before the origin, held once per axis (`GameBoard.offset`, read by `mask`).
- **GameBoard**: the box of Nodes, `shape` [X, Y, Z] as declared, grown beyond its receding faces as the run goes (`GameBoard.shape` the shape now); in code `GameBoard` (`src/event_universe/game_board.py`).

## 9. How to build an experiment

The recipe a reader with the repository alone follows to declare a world, write its blind first, run it and read it, derived from the shipped folders of section 4 (the newest, `examples/events/nuclide/`, is the pattern; `examples/events/two_slits/`, `examples/events/bell/`, `examples/events/ghz/`, `examples/events/like_or_unlike/`, `examples/events/matter_alone/` and `examples/events/charge/` follow it), from the loader (`src/event_universe/loader/`, `src/event_universe/world_files.py`), the reports (`src/event_universe/reports.py`) and the tools (`tools/`); every key and command below is the code's on `main`. An experiment is one folder under `examples/events/` holding its design (`design.json`), its universe file, its world file with the generator's mode file beside it, its builder (`build_world.py`) and its blind (`expectation.json`); the engine holds no number, so every number of the experiment stands in these files and in nothing else, and the folder is the whole of it.

1. **The universe file**, one JSON object of exactly two keys, `integers` and `families` (`loader/universe.py`, `universe_of`; any other key is refused by name, as everywhere in the files). `integers` holds exactly four, each an integer from 1: `node_clock` (Gamma), `quantum_action` (T), `width` (the bits of the run's integers: at or under 63 every array is the hardware's 64-bit integers, above it Python's, exact and about a hundred times slower) and `link_unit` (G, a power of two, refused otherwise); the shipped files declare 6000, 32768, 63 and 16, and a new universe file copies them (`examples/events/rule.json` is the rule's own). `families` is a list of one row or more, each `name` (its own; a name twice is refused) and `pair` [num, den] with den from 1 and num from -den through den, |num| = den admitted only as num = den, the massless pair (a massive pair has den above |num|: [6000, 6000] is light's, [4000, 6000] matter's, [4004, 6000] the nuclide's, [2400, 2401] the binding holder's, and [-6000, 6000] is refused by name), and then exactly one of two keys: `dimension`, for a family of quanta, 1 (one real line), 2 (a plane, re and im, whose body the generator lays with a sense, item 2) or the shape [parts, dimension] (that many records of that dimension laid as one event and never summed at a Node: the pair family [2, 1], the GHZ family [4, 1], the nuclide [2, 2]; a shape of three numbers is refused); or `held`, for a held row, which declares no dimension: `sources`, exactly one of ["form"] (a holder of the content), ["form", "tensions"] (the massless row of the content with its three axis lines, gravity's) or ["wronskian"] (the holder of the sign, whose own record is light), `level_weight` from 1 (the quanta of form that write one level of the row), optionally `rest` (the vacuum content, an integer from 0 within the width, admitted on the massless row sourced by the form and refused on the holder of the sign and on a gapped row) and optionally `act`, `pace` (the plain read into the readers' paces, the act of a row without the key) or `rotation` (the turn of the two-part record, admitted on a holder sourced by the Wronskian alone). Who reads whom is derived and not declared today (`loader/derived.py`, `family_rules`; ALGEBRA.md #a-familys-declaration): every family reads every held row sourced by the form at the weight 1, a holder of the content its own level among them; a family of dimension 2 reads every held row sourced by the Wronskian at the weight 1; a holder of the sign reads none of the sign; and each family sources what it reads at the same weight, the hold's reciprocity (the keys of the engine round: see section 4). A universe file is read only through a world that names it under `universe`, so its first check is the run of item 4; `examples/events/nuclide/deuteron.json` is the shortest shipped one, two rows and no holder of the content.
