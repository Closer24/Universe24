# The engine

This document describes the engine as the code holds it on `main`, beside [ALGEBRA.md](ALGEBRA.md) (the law) and [HIGHLIGHTS.md](HIGHLIGHTS.md) (the decisions in force). The engine is the law's implementation and nothing else (the owner's decision (c) of 2026-09-29 on #1495): a GameBoard of Nodes, every family's NodeState at every Node, and one interval of acts in the law's order, each a call of Rule3 (`src/event_universe/core/rule3.py`) on whole-board arrays, every neighbour read through a Port (`src/event_universe/core/ports.py`). The engine holds no number, no formula, no family's name and no flag: every physical value comes from the run's files.

## 1. The words

- **Node**: one place of the GameBoard. Its whole local information is its **NodeState**, the law's own numbers and nothing else (section 2).
- **Port** and **Link**: a Node has six Ports, +x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z; a Link is the pair of Ports between two neighbours. A level reaches a neighbour only through a Port (`arrival`). An axis is periodic (it wraps), closed (a wall, read as 0) or open (a wall read as 0 whose outer layer of `face_depth` Nodes is told to report); an axis of one layer folds, its two Ports returning the Node itself.
- **GameBoard**: the box of Nodes, `shape` [X, Y, Z]; in code `GameBoard` (`src/event_universe/game_board.py`).
- **Family**: one kind of physical value, a row of the universe file: its name, its pair [num, den] and what it holds (`held`: the content or the sign, at the divisor E_s). The rule derives the rest ([ALGEBRA.md, the families from the rule](ALGEBRA.md#a-familys-declaration), `src/event_universe/loader/derived.py`): a family that does not hold the content carries quanta (two level pairs, the real one and the second, the rotation sense; a count, a sense and their currents); a held family has its parts, 1, 1 + 3 or 1 + 3 + 6 components, and is stepped where its pair has no gap (num = den) and laid as the well each interval where it has one (`FamilyRule.gap`); a family of quanta reads every holder of the content plainly and every holder of the sign of a higher rank by its own q, and sources what it reads at the same weight; a family gives into the one holder of the sign it reads.
- **Record**: a line of the books, never a level of its own: a family's level at a Node is one number.
- **Body**: a ledger and no state of the law: its family, its Nodes (where its family's count stands, followed each interval within one Link), its declared count and its clock (its family's level now summed over its Nodes). A crossing of the clock from at most 0 to above 0 is the body's **click**, a reading that fires nothing; a body gives at the outer Ports its count's line crosses (section 3).
- **Detector**: a Node told to report. Its **report** of a quantum the count's line brought it is the one measurement; every other number (a level, a count, the books) is a GameBoard reading, a diagnostic.
- **Interval**: one step of the whole GameBoard; `ticks` intervals make a run.

## 2. The NodeState

The engine is six modules under 400 lines each: `node.py` (the NodeState and the Node's acts: the read, Rule3 on the levels, the lay, the lines, the well and the hold), `flow.py` (the flows into a held row's vector and tensor parts), `bodies.py` (the bodies' ledgers, their Nodes following their counts, the givings and the scaled write), `reports.py` (the detectors, the output lines and the books), `game_board.py` (the GameBoard that walks the interval forward and back) and `world_files.py` with `loader/` (the files), on `core/` (Rule3, the Ports, the working bound) and the folders of `features/`.


`src/event_universe/node.py`, `NodeState`, one per family, every array over the GameBoard in 64-bit integers:

| Part | Held by | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `levels` (`now`, `before`, `remainder`) | a family of quanta | its real level pair and Rule3's remainder |
| `second` (`now`, `before`, `remainder`) | a family of quanta | its second level pair, the rotation sense (0 on a real record), stepped by the same rule |
| `count`, `count_remainder` | a family of quanta | its count and the count's line's remainder, laid at the first act |
| `sense`, `sense_remainder` | a family of quanta | its sense (the booked Wronskian) and the sense's line's remainder, laid at the first act |
| `well_remainder`, `wronskian_remainder` | a family of quanta | the remainders of its well, (D_i + r) div T, and of its Wronskian's quanta, (W_i + r) div T |
| `parts` | a held family | each component's two levels and remainder (the time part, the vector's three, the tensor's six) |
| `carry` | a held family | the hold's carry at its time part |
| `flows` | a held family | per sourcing family, the carries of its vector and tensor writes, at half their divisors from the first act |

## 3. The interval

Before the first interval every held family's time part stands at its sources' rest, the sources each body's form, the vacuum's share of its two level pairs laid at the count's wall (`node.share` at the content 0, `node.laid`) at its Nodes, at the weight with which its family sources the held family: a row without a gap at the rest of its line (`features/start`, the division act iterated from nothing until the levels repeat, 6 den b = num S_6(b) + 3 den sigma at a fine unit from the file's width; the same iteration serves the generator), a row with a gap laid, the sources through its divisor with the remainder's origin at half of it. `GameBoard.step()` walks the acts in order, each one loop over the families (or the bodies and the detectors) with no case for any of them (ALGEBRA.md #the-interval):

1. **The signed read and Rule3** (`GameBoard.stepped`, `node.signed_read`, `features/signed_read`, `node.step`): for every family of quanta the content c = SUM over its reads of (weight x by x the read family's time part, for a held row with a gap the well it laid) and the axis contents t_a from the tensor's parts, (weight x by x the aa part + 1) div 2, by sign the reader's q the sign of its own sense at the Node (`node.sense_sign`); no floor and no clamp; the guard 0 < p <= P on p_0 = isqrt((Gamma - c)^2 + c^2) and on every Link's pace Gamma - 2 c - t_a, P = isqrt(2 den Gamma^2 div (den + num)), ends the run by name. Both level pairs step by `coefficients(num, den, Gamma, c, t)`, forward `rule3` with the direction +1; a level beyond the amplitude bound A refuses the run by name. The well of every family of quanta is formed here from both pairs' forms, D_i = now^2 - next x before, (D_i + r) div T, and its Wronskian's quanta, W_i = re_now im_before - im_now re_before as the step left them, (W_i + r) div T (`node.form`, `node.wronskian`, `node.well`).
2. **The lay** at the first act (`GameBoard.lay`, `node.lay`, `node.lay_sense`): every family's count from the weighted share of its two level pairs at the paces of the read, e_i = 3 [w (now^2 + before^2) - S now before] div (2 SUM_a p_a^2) - num now S_6(before), W_c c + r = e_i + W_c div 2, W_c = 3 den T (`node.share`, exact in integers of any size), and its sense from w W_i div (2 p^2) the same way (`node.sense_share`); the quanta a body `holds` of other families added at its Nodes; each body's declared count checked within 2 isqrt(c) + 1 of the count laid at its Nodes, refused by name beyond; the holds' vector and tensor carries set at half their divisors.
3. **The count's and the sense's lines** (`GameBoard.lines`, `node.count_line`, `node.sense_line`, `features/counts_line`): the six currents num (now_i before_j - before_i now_j) of both level pairs move each family's count, and the Wronskian's currents num (im_i re_j - re_i im_j) at the levels the step started from move its sense, no block and no clamp (a hole is conserved and filled); the writes carry the current through every Port (`through`) and the travel per axis, the current through the Node along +a (`travel`).
4. **The givings** (`GameBoard.give`, `bodies.crossings`, `bodies.converted`): at every outer Port of a body's Nodes (a Port to a Node outside them), -((r + F_p) div W_c) quanta where that is above 0, r the Node's remainder before the line and F_p the Port's current, one division per Port; each converts at the Node it enters: -1 of the body's family and +1 of the family it gives there, and the given family's two levels there gain the body's family's two levels there scaled so that the form the write adds lays exactly one count at the given family's paces (`bodies.one_quantum`: the scale bracketed by halving and doubling, then bisected; refused by name where no scale does); one `giving` line per quantum.
5. **The bodies and the reports** (`bodies.following`, `GameBoard.report`, `reports.gathers`): the bodies' Nodes follow their counts, and every detector (a detector's Nodes, the Nodes of the body it names, the open faces' layer) reports per family the rise of the family's count summed over its Nodes this interval (the line and the givings), the net inflow across its boundary, one `gather` line per unit of rise; nothing is handed over.
6. **The hold** (`GameBoard.hold`, `node.held_step`, `flow.flow_hold`, `features/hold`): a held family with a gap is not stepped: its time part is laid, (w x n_i + r) div E_s with its own remainder, the level before kept beside it (backward the level before returns, so its inverse is exact over one interval and over more where its level stands still); every held family without a gap steps its parts by the plain rule (the pace 1, the wall 3 den), and its time part gains (w x n_i + r) div E_s by the write's carried division (`features/write`), n_i the wells of the families that source it by plain and the Wronskian's quanta of those that source it by sign; its vector part a gains (w x j_a + r_a) div (E_s W_c) and its tensor part ab (w x j_a j_b + r_ab) div (E_s W_c^2 c_i) in one act where c_i is above 0 (a count 0 skips the act and keeps r_ab), j_a the travel of the sourcing family's count's line (by sign, its sense's line), c_i its count after the line (`flow.Flow`).
7. **The clocks** (`GameBoard.clock`, `reports.clock_lines`): each body's clock, a `click` line where it crosses and a `block` line every interval, readings that fire nothing.

`GameBoard.step_inverse()` runs the acts back in reverse order with Rule3's direction -1: the count's and the sense's lines back first (their currents read from the levels as the step left them, so the reads find the interval's start), the reads and both level pairs back with the wells and the Wronskian's quanta, the holds back with the same flows. An interval with no giving returns every level, remainder, count, sense and carry bit for bit, unless a count fell below a tensor carry's divisor (the carry kept as it is writes its whole divisors at once, and the act back does not return them); the givings and the bodies' Nodes are not taken back, and the lay is not undone. The readings: `books()` per family of quanta (`reports.book`) (the count's sum, the remainders' sum, SUM (W_c c + r) against its laid value moved by the givings alone, the givings, the reports, the sense's sum and its total against its laid value) and `contents()` per body (each family's count at its Nodes).

## 4. The loader and the files

`src/event_universe/world_files.py` (`load_world`) reads the world file, the universe and engine start files it names by their repository paths and the generator's mode file beside it, and `src/event_universe/loader/world.py` (`parse_world`) checks every key and refuses every other key as unknown by name; no default is written.

| File | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| world | `shape` [X, Y, Z]; `boundary` {`x`, `y`, `z`: `open`, `periodic` or `closed`}; `face_depth` (from 1, required with an open axis); `ticks`; `universe` and `engine` (repository paths); `measured`, a list of bodies, each `family` (a family of quanta), `nodes` [{`node` [x, y, z], `count` from 1}] (no Node shared) and optionally `holds` {family name: count}, the quanta of other families of quanta laid over its Nodes in proportion to its counts by the carried division; `detectors`, each `name` (not `face`) with `positions` (its Nodes) or `block` (the number of a body, whose Nodes report each interval) |
| universe | `integers`: `node_clock` (Gamma), `quantum_action` (T) and `width` (the integers' bits, at most the host's); `families`: each `name`, `pair` [num, den] (den from 1, |num| below den or num = den) and optionally `held` {`count`: `content` or `sign`, `divisor` from 1} |
| engine start | `mode` |
| mode (`<world>.mode.json`, `tools/pixel_mode.py`) | `world_digest` (the world's digest, `input_digest`) and `bodies`, one per body: `family`, `pair` and `moving` {`now`, `before`, and for a body laid with a sense `im_now`, `im_before`, both or neither} (the level pairs, one integer per Node in x-major order; a second pair on real levels at 0 is refused by name), with the generator's readings `profile`, `count`, `seed`, `carried`, `period`, `amplitude`, `clock` |

The amplitude bound A is derived from the file's width, never written (`derived.amplitude_bound`): the largest level at which Rule3's total 6 A R + A |S| + w (A + 1) at the levels 0, Gamma div 2 and Gamma - 1 of every pair and the count's line's total 6 x 2 x 2 |num| A^2 + W_c (most + 2) of every family of quanta (two products on each of two level pairs) stay inside the width. The shipped files are `examples/events/rule.json` (the rule's own universe, the universe the tests run on: Gamma 6,000, T 32,768, every divisor 1) and `examples/events/engine_start.json`; `examples/events/universe.json`, `planck.json` (Gamma 24) and `planck_6000.json` (Gamma 6,000) are not the law's and stay this round, read by no test, until the owner decides on the numbers.

## 5. The output

The observer handed to `GameBoard` receives one dictionary per line: `click` (`tick`, `measured`, `family`, `node` the body's lower corner; a reading), `giving` (`tick`, `measured`, `family` the given one, `node` the shell Node it gave from, `axis` [axis, side] the Port the quantum crossed, the Node beyond it the one it converted at, `count` the body's count after), `gather` (`tick`, `family`, `detector`, `count` the detector's count after, `taker` the body a detector names or None; the measurement) and `block` (`tick`, `measured`, `corner`, `sum` the clock's total; a reading). `tools/run_inputs.py` writes one `<name>.output.json` per world with the verdict (`LAWFUL`, or `REFUSED` with the reason), the intervals run, the `click`, `giving` and `gather` lines and the books.

## 6. How to run a world

1. Write a world file with its bodies declared on their Nodes, naming a universe file and `examples/events/engine_start.json`.
2. Lay its bodies: `PYTHONPATH=src python tools/pixel_mode.py --input <world>.json [--sense +1 -1 ...]` finds every body's fixed point in the rows its family reads (its sources the vacuum's share of its record at the written moment), rewrites the bodies' Nodes and counts (the weighted share the engine lays) and writes `<world>.mode.json` beside it; a sense, one per body in the world's order, lays the body rotating (its second level pair the record a quarter period on), two bodies or more in two passes.
3. Run it headless: `PYTHONPATH=src python tools/run_inputs.py --out runs/first --jobs 4 <world>.json`, one output file per world, one summary line printed per world.
4. Read the verdict first: `REFUSED` names the key, the value or the guard that refused; `LAWFUL` carries the lines and the books.

## 7. How to add a family

A family is a row of the universe file, never a line of the engine: its `name`, its `pair` and, for a held family, `held` {`count`, `divisor`}. The rule derives its parts, its reads, the rows it sources and the family it gives; the engine steps it, counts it, holds it and reads it with the code it already has. A new act of the law starts from its line in ALGEBRA.md (CONTRIBUTING.md), is a pure function of arrays that calls Rule3 alone, and comes with its test.

## 8. The gates

`python tools/check.py` selects the changed files and their consumers and runs `ruff`, `ruff format --check`, `mypy` (strict) and `pytest` on them with the tests of `tools/every_pull_request.txt`; `--full` runs everything, and CI runs the suite in shards. `tests/test_rule3.py` holds the rule's arithmetic to `core/rule3.py` and every shift of a level across a Link to `core/ports.py`; `tests/test_integer_algebra.py` holds every module of the package to integers alone; `tests/test_the_node.py` checks the acts against Rule3 by hand, the interval on a closed cube (the count conserved, the 48 symmetries with the givings, the inverse bit for bit over two intervals with no giving and no fallen count), the tensor's one act by hand with its dump, the giving at the Ports the line crossed on the chain with the light band reading the well, the vector part of a moving record and the sense booked by its line; `tests/test_documents.py` holds every heading of the documents in sentence case and no run of three capital words in their prose or in the code's docstrings, comments and strings; `tests/test_repository_language.py` holds every text file to ASCII letters; both run on every selection (`tools/every_pull_request.txt`).
