# The engine

This document describes the engine as the code holds it on `main`, beside [ALGEBRA.md](ALGEBRA.md) (the law) and [HIGHLIGHTS.md](HIGHLIGHTS.md) (the decisions in force). The engine is the law's implementation and nothing else (the owner's decision (c) of 2026-09-29 on #1495): a GameBoard of Nodes, every family's NodeState at every Node, and one interval of acts in the law's order, each a call of Rule3 (`src/event_universe/core/rule3.py`; Rule3, named for the three directions of the vector it works with, the Link's paces: one read coefficient per direction, serving its two Ports; the six Ports are the three directions twice; three levels in time) on whole-board arrays, every neighbour read through a Port (`src/event_universe/core/ports.py`). The engine holds no number, no formula, no family's name and no flag: every physical value comes from the run's files. No act of the interval takes a square root and no act reads a Node to decide anything: the guard reads the whole initial state once at load, as squares. The repository holds the engine, the tools that lay, run, gate and read a world, and the worlds that gate the engine, the two slits and Bell, with the GHZ gate beside Bell's, the same reader over three sides (HIGHLIGHTS.md **One experiment and one gate**); sections 4 to 6 are the input and the output explained with the two slits and Bell as the examples.

## 1. The words

- **Node**: one place of the GameBoard. Its whole local information is its **NodeState**, the law's own numbers and nothing else (section 2).
- **Port** and **Link**: a Node has six Ports, +x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z; a Link is the pair of Ports between two neighbours. A level reaches a neighbour only through a Port (`arrival`). An axis is periodic (it wraps), closed (a wall, read as 0) or open (a wall read as 0 whose outer layer of `face_depth` Nodes is told to report); an axis of one layer folds, its two Ports returning the Node itself. An inner face is the same rule declared inside the board (`faces` in the world file, section 4): a plane of Nodes across an axis, beyond the board but for its gaps; a Node beyond the board is read as 0 through every Port and reads 0 through its own (`core/ports.py`, `Wrap.beyond`), so no level, current or source crosses its Links, nothing is laid or sourced there, and no body, message or detector may stand there (refused by name). A **receding face** is a face of an open or closed axis the world declares receding (`receding`, section 4) with the axis's largest size and the layers per growth, both the file's numbers: beyond it the GameBoard grows by that many layers of zeros whenever a level other than 0 of any family stands on the layer before it, so no wave meets it (`src/event_universe/growth.py`; ALGEBRA.md, **The unbounded board**); with the axis at its largest size the run ends there, named, rather than reflecting; a face before the origin (the low side) grows by prepending, and every declared coordinate (a body's Nodes, a message's envelope, a detector's positions, an inner face) stays the file's by the offset of the layers grown before the origin, held once per axis (`GameBoard.offset`, read by `mask`).
- **GameBoard**: the box of Nodes, `shape` [X, Y, Z] as declared, grown beyond its receding faces as the run goes (`GameBoard.shape` the shape now); in code `GameBoard` (`src/event_universe/game_board.py`).
- **Family**: one kind of physical value, a row of the universe file: its name, its pair [num, den] and either its dimension (`dimension`, a family of quanta: 1 one real line, 2 a plane of two lines, re and im, charged matter, or its shape [parts, dimension], that many records of that dimension laid as one event and never summed at a Node: the pair family [2, 1], two real lines of light's pair, ALGEBRA.md #the-click-is-the-meeting) or, where it is held, `held` {`sources`: what sources it, `["form"]`, `["form", "tensions"]` or `["wronskian"]`, one real line per source; `level_weight`: the row's level weight E_s, the number of quanta of form that write one level of the row (gravity's the level weight LW of the law, ALGEBRA.md **The two rows**); on the massless row holding the content optionally `rest`, the vacuum content c_vac, the level at which it rests everywhere, up to every face and beyond it, 0 by the owner's decision of 2026-09-30, 23:15, ALGEBRA.md #what-is-open, item 22; on the holder of the sign optionally `act`, how the row acts on its readers: `pace`, the plain read of its level into their paces (the act of a row without the key, every holder of the content's), or `rotation`, the turn of every two-part reader's record by the angle theta = its level over Gamma, under which the row carries three odd axis lines beside its time line (section 3; ALGEBRA.md **The sign holder rotates the two-part record**, the engine's declared act)}. The rule derives everything else from the pair and the shape with no name ([ALGEBRA.md, every family has a dimension](ALGEBRA.md#a-familys-declaration), `src/event_universe/loader/derived.py`): a held row sourced by the form holds the content and steps at the pace 1 (its lines the time line alone or with the three axis lines, the parts Rule3 reads and no other); a held row sourced by the Wronskian holds the sign and carries quanta, its time line its record, light, alone under the plain read and with the three odd axis lines x, y, z under the rotation; every other family carries quanta (its count is its record's share and its currents the detectors' reading, readings of the record); every family of quanta reads every holder of the content plainly, a family of dimension 2 reads every holder of the sign, plainly into its pace or, where the holder declares the rotation, as the turn of its two lines by the holder's time level at the Node and by the odd line of each Link it reads through, light reads none (a row never reads its own level), and each sources what it reads at the same weight, by the hold's reciprocity (the form and the tensions for a holder of the content, the Wronskian and, under the rotation, the momentum density for the holder of the sign); a second level pair laid on a family of dimension 1 is refused by name; a message or a body is laid on every part of its family alike (the symmetric lay, `GameBoard.lay`); no family gives another anything.
- **Record**: a line of the books, never a level of its own: a family's level at a Node is one number. The holder of the sign is one record of dimension 1: its time part is its real level pair, the light's own record; its odd axis lines under the rotation are the phase on the Links and no record.
- **Body**: the initial state the world file declares and no ledger: its family, its Nodes with their counts, the counts checked at the start against its record's share in quanta at those Nodes (the gate, section 3). Its Nodes at a later interval are where its family's share stands in quanta about its declared Nodes, derived when a report needs them (`reports.standing`) and never kept; its quanta that leave it stay its family.
- **Message**: a record the world file lays and no body (`messages`): a packet of a family of quanta along an axis, its wave number a fraction of pi per Link, its amplitude and its envelope declared, its levels the generator's (`tools/pixel_mode.py`, the message lay); or the same lay on a holder of the content, a kick: the row's own travelling events added to its rest after the start, no count and no source (`tests/test_the_node.py`, the vacuum content's test).
- **Detector**: a region of Nodes declared in the file to report (its `positions`, one connected region of two or more Nodes, at least half the wavelength of its family's messages across the beam, q / p Nodes for the wave [p, q], the loader's gate `loader.world.regions_of_the_law`, the depth along the beam not gated; the Nodes of the body it names; the open faces' layer; its depth along the beam the instrument's declaration, the owner's word of 2026-10-01, 09:00), a declared instrument (ALGEBRA.md #the-count-is-the-records-share, the click is the detector's report; the owner's words of 2026-09-30, no click names a Node), optionally with its `basis`, the instrument's setting (p, q), the coefficients of its credit, and its `pattern`, one integer pair [alpha_k, beta_k] per part of the record it reads, the + port reading the part k as alpha_k p + beta_k q and the - port as alpha_k (-q) + beta_k p (the pair's pattern [[1, 0], [0, 1]]: the parts read as (p, q) and (-q, p); the GHZ gate's three patterns of four parts), a declaration validated by the loader (`loader/instrument.py`: a pair per part, none [0, 0], as many as the parts of the record of several parts the world lays, a pattern only with a basis) and read by the reader `tools/bell_gate.py` and by nothing in the engine; the open faces' layer is the loader's own row, named `face` and added last where an open face does not recede (`loader.faces.layer_of`), no part of the instrument, as a body's detector is not (`DetectorRow.declared`). Its **click** is its report of one interval: per family of quanta the net current into the region through the instrument's front boundary Ports at its Nodes, in the current's units (`inflow`; the front: the Ports leading in from the declared board outside the instrument, the union of the declared regions, `GameBoard.instrument`, not those between two regions nor those toward a receding face's grown layers), one `click` line per interval, family and region where it is not 0, with the region's name and never a Node; the one measurement, the host's reading for the credit by the shares (`tools/click_counts.py`); for a family of several parts (the pair family, the GHZ family) its report carries beside the click the parts' signed level sums over its region at the interval's start, per line (one per part of a family of dimension 1) [now, before], one `parts` line per interval, family and declared region where a sum is not 0, the instrument's read that the credit pairs through the root (`reports.level_sums`, `reports.parts`; ALGEBRA.md #the-click-is-the-meeting); an opaque detector is a region backed by a receding face, the board's last layer before it, through which what enters leaves the declared board and never returns (a region whose back faces declared Nodes passes the density on, and its net front inflow over a run reads nothing); every other number (a level, the share, the books) is a GameBoard reading, a diagnostic, the `field` line among them.
- **Interval**: one step of the whole GameBoard; `ticks` intervals make a run.

## 2. The NodeState

The engine is six modules: `node.py` (the NodeState and the Node's acts: the read, Rule3 on the levels, the readings of the currents, the tension, the momentum density, the form, the Wronskian, the well as a reading, the turn of a plane under the rotation and the one write per held part), `share.py` (the share, the count as a reading of the record), `reports.py` (the detectors' reports, a body's Nodes derived for a report), `growth.py` (the receding face: the GameBoard grown by layers of zeros where the front reaches it, the layers taken off on the way back), `game_board.py` (the GameBoard that walks the interval forward and back) and `world_files.py` with `loader/` (the files; `loader/derived.py` the families from the rule, each held family's one write per part with its walls, `derived.held_write`, and the amplitude bound), on `core/` (Rule3, the Ports, the working bound) and the folders of `features/` (`currents`, `hold`, `read`, `rotation`, `start`, `write`).

`src/event_universe/node.py`, `NodeState`, one per family, every array over the GameBoard of the kind the file's width chooses at load (`World.kind`, section 4; the hardware's 64-bit integers at a width at or under the host's, Python's integers above it):

| Field | Held by | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lines` (each `now`, `before`, `remainder`) | every family alike | its lines, each a record of dimension one (two levels and Rule3's remainder), as many as the loader derives for the family (`FamilyRule.lines`): a family of quanta's record, one line (dimension 1) or two (a plane, re then im) per part, the pair family's two parts two real lines; a held row's sources' count, the time line first and then, where the tensions source it, the three axis lines xx, yy, zz; the holder of the sign's time line is its record, light, and under the act `rotation` the three odd axis lines x, y, z follow it, each the phase on that axis's Links; a row with a gap as a row without one |
| `write_remainders` | a held family | one remainder per line of its one write, in [0, wall), at half the wall from the start: the time line's wall E_s T, each tension line's E_s W_c and each odd line's E_s den T (`derived.held_write`); empty for a family that holds nothing |

The engine's step receives lines with their coefficients, their sources and their readers and knows no family, no dimension and no name; the GameBoard groups the lines into families as the loader derived them, for the readings and the sources alone (the owner, 2026-10-01, 03:25, "the engine works only with families of dimension one"). No count, no sense and no remainder of either stands at a Node: a family of quanta's count is its record's share, e_i = 3 [w (now^2 + before^2) - S now before] div (2 SUM_a p_a^2) - num now S_6(before) at the paces of the read, in quanta (e_i + W_c div 2) div W_c over the wall W_c = 3 den T (`share.share`, `share.family_share`, `share.quanta_of`, `GameBoard.share_of`, `GameBoard.quanta`); its currents are F_ij = num (now_i before_j - before_i now_j) through each Port, every line's added (`node.currents_of`, `features/currents`); its form D_i = now^2 - next x before summed over its lines and its Wronskian W_i, the bilinear of a plane's two lines (the integer 0 for real lines, the pair family's among them), are read about the step (`node.form`, `node.wronskian`), the writes' sources, its momentum density P_a = (F_(+a) - F_(-a)) / num, the bare currents through the +a Port less the -a Port (`node.momentum_of`), the odd lines' source under the rotation, and the well D_i div T is a reading (`node.well`); every one of them read from the record and kept nowhere (ALGEBRA.md #the-count-is-the-records-share; #the-primitives, a family's write is one act). The structural gate `tests/test_rule3.py::test_the_generic_node_is_closed_for_building` holds this table to the code's fields.

## 3. The interval

Before the first interval every held family's time part stands at its sources' rest (`GameBoard.start`): the sources each body's share in quanta at its declared Nodes and each message's over the whole GameBoard, the share of the two level pairs at the content 0 (`share.family_share`, `share.quanta_of`), for a held row sourced by the form and its Wronskian's quanta at the written moment, W div T (`node.wronskian`, `node.well`), for the holder of the sign (its rest, sources of either sign; the holder keeps its laid record where nothing sources it), each at the weight with which the record's family reads the row: every row at the rest of its own line, with or without a gap (`features/start`, the division act iterated from nothing until the levels repeat, 6 den b = num S_6(b) + 3 den sigma at a fine unit from the file's width, the sources of either sign or both where the board has a sink or the pair a gap, the source over the row's level weight; the same iteration serves the generator), the remainder at the half wall of the rule the row steps by, a row with no source at 0 with the same remainder; every write remainder stands at half its wall from the state's making (`node.empty_state`, `node.write_origins`), the walls derived from the rows (`derived.held_write`: E_s T for the time part, E_s x 3 den T for each tension part with the sourcing families' den, their least common multiple where they differ, and E_s den T for each odd axis line of a holder under the rotation); a row with a `rest` has it added at every Node, both levels, its remainder at the half wall with or without a source, and reads it through every Port beyond an open or inner face (`node.step`, `fill`), the same vacuum on both sides, so a uniform rest is the row's own fixed point bit for bit; then every kick is added to its row; the axis parts start at 0. Then the guard, once: every family of quanta's read of the initial state is checked as squares, 0 < p_a and p^2 (den + num) <= 2 den Gamma^2 at every Node on the clock's square and the three Link paces (`node.guarded`, `features/read`; a family that reads nothing stands at Gamma), refused by name outside, and under the rotation every turn's tangent half-angle at most 1 at every Node, |L| <= 2 Gamma for the time level and |L_a(i) + L_a(i + a)| <= 4 Gamma on each Link, refused by name naming the Node (`rotation.guard`); no act of the interval reads a pace or an angle to refuse. Then the gate (`GameBoard.gate`): every body's declared count c against its family's share in quanta read at its declared Nodes at the paces of the read, ((|c - read| - 1) div 2)^2 <= c, refused by name beyond it, a reading of the laid record and no lay; the books' origin is every family's share as laid (`GameBoard.laid`).

`GameBoard.step()` walks the acts in order, each one loop over the families (or the detectors) with no case for any of them (ALGEBRA.md #the-interval), after the receding faces are read (`growth.grow`): where a level other than 0 of any family (every record's two levels, a held row's tensions among them, the massless row's time part read against its rest) stands on the layer before a receding face, the GameBoard grows by the face's `layers` layers of zeros on that side (`growth.resize`: every NodeState, the detectors' declared Nodes, the inner faces' Nodes, the shape, the offset of the layers before the origin and the laid shares), every grown Node at the NodeState the start gives a Node with no level (every level 0, the massless row's time part at its rest, a held row's time part at the remainder the start gave the row, every write remainder at half its wall, every other remainder 0), so that the run is the run of the larger GameBoard bit for bit; with the axis at its largest size the run ends, `GameBoard.ended` naming the interval, the axis, the side and the size, no act is taken and a further step is refused; the growth copies every array once per `layers` intervals per face and keeps nothing else, one ledger line per growth (the interval, the axis, the side, the layers):

1. **The currents** (`GameBoard.currents`, `node.currents_of`, `features/currents`): every family of quanta's current through each of its six Ports at every Node, F_ij = num (now_i before_j - before_i now_j) into the Node from its neighbour, both level pairs added, read from the record as it stands at the interval's start, the pair the step starts from, so that the share's change over the step is exactly their sum at standing paces (ALGEBRA.md #the-count-is-the-records-share), and, where a holder declares the rotation, every plane's momentum density P_a = (F_(+a) - F_(-a)) / num on each axis, the bare currents through the +a Port less the -a Port (`GameBoard.momenta`, `node.momentum_of`), odd under the time reversal with the current, the odd lines' source of act 5; a reading, nothing written.
2. **The read and Rule3** (`GameBoard.stepped`, `node.read`, `features/read`, `node.step`, `node.rule_of`): for every family the content c = SUM over its reads of (weight x the read family's time part) and the axis contents t_a from the tensions, (weight x the aa part + 1) div 2, the reads the family's dimension's (`derived.family_rules`: every holder of the content, and for a family of dimension 2 every holder of the sign; light, the holder's own record, reads none), a holder under the act `rotation` entering no pace (`node.read` leaves it out; `derived.turns` says whose record it turns), no coefficient read from a Node's own state; no floor and no clamp. Every line of the family steps: a family of quanta's lines by `coefficients(num, den, Gamma, c, t)` at its reads' paces (the holder of the sign's lines the same), a holder of the content's lines by the plain rule of its own pair at the pace 1 and the wall 3 den, with or without a gap (the reads num, the self coefficient 0); a plane a holder turns steps under the rotation (`node.step_plane`, `features/rotation`; ALGEBRA.md **The sign holder rotates the two-part record**): with z = re + i im, theta the holder's time level L at the Node over Gamma and theta_a the Link's angle, e^(i theta) z_next + e^(-i theta) z_before = (S z_now + SUM_a R_a (e^(i theta_a) z_(+a) + e^(-i theta_a) z_(-a))) / w, the level before turned by the time angle, each neighbour's pair arriving turned through its Port by the Link's angle (+a by it, -a by its opposite), Rule3 on both lines with one rule and their own remainders, and the stepped pair turned the same way; every turn three shears, x1 = x - (n y) div w, y1 = y + (2 n w x1) div (w^2 + n^2), x2 = x1 - (n y1) div w, at the tangent half-angle n / w, L / (2 Gamma) for the time angle and (L_a(i) + L_a(j)) / (4 Gamma) on the Link ij (`node.turned_sums`), each shear the division act with no remainder kept and no new state, undone in the reverse order bit for bit (`rotation.turned`), a turned level within twice A (`rotation.TURNED_REACH`, the amplitude bound); the engine's convention: the turn is against the sense of the record of positive Wronskian (z_before = z_now e^(i omega), clockwise), so in a positive level that record rotates faster by theta and the record of the opposite sense slower, like senses apart (the law's line as written, e^(-i theta) z_next + e^(i theta) z_before, with the engine's W convention gives the positive record omega - theta and like senses together; the engine builds the sign the law's row (f) names, and both readings stand in ALGEBRA.md); every read is of the interval's start, the stepped lines assigned after every family has read; a level beyond the amplitude bound A refuses the run by name. The form of every family of quanta is read here over its lines about the step, D_i = now^2 - next x before, and its Wronskian W_i = re_now im_before - im_now re_before as the step left them (`node.form`, `node.wronskian`), the sources of act 5, for a turned plane from the booking, the step's levels before the final turn against the turned level before (D = |z_now|^2 - v . u and W = Im(conj(v) z_now), v Rule3's output and u the turned level before), the bookings carrying the angle and the same numbers in either direction; nothing is divided and nothing kept.
3. **The tension** (`GameBoard.stresses`, `node.stresses_of`, `features/currents`): every family of quanta's tension on each axis at every Node from its levels now as the step left them, T_aa(i) = num (G_aa(i) + G_aa(i - a)) div 2 with G_aa(i) = now_i (now_(i+2a) - now_i) - now_(i+a) (now_(i+a) - now_(i-a)), the neighbour's difference brought through the Port (`node.axis_differences`), every line's added; read and never written.
4. **The reports** (`GameBoard.report`, `reports.inflow`, `reports.front`): every detector (a detector's declared Nodes, the Nodes of the body it names derived now by `GameBoard.body_nodes`, the open faces' layer), each one region, reports per family of quanta the net current into it this interval through the instrument's front boundary Ports at its Nodes, from the currents of act 1 (the front: the Ports leading in from the declared board, `GameBoard.declared_board`, outside the instrument, the union of the declared regions for a declared detector and the region's own Nodes for a body's detector and the faces' layer), inward positive, summed in integers with their signs: one `click` line per family and region where it is not 0, with the region's name, the family and `inflow`, never a Node (the advisor's correction, #1515 comment 5912958018); for a family of several parts one `parts` line per declared region where a sum is not 0, the parts' signed level sums over the region at the interval's start, [now, before] per part (`reports.level_sums`), the instrument's read of the pair; nothing is handed over. Beside it the field reading (`GameBoard.fields_read`), a GameBoard reading labelled so: per family and declared region (not a body's detector nor the faces' layer) the family's density over the region, for a family of quanta its share in quanta summed, for a holder of the content the square of its time part's deviation from its rest summed, one `field` line where it differs from the last interval's.
5. **The hold** (`GameBoard.hold`, `node.write_sources`, `node.held_write`, `features/hold`): every held family takes one write per line by the write's carried division (`features/write`) with the one remainder kept at the Node: its time line gains (SUM over the sourcing families of w x q + r) div (E_s T), E_s the row's level weight, the sourcing families its readers (`derived.readers_of`, the hold's reciprocity) and q what the row's sources name, the form D of each reader for a row sourced by the form and the Wronskian W of each for the holder of the sign (light is born by this write: a breathing or moving rotating body writes a varying source at its Nodes and the row carries it away as a wave), and each axis line aa gains (SUM w x factor x T_aa + r_aa) div (E_s W_c) from the tensions of act 3 (the factor 1 where one den serves every source, `HeldWrite.factors`), and each odd line a of a holder under the rotation (SUM w x factor x P_a + r_a) div (E_s den T) from the momenta of act 1 (its sources the planes it turns; a one-part reader sources none and light none), no count in the divisor and no branch.

`GameBoard.step_inverse()` runs the acts back with Rule3's direction -1: a held family's hold back once every family that sources it is booked back, a family of quanta booked back (`GameBoard.booked_back`: its tension read from the levels as the step left them, as the forward write read it, its records back, a turned plane by the shears in the reverse order at the same angles, its form, its Wronskian and its momentum density read from them) once its own write is off, so the holder of the sign goes before the rows it sources, then every held row of the content stepped back, the records assigned after every read of the interval's start; then the layers the same interval grew beyond a receding face are taken off (`growth.resize` with the direction -1), the grown Nodes having returned to the state they were laid in, bit for bit, so the GameBoard's shape returns with its state. Every interval returns every level and remainder, the writes' included, bit for bit where every number a family read at the interval's start is one the state after the interval still holds (a held row's `before`, the hold booking the same interval's well into the row after its step; ALGEBRA.md #what-is-open, item 21), and the readings follow the record; every number a step reads is a held row's line at the interval's start and none a Node's own record, so the gate is MATCH without exception (the reader's q left with the sense, HIGHLIGHTS.md **Every family has a dimension**). ## 4. The input

A run reads four files, all JSON: the world file, the universe file and the engine start file it names by their repository paths, and the generator's mode file beside it, `<world>.mode.json`. `src/event_universe/world_files.py` (`load_world`) reads them and `src/event_universe/loader/world.py` (`parse_world`, with `loader/keys.py`, `loader/faces.py`, `loader/messages.py`, `loader/mode.py` and `loader/instrument.py`) checks every key and refuses every other key as unknown by name; no default is written, and a refusal names the key, the value and the reason (`REFUSED` in the output, section 5).

| File | Keys |

1. ## 7. How to add a family

A family is a row of the universe file, never a line of the engine: its `name`, its `pair` and its `dimension`, or for a held family `held` {`sources`, `level_weight`, optionally `rest` on the massless row of the content and `act` on the holder of the sign}. The rule derives from the pair and the shape what it holds, its lines, its reads and the rows it sources; the engine steps it, reads its share and its currents, holds it and reads it with the code it already has; a charged body of a pair is a second row of that pair at the dimension 2. A new act of the law starts from its line in ALGEBRA.md (CONTRIBUTING.md), is a pure function of arrays that calls Rule3 alone, and comes with its test; a new reading of the record is a function of the record's arrays and nothing kept beside them.
