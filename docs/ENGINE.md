# The engine

This document describes the engine as the tree of the branch engine-5.0-test holds it, `src/event_universe/`, and nothing else: what a Node holds, the acts of one interval in the order the code runs them, what the loader reads and refuses, what the run writes, how a world is run, the gates that hold the engine to the law, the tests, and where the engine departs from the law's sentence of the Node. The law is [docs/ALGEBRA.md](ALGEBRA.md); the decisions and the postulates are [docs/HIGHLIGHTS.md](HIGHLIGHTS.md); the run, its gates and what is open are [docs/UNIVERSE24.md](UNIVERSE24.md). Every function, module and test named here stands in the tree under that name (the keeper's check of 2026-10-25, every name tested against the tree); a thing the engine no longer holds is not described here, the former engines standing in the git history, in CHANGELOG.md and in the law's dated clauses by name. Main's engine (4.0, the tree of 0fcc0403d, the families declared) is described by main's own ENGINE.md and not by this one.

## 1. The words

- **Node**: one place of the lattice; its whole local information is its NodeState (section 2), the law's own numbers and nothing else; it has no name and no role and differs from another place in its position and its values alone.
- **Port** and **Link**: a Node has six Ports, +x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z, one Link through each to one neighbour; a level reaches a neighbour only through a Port (`core/ports.arrival`); every axis wraps, so the board is a torus (`loader/world.TORUS`), and an axis of one layer folds, its two Ports returning the Node itself.
- **Lattice**: the torus of Nodes of the world's `shape`, every family's NodeState over it, stepped one interval at a time (`lattice.Lattice`).
- **Row** and **family**: one line of the universe on the lattice, derived at load from den, Gamma and T (`loader/universe.rows_of`; section 4): the holder of the volume, the holder of the sign, the one massless row of quanta (light), and one plane row per point of den's circle, each named by its vector "point e1 e2 e3"; a family is what the decoder calls a row's quanta. The engine's step receives lines with their coefficients and knows no family's name.
- **Line** and **record**: a line is two levels, now and before, and Rule3's remainder, over the Nodes; a record is a row's lines (one real line for light, two for a plane, re and im), stepped by Rule3 as lines of dimension one; a point's records are born at its first lay (`registry.py`, `Lattice.born_point`).
- **Held row** and **holder**: a row written once per interval by the held write from the records' bookings: the holder of the volume (gravity's row, its time line the volume's level X, sourced by the form and the tensions) and the holder of the sign (sourced by the Wronskian, one row per charged record and a free row).
- **Seed**: the lay of light at one quantum on every Node of the torus but the origin, where no quantum is laid, the hole (`seed.uniform_seed`, `seed.ORIGIN`).
- **Click**: the board's own read of itself at a Node at the interval's close, its birth, its take and its credit line (`meeting_board.closed`).
- **Interval**: one step of the whole lattice; `intervals` intervals make a run.
- **Logs**: the clicks log, the record (the credit, birth and lay lines); the eras log and the nodes log, the board's readings beside it, looks and no record.

## 2. The NodeState

The engine is thirty modules (the Python files of `src/event_universe/` beside `__init__.py`; the subpackages hold fourteen more, `core/` four, `loader/` nine and `features/start/rests.py`, and the seven `features/*/__init__.py` carry their code themselves). The thirty, each with its one sentence from its own docstring:

- `act.py`: the one act of the click, a draw among its alternatives by the read of the board, the lays it books (`click`, `written`, `affordable`, `born_table`, `born_list`, `born_items`).
- `board_readings.py`: the board's readings, each one loop over the records and no act (`stresses`, `sense_currents`, `sense_fluxes`, `uniform_mode`, `eras`, `volume_sums`, `row_level`).
- `bookings.py`: the bookings of the records, the lines a held write sums (`sources_of`, `record_lines`, `booked_of`, `booked_sources`).
- `bound_body.py`: the bound body's click as the Node's act, the period read from the record's own line (`beats_of`, `body_records`, `bodies_closing`).
- `conversion.py`: the lists two light quanta make, the conjugate pairs of the plane rows within the two quanta's energy (`candidates`, `lists_of`, `walls_of`, `birth_of`).
- `credit.py`: the credit's books, the units and deficits per family (`Books`, `born`, `counted`, `count_of`, `record_unit`, `support_of`).
- `digits.py`: the click's read of the board, one Link and one number (`frame_of`, `digits_of`, `lattice_of`, `read_of`, `at_origin`).
- `emission.py`: the born children laid into the point's records, and the point rows born and unborn (`emitted_lines`, `laid_by_count`, `born_point`, `unborn_point`).
- `flux.py`: the step, Rule3 in the law's flux form on every line (`link_fluxes`, `turned_link_fluxes`, `step_line`, `step_momentum`, `step_plane`, `step_family`, `step_records`).
- `frames.py`: the frames a turned record reads its neighbours' planes with through its Ports (`frames_of`).
- `hole.py`: the hole, the write that takes what the birth books and nothing else, the exact take (`walls_of`, `moved`, `taken_at`, `take_of`).
- `lattice.py`: the lattice, every family's NodeState, the interval's acts in order and their inverse (`Lattice`; section 3).
- `lay.py`: the one lay act, every write of levels from outside Rule3 (`shares_of`, `division_act`, `guarded`, `corrected`, `written`, `laid`).
- `meeting.py`: one family's step over the board (`stepped`).
- `meeting_back.py`: the run back's log of the lays and the snapshots the run back compares (`logged`, `snapshot`, `first_difference`).
- `meeting_board.py`: the board reads itself, the close at every Node (`meetings`, `child_of`, `drawn_among`, `closed`, `holes_of`, `refused_by`, `readings_at`).
- `node.py`: the Node's acts on every family's NodeState, each a pure function of whole-board arrays reading one Link (`read`, `rulers`, `rule_of`, `held_write_at`, `phase_act`, `phased`, `phase_counts`, `turning`).
- `plane.py`: the Link's pairs of a plane under the rotation, the Peierls phase on every Link (`link_pairs`, `sign_pairs`, `phase_links`, `frame_signs`).
- `readings.py`: the readings of a record's lines at every Node, one Link's reach each (`currents_of`, `momentum_of`, `stresses_of`, `sense_current_of`).
- `records.py`: the records' lines and the sign's rows, the arrays' shapes, the form, the Wronskian, the held write's sources (`NodeState`, `form`, `wronskian`, `write_sources`, `walls_at`, `phase_row`, `zeros`, `full`).
- `registry.py`: the records' registry, the live count of a family's records read from its lines (`records_of`, `rows_of`, `row_of`, `start_records`).
- `reports.py`: the output's words and lines, the credit, lay, birth and refused lines (`credit`, `lay`, `book`, `birth_line`, `refused_line`; section 5).
- `seed.py`: the uniform seed as a lay with the hole at the origin (`drift_of`, `uniform_seed`).
- `share.py`: the share, the count as the record's weighted share at every Node, a reading and no line (`share`, `family_share`, `board_share`, `quanta_of`, `charge_of`).
- `shear.py`: the shear's momentum carried at the volume's wall (`shear_lines`, `written`, `stepped`, `inverted`).
- `slabs.py`: the board stepped in slabs by several processes with one-Link halos, the same logs byte for byte (`slabs_of`, `tile_of`, `Slabs`, `served`).
- `swap.py`: the swap at the top, the fifth act, and the floor (`kept`, `at_top`, `floor_of`, `at_floor`, `swapped`, `stepped`, `inverted`).
- `take.py`: the take's item (`Item`, `below_quantum`).
- `walls.py`: the held rows' walls and sources at the start (`sourced`).
- `world_files.py`: the host's reading of the world file and the universe file for the loader (`world_files`, `load_world`, `input_digest`).

Under them `core/` (`rule3.py`, Rule3 in one place, the one carried division and its inverse; `paces.py`, the composed paces, the clock, the Link's pace, the write's factor, the turn's factor and the two roundings `rounded` and `odd_rounded`; `ports.py`, the six Ports, the arrival and the Link unit `LINK_UNIT` = 16; `integer.py`, the host's integer range), `loader/` (`world.py`, `universe.py`, `keys.py`, `derived.py`, `circle.py`, `charge.py`, `rooms.py`, `seed.py`, `interval.py`; section 4) and `features/` (`click`, `currents`, `held_write`, `phase`, `read`, `start`, `volume`).

A NodeState (`records.NodeState`), one per family, holds three arrays over the lattice of the kind the derived width chooses at load: `lines` (each `now`, `before`, `remainder`: the family's lines, one real line for light, two for a plane, and for a held row its time line and its axis lines), `write_remainders` (a held family's one remainder per line of its write, in [0, wall), born at half the wall) and `phases` (the holder of the sign's ten lines per row beyond the free row: the cosine and sine lines of each axis's Link, the three count lines and the Node's phase row, 30 integers per Node per record). Beside the arrays the lattice keeps per Node the time count `proper_count` and the closes, and per charged record its `standing`, `crossed` and `beats` for the bodies' closings. The count of integers a Node holds is read by `tools/node_integers.py`: 35 before the first birth (the volume's row 16, the sign's free row 16, light 3) and 52 more per record born (section 10). No count, no sense and no name stands at a Node: a family's count is its record's share, a reading (`share.share`), its currents are read through the Ports (`readings.currents_of`), its form and its Wronskian about the step (`records.form`, `records.wronskian`).

## 3. The interval

One interval is `Lattice.step`, two halves so that the slabs runner can re-impose its halos between them (`advanced_to_the_close`, then `closed_after`), every act reading the interval's start and one Link (ALGEBRA.md, The Node; The interval's dependency radius is one Link). In the code's order:

1. **The start kept.** The interval counted; the lines and the phase lines of the start kept for the swap at the top and for the close's read (`swap.kept`).
2. **The phase act.** The holder of the sign's phase lines iterated by the potential's difference across each Link, read at the start (`node.phase_act`, `features/phase.iterate`, `plane.sign_pairs`); first, so that every reading of a turned record takes the Link pairs the step reads.
3. **The rulers and the time count.** Every family's paces from the held rows' levels at the start (`Lattice.rulers`, `core/paces`), and the seed's clock summed into the Node's `proper_count`, the time count every family of quanta reads alike.
4. **The readings.** The stresses and the sign currents of the records, read once per interval at the start (`board_readings.stresses`, `board_readings.sense_currents`).
5. **Every family stepped by Rule3.** Each line stepped by the one carried division from its six arrivals (`Lattice.stepped`, `meeting.stepped`, `flux.step_records`, `node.rule_of`, `core/rule3.rule3`), the amplitude bound checked (`Lattice.bounded`), and the families of quanta booked for the held writes (`bookings.booked_of`: the forms and the Wronskians).
6. **The held writes.** Each held row's one write per line from the bookings, scaled by the write's factor at the sourcing family's paces, the tensions' parts for a row of the content, the sign currents for the holder of the sign, one carried division per line with its remainder kept (`Lattice.held_write`, `records.write_sources`, `node.held_write_at`, `features/held_write.held_write`); the volume holder's time line the volume's level (`features/volume`).
7. **The swap at the top.** A Node whose volume's time line passes a weak maximum ends the interval with its levels exchanged and its remainders complemented, the floor turning the same way (`swap.stepped`, `swap.at_top`, `swap.at_floor`).
8. **The close.** The tick counted and the board's own close at every Node on the state the interval started from (`closed_after`, `meeting_board.closed`): the seed's share at every Node (`Lattice.share_of`); the meetings, the Nodes where the share holds a whole quantum (`meeting_board.meetings`); the lists within the two quanta's energy (`child_of`, `conversion.birth_of`, `act.born_table`); where one whole quantum stands with a share above it the whether, the Node's read of itself and its six Links as one number divided by the unit once (`digits.frame_of`, `digits.read_of`, `features/click.drawn_from`), the remainder the whether and the quotient the pick among several lifts (`drawn_among`), the read's refusals by name (origin, vacuum, unit); the bodies' closings, a charged record standing at a Node at a whole quantum of its own unit through one period of its beat (`bound_body.bodies_closing`, `bound_body.beats_of`); the takes, the hole taking from the light what the births and the bodies book and nothing else, refused by name where the full stop cannot pay (`hole.take_of`, `meeting_board.refused_by`, `holes_of`); then the one act of the click per pair, every face read before any write (`act.click`): the holes laid (`lay.laid`), the children laid into their point's records (`emission.laid_by_count`, a point's records born at its first lay, `Lattice.born_point`), the quantum into a body's record; and the lines written (`reports.credit`, `reports.birth_line`, `reports.refused_line`).

Backward (`Lattice.step_inverse`) the same acts in the reverse order with Rule3's direction -1: the lays of the interval undone from the books' log (`meeting_back.logged`), the point rows born at the close unborn (`unborn_point`), the rulers read, the start kept, every held write back once its sources are booked back and every family stepped back (`booked_back`, `held_write` at -1), the shear and the swap inverted (`shear.inverted`, `swap.inverted`), the phase act back (`node.phase_act` at -1); the run back returns the board to its seed bit for bit through every birth (`tools/back_in_time.py`; tests/test_the_run_back_is_exact.py). The board may be stepped in slabs by several processes with one-Link halos re-imposed before the close, the same logs byte for byte (`slabs.py`, `tools/eras.py --slabs`; tests/test_the_board_runs_in_slabs.py).

## 4. The input

Two files and nothing else. The world file, `{"universe": <path>, "shape": [X, Y, Z], "intervals": N}` (`loader/world.WORLD_KEYS`, every key required): the universe it names by its repository path, the torus's shape and the run's length. The universe file, `{"integers": {"den": ..., "node_clock": ..., "quantum_action": ...}}` (`loader/universe.UNIVERSE_KEYS`, `INTEGER_KEYS`): den, Gamma the Node clock and T the whole piece's action. Every other key is refused by name at load (`loader/keys.keyed`: "holds the unknown key ..."; `loader/universe.gone_refused` for the former `families` rows; `width` and `link_unit` refused as integers of the engine and not of the universe), a lacking key likewise; a world naming `bodies`, `node_detectors`, `faces`, `boundary` or a mode file is refused. A board periodic on three even axes with a massless row is refused (`loader/world.even_box_refused`).

The law's line of 2026-10-25 (ALGEBRA.md, **The universe holds the lines' pairs as numbers**) puts the lines' pairs in the universe file beside the three integers; the engine of this branch reads the three alone and derives den's circle at the load, and it holds the Link unit G = 16 as its own where the law's line of the same day (no number in the code, **The Node**) puts G in the universe file as `link_unit`, the loader refusing the key (`loader/universe.GONE_INTEGERS`); both departures named in section 10. From the three integers and the shape the loader derives everything the Nodes run on (`loader/universe.rows_of`, `loader/derived.family_rules`): the holder of the volume at the clock's one level, the pair (den - den div Gamma, den) (`volume_pair`); the holder of the sign at (den, den); the massless row, light; one plane row per point of den's circle with both legs above 0 (`loader/circle.circle_points`, den factored into its primes 1 mod 4, 26 points at a den of three primes), the lifts of den's primes the candidates of a birth (`circle.lift_points`), the lightest point (`circle.lightest_point`); every row's reads at the weight 1 and its held write (`derived.with_reads`, `derived.held_write_of`), a reader with an irrational sine refused (`loader/charge.pythagorean_readers`); the width of the integers from the level the law names (`loader/rooms.width_of`) and the amplitude bound (`derived.amplitude_bound`); the Link unit G = 16 the engine's own (`core/ports.LINK_UNIT`); the seed, light at one quantum on every Node but the origin, the volume holder at rest at Gamma (`loader/seed.seed_of`, `seed.uniform_seed`). The record's world is `examples/events/universe24/universe24.json` with its universe file beside it.

## 5. The output

The run writes three logs (`tools/eras.py`), one JSON line each. The clicks log is the record, three kinds of line: the **credit** line of every click (`reports.credit`), with `event`, `label`, `interval`, `family`, `node_detector` (the Node's x-major index), `window`, `proper` (the Node's own clock), `windows`, `before`, `realised`, `kept`, `count`, `left`, `absorbed`, `emitted`, `momentum`, `lost`, `source`, and the readings `point`, `kind`, `sense`, `support`, `beat`, `identity` (`reports.READINGS`), and on a bound body's credit the take's `taken`, `walls`, `excess`, `step` (`reports.TAKE_KEYS`); the **birth** line of every close that births (`reports.birth_line`): `event`, `label`, `interval`, `family`, `children` (the points' names), `senses`, `nodes`, `records`, `points`, `taken`, `walls`, `excess`, `step`; the **lay** line of every write of levels from outside Rule3 (`reports.lay`): `event`, `label`, `interval`, `family`, `node_detector`, `line`, `before`, `after`, `node`; and a **refused** line for every read or take refused by name (`reports.refused_line`), its cause and its two numbers. The eras log's header names the world's integers, the width, the shape, den and its primes, the seed, the families, the points, the held rows, the intervals, the lightest point and the amplitude bound; each line carries `interval`, `seconds`, `rows` (every row's level readings), `quanta` per family, `fires` (the Nodes the fifth act turned) and `horizon` (the Links at the wall). The nodes log carries per interval every Node's `X`, `length` (e^U), `direction`, `quanta`, `horizon` and the `turned` list. The readers: `tools/clicks_read.py`, the one decoder, thirteen tables from the clicks log with the two looks beside it (`readings_clicks.json`); `tools/record_gates.py`, the nineteen gates of the run on the three logs, SHOWN or NOT SHOWN by name (UNIVERSE24.md, section 2); `tools/look/eras_page.py` and `tools/look/torus_page.py`, the two pages drawn from the logs, with `tools/look/captions.py` naming the lifts from the header's integers; `tools/back_in_time.py`, the run back through a window; `tools/node_integers.py`, the integers one Node holds; `tools/step_oracle.py`, the step's first form as the oracle of the flux form; `tools/run_inputs.py`, a world run to its output file.

Python 3.14 or later, the package installed (`python -m pip install -e .`); from a checkout's root, the run with its three logs, the decoder, the gates, the two pages and the run back:

```
PYTHONPATH=src python tools/clicks_read.py examples/events/universe24/universe24.clicks.jsonl --shape 5 5 5 --eras examples/events/universe24/universe24.eras.jsonl --nodes examples/events/universe24/universe24.nodes.jsonl --out examples/events/universe24/readings_clicks.json
PYTHONPATH=src python tools/record_gates.py examples/events/universe24
PYTHONPATH=src python tools/look/eras_page.py examples/events/universe24/universe24.eras.jsonl --clicks examples/events/universe24/universe24.clicks.jsonl --nodes examples/events/universe24/universe24.nodes.jsonl --notes examples/events/universe24/notes.md --out examples/events/universe24/universe24.html
PYTHONPATH=src python tools/look/torus_page.py examples/events/universe24/universe24.nodes.jsonl --eras examples/events/universe24/universe24.eras.jsonl --clicks examples/events/universe24/universe24.clicks.jsonl --world examples/events/universe24/universe24.json --notes examples/events/universe24/notes.md --out examples/events/universe24/universe24.torus.html
PYTHONPATH=src python tools/back_in_time.py examples/events/universe24/universe24.json --from K --intervals N
PYTHONPATH=src python tools/node_integers.py examples/events/universe24/universe24.json --intervals 13 --bits 128
```
