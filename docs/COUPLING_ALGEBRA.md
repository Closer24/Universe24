# Reference coupling: group action, cube and signs

This describes `algebra/coupling.py` and `core.tensor.reference_block`, not every
polynomial permitted by the generic Tensor class. Padding a research model to
48 channels does not turn it into this reference coupling.

The labels are the 48 unit quaternions of the binary octahedral group 2O.
Conjugating imaginary quaternions gives a cube rotation R_g. There are 24
distinct proper cube rotations, each with two lifts g and -g. The kernel is
{1,-1}; these 48 labels are not the 48 rotations-and-reflections of the cube.

For a world face direction d, the implementation computes the body direction
v=R_g^{-1}d and then the destination label P_d(g)=g q_v. The actual table is:

| Body direction v | Right multiplier q_v | Weight for (+,-,+) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| +x, -x | 1 | +1 |
| +y | QY | -1 |
| -y | QY inverse | -1 |
| +z | QZ cubed | +1 |
| -z | QZ to power -3 | +1 |

QY=(1+j)/sqrt(2) and QZ=(1+k)/sqrt(2). The group multiplication is ordinary
Hamilton quaternion multiplication. The complete map P_d is a permutation,
not multiplication by a single fixed group element: q_v depends on g.

In the sender convention, the signed permutation operator is
T_d e_g = w_d(g) e_{P_d(g)}, with w_d(g)=s_axis(R_g^{-1}d). It sends the
integer amplitude in channel g at x to channel P_d(g) at x+d, multiplied by
the indicated sign. The tensor sums incoming contributions. `matrix(d)` stores
sender rows/receiver columns; a column-vector implementation uses its transpose.
The default signs are (+,+,+); (+,-,+) is a separately prepared parameter choice.
It weights the local y axis negatively, not a fixed world y axis and not the
quaternion lift sign. It does not change ports or implement a spatial reflection.

Exact finite checks in `test_coupling_algebra` establish:

- P_-d = P_d inverse, with matching reciprocal weights.
- P_(R_k d)(kg)=k P_d(g), and the weights obey the corresponding covariance.
- Each directional permutation has order 8 and fixes 16 labels.
- The subgroup of S_48 generated by the six ports has order 196608, not 48.
- For (+,-,+), 16 weights per world direction are negative. The signed
  permutation group also has order 196608 (computed on 96 signed labels).

Thus the familiar structures are quaternion multiplication, the double cover
of cube rotations, and a representation by signed permutation matrices. The
specific direction-dependent routing table is an additional model choice.
It is not the regular representation g -> gh for fixed h, nor by itself a
derivation of a Lie bracket, Clebsch-Gordan tensor or physical particle content.

## Mandatory runtime symmetry

The versioned contract `2O-left-regular-cube/1` in `core/symmetry.py` uses the
ordered quaternion labels in `algebra/group_2o.py`. For every retained bank
v in (a,b,r), define

    (A_k v)[k*g, R_k x] = v[g,x],  x in (Z/LZ)^3.

Thus A_k A_h = A_(kh). This is an **unsigned** permutation of all three banks.
The central element -1 exchanges g and -g at the same node; it does not negate
amplitudes or replace r by 5-r. The body weights (+,-,+) belong to the coupling,
not to this symmetry action. Both reference sign choices satisfy the contract.

For each of the 48 k, the validator transforms every polynomial monomial:
target g becomes kg, factor h becomes kh, and its offset e becomes R_k e.
The factor bank and coefficient are unchanged. Equality of the complete
canonical coefficient maps proves u(A_k s)=A_k u(s), independently of the seed
and lattice size. Componentwise fixed-six Rule3 then gives
F(A_k s)=A_k F(s). Bijectivity gives the same identity for F inverse.
Tests separately exercise both identities on complete states, all remainder
values, big integers and different cube sizes, including a nonlinear cubic law.

This contract currently covers exactly one regular orbit of 48 **node**
channels, with one common passive label so the official/audit mask is also
invariant. It does not cover Yee link representations, which need endpoint and
base-node transformations. Names, ownership and reciprocity sign explanations
are audit metadata, not extra evolving degrees of freedom. No invariant seed
is required: symmetry relates different preparations, including asymmetric ones.

There is a modeling consequence: on a single transitive orbit, an invariant
nonempty old-a dependency graph has positive indegree at every vertex and hence
a directed cycle. The existing acyclic inverse requirement therefore excludes
old-a reads in supported bundles with this representation. Nonlinear b-only
interactions and constants remain possible. Other representations would need
their own explicit contract; merely using 48 slots is insufficient.

The following metadata is accepted alongside id, version and domain in the
sealed model.json; the symmetry remains mandatory even when these fields are omitted:

```json
{"role": "universe-candidate", "symmetry": "2O-left-regular-cube/1"}
```

Preparation, save, load and execution check every model, including `research`.
Absent role still means research, but it grants no exemption. Absent symmetry
means this mandatory system action; explicit null or a different action fails.
Schema 3 requires all 48 channels before preparation, with no automatic
padding. The manifest binds the symmetry in its law certificate. Run reports
record `model_contract` with status `passed`; replay recomputes it and binds
the checker and group source hashes. Old schemas must not be relabeled or
silently upgraded. Compatible inputs require fresh preparation and execution.
This does not establish any physical acceptance gate.

## Arithmetic clicks

After summing the tensor input u, Rule3 computes divmod(2u+r,6). Consequently
(r_next-r) mod 6 = 2(u mod 3). On a transition that actually emits a click,
u mod 3 = 0,1,2 corresponds respectively to C0,C1,C2. Emission additionally
requires a nonzero quotient and a changed complete triple. A signed incoming
contribution can change the total u and hence the click; it is not itself a
click class. (+,-,+) and (C0,C1,C2) are different objects. No group-theoretic
particle or detector interpretation follows from this arithmetic identity.
