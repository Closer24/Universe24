# The history of Universe24

This file is a record of how the system came to be, from its first code to 2026-09-26.
It defines nothing; the rules live in [HIGHLIGHTS.md](HIGHLIGHTS.md), [ALGEBRA.md](ALGEBRA.md) and [ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md).

Every date is YYYY-MM-DD and every time is UTC.
A record is cited by its number in the dated logs: [2026-09-17 and 2026-09-18](LOG_2026-09-18.md), [2026-09-19](LOG_2026-09-19.md) and [2026-09-20 onward](LOG_2026-09-20.md).
The log of 2026-09-20 holds every record from 2026-09-20 to today, numbered on without a restart.
A name marked "retired" is quoted as it was used then; it is not in use now.

## 1. The first code and the Google Doc (2026-09-10 to 2026-09-16)

The project began before this repository, on the model owner's own machine; its start date is not recorded here.
The repository on GitHub begins on 2026-09-10 with the import of that local project at its fifth version, whose model was then named `scalar-field-v10-contact` (a retired name), with its README in Hebrew.
The earliest dated evidence inside the repository is a validation of the same day ([VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md#historical-validation--2026-09-10)).
It shows an integer simulator of Nodes with local rules, 206 passing tests and runs whose total momentum stayed constant at every tick.
On 2026-09-12 a consistency cleanup separated the generic engine from older particle models ([MIGRATION.md](MIGRATION.md#explicit-historical-component-names)).
On 2026-09-14 the first archived version, 0.2.0, was deposited on Zenodo ([CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md#020---2026-09-14)).
Version 0.3.0 followed the same day, with 153 more commits: the framework took the name Reality Theory (Universe24), and the runs covered two-arm interference and Bell's test.
Version 0.3.1 of 2026-09-15 added a second manuscript, on redshift from delay growth.
In these days the specification was not in the repository.
It was the Google Doc "Universe 24 Highlights" of the model owner, Alon Gonen ([README.md](../README.md#project-specification-google-docs)).

## 2. The Highlights move to Markdown; locality without exception (2026-09-16 to 2026-09-17)

The file docs/HIGHLIGHTS.md was taken verbatim from the Google Doc's revision of 2026-09-16 at 11:57Z ([HIGHLIGHTS.md](HIGHLIGHTS.md)).
From 2026-09-17, by the owner's decision, the Markdown file is the one that is edited; the Google Doc stays as the historical source and is not resynced.
**On 2026-09-17 the shared quantum resource was deleted, and locality has held without exception since** ([the transition history, entry 1](designs/algebra_transition/HISTORY.md#1-2026-09-17-the-shared-quantum-resource-is-deleted-locality-holds-without-exception)).
The same day removed the bond registry, the lottery capture and the historical particle candidates, and cut the test suite to one test per rule ([MIGRATION.md](MIGRATION.md#test-suite-reduced-on-2026-09-17-one-test-per-rule)).
It also added the ray-event model: rays with hidden state, a Detector bit at a Node, wave-ray families and binding as a loop.
The owner made the Detector's bit a property of the ray ([record 3 of 2026-09-18](LOG_2026-09-18.md#03-the-detectors-bit-is-a-property-of-the-ray)).
In this period the law was a text, the engine its reading, and a claim was checked by a run whose numbers were pinned in tests ([CODE_TO_FORMULAS.md](CODE_TO_FORMULAS.md#1-the-code-era-2026-09-16-to-2026-09-19-the-run-is-the-evidence)).

## 3. The law of the bit and the law of the shadow (2026-09-18)

The Detector's old rule, PASS and RETURN, was superseded ([record 1 of 2026-09-18](LOG_2026-09-18.md#01-pass-and-return-the-sections-text-before-2026-09-17-superseded-thrice)).
A screen had stopped clicking because a clicked quantum spread on; the owner ruled that a click is an absorption ([record 4 of 2026-09-18](LOG_2026-09-18.md#04-a-click-is-an-absorption)).
The law of the bit followed: every ray carries one bit, 1 for the thing itself and 0 for its shadow, the field ([record 7 of 2026-09-18](LOG_2026-09-18.md#07-the-law-of-the-bit-the-thing-or-its-shadow)).
In the evening the owner replaced it with the law of the shadow: "No real and shadow. There is only shadow. There are events, which are a whole quantum." ([record 29 of 2026-09-18](LOG_2026-09-18.md#29-the-law-of-the-shadow-only-shadows-and-events)).
Each law was written, built, run and pinned within the day.
The problems of these two days, and who solved each one, are in [PROBLEM_SOLVING.md](PROBLEM_SOLVING.md).

## 4. The law of events and the law of the ray (2026-09-19)

The owner then said there are no fields, no matter and no registers, only events: "Everything is derived from vector operations" ([record 2 of 2026-09-19](LOG_2026-09-19.md#02-the-law-of-events-no-fields-no-matter-no-registers-only-events)).
The same day he decided the law of the ray ([record 15 of 2026-09-19](LOG_2026-09-19.md#15-decided-the-law-of-the-ray)).
Under it the Node holds no wave; a unit is a ray with a record, moving along the digital line of its momentum at one speed.
Rays that meet at a Node are permuted by a table, and the click is the only one-way border.
The ray's law became one generic function, first named GonenBeam and then NatureBeam, both names now retired ([record 16 of 2026-09-19](LOG_2026-09-19.md#16-the-ray-is-one-generic-function-gonenbeam-gonen_beam), [record 19 of 2026-09-19](LOG_2026-09-19.md#19-try-check-what-code-can-be-cleaned-the-ray-is-naturebeam-everywhere)).
Three older engines were deleted that day: the law of the bit in the morning, the law of the shadow in the evening and the law of events in the night ([PROJECT_STATUS.md](PROJECT_STATUS.md#where-the-project-stood-on-2026-09-19-history)).
One engine remained, in `src/event_universe/events/`.
