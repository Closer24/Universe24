## The tests and gates

- **The shape of the code.** A docstring is one line naming what the function does and its ALGEBRA.md line; no decision or history in `src/`; small modules, `core/` a few hundred lines; no Rule3 arithmetic outside its one function; a ratchet test lets no file grow its prose, its references or its length, and the Boss merges no pull request that grows them.
- **The code does not grow.** `tests/` may not grow, only shrink, until it is below `src/` (a gate against the merge base); every pull request states in its body the lines it adds and deletes in `src/` and `tests/`, and the Boss checks it before the merge ([#1211](https://github.com/Closer24/Universe24/issues/1211)).
- **A gate for every cause.** Every cause found of a bloated or recurring defect gets a technical rule, a gate test in CI, so it cannot come back: for `tests/`, a size ratchet against `src/` and a cap per file, no copied setup, no history in docstrings, no skipped or retired tests kept, and a ratchet on tests that pin a GameBoard number; for the engine, a ratchet on numeric literals and family names in `src/` and no new core module without the Boss's `APPROVED-CORE`; a finding the Boss's review makes twice becomes a CI gate.
- **One test per rule.** A test exercises one generic rule alone on a minimal GameBoard, its expected integers written before the first run; every body's kind is tested alone on a small world before it is composed; the most important test is that with a new primitive undeclared every old run comes out identical to the bit, the worlds recorded on the earlier engine are no reference for this one, so no world replays in CI: no five worlds on a pull request, no record, no record job and no world shards, the test suites alone, and the first world checked on the new engine is Bell when it is right (the owner, 2026-09-27); a check is proportional to the change, the whole suite only when the shared core changes; the owner's word is for a change of the law itself (the owner, 2026-09-27).
- **The record.** The Boss keeps the record on the GitHub issues ([#1495](https://github.com/Closer24/Universe24/issues/1495) the findings and the advisor's numbers, [#1509](https://github.com/Closer24/Universe24/issues/1509) the owner's decisions relayed and their discussion) and checks every item in the code, with its commit, its test and its algebra line; after the engine, one all-families world where every family works with every other, and the advisor's reading of ALGEBRA.md one to one against the code.
- **The board goes back in time, as a gate (the owner, 2026-09-30, #1509 comment 5903548504).** With the giving, the ledgers, the root and the count in the divisor gone, the whole interval is invertible by construction, so the check is the whole run: N intervals forward, N back by Rule3's direction -1, and every array of every family compared bit for bit (the levels, the remainders, the counts and their remainders, the well's remainder, every held part and its carry, the sense and its remainders, the interval), MATCH, or MISS naming the first interval and the first Node that differ; it runs in the tests on the smallest world that holds every act and in check.py's selection on every pull request, and a look is not run before it passes on the look's own world.
