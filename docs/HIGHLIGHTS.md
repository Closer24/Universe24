# The decisions

The model owner's decisions in force, one line each, grouped by topic; a new decision replaces the line it changes and a line that no longer holds is deleted. The law is [ALGEBRA.md](ALGEBRA.md) and the engine is [ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md); git keeps the history.

## The law's words

- **The click.** A click is the passage of information from Node to Node, at most one Node per interval, its content an amplitude with phase; reality is the clicks, and Lorentz is the symmetry of the clicks Outside, arrived at from the two assumptions of the click theorem.
- **The measurement rule.** Only a detector's click is a measurement, compared with nature or pinned; a GameBoard reading (the host's view of levels, remainders and counts) is a diagnostic labelled so; no experiment reads the GameBoard and nobody measures the board; a number whose kind is not named is not a result.
- **Inside and Outside.** Inside is inside the GameBoard, where no one measures; Outside is the game above the board, the detectors and their clicks, which the paper claims represents reality and shows the match, never that nature is so; a detector's clock is what it emits and receives back, on its own record.
- **The click, the one non-local act.** The click is the only non-local thing in the law: the record ends at once, everywhere; everything else is local, at most one Link per interval; no information passes through a click faster than light (no signalling, proved); the click is the only thing the Outside reads.
- **No one asks a Node.** The one read is the click, from above; no place in the law reads a Node's value to decide anything but the click; the whole initial state is checked at load.
- **The three tests.** Generic (one primitive with declared integers, no family name or kind), vector (one of the six verbs on the state vector, no root, no float) and local (its own record and the six neighbouring Nodes, nothing kept at a Node): the admission test of every rule; a rule that fails one does not enter the law; a hypothesis that needs more is stated under its own identity, outside the law.
- **The six verbs.** Every integer of the law is made of six operations only: the translation of an accumulator by its rate, the bilinear form with a declared matrix, the group-ring addition, the permutation, the evaluation at the primitive root of unity, and the Euclidean division with the remainder kept; a root, a true division or a draw is outside the law.
- **The one algebra.** The whole board is one element of the algebra and the law one operator on it, covariant under the 48 and the translations; the click is the one act that deletes and the giving click the one that writes; a body, a tool, a family, a record and a face are regions, summands or names of that one element; one border for all.
- **Rule3.** The one rule is ALGEBRA.md 9.57 (1), named Rule3: every record steps at its Node's own pace from its own values and its six neighbours', the pace entering twice, squared on the six-neighbour sum (the ruler) and on the mass term with the clock's second order; every level read is the interval's start value; nothing is read from a neighbour's clock; light slowing light is a hypothesis outside the law.
- **The rule first.** What can be built with Rule3 is built with it; what cannot is not forced into it: it is marked beyond the rule, kept as its own declaration and studied to see what happens there; the mathematician confirms, row by row, that the whole engine is Rule3 and the families' declarations alone, and a row that cannot be is named with the line that shows why.
- **Nothing at a Node but the law's numbers.** No register, no table, no formula and no draw at a Node: each record's two levels and its division's remainder, the family's pair and the Node clock; an integer kept at a Node (a Port's accumulator, a body's spin, momentum or position, a record's age) is a declared family's level with the KEEP step and counts among the law's own numbers; anything else kept is a register and is refused at load.
- **Nature pushes toward whole numbers.** At every step at every Node the division keeps the whole part as the value and puts the fraction aside as the remainder, which stays at the Node, bounded, never reset and never erased, where chance lives, with no coupling and no draw; what passes to the neighbours is whole; at the click one whole quantum moves, the moment a fraction becomes whole; the residue of a giving click is the rule's own remainder, never declared.
- **No remainder, no click.** An exact record (its multiplier 2 cos omega, omega the mode's turn per interval, equal to -1, 0 or 1: the periods 3, 4 and 6) never clicks and only turns; the cube of side 3 is the smallest universe that turns exactly; exact worlds are property tests, not experiments.
- **The count's line.** The quanta move by Rule3 read on Rule3's own current: at every Node, T c_next + r' = T c_now + the current entering through the six Ports minus the current leaving + r, with 0 <= r' < T (ALGEBRA.md 9.121 item 3); a quantum moves whole when the remainder crosses T, so the click, the hop, emission and detection are this one line, the count is conserved exactly, and a moving body's clicks keep its velocity; T is the family's quantum norm, a family row.
- **The click rule as built.** Every record clicks once, at the first interval at which its running total reaches its threshold; the total accrues through the set's Ports from outside where the record moves (the detector's pace, the inward flux) and at the set's own Node where the record stands (the emitter's pace, its own clock); one click, two sources of its pace (ALGEBRA.md 9.17 (7) (f)), until the count's line replaces it.
- **The giving click and the taking click.** Every click has two ends: the taking click (a body takes one whole quantum, its content up by one) and the giving click (a body gives one, its content down by one); a giving click writes the given family's lowest mode on the body's Nodes alone, zero beyond, times the character of its momentum, no window, no taper, no period count; a body's own record is never rewritten; the word birth is retired.
- **The surface rule.** Every click is read at the body's outer shell, the Nodes with a Port, at both ends: one gather, one threshold, one ladder; the interior is never read.
- **The family of clicks.** The fourth family: its level at a Node is the Node clock, its amplitude; its pair [1, 1]; its source the content held at a body's Nodes, written whole at each click, the hold's count s = SUM_k w_k M_k with w_k the quantum's weight of the family k, the key `quantum` beside `pair` on every family's row, an integer from 1, required (the owner, 2026-09-27); it spreads by the step. Its content below zero beside a body is a bug, not a law: the count's line and the family's pair remove it, and the signed read binds only then, bit for bit, the law's two-sided guard as written and the loop's one-sided guard until then (the owner, 2026-09-27).
- **The coupling is the click alone.** No bilinear coupling between families is in the law; the world of clicks (bodies, detectors, content, the family of clicks) and the world of Nodes (the records) run as one loop and touch only at the click and at a body's Nodes; the charge rides under its own identity (ALGEBRA.md 9.48); gravity by energy is the body's energy count read at the click, with no rule added (ALGEBRA.md 9.51).
- **The Node clock and the ruler.** The clock's slowing is law, Einstein's weak field from the Node alone: with Gamma (the world's clock constant, one declared integer per world), c (the level of the family of clicks), p = Gamma - c (the Node's pace) and U = c / (2 Gamma) (the potential), the mass term is slowed once by the clock and the Link term twice, by the clock and by the ruler, so for an observer in the well the clock ticks about 1 - U slower and each Link is about 1 + U longer and light's speed there is unchanged (ALGEBRA.md 9.56 (3)); at a mass every clock slows, light included, and light is delayed and bent into it; the slowing falls as 1 / r.
- **A moving body.** A large packet of a massive family, carried by the character of its momentum and already moving at interval 0; its clock slows by its own dispersion, with no rule; no acceleration during a run; a detector or emitter it carries is a moving name, its Nodes translating at the body's group velocity; its given rows carry its motion in the given family's own representation, light from it compressed ahead and stretched behind (ALGEBRA.md 9.62 (4)); a moving rigid arm is not defined in the model (clocks and no rods).
- **What a body is.** No law is written for a body: the one rule is written for the Node, and a body is the support of a mode of that rule; a body can be one Node; the click is the one write at a body's scale; a detector is a body (a matter well with its bound mode, the Nodes that click together), its content up by one at its click and its own levels untouched; detectors stand apart so that their modes do not merge.
- **Direction for every family.** Direction is one generic function: every family may declare its representation of the group of 48 (scalar, vector, tensor), the one rule steps every component alike, and the step, the hold, the pace and the click are written once for a family with components; gravity and the charge are declarations, not engine pieces.
- **Vectors from the start.** Every property with a direction is an integer vector (momentum, current, recoil, the click's direction), never a magnitude with an angle, a root or a fraction.
- **From the clicks to the Node.** Every family's representation at the Node is derived in the algebra from what its clicks show: the smallest representation whose clicks give exactly what is recorded, nothing at the Node that no click needs; the strong and weak forces are carried this way, by generic primitives with no force named.
- **A conservation is not decreed.** A conservation follows from the rule at the Node or does not exist; no family and no rule is added so that it comes out; always look in the algebra for the generic solution.
- **Clicks go only forward; the backward run is exact.** The click is what happened in the world and goes only forward in time, the arrow of time the sequence of clicks; everything between clicks runs backward exactly, also where the family of clicks falls, and a lost inverse is a defect to fix; the backward run of the whole engine stays the host's test tool, not a law, with no click journal.
- **Where a tensor enters.** In one place only: the pair, a record of fixed rank 2 (two arms, two labels each), created only by the crystal during the run (the click of the arriving record, then the giving click of an entangled pair, its material the maximally entangled channel, not birefringent), never written at interval 0; Bell is a one-arm emitter firing at a crystal, two polarisers and two receivers, its entanglement the addition of the crystal's channels.
- **Algebra first; a formula gives, a run proves.** Whatever can be computed algebraically is computed algebraically and is not re-derived by a run; every expectation is derived from the law before the run and registered with its derivation, blind, and an experiment names before the run the vector or tensor it reads; a pin the paper cites is recomputed by the mathematician when the Boss judges it needed; a finding changes the algebra first.

## The engine and its files

- **There is the engine.** One engine, called the engine, running Rule3; no law's name and no version anywhere; the old engine's code, worlds, tests, tools and documents are disconnected from it and no old name stays in the living documents; Einstein is not a module of it but the one rule at the Node's own pace.
- **The genericity of the families.** The engine knows no family: no family name and no family role in the code and no branch on one; the files hold everything a family is and the engine supports only the operations, written once for any family, each backed by its line in the algebra; a body declares what it writes to each family, its support and its giving window, and its clicks, its own clock included, go into the output.
- **No numbers in the engine.** The engine holds no number and no formula: every physical value comes from the input files, so anyone can run his own experiments; the generator's amplitude unit A is derived as the largest value that keeps Rule3's total inside the integer bound, never written; a product of two integers in the engine is a booking, a reading with declared coefficients, never a write; a record's store across walls is a remainder against one declared universe wall L, the least common multiple of the world's declared wavelengths; the phase tables live only in the generator and the host's readers.
- **One universe, one body.** The families and the universe's integers live in one universe file, `examples/events/universe.json`, named by every world under the key `universe`, with each family's representation, integers and couplings, at most 20 families; the world file holds only what does not repeat (which body where: its kind, its Node or cube and its declared numbers, placed exactly, refused when it does not fit, never cut); the start file holds only the run's mode; a missing key is refused by name; no default and no flag in the code; the engine keeps one entity, the body, and no role: an emitter, a detector or a mirror is a body with its declarations, the role a label for the reader; one meaning per word.
- **Every family on in every run.** Every run has every family and coupling on, as in the real world, and none turns anything off; a family with no source stays exactly zero; the exchange (the recoil at a click) and the point emitter are part of the law, on in every run, no key.
- **Three programs and nobody else.** The experimenter writes an experiment's list (for each entry its family, how much and where); one generic generator that declares nothing (no seed, stop, mode or profile by hand) turns it into the input file, the initial state computed from the algebra with every remainder 0 and named with the law it was made under; the engine runs it and writes one output file (the clicks and the registered comparison with its blind pin); the loader checks the file in integers only, LAWFUL or REFUSED with its reason, and refuses a file made under another law; one command runs each experiment in its own process on separate cores.
- **The generator.** The generator is the board's own operator iterated in integers on the world's final board, stopping when the board only turns by its own clock, the stop from the integers; each body is generated alone, at rest or moving, one Node or a cube, by one unified procedure, and composing is placing, never regenerating; two bodies are two while each one's tail at the other's Nodes is below the rounding floor, nearer they are one body made as one; a travelling body is the same shape carried by its momentum, and records that cross pass through each other; with no curvature the clicks keep a moving body at constant velocity, and a failure names the missing law; a moving body is the resting envelope with the phase k per Link, k the pair at which the packet's own current equals the count's line's velocity T n / (3 Q), n and m the body's own numbers in the world file and k derived (the owner, 2026-09-27); the generator's worlds are built only on the generic engine, standing and working by the law, by playing with the engine's or the generator's parameters, never on the loop of today; the feed and the induction bind right after the count's line, by their owner, the worlds move and the record is re-recorded once (the owner, 2026-09-27).
- **A lab tool is a body.** A tool is an operation of the group carried by a body: its material integers, its momentum, at most a giving click and a name, derived from the algebra and checked at load; no code belongs to a tool and no per-Node branch that is not a group operation exists; a tool declares its orientation, never the directions of what leaves it; an experiment is only cubes on a torus, a cube's orientation has no meaning, and a direction comes only from the placement of cubes, the arriving record or a polariser's label axis.
- **A detector as nature's instrument.** A detector is one Node by default, where the one-Node equivalence passes, and a cube only where its count itself is compared with nature, each world file saying which and why; a screen is many bodies each on one Node, like pixels, each clicking alone with its place known; a counter is one body whose support is the size its sensitivity needs; a click is the detector's, reported per detector, never per Node; a detector's sensitivity may be declared per detector and family; Born's rule for a detector is the sum of its Nodes' shares.

## The step and the loop

- **One main loop file, every other thing its own file.** `src/event_universe/core/main_loop.py` takes the input, runs the step and writes the output and uses everything else; the step, the Node and the register are their own files in `core/`; every primitive is its own folder under `features/`, found by the register from its folder, so no two agents ever work on one file.
- **The step file.** The step's order lives in one data file shared by every world; the loop reads it, the folders carry no order, reordering needs no code change, and every run writes the file's digest; a change to it is a change of the law and needs the owner's decision.
- **The step is defined by the run's files.** What each Port sends, what it receives, how long it waits and what operation acts on it, all generic and local, none of it in the code.
- **The loop's guarantees.** The generic loop enforces at run time one step per tick (primitives read a read-only view of tick t, writes are applied at the tick's end), writes only as a card names them with no double write unless the step file orders it, and exchange only through the Ports (each of a Node's six Ports carries one send out and one arrival in per tick; no primitive sees a neighbour's array), with tests of cheating primitives.
- **The count's line binds now, path B cancelled (the owner, 2026-09-27).** The count's line replaces the loop's hop and the click's count move at (ii) now, whole in one pull request by the folder's owner with Main Loop's hand-over line, the shipped worlds move and the regression record is re-recorded once with the reason on its line; the generator's worlds replace the shipped bodies when they come, and the signed read binds right after the count's line (the owner's word of 06:16Z); the recoil keeps its store until the law shows L below 2^63 in every declared world.
- **The critical path first.** The loader, then the loop switched to it, then the generator's worlds; work that shortens this chain comes first: Main Loop finishes the step file before any second writer binds a folder, the loader's cuts merge ahead of it, the mathematician's generator comes before his folders with no stacked pull requests, Nature24 does the loader only and the paper writer takes the rest; every 30 minutes the Boss asks how to reach the goal faster and acts on it ([#1198](https://github.com/Closer24/Universe24/issues/1198)).

## The folders and Rule3

- **The register of primitives.** A primitive's identity is its unique English name, never a number; one register in the engine, a name twice refused at load; every primitive declares its reads, its writes and its place, acts only when the run's files declare it, and the loop refuses two writers of one value at one place unless their order is declared; the undeclared primitive keeps every old run bit for bit.
- **Rule3 takes over first.** Every row ALGEBRA.md 9.119 and 9.120 already write as Rule3, a composition of its acts or the click is built so now, without waiting for the open questions; the mathematician writes each folder's composition calling `core.rule3` alone, Main Loop binds the loop to it and deletes the loop's own method, one folder per pull request; each folder moves to Rule3 bit for bit, a changed number is a physics change and stops the move, and a folder is bound as soon as it lands, since the binding deletes the loop's copy.
- **Unweave the engine into generic parts.** The central loop is cut from the attributes, each attribute and operation a small function in its own folder behind one interface, and every other dependency between parts is broken the same way, bit for bit.
- **A new feature, a bug, a change of the law.** A new feature is requested by an issue naming what it does, passes the mathematician's line with the three tests and the owner's word when it changes the law, and lands as one folder with its card, its line in the step file, `APPROVED-MATH` and green CI; a bug (the code differs from the law) is fixed by whoever finds it with a test that fails before and passes after, in the core by Main Loop; a change of the law follows the step file's path with a regression test naming every world that moves.

## The experiments

- **An experiment checks only clicks.** An experiment's run is compared with nature and its blind pin through the receivers' clicks alone; the operator, the tools, conservation and the initial state are proved in the algebra and checked by the property test and the load check, never by a run; a result is computed in the algebra outside the board or by the board, the algebraic value registered before the run; a new experiment is a new world and no line of code.
- **The simplest faithful setup.** Every measured experiment meets the conditions of the real experiment in the simplest placement that does so, the tools in the middle of the board away from its faces, composed as its real apparatus (every block a thing in the lab, nothing declared that the law produces: a direction, a phase, a state, a pair; no block doing two blocks' work), and states what its real apparatus has between its tools and why the board neglects it, as a number against the pin's band.
- **The list.** Eighteen measured experiments, run on the board and read in clicks against blind pins (the two-qubit computer in its minimal form, Mach-Zehnder by clicks alone, Sorkin's three openings among them), and six algebraic ones, the model's number beside nature's, labelled COMPUTATION and never a measurement; the crystal and Bell are one experiment.
- **Check mode, then the Go.** Every experiment runs on a green `main` commit in check mode, read beside its blind expectation with no pin compared, with every family on; a comparison against a pin waits for the owner's word that the engine is stable, and before the Go an independent physicist reports for each experiment what nature's experiment tests, how the list reflects it, every declared input and the pin's provenance.
- **Each plays with experiments.** Once the engine is generic (the list of [#1198](https://github.com/Closer24/Universe24/issues/1198) done), each role runs experiments of its own on `main` without pins or claims, on worlds the generator builds, and reports on #1198 whether the engine behaves as nature shows in kind; a disagreement names the missing law or the bug and patches no body's numbers; everything must stand on the one law or its gap is stated with a generic answer from the Node's law or a plain reason why not.
- **The paper.** The paper is rewritten on the new engine: first the general formula and the algebra chapter (the one operator, the click, the giving click), then the experiments' rows after their new-engine runs with blind pins; it shows how it arrived at Einstein, Lorentz and the step-above formula without deriving from them, their forms only on the comparison side, never an input; its writer takes what is not on the critical path.

## The tests and gates

- **The shape of the code.** A docstring is one line naming what the function does and its ALGEBRA.md line; no decision or history in `src/`; small modules, `core/` a few hundred lines; no Rule3 arithmetic outside its one function; a ratchet test lets no file grow its prose, its references or its length, and the Boss merges no pull request that grows them.
- **The code does not grow.** `tests/` may not grow, only shrink, until it is below `src/` (a gate against the merge base); every pull request states in its body the lines it adds and deletes in `src/` and `tests/`, and the Boss checks it before the merge ([#1211](https://github.com/Closer24/Universe24/issues/1211)).
- **A gate for every cause.** Every cause found of a bloated or recurring defect gets a technical rule, a gate test in CI, so it cannot come back: for `tests/`, a size ratchet against `src/` and a cap per file, no copied setup, no history in docstrings, no skipped or retired tests kept, and a ratchet on tests that pin a GameBoard number; for the engine, a ratchet on numeric literals and family names in `src/` and no new core module without Main Loop's `APPROVED-CORE`; a finding the reviewer makes twice becomes a CI gate.
- **One test per rule.** A test exercises one generic rule alone on a minimal GameBoard, its expected integers written before the first run; every body's kind is tested alone on a small world before it is composed; the most important test is that with a new primitive undeclared every old run comes out identical to the bit, and the regression test of every shipped world runs on every pull request that touches what runs a world (`src/`, the law, the world files, the record, the tools) and is skipped for documents, skills and other tests (the owner, 2026-09-27); a check is proportional to the change, the whole suite only when the shared core changes. A fix that brings the code to the law's row moves the worlds and re-records the record once with its reason on the line, no word per case; the owner's word is for a change of the law itself (the owner, 2026-09-27).
- **The acceptance list.** The Boss keeps one acceptance list ([#1198](https://github.com/Closer24/Universe24/issues/1198)) and checks every item in the code, with its commit, its test and its algebra line; after the engine, one all-families world where every family works with every other, and three fresh reviews (an architect, a physicist, a mathematician), with an outside reviewer checking ALGEBRA.md one to one against the code.

## The documents

- **Three documents.** The project keeps three current documents (the law, ALGEBRA.md; the engine, ENGINE.md; the decisions, this file), the skills (each a short role card) and the entry files (README.md, AGENTS.md, CONTRIBUTING.md); everything else is deleted; a newcomer understands the whole project from what remains; ALGEBRA.md is one proof from the first step to today and the engine is its implementation and nothing else, a section in ALGEBRA.md before every build.
- **The lock on the documents.** A change to a document replaces what it touches and deletes what no longer holds in the same pull request, the pull request body naming what was deleted; no history note or record number stays; a gate test allows only the kept documents under line caps and with no history marker; no log is kept, and a decision of the owner replaces its line here; this file carries no line that contradicts the current law.
- **Large items in pull requests and issues.** A finding or approval of one pull request is a comment on it; a list across pull requests is one issue with a checklist; messages stay two lines and a link; every session reads what it was sent at each check-in.
- **Names with meaning.** No code letter or number stands for a thing in any artifact or message; everything is named by what it is; a location on the GameBoard is called a Node and nothing else (a body is a number of Nodes), the lattice is the GameBoard, and every symbol is named in English at its first use, its kind shown by its type (a scalar plain, a vector in bold lowercase, a matrix or an operator in bold uppercase); the Boss routes every rename.
- **Short sentences.** Every message between roles, to the Boss and to the owner, in short sentences, one idea each, the central ideas first, a question on its own line; no long paragraphs.

## The team's way of working

- **The team.** Four sessions, each known to all by name: the Boss (leads, merges, writes no code and stays available), the mathematician (the law's lines, the generator, the folders' compositions), Main Loop (the core) and Nature24 (the loader and the hard questions), and a paper writer; every message between sessions is a direct message, a Routine only for a later time.
- **The method of work.** The owner's word is relayed verbatim to the Boss and recorded; one text goes to the mathematician and Nature24; the algebra, then the build at once, then the gate before the merge; the algebra, the engine, the clicks against nature, and back to the algebra; now, not later.
- **A big decision takes three.** A decision that changes what the engine is enters only when the mathematician confirms it is how the algebra works, Nature24 confirms it is how the code works, the Boss checks that the two agree, and the Boss has told the owner in plain words what it is; the owner's own decisions stay his.
- **The short procedure.** One working `main`, pushed to by no one; one short branch per task, merged within days and deleted; only the Boss merges, on green CI with `main` merged in; only Main Loop touches the core; a result names the `main` commit it ran on, no tags; the agents talk by direct messages, not issues.
- **One owner until it works.** Every area has one owner and a thing stays with its owner until it works whole, then comes back: Nature24 the loader, the files' schemas and ENGINE.md; Main Loop `core/`, the loop, the step file and the output; the mathematician the law, the generator, the feed and induction folders and, once the engine is generic, the generator's worlds; Mathematician 2 the five Rule3 folders with their bindings; the paper writer the gates, the shared test helpers, the regression of the shipped worlds, the selector and the paper; the Boss the decisions, the skills, the entry files and the merges; a test file follows the code it tests. A thing is whole in one pull request (a folder with its binding), no half on `main`; a binder touches the loop and the step file in two lines with Main Loop's hand-over line in the body; whoever needs a change in another's area asks the owner for the line, never touches; the Boss neither splits nor moves a thing unless its owner is gone two hours; a gate refuses a pull request in another's area without the hand-over line.
- **Merge the engine as it is done.** Every finished piece enters `main` as it is done, provisional pieces included, with no pin compared; convergence is checked on `main` with the rule and everything on; a miss is a gap in the algebra, not a stop; every run is a run of the engine on `main` through its input and output, no side runner.
- **Review only where CI cannot judge.** The Boss's reviewer reads only a change to the law, a physics folder or `core/`; everything else merges on green CI with `main` merged in; the Boss watches every open pull request.
- **Propose and proceed.** Whoever waits for another's word (the mathematician's line, Main Loop's reading, the Boss's decision) proposes the answer himself in the same message, builds on it, and the other confirms or corrects; nobody stands idle on a question he can answer himself, and a correction is one more push.
- **Every task closed by its owner.** Every agent closes its own tasks and reports done in two lines and a link; an optimization, a bug or a fix is the agent's own; every new task starts a new session from the state, not the history; heavy runs go to a stronger machine when needed.
