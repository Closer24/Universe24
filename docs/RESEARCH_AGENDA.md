# Universe24: one system and the remaining research

The supported system is `python -m universe24`: prepare complete inputs, run
one immutable local integer tensor, and verify its complete saved history.
`python -m validation.system_acceptance --out runs/new-acceptance` is the
central research acceptance entry point. Its separate experiments use this
same runtime; they are not interchangeable parts of a proven physical law.

## Central postulate

The research proposes one local integer law for the whole universe. A node
reads its own old state and immediate face neighbors; influence reaches a
distant node only through successive local updates. Every retained channel
contains both integer histories and its remainder. No observer or remote
controller changes the running state. Quantum behavior, physical spacetime
and persistent organization must be derived and tested within that proposal.

global choice of the most complex future. Local selection must be implemented
by the counted interactions and retained information.
