# Staged building blocks: preparations, interactions and evidence

Base code: `b552f90dd8e19f836424411019dccd573c10fa6c`. The new research package is additive. The engine, Rule3, 48-channel node, six spatial connections and coupling construction are unchanged. All prepared sources and apparatus are installed before the first update; no observer, solver or display controls a running board.

Three bounded interfaces are demonstrated: **signal-controlled phase synchronization, state-dependent transmission, and a scheduled two-lane stop/wait/restart**. In the first, a signal-controlled phase synchronizer works as follows. A transported carrier sets the phase of an already running local clock, leaving a relative-phase record against an untouched reference clock on the same board. All 64 registered four-control groups match the prediction: 48 store a changed phase and 16 begin already aligned. The latter do not record the carrier's presence. This is not a clock started by light, a physical phase comparator, or an approval/release device.

The package also provides a common way to assemble compatible preparations, execute their controls, preserve complete trajectories and view them in the generic Presenter. A valid assembly does not establish every intended capability. Quiet waiting, approval, one-input splitting, routing and finite acknowledgment retain separate acceptance gates.

Two additional bounded interfaces work: prepared apparatus state controls exact carrier transmission, and two initially prepared packet trains stop a clock, hold its center state, then restart it. The latter is an internally driven, scheduled pause. It does not compute approval and its surrounding region is not an autonomous quiet memory.

## What can be run now

The four-component demonstration combines a guided carrier, its guide, a prepared local clock and a passive receiver. All are ordinary counter/remainder preparations. The isolated arrangement demonstrates coexistence. The connected arrangement places the receiver in the incoming carrier's path and measures an actual source-dependent change. Neither arrangement inserts a special detector update.

Run commands and component APIs are documented in the [package README](../research/staged_blocks/README.md). Its `list`, `demo`, `study`, `collect` and `audit` commands separate preparation, computation, observation and evidence review. Composition rejects disagreements in board size, initial time, preparation seed, background, complete tensor, channel assignments and declared reservations. Source-off controls remove only designated initial counters and preserve all remainders.

## Algebra and the meaning of a clock

The [algebra note](../research/staged_blocks/algebra.md) states the exact preparation assumptions. With one center counter pair `(A,B)`, all other counters initially zero and a suitable complete signed halo, the center rotates through a four-cycle. The halo remainder prefixes are `r_h + 2w{0,B,B-A,-A,0}`. Suitable remainders exist exactly when `|A|+|B| <= 2`. On `L >= 3`, the six receiving addresses are distinct; all relevant triples and inputs are retained.

This clock can rotate at every board update while producing no arithmetic C. A phase-projection readout can cross its threshold repeatedly without producing detector clicks. The counters `(a,b)`, center remainder and halo jointly carry state; the remainder alone is not a complete memory description.

An incoming whole-board state outside an exact periodic orbit cannot enter that orbit under an invertible map. A local memory may coexist with external moving information, but the complete board must retain the distinction. Candidate capture therefore specifies a finite local holding interval and preserves all external dynamics. No unobserved channel is erased to make a record appear permanent.

## Couplings and controls

Each experiment chooses its allowed sign triple before its first update. Changing `+++` to `+-+` with the same numerical initial state is a different preparation experiment from changing the signs together with the corresponding complete transformed carrier, guide and halo. A negative coupling term is not a universal cancellation switch. Actual cancellation depends on the states supplying all six signed inputs, their phases and retained remainders.

For the change `+++` to `+-+`, two coupling paths give a verified obstruction to a local diagonal-sign/K gauge. The separately specified sign subfamily in the algebra note admits a checkerboard transformation on an even periodic cube, or locally before boundary influence on an odd cube. These statements do not make all eight sign triples gauge-equivalent. Separate signed controls and their complete states are needed for any cancellation claim.

The [signed result](../research/staged_blocks/evidence/interactions/signed/result.json) retains both the same-seed comparison and complete tensor-aware preparations. Both tensor-aware pristine carriers pass. This is why a broken untransformed `+-+` packet must not be counted as a successful cancellation device. The 12 exact-pattern suppression groups still emit other activity and retain complete reversible information.

## Translation and physical light

The [motion analysis](../research/staged_blocks/evidence/motion_v1/result.json) and [independent audit](../research/staged_blocks/evidence/independent_audit/motion.json) analyze 13 pinned recordings, 389 complete states and 8,556 relative comparisons. All 78 one-step translation-covariance checks pass. Only the isolated carrier has a registered nontrivial match (one update, +X); the complete four-component, phase and coupler apparatuses have no match among periods 1–4 and the six unit shifts. This finite result is not a general impossibility theorem. The analysis distinguishes translating a preparation, sampling a moving coordinate path and observing a complete apparatus translate under its own dynamics; it preserves all counters and remainders. A moving carrier alone does not establish a moving clock/detector assembly; a coordinate change does not supply that missing dynamics.

The [light-bridge note](../research/staged_blocks/light_bridge_hypothesis.md) treats the connection between a board carrier and observed photons as a research hypothesis. An upper causal reach of one edge per board update is not a measurement of physical light speed or observer-independent isotropy. A claim that light is the sole observable invariant requires an operational derivation and separate tests; mathematical invariants of the board need not already be observable physical quantities.

## Next decisions

The original quiet-memory criterion remains intact. The original and parity-preserving solver encodings address the same `3^22` initial remainder assignments; a timeout does not mean that these assignments were exhaustively tested. The separately registered phase-memory success does not repair the original zero-change-invariant memory failures. The new phase component needs a later frozen interrogation/release test before approval or a unique directed output is claimed. Signed-coupling and state-dependent transmission studies retain separate controls and acceptance definitions.

No result here establishes physical photons, Born probabilities, quantum collapse, entanglement or a preference for more complex paths. The next accepted component must be identified by its actual preparation, inputs, measured output, response time, holding interval and composition limits.
