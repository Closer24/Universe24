# Historical conformal-clock experiment

The former conformal-clock runners are retired from the active fixed-six
model. They used a sequence of tensor and divisor calibrations to study a
particular temporal-continuum limit. The current engine has no adjustable
divisor or supported resolution-lifting parameter.

The [original derivation and evidence](archive/pre-fixed-six/docs/CONFORMAL_METRIC_EXPERIMENT.md)
remain historical. Its numerical convergence rates, reciprocal source
bounds and clock comparisons cannot be claimed for a fixed-six replacement
without a new encoding and a new proof. Rounding the former coefficients or
substituting a different divisor is not such a proof.

The continuum idea remains a possible research target: if several probes are
claimed to share a conformal metric, define that metric and their dynamics,
then check that actual clock and signal observations agree with it. This
requires a valid integer construction and explicit error/control estimates.

Current executable geometry examples are listed in the
[experiment index](../experiments/geometry/README.md).
