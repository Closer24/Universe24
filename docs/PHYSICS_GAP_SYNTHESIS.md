## What the new checks resolve

### C2 is an arithmetic class, not a complexity objective

Every legal transition satisfies `r_next-r = 2u-6*ell`. Conditional on an
official event, C0/C1/C2 correspond exactly to `u mod 3 = 0/1/2`.
This does not compare alternatives, count clocks, or select a more complex
outcome. A homogeneous single-gear control with `u=2` follows the exact cycle

```text
(0,0,2) -> (0,1,0) -> (1,0,4) -> (0,0,2).
```

It generates 32 C2 events per node in 48 updates, with just three complete
states and no accumulating spatial structure. A different coupling could
generate structure; the label C2 itself does not establish it.

In the present one-way wave detector, a single packet passes unchanged through
two receivers and produces records at updates 3 and 6. Repeated arrivals can
also restore the local memory to its initial triple. This is correct for its
declared probe wiring, but not a demonstrated absorbing-photon instrument or
a durable outcome record. A nondemolition interpretation would need its own
quantum measurement law as well.

### The square exists; the sampling law is different

A complex coordinate `z=x+i*y` has squared norm `x*x+y*y`. If a weight is
assumed to be a positive homogeneous quadratic form invariant under phase
rotation, this form is forced up to a constant. Those assumptions motivate
the square but supply no measurement ensemble or probability assignment.
Coherent alternatives must be added before squaring, so relative phase
creates constructive and destructive cross terms [1].

The present detector's tested preparation has `a=b=0`, integer input `I>=0`
and uniformly enumerated `r=0,...,5`. Its actual event law is

```text
P(click | I, this preparation) = min(1,I/3).
```

Thus input weights 9:1 give 36:12 binary clicks in 36 trials, including 12
double-click trials. Scaling the amplitudes by two gives weights 36:4 and
36:36 clicks, all double. At fixed arm strengths, three relative phases give
weights 80:20, 50:50 and 20:80, yet each gives 36:36 clicks. Coherent algebra
works; threshold saturation loses the probability contrast in this readout.

The scaling test is conditional on the intended encoding. Scalar multiples
of a single-particle ray represent the same state; changing classical field
strength or occupation can represent a different physical experiment.
The missing encoding and apparatus calibration must be declared before
treating raw board values as probability amplitudes.

Reversibility is also not a sufficient identification of amplitudes. The
prepared transport sector preserves its chosen squared norm, but an
off-preparation control has `sum b^2 = 1,2,3,4` with exact rewind. Conversely,
any bijective classical map formally induces a unitary permutation on a
Hilbert basis of complete configurations. That formal embedding does not
derive physical observables, Born weights or entangled preparations.

### Bell requires an explicit change in the current probabilistic model

Let lambda include the shared past, clocks, retained memory and common random
variables. With setting independence and Bell-local conditional factorization,

```text
P(A,B | x,y,lambda) = P(A | x,lambda) P(B | y,lambda),
rho(lambda | x,y) = rho(lambda),
```

all-emission CHSH obeys `|S|<=2`, including fresh stochastic choices at
measurement. The analytical argument and assumptions are given in the Bell
report [2]. The independent checker enumerated 6,561 local stochastic models
and every fixed-source response table; all satisfy the bound.

The deliberate C2 selection control gives S=4 only after retaining 2/8 trials
per setting. The retained source ensembles for different settings have total
variation distance 1: they are disjoint. Their apparent nonsignalling
marginals do not repair this selection. Including every trial with a declared
local nondetection assignment gives S=3/2.

The external singlet benchmark has exact S=2.8 and sampled S=2.8037. It uses
joint amplitudes and both settings, assumes Born's rule, and supplies no
Engine outcomes. Observable no-signalling is compatible with these joint
quantum predictions; it is distinct from Bell-local factorization.

A common universe or clock does not specify which assumption changes.
Possible research directions include an explicit quantum joint-state model
or a different declared causal/probabilistic law. A measurement-dependent
source, nonlocal conditional law or retrocausal construction would need its
own testable specification; none follows from complexity alone. Keeping the
present local classical factorization and merely tuning detector weights
cannot produce the required correlations under independent settings.

Laboratory benchmarks constrain more than one S value: preparation,
analyzers, separation, nondetections, marginals, stopping rules and finite
statistics matter. Hensen et al. supply one concrete event-ready example [3].
The current audit did not fit or replay an experimental raw dataset.

### One Engine currently contains several disconnected demonstrations

The four assembled owners have zero cross-owner factor edges. In real ADM
counterfactual runs, doubling a wave lane leaves every Einstein register
unchanged; changing the initial spatial metric leaves every causal-wave
register unchanged. All 21 complete snapshots rewind. The construction is a
direct sum, not a coupled metric/wave/detector model.

This does not erase the positive ADM result. Fresh tests independently verify
matter-to-curvature and geometry-to-scalar terms, analytic four-dimensional
Einstein solutions and spatial discretization convergence. Exact integer
evaluation of a selected discrete update is distinct from exact continuum
dynamics or empirical validation.

Frame48 removes the growing compiler channel budget for a separate vacuum
candidate. It still fails general gates: a constrained-slice pair develops
H=4, and another invertible pair becomes singular at all 27 nodes. The moving
probe also fails its off-axis metric clock test: squared rate 3 instead of

### More ticks, expansion and repeated bursts do not imply new structure

The current L=5 communication control, run for 600 updates, gives 120 carrier
refocuses and zero reference-board reassemblies. Its site-pattern count
reaches 125 at update 15. The L=7 periodic control gives ten reassemblies,
all with score `(6,6,0)`, and returns completely every seven updates.

The new prepared FLRW control grows its scale factor from 14 to 3502 in 32
updates while its settled spatial score stays `(1,1,1)`. The clock and
expansion are real within that restricted construction, but new spatial
organization does not follow. The tested positive expanding FLRW sector has
no future recollapse by its stated equations.

If the entire retained state returns, any single-valued state score returns
to the same value. Macroscopic cycles with cumulative memory therefore need
different complete states between cycles. The current bounded structural
proxies cannot increase forever on a fixed finite cube. Unbounded integers
avoid a finite-state recurrence theorem but do not turn magnitude into
structure. All eight new recurrence/FLRW controls rewind exactly.

## How the clock, quantum and complexity ideas can be connected testably

The most useful interpretation to investigate is one retained interacting
universe, with clocks as relations between local physical processes. It does
not require copying a global age into every node. It does require actual
couplings, a declared map to physical observables, and mutually consistent
clock, propagation and detector measurements.

Oscillatory processes can carry phase. Phase differences can control coherent
amplitudes. Interactions with an apparatus can establish correlated records.
Retained records can affect later local processes. This is a proposed chain
of mechanisms; the current separate constructions do not yet implement it
as one verified quantum physical model.

A complexity preference needs particular care. For fixed measurement
amplitudes, adding a weighting

```text
p_j proportional to |psi_j|^2 * f(C_j)
```

generally changes Born ratios whenever f differs between nonzero branches.
To retain Born probabilities for that fixed state and measurement, f must
be common across those branches. Complexity could instead enter a declared
physical interaction that changes the amplitudes or apparatus before the
measurement; those changed predictions would then need their own comparison.
There is no derivation here of either mechanism.

A tractable hypothesis is to investigate structural memory in phase dynamics
or record formation, rather than silently post-weight already specified
quantum outcomes. Define the structural statistic, retained registers and
local coupling first. Measure whether it improves durable predictive
correlations against noise, rescaling and periodic controls. Calling a state
more complex or counting more clocks supplies no Born law on its own.

## Prioritized acceptance gates

1. **Physical state and measurement contract.** Declare the excitation,
   amplitude decoder, observables, composite-system structure and apparatus
   ensemble. Choose whether Born is an explicit reference axiom or a quantity
   to derive. Identify the Bell-local premise that a proposed violating model
   changes. Preserve this decision across all tests.
2. **One coherent detector experiment.** With the same law and fixed
   calibration, test interference, single-excitation anticorrelation, zero/
   double detections, durable records, conditional repeated measurements,
   phase and scale controls, and complete-state recovery. Match complete
   distributions on held-out preparations, not normalized selected counts.
3. **One operational geometry.** Couple that carrier and apparatus to one
   metric authority. Compare independent dynamic clocks, arrival times,
   phases, lapse/shift changes and refinement. A classical scalar-wave
   analogue can be an explicit first integration test; it is not yet a photon.
   Declare backreaction or a controlled test-field approximation.
4. **Entangled two-party experiment.** Extend the verified source/instrument
   to independently chosen analyzers, spacelike measurement regions and
   predeclared all-trial accounting. Predict joint probabilities and marginals
   over multiple settings; verify no-signalling under local interventions.
   Use valid finite-statistics methods for comparisons with laboratory data.
5. **Autonomous memory-driven cycles.** In a separate controlled gate,
   implement reversible local novelty memory, a causal stagnation sensor and
   reassembly feedback. Require four separated cycles with persistent new
   structure and exact rewind, plus matched disconnected, periodic, noise
   and amplitude-scaling controls. Success here would not replace gates 1–4.

These gates identify bounded scientific progress. Additional particle,
spectral, interaction and gravitational observations would remain before any
broader claim of reproducing nature. Internal tests establish the implemented
law's behavior, not universal empirical agreement.
