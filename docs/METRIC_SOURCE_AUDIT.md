# Metric sources and retained remainders

For the active scalar law, the exact identity is

    6*(b_next+a) = 2*u+r-r_next.

Any proposed field equation must account for the retained remainder term.
Dropping it requires a divisibility condition, an invariant sector or a
quantitative error bound. Reversibility alone does not justify its removal.

A source interpreted as stress-energy must obey the appropriate balance
identities of the proposed discrete geometry. A reciprocal polynomial
interaction is not automatically a conserved Einstein source. The
[constraint analysis](DYNAMICAL_GEOMETRY_CONSTRAINTS.md) gives the remaining
conditions and explicit fixed-six controls.

The former large-divisor checkerboard-drift protocol and its million-step
reduced histories belong to a retired construction. Their exact original
assumptions and results remain in the
[historical audit](archive/pre-fixed-six/docs/METRIC_SOURCE_AUDIT.md).
They are neither erased nor presented as validation of a newly normalized
fixed-six metric model. The corresponding old runner is unavailable.
