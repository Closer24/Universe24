# Errata

The findings in the text and what was done about them. A finding is a statement of the law ([ALGEBRA.md](ALGEBRA.md)), of the first paper or of its supplement (Online Resource 1) that a check under `tools/derivations/` found false as printed at a named commit. The proofs inventory (`tools/derivations/proofs_inventory.json`) keeps each as a row with the verdict "finding", and `tests/test_proofs_check_the_law.py` asserts at every run that the printed statement still fails its check, so the record cannot fade; the same rows stand in [CLAIMS.md](CLAIMS.md) with the rest of the map. The table says, for each, where the statement stood and at which commit, what was printed and what is correct, whether the current text carries the correction and at which commit it entered, and whether a published version of record carries the finding, in which case it stands here as an erratum against that version, the published file unchanged. A new finding enters here with its inventory row, in the same pull request.

## The erratum against Online Resource 1

Derivation S.45 of the supplement (page 52 of the PDF of 2026-10-06) says in a parenthesis: "at non-uniform static paces the momentum density gains the Link-wise differences of the coefficients, Section 3.3". Section 3.3 of the paper states the uniform case only since the short version, so the parenthesis points to a statement the paper no longer carries; the exact non-uniform identity stands in the inventory's row F06 and its check (`tools/derivations/proofs_far_regime.py`, `check_the_momentum_identity_at_non_uniform_paces_as_printed`) and in no line of the law or the papers. The parenthesis is to be read with the identity of row F06 in place of the pointer. The papers' text is the version of record and a change to it is the author's (CONTRIBUTING.md), so the source line stays until the author's next version.
