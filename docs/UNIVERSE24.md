## 1. What Universe24 is

One world, one universe, one Node. The world file is three things and nothing else, `{"universe": ..., "shape": [5, 5, 5], "intervals": 2400}`: the universe, the board's shape (a cubic lattice wrapped on every axis, a torus) and the run's length. The universe file is three integers and nothing else, `{"den", "node_clock", "quantum_action"}`: den, Gamma the Node clock and T the whole piece's action, nature's constants alone; a file naming any other key is refused at load (HIGHLIGHTS.md, the postulates 1, 6 and 8; ALGEBRA.md, **The Node**; **No family is declared: the families emerge at the click**). The loader lays the seeds (light at one quantum on every Node of the torus but the origin, the hole) and reads the three integers and the shape; after that everything happens by itself at the Nodes: every Node does Rule3 and the same fixed acts from what it reads over one Link, and writes a line, a click, where it reads a whole quantum (ALGEBRA.md, **The Node**; **The board reads itself**; **The bound body's click is the Node's act**). No body, no detector, no family, no window and no rate is declared; the births, the bodies, the families, the masses, the clock shifts, the bangs and the horizon are the decoder's readings of the clicks (postulates 7 and 9).

The one run is the project's only main run (HIGHLIGHTS.md, The runs and the record): Universe24's run of its declared intervals through `tools/eras.py` with its three logs, re-recorded whenever the engine changes under it, its pages drawn from the logs alone and its numbers in the folder's notes and in this document, never as a test's pin. The former worlds (the two slits, the big bang and the cycle, the atom, the clicks worlds, Bell and GHZ) stand as frozen records and run nothing (README.md, The former courses, by name).
