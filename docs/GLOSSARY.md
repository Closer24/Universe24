# The closed glossary: one name per term, the retired names beside it, and the source of each (the Glossary Closer, 2026-09-24; docs only)

The model owner's word of 2026-09-24, about 00:20Z (Hebrew, through the
Boss, in substance): "Request a closed glossary of terms: some say the
group of 48, some say the group of 24; close on the terms, on all of them,
in git under GLOSSARY. And also our way of passing to nature, to the
experiments, in the world of the clicks: there are three worlds, the
GameBoard, the clicks, and nature; on the GameBoard we put cubes that
represent external bodies; all this must be clear in the right words."
The Boss ordered this page from that word, and relayed the chief
physicist's six glossary lines of the same night (the 48 and the 24, the
three worlds, the kinds, the pin and the band and the reader of record,
GameBoard and lattice, the foreign object and the tables), folded into
the rows below; the record of the word is the Boss's to append to the
day's log.

This page is the closed list. One row per term; the first column is the
one name to be used from now; the second its definition in one sentence
of simple English; the third every other name found in the repository for
the same thing, so that a reader who meets an old name finds the closed
one here; the fourth the file and section where the term is defined or
the owner fixed it. Where the owner fixed a name
([ALGEBRA.md 1.4](ALGEBRA.md#14-the-names-fixed-by-the-owner),
[Highlights 5.4](HIGHLIGHTS.md#54-the-detector)) that name is the closed
one; elsewhere the closed name is the one the canonical page uses. No
name is invented here, no physics is changed, and no term is changed in
any other file by this page: [TERMINOLOGY.md](TERMINOLOGY.md) keeps the
state vocabulary contract (the definitions in the law's own words, the
symbols table, the retired words with their dates), the paper follows
this glossary once it is closed, and the Boss orders the renames. Where
two sources disagree and the owner has not fixed the name, the row
carries both names and the mark **OPEN: the owner's word**; the Boss
decides which goes to the owner. Every symbol is named in English at its
first use; a scalar is plain, a vector bold lowercase (**p**), a matrix or
an operator bold uppercase (**C**) (the notation rule,
[skills/workflow.md](../skills/workflow.md)).

A retired name may still appear in a quoted decision, a log record, a
MIGRATION entry or a historical section, which say so; a standard
external term (the hyperoctahedral group B_3, the full octahedral group
O_h, S_4) may follow the closed name in apposition where the isomorphism
is stated, as the owner allowed for the group of order 24, and is never
the name.
