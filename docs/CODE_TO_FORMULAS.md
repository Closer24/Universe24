# From code to formulas: the chronology (2026-09-16 to 2026-09-21)

The model owner, 2026-09-21 (translated): "put in git the chronology of how we
went from code to formulas; very interesting." Written by the Boss from the
dated logs; every step names its record in `LOG_<date>.md` and the document
that holds it. Nothing here is a rule; the rules stay with their owners
([HIGHLIGHTS.md](HIGHLIGHTS.md) 5.4 and 5.7, [BEAM_LAW.md](BEAM_LAW.md),
[DERIVATIONS_BEAM.md](DERIVATIONS_BEAM.md)). Notation per record 184: a scalar
plain, a vector in bold lowercase, a matrix or an operator in bold uppercase.

## 1. The code era (2026-09-16 to 2026-09-19): the run is the evidence

- 2026-09-16 and 17: the Highlights taken from the Google Doc and edited in
  git; the ray-event model; the shared quantum resource deleted (LOCALITY-1
  without exception). The law is a text and the engine its reading; a claim is
  checked by a run whose numbers are pinned in tests.
- 2026-09-18: the law of the bit ("the thing or its shadow"), then the law of
  the shadow. Each law is written, built, run and pinned within the day.
- 2026-09-19: the law of events and, the same day, the law of the ray, `beam-v1`
  (the model owner: "there is no shadow, no real; there are only events on the
  event board; there are detectors by sensitivity"). One engine; the older
  engines deleted; every registered world a set of pinned integers. The
  register is the source of truth, and a number is discovered by running.

## 2. The bridge (2026-09-20, day): formulas checked against the register

- The couplings as one bilinear form over declared columns (the model owner:
  "just check that it is correct and works"): the mathematician verifies
  bit-exactness on paper against every registered integer before the
  implementer builds (`COLUMNS.md`; the first formula that preceded its build).
- "The law in one formula" (record 62 of 2026-09-20; `ONE_FORMULA.md`): the
  smallest set of formulas that reproduces every registered integer, the
  primitives that are the same thing named and proved, the four unifications
  that followed (the pointer as the first moment, every age against a key as
  one clock, one moment table, the electric column floored).
- The entity catalog, the strong and the weak force, amplitude-v1 (the click)
  and the meeting: each designed on paper by the physicist and the
  mathematician with its expected integer before any run (records 70, 80: "the
  deuteron's expected integer before any run").
- The trimming, part 1 (record 87): the records leave Highlights, 5.4 keeps the
  decisions one line each, the register becomes the one source of a number.

## 3. The turn (2026-09-20, night, to 2026-09-21): the law as one map

- The fraction-free law (records 133, 150): every count is an accumulator on a
  record, `s <- s + r; e <- [s >= d]; s <- s - e d`; the law becomes one
  piecewise-linear map **F** per interval on the integer torus, with a linear
  block (the rows, a closed form of their births) and a feedback block (the
  bodies, iterated). Landed in PR #412 on 2026-09-21.
- The model owner's direction (records 159, 161, 166, 167, 170): "a formula
  mathematician whose whole role is to derive formulas without runs, to reach
  the known formulas only from our vector, scalar and tensor activity, which is
  an infinite activity on the board"; the derivation mathematician's session
  and `DERIVATIONS_BEAM.md`, seven targets: the inventory, Doppler, Newton and
  Coulomb, special relativity, the delay field, the information cost, Young
  and Bohr.
- Record 156: the two slits' 64 registered clicks computed by a map before the
  run; the cone's pins before its run; the Bell cells by the paper's formula:
  "computed before the run" verified on the register.
- Record 173 (the owner): "first solve the amplitudes: how not to do a square
  in the code but a vector operation": the click's weight as the inner product
  of the pointer with itself through the law's one bilinear primitive.
- Record 176 (the owner, through the paper session): **the main course:
  equations first, runs as confirmation**, written into every skill. The order
  of work reversed: a number is derived, registered with its derivation, and a
  run compares.
- Record 177: the generic vector form first (a rule is written as the operation
  of a declared matrix, table or rate before its physical name).
- Record 181 (the owner): "the system becomes vectors, tensors and operations
  on them, where the GameBoard checks and computes when there is a limit": the
  statement and why it holds, in Highlights.

## 4. The formula era (2026-09-21): the code follows the algebra

  register beside it.
- The vector program (record 191, the owner: "proceed then, with everything
  represented in vectors and matrices, from group theory"); the world through a
  detector (record 196); Lorentz from the delay field ordered without a seventh
  verb (record 200, section 12); the three tests of every rule (record 202:
  generic, vector, local); a formula gives, a run proves (record 205); the one
  constant K per Node as the direction that reduces the grain's freedom
  (records 209, 213 ordered); the transformation from the GameBoard to the
  observed values, the dictionary (record 210).

## 5. What changed, in one line each

1. The evidence: from a run's pinned number to a derived number that a run
   confirms (record 176; the register's `derivation` field, record 205).
2. The object: from a text read by an engine to one map **F** on the integer
   torus with six verbs (records 150, 167, 181).
3. The admission of a rule: from "it runs and the pins hold" to the three tests,
   generic, vector, local (record 202).
4. The comparison with nature: from a board number against a value to a named
   reading, the transformation (record 210).
5. The code: from the law's home to the algebra's evaluation, bit-identical
   where the algebra says so (the click's Gram form, form B, the event form).
