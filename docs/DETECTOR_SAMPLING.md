# Detector-owned sampling and historical research profiles

The canonical contract is `detector-only-v1`. Only an actual external Detector
encounter may authorize a recorded random decision. Creation, propagation,
ordinary contacts, phase changes, field emission, strong/weak coupling and
quantum preparation do not authorize a draw. An entity name, diagnostic observer,
seed or supplied ticket does not establish an external Detector.

This supersedes autonomous sampling as a universal interpretation of
[postulate 22](../POSTULATES.md#22-historical-autonomous-sampling-candidates).
Its earlier local-lottery, bond and native-contact laws remain historical
research candidates. Their mathematical results do not establish compliance
with the canonical Detector contract.

## Configuration and admission

`sampling_profile` is immutable initialization metadata, with two accepted values:

| Value | Admission behavior |
| --- | --- |
| `detector-only-v1` (default) | Reject currently unbound ray lotteries, bonded rays and native instrument/contact bindings before constructing a world or consuming a ticket. Deterministic ordinary evolution, coherent preparation and unobserved channels remain available. |
| `historical-autonomous-v1` | Explicitly select the earlier autonomous-sampling research laws. Preserve their model identities, numerical rules, seeded results and accounting; never report them as Detector-only compliant. |

The historical profile is a research model choice, not a Detector permission or
an implementation of the canonical rule. Existing sampling examples declare it
explicitly; old inputs without the field fail closed when they request an
unsupported sampler. Preflight and run metadata record the selected profile.
No model ID, entity label or observation callback changes admission.

Validate both the parsed immutable initial state and the responsible local law
or native resolver. Direct typed construction must not bypass the same boundary.
A native program containing only coherent or unobserved evolution remains
admissible. Any instrument/contact binding is rejected in the canonical profile,
including a binding that happens to have a certain outcome: the current adapter
has no external encounter owner. This is an unsupported-composition rejection,
not a claim that deterministic quantum operations require sampling.

No physical probability, transport, conserved quantity, ownership layout, phase
rule or timing law is changed by this admission correction. The full native
external Detector exchange remains unavailable; existing output-clock/quantum
composition guards stay in force.

## External exchange implementation boundary

The adopted exchange distinguishes action selection from generated content:
`1 = PASS`, `0 = GENERATE_RETURN`. PASS preserves received content and its original
SPACE/DETECTOR provenance. Receiving a Detector-generated value under PASS adopts
that value without another content draw. GENERATE_RETURN emits a fresh value
through the incoming Port, with forward model time and ordinary Link transit.
A repeated committed decision reuses its immutable result without another draw,
output or inventory charge. These requirements are accepted; they are not the
implemented historical quantum instrument.

The following physical choices still prevent an honest full implementation:

| Owner | Missing definition | Acceptance after closure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physics | Action-bit distribution and allowed dependence on causal settings/input | Predetermined action counts and probabilities; no implicit fair coin or deterministic replacement |
| Physics | Return value domain/distribution, phase and relation to action selection | Independent content expectations and exact separate action/content draw counts |
| Physics and architecture | LOCK lifetime, repeat/simultaneous encounters and PASS outgoing-Port mapping | Symmetric Detector identities, declared retries/conflicts and unchanged forwarded content |
| Architecture, then field developer | Bounded selected-branch/previous-interaction identity, causal cancellation arithmetic and inventory | One-Link-at-a-time return; no remote/global erase; complete retained/transferred amounts and remainders |
| Architecture, then engine developer | Detector/model clock mapping, bounded transaction capacity and output-clock composition | Fixed neighbor transit H plus defined output delay, atomic once-only publication and rejected overflow |

An opposite-going ray alone does not define cancellation. A quantum instrument
or ordinary absorber cannot be renamed a Detector to fill these gaps. A minimal
PASS/RETURN stub would leave the required behavior undefined, so it is not
published as physical support.

## Required evidence

1. The previous ordinary two-lamp lottery is rejected by default before any draw;
   adding an observer or naming its absorber Detector cannot authorize it.
2. Direct immutable-state/local-law construction rejects the same sampler;
   native direct resolver construction and ticket sampling obey the same gate.
3. Deterministic ordinary/coherent controls consume zero tickets and preserve
   their exact traces. Every actual simulation run retains its canonical HTML.
4. Explicit historical candidates retain their numerical contracts and advertise
   their historical sampling profile in saved metadata. Their results are not
   canonical acceptance results.
5. Native PASS/RETURN, causal branch cancellation, real Detector authorization
   and output-clock composition remain separately blocked until their contracts
   and implementation meet the table above.
