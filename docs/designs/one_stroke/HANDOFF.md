## 7. The traps

1. THE GATE'S SLOW SUITES: `test_massive_record.py` and
   `test_weak_field_rule.py` take about a minute each; `test_toward_nature.py`
   and `test_point_emitter.py` build worlds; the full pytest is about 8.5
   minutes. Launch it detached (`setsid nohup ... & disown`) and wait on its
   "== done" line; a tool call's timeout is 10 minutes.
2. THE INT64 ROOM: the rule's totals are int64 arrays. At Gamma = 10^4 the
   coefficients are of order 6 den Gamma^2 (about 10^9 for den 10^2), times
   the amplitude bound 2^20: about 10^15, inside int64 but with little room
   for a larger den or a larger bound; the run's guard on the amplitude bound
   (issue #1085) refuses a run past A. The twist table's k (9.96 (2) (c))
   reaches 4 x 10^10 and its products need care.
3. THE FIXTURES BOUND TO THE FAMILIES FILE: a test that builds a world by
   hand must either bind `"families": "examples/events/families.json"` or
   carry an inline families list with every attribute; the loader refuses a
   world that turns a family or a coupling off, refuses an integer
   `phase_per_link` under the law on every family, refuses a giving into a
   held family without clicks, requires `kind` on a body of a pair-on-body
   family and refuses it elsewhere, requires `charge`, `spin` and `moment` on
   every block, and requires `age_bound` on an all-periodic board. A test's
   `LiveRecord` built directly must leave `pair` None (the family's pair):
   the pair (1, 1) default broke five suites at commit 1.
4. THE SEPARATION RULE: at commit 1 the loader still refuses a second well of
   the emitter's family on its chain (9.35); 9.96 (4) retires it. The
   redshift's bottom holder was rebuilt as one-Node bodies of matter to pass
   it; either form is allowed now.
5. THE PERMISSION GATE ON MASS DELETION: the session's host refused `git rm`
   of the ray law's folders and of two world files as an irreversible
   destruction, and the refusal binds the outcome (no piecewise deletion, no
   other tool). Record 2102 supersedes deletion anyway: mark cancelled in one
   file and disconnect; the owner deletes from his side. Regenerate a world
   in place rather than removing it.
6. THE MOVING BODY'S HOLD: the hold writes `now` as this interval's value and
   `before` as the previous value, at the block's current mask (the body
   hops); the dipole is subtracted from `now` alone at the inverse; the
   start-of-step hold rewrites only a hopped body's parts. Reading a hold at
   a stale mask, or asserting a cumulative dipole, fails.
7. THE EDIT SCRIPTS: an idempotent guard that tests `new in text` re-applies
   an edit whose `new` contains `old` (duplicated keys at commit 2). Assert
   `text.count(old) == 1` and nothing else.
8. THE POINT EMITTER'S WINDOW: its `window_read` digests moved 1503 to 1498
   and 1009 to 1008 at item 58 (the click order) and are recorded; a world
   file last written before an engine change carries the older digest until
   regenerated.
9. OFF THE GATE, FAILING BEFORE THE STROKE: `tests/test_json_documents.py`
   on `examples/events/two_slits.json` (the ray law's world; its screen of
   side 1 is refused by the detector rule of record 1899). It is the cancelled
   path's, not the stroke's; commit 9 disconnects it.
10. THE MYPY GATE: a new function without annotations fails the gate at the
   end of the 9 minutes; type it on the rule module's `Integers` or with
