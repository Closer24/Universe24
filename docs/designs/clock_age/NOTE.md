## 3. The three tests, one line each

- **Generic:** one primitive, `sum amount x age` over the rays of another
  number at the Node, the amount and the age both declared integers of the
  row (the age whole to `age_bound`), no family name, no branch on a kind.
  Pass.
- **Vector:** the count is bilinear in the state (amount x age, two record
  scalars) and enters the owed accumulator by its rate (`by_drive`), the
  first verb; no root, no float, no run-time rounding. Pass.
- **Local:** it reads the rows at the body's own Node; the age is on the
  row's record, nothing is kept at a Node; fixed work per row for fixed K.
  Pass.

The tests pass because they passed on 2026-09-20 for the declared option;
the word changes a default, not a verb.
