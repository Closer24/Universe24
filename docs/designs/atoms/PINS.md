## 2. Hydrogen at r = 12: the pins (GAMEBOARD unless labelled)

**Form B's pace.** On an axis a body's pace is `n / (Q S + n T_D / Q)` with
n = p / M_e and `T_D / Q = 110 / 64 = 1.71875` (today's `n / (Q S + n)`), so
at the same momentum the pace is `1 / (1 + 0.72 v)` of today's; on the
diagonal the same to a percent (the direction's own T_D). The circular
orbit re-solves the momentum: `n^2 / (Q S + 1.71875 n) = A`, `A = 16 Q
E_body(r) r / 10` with E_body the entries per shell the electron's three
Nodes receive on the ring (series H's derivation, the engine's own flight
lines: 13.500 at r = 8, 6.588 at r = 12).

| r | today: n, p, v, T | form B: n, p, v, T | v_B / v_today | beta = v / c |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | 184320, 338411520, 0.06000, 838 | 188461, 346015159, 0.05868, 857 | 0.978 | 0.102 |
| 12 | 156999, 288249497, 0.05156, 1462 | 160013, 293783192, 0.05059, 1490 | 0.981 | 0.088 |

**The closure `2 pi p r = j h`** (DERIVATIONS_BEAM 7.2). The action
re-fixed by series H's rule under form B, `h_B = 16 p_B(8) = 5536242544`
(1.0225 of the registered 5414584320): at r = 12, j = 4.001, whole within
0.001; against the registered h, 4.091; series H's registered p and h gave
4.014. The world declares h_B. The phase's turn per orbit is then 4.001
circles, 0.1 step of 64 beyond whole per orbit; per quarter orbit 1.0003
turns. The kicks: 149 per orbit of 2.42 degrees (series H: 146 of 2.5).

**The period and the return** (the criteria of series H): T = 1490
intervals, within 15 percent 1267 to 1714; the return within r / 4 = 3
Links at the closing of the angle. What the register says of this radius:
under the step drive r = 12 closed five times (T 1454 to 1658, every
return within r / 4, its count a defect since corrected), under the signed
drive it turned 0.93 and left through face:+x at 2059; form B changes the
pace by 2 percent and the closure by 0.1 percent, so the honest expectation
is the signed drive's, no closing, and a closing within r / 4 with T
within 15 percent is the finding.

**The precession per orbit, what the law has and has not.**

1. The retarded field: the proton is fixed and its flux steady, so no
   retardation acts on the electron (DERIVATIONS_BEAM 5.3, 12.1); no term
   of order `v^2 / c^2` or `(G M / r c^2)^2` enters the push, so general
   relativity's advance has no source (5.3). 0 by the law.
2. The reader's velocity term (12b.1), the count `1 - n . beta` per
   direction: on a circle n is radial and beta tangential, 0 at first
   order; the second order beta^2 = 7.7 x 10^-3 of the push bounds its
   precession by 2 pi beta^2 = 2.8 degrees per orbit if it were all radial.
3. The fan's grain, the one closed form: the ring mean E_body(r) at r =
   10, 11, 12, 13, 14 reads 8.43, 8.44, 6.59, 6.36, 5.09 (not smooth, as
   series H registered for r >= 13), the local exponent of `E ~ r^-k`
   between 1.35 (10 to 12) and 1.69 (11 to 13); a near-circular orbit under
   `F ~ r^-k` has the apsidal angle `pi / sqrt(3 - k)`, so its perihelion
   regresses by 45 to 80 degrees per orbit (0 at k = 2). This is the
   derived cause of the registered loops' precession, and the number the
   run's loop will be read against: the angle between successive closest
   approaches, GAMEBOARD.
4. The whole kicks (one shell per 10 intervals, 149 of 2.4 degrees per
   orbit) have no closed form: registered as what breaks the orbit at
   every radius, not pinned.

**What the detector at the proton reads** (the detector's EXPECTED
readings). The electron releases one ray per in-plane heading every 10
intervals; the ray on -X from its middle Node reaches the proton's Node
after 12 x 1.71875 = 20.6 intervals. A click at every crossing of an axis
of the proton by the electron's middle Node: four crossings per orbit, T /
4 = 373 intervals apart, the orbital frequency 1 / T = 6.7 x 10^-4 per
interval read as 4 / T = 2.7 x 10^-3 clicks per interval; the electron
holds a Node for about 1 / v = 20 intervals at a crossing, so one or two
releases fall on the axis: 4 to 8 clicks per orbit. The first click about
tick 31 (the first release at age 10, the flight 21), then about 403, 776,
1148, 1521. Each click carries the electron's phase at release (the family
`e` has no `phase_per_link`): a whole closure reads the same step at the
same axis every orbit within +0.1 step per orbit, successive axes 1.0003
turns apart (0.0 step beyond whole); a loop that does not close reads its
own crossings, and the intervals between the clicks at one axis are its
own period. The reading a detector cannot give: Bohr's levels and lines
(NATURE row 6, 7.2: no energy of an orbit, no transition).

## 3. Helium: the pins (GAMEBOARD unless labelled), the second electron's push stated

**The push products per ray** `|rho_e rho_k - 1|` (the bilinear coupling,
DERIVATIONS_BEAM 3.4): a proton of charge 4, 61 inward; a neutron, 1
inward (gravity alone); the partner electron, `rho_e^2 - 1 = 224`
outward. The fluxes from the generator's count of the engine's flight
lines:

| what | at the start Nodes, exact | on the ring (the orbit's mean) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the nucleus's four sources, weighted and projected on the centre's direction | 743.4 entries per shell | (2 x 61 + 2 x 1) x E_body(12.51) = 124 x 6.545 = 811.6 |
| the partner at 2r | 7 entries per shell, x 224 = 1568 | 224 x E_body(25.02) = 224 x 1.300 = 291.2 |
| the net inward | | 520.4 (hydrogen's 16 x 6.588 = 105.4 at r = 12) |

**The second electron's push, honestly.** The radial part is the closed
form above: the same fan's flux at 2r, the entries the partner's three
Nodes receive, times 224; on the ring 291.2, 36 percent of the nucleus's
811.6. With series H's proton (charge [1, 1]) in place of binding-v1's the
nucleus gives (2 x 16 + 2 x 1) x 6.545 = 222.5 against the partner's
291.2: the net -68.7, outward, no orbit at any radius (both fall with the
same fan), because the register's electron carries 15 times the proton's
charge per unit of content and two electrons repel each other 14 times as
strongly as a proton holds one; the diametral helium of these declarations
is bound only by binding-v1's charge 4. The tangential part is
DERIVATIONS_BEAM 12.1's first-order term: the partner's rays reach the
electron from where the partner was `2r / c = 43` intervals earlier, 4.70
Links behind along its orbit, so the repulsion is tilted backward by the
angle whose sine is beta, a drag of `beta x 224 x E_body(2r)` per interval,
and the reader's factor on those rays is `1 - beta^2 = 0.965`. Nothing
else is derivable: the kicks (one shell per 10 intervals from six sources)
and the partner's own loop have no closed form.

**The orbit and its decay.** The circular orbit under form B with the net
inward push: `A = Q r (net) / 10 = 41668`, n = 384530, p = 705997315, v =
0.1084, T = 725 intervals, beta = 0.188. Against hydrogen's h_B the
closure reads j = 10.024 (whole within 0.024; 1.5 steps beyond whole per
orbit), a coincidence of the declared charges and not a ladder. The drag:
349.8 per unit of content per interval against the net inward 3330.8,
0.105 of it, taking 253 700 of n = 384 530 in one orbit: 0.66 of the
momentum per orbit. So the diametral pair is not a closed orbit of the
law: the pinned reading is the DECAY, the electrons' momenta falling by
about half per orbit and the pair spiralling in (or scattered apart by the
kicks) within two turns, read on the electrons' `step` lines (GAMEBOARD)
and on the `at_nucleus` and face clicks (DETECTOR: the crossings' intervals
shortening as the loop shrinks). A pair that closes at r = 12.5 with T
within 15 percent of 725 for two turns refutes 12.1's drag as stated.

**The fail clauses.** Hydrogen: a run not completed or the books off at
any tick (a record check); the pins above read inside or outside and
never moved. Helium: the same; the electron's momentum read on its rows
at the first closing of the angle against 0.34 to 0.66 of the start (the
drag's first-order estimate, its own uncertainty the kicks').

## 4. The three tests on the arrangement

- **Generic.** Every key is a declared one of the register (`charge`,
  `held`, `fixed`, `span`, `momentum`, `phase_by_momentum`, `action`,
  `release`, `width`, `detectors`), every family a declared row of the
  tables (the register's electron, series H's proton, binding-v1's
  nucleons); no kind, no name branched on, no new integer, no new key; the
  push is the one bilinear coupling on the charge columns (3.4), the same
  rule for the nucleus's pull and the partner's push.
- **Vector.** The push is the bilinear form of the flow's moments with the
  charge columns; the drive is form B's one flight primitive (the
  translation on the momentum's digital line with a wall, `by_drive`); the
  turn by momentum is one accumulator row of the counts table (note 41);
  the click at the detector the ladder: no root, no float, every
  intermediate a bounded integer. The pins' arithmetic (a square root in
  the circular orbit's n, the exponents) is the host's, on paper, not the
  GameBoard's.
- **Local.** Each body reads the rows at its own three Nodes and steps by
  its own record; the fixed sources release on the declared fan from their
  own Nodes; the detector reads arrivals at its Node; nothing is kept at a
  Node beyond the events there. The retarded drag is not a rule but what
  the local reading of rows in flight gives (12.1).
