## 1. The doubt: right, and on the GameBoard for a sharper reason

Nature's bending, `(1 + gamma) 2 G M / (b c^2)`, is the time part (the
clock's slowing, the metric's `g_00`) plus the space part (the ruler, the
metric's `g_ij`, PPN gamma). In the force language the time part is the
geodesic's `Gamma^i_00` term, Newton's pull on a particle at the pace c,
`2 G M / (b c^2)`; in the wave language the same time part is the index
`1 + k` from the clock's slowing, Fermat's `2 G M / (b c^2)`. They are ONE
term in two languages. Issue #605 added them ("the wall's half plus the
push's half"), which counts the time part twice and leaves the space part
without a verb. The doubt is right.

On the GameBoard it is sharper than a double count, because the two
languages are not both there for one row:

- **What the wall's stretch does to a single row: a delay, never a turn.**
  A row's heading is an index into the world's table; a scalar (k) can
  enter only the flight accumulator's wall (rate `2 S_1 Q d`, wall
  `2 T_D (d + f n a_tau)`): the row's pace falls to `c_h / (1 + f k)` and
  it stays on its digital line. The light-bending note's finding, and the
  law's: a scalar enters only the wall; only a vector turns a heading.
- **What the push does: the turn, and only the turn.** The vector verb (a
  translation of a three-integer accumulator on the row at a rate linear
  in the transverse flow **V**, the comparison against the fan's grain, the
  permutation to the neighbouring direction) is the sole way a row bends.
  Its constant is the time part's: the flow is `-(Q / 3) grad a_tau` in
  the continuum (DERIVATIONS 5.1), so a turn at the clock's constant IS
  Newton's half, `2 G M / (b c^2)`, whether it is written as "the push on
  the row's energy" (#605) or as optical-v1's verb 2 at f = 1. Two names,
  one term.
- **The wave form does not add a second bending.** A fan of rows whose
  walls read k gains a phase per path equal to its delay, and the click's
  exact phase picks the stationary-phase direction, which is Fermat's ray
  of the same index: the same `2 f G M / (b c^2)`, not a second one. And a
  record's rows are still placed by their own lines: the click lands on
  the sets the rows reach, so without the turn verb the wall moves no

  form and the ray form are one deflection when the turn's constant is
  the wall's gradient; the design ensures that; they are not to be added.

So the two are one term with two faces: the wall gives it as a delay, the
