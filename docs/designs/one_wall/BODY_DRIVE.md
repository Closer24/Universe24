   by the crowd's age moment A at the coefficient 1 + gamma; a body's
   clock (`owed`, the coefficient 1) is stretched by the same A, one
   member of the same set, so its self-creations come one per (d + n A)

   (`step_axis`, coincident fires lost). With form B the drive walks the

   and the body's gravity charge becomes the rows' weight on its own
   momentum, (E'^2 + 3 gamma **p** . **p**) // E' over the label scale Q S;
   nothing kept at a Node, the clock's count as it was.
2. **The generic solution.** One set of the one wall function with a
   coefficient per member, declared by the identity: `owed` 1, `flight`
   1 + gamma, `drive` gamma. The body's two members sum to the flight's
   one: the clock carries the time part, the drive the space part, and a
   row, which has no clock, carries both in its wall. One weight function,

   declares `drive_b` or is refused at load naming it.
3. **Why it works, and what refutes it.** In the parametrised
   post-Newtonian metric the coordinate pace of anything that moves is
   its local pace times 1 - (1 + gamma) U: for a row the wall carries
   (1 + gamma) U; for a body the proper time carries U (the clock's
   member) and the space part gamma U is the drive's. The push on a
   mover is gamma_L (1 + gamma v^2) times Newton's on its content, with
   gamma_L = E' / (Q S M) and v^2 = 3 **p** . **p** / E'^2 (the one-wall note's
   section 4, the drive's integer form). The deciding worlds (section 4):
   a fast body (v^2 = 3/4, gamma_L = 2) beside the light worlds' mass at
   five impact distances, at gamma 0 and 1. The readings that show it,
   DETECTOR: the body's click on the far face, its tick and its Node,
   pinned per world by the map's body walk; at gamma 1 the tick is 3 to 4
   intervals later than with the drive unstretched (the member read), and
   the Node is 2 to 6 pixels lower than with the charge held at (M, 1)
   (the weight read). The readings that refute it: the unstretched tick
   with the pinned Node (the member does nothing), or the (M, 1) Node
   (the weight does nothing).
4. **Why do it.** Without it a body under `optical` bends light and
   massive rows beside it but walks itself as if the crowd's age moment
   were not there, and a moving body is pushed by its content alone
   whatever its speed: the one field of step 2 stops at the bodies. With
   it every mover, row or body, reads the one crowd through the one wall

5. **The price.** The root on the state per moving body per interval

## 1. The decisions

The chief physicist's, as the owner asked for them by name:

- **D1, the body's pace under the law alone.** Form B's wall Q^2 S M +
  |**p**|_1 T_h (linear, no root; the owner's approval) and not the rows'
  triple isqrt((Q S M)^2 + 3 **p** . **p**): the two agree at Newton's limit
  and at the cap on a heading and differ between (drive_b/DESIGN.md
  section 7: 0.775 on an axis at |**p**|_1 = 6000, M = 64); the difference
  is a hypothesis read at a detector (J4's muon at 0.86 c: 0.54 against
  0.42), not a rule chosen here. The body's E' (the rows' root form) is
  used for its weight only, where the rows use it too.
- **D2, the cap off the headings** (Manhattan-isotropic, 64 / 110):
  accepted from the builder; the alternative needs a root on the body's
  direction at run time.
- **D3, an idle axis with a residue** does not step (note 17's rule):
  accepted.
- **D4, the drive's coefficient: gamma, not 1 + gamma.** The one-wall
  note's table gave the body's drive "the flight's, 1 + gamma" before
  form B; with form B the drive advances per self-creation and the
  self-creations are already stretched by the clock's member at 1, so
  1 + gamma on the drive would count the time part twice (the coordinate
  pace 1 - (2 + gamma) U). The identity: the clock 1, the drive gamma,
  the flight 1 + gamma; at gamma 0 the drive member is declared at 0 and
  unstretched (the wall function's coefficient is positive; it is not
  called), the clock alone slowing the body.
- **D5, the moving body's weight** under the two keys: (w, Q S) with w =
  (E'^2 + 3 gamma **p** . **p**) // E', E' = isqrt((Q S M)^2 + 3 **p** . **p**):
  gamma_L (1 + gamma v^2) times the content, the rows' formula on the
  body's own momentum; only under the keys (without them series D would
  move by 8 per cent, the one-wall note's section 4).
- **D6, a world under `optical` with a moving body and no `drive_b`** is
  refused at load naming the rule; a fixed body and a body at rest load.
