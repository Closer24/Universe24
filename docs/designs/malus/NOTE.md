## 1. What A12's polariser needs, and which entry in force does each part

The entry [A12](../../EXPERIMENTS.md#a12-maluss-law-and-the-three-polarizer-chain-after-feature-11)
was measured on 2026-09-17 on the earlier model (the crowd form, the
feature `ray-polarization-v1`: a polariser as an external body with a sink,
2048, 1752, 1024, 296, 0 of 2048 through one polariser, 512 through the
chain y, 45°, 90°); that model is history, and
[NATURE row 9](../../NATURE.md) reads NOT YET on `main`: "needed, a
polariser as a coupling table on a two-state label of the row and the
three-polariser chain's fractions". A polariser needs three things; each
is an entry in force under the click of 7e523c55
([ENGINE.md](../../ENGINE.md), the world file's `measured` entries;
[amplitude-v1 DESIGN.md](../amplitude-v1/DESIGN.md) sections 2.2 and 4.1):

1. **A two-state label on the row.** The record's joint label (`branches`
   on the lamp, `[[label, weight], ...]`, the bit k of a label its value on
   arm k): a row born with the default `[[0, 1]]` is on label 0 alone, and
   the bit is the two states, "along y" (0) and "across y" (1). The Bell
   pair's `[[0, 1], [3, 1]]` is the same bit on two arms. Nothing new.
2. **The rotation of the label by the polariser's angle.** The `rotate`
   entry of a `rerelease` (`{setting: s, bit, turn}`): every row of a record
   at the entry becomes two rows, `w C'[s]` on the bit cleared and
   `w S'[s]` on the bit set, on the half-angle tables of 2N at scale 256
   (`U_s = [[C', S'], [-S', C']]`, `U_s^T U_s = n_s I` exactly with
   `n_s = C'^2 + S'^2`; the multiplicity times 65 536; not a click, the
   rows kept: the design's 2.2). The setting s is the angle 180 s / N
   degrees: at N = 256, 45° is s = 64, 90° is s = 128, 22.5° is s = 32. The
   same rotation stands at the end as a `sum` set's `phase_window: s`,
   "the rotation U_s followed by the label click" (4.1): the channels + and
   -, the residual amplitudes `(C'[s], S'[s])` and `(-S'[s], C'[s])` on the
   labels (0, 1). This is what the Bell counters of A2 are under the key
   (the register's L3, the four counters' windows the settings).
3. **The projection: one state passes, the other is absorbed.** The
   which-path `read` at a `sum` set (`{"rule": "read"}` on a counter with a
   detector reading `sum`): "a which-path reading whose rows go on, a factor
   that selects the label" (the layer's own statement in
   `src/event_universe/events/amplitude.py`). The click's cells split by
   the label at the read: the which-path world's cells are named by the
   read's label and then the channels, `0++`, `3--`
   (`examples/events/amplitude/expectations.json` under `pair`,
   `which_path`), and the registered read on Alice's arm took E(16, 24) × 64 from 44
   to 0 (L3). So the rows a polariser passes are the ones in the cells of its
   pass label, and the rows it absorbs are the ones in the cells of the
   other label: they go on to the end and click there, in their own cells;
   no body sinks them, and the fraction transmitted is the pass cells'
   count over the births. A sheet polariser's sink counts the absorbed;
   here the detector counts them, the same reading.

What is NOT a polariser: a `rerelease` entry on a `sum` set re-creates "one
new record of all its re-created rows" (ENGINE.md), the labels as they
were, unprojected. A chain of rotations without a read composes to one
rotation (rotations about one bit add their angles; the register's
`rotations_3`), so the chain y, 45°, 90° without the read would pass
`cos^2 (90°) = 0`: the fail clause A12 named ("the two-state property does
not carry the intermediate angle"). The read is what carries it.

The limit of the click as built: one which-path read per arm with a
rotation before it. The arm's element is the product of its factors'
residuals over ONE label (the reads', then the end's), so a rotation
between two reads on one arm is not covered (the label the second read
selects is not the label the first read selected). A12's four-polariser
chain at 22.5° steps needs two reads with a rotation between them and
waits on that; the three arrangements of NATURE row 9 need at most one
read, and the chain y, 45°, 90° needs exactly one (the polariser at 0° is
the born axis).

## 3. The pin before any run

Every number in this section is the detector's EXPECTED reading, derived
from the declared tables by the click's own arithmetic before any run
([malus_map.out](malus_map.out) sections 1 to 3); nothing is measured yet.

**How each integer is reached.** (i) The tables: `C'[s] = round(256 cos
(180 s / N degrees))`, `S'[s]` its sine, from `phase_cosines(512)` and
`phase_sines(512)`: at s = 64 the pair (181, 181), `n_64 = 65 522`; at
s = 128 the pair (0, 256); at s = 32 the pair (237, 98), `n_32 = 65 773`.
(ii) The cell weights: for a row born on label 0, the end's channels + and
- weigh `C'[s]^2` and `S'[s]^2` (4.1); with the rotation s1 before the read,
the read's label 0 carries `w C'[s1]` and its label 1 carries `w S'[s1]`,
and the four cells (0+, 0-, 1+, 1-) weigh `(C'[s1] C'[s2])^2`,
`(C'[s1] S'[s2])^2`, `(S'[s1] S'[s2])^2`, `(S'[s1] C'[s2])^2`; the amount w
and the read's factor `256^2` multiply every cell alike and cancel in the
rungs. (iii) The counts: the click's rungs on W = 256 births,
`b_k = (2 W C_k + T) // (2 T)` on the cumulative weights `C_k` and the
total T (note 46), the cell k holding `b_k - b_(k-1)` of the births because
`u = ordinal × r mod 256` runs over every residue once for r odd. The
cells are in the click's order: the read's labels ascending, then the
channels + before -.

| (a) one polariser at 45° | the read, the window 64 | 0+: 181², 0-: 181² (× 256²) | 0, 128, 256 | 0+ 128, 0- 128 | 1/2: 128 of 256, exact |
| (b) two crossed | the read, the window 128 | 0+: 0, 0-: 256² (× 256²) | 0, 0, 256 | 0+ 0, 0- 256 | 0: exact |

Beside them, the tables' rounding where it shows (the same map, sections
2 and 4): one polariser at 22.5° passes 219 of 256 (0.8555 against
`cos^2 22.5° = 0.8536`, the table's `C'[32]^2 / n_32 = 0.8540`; the
crowd-form run of A12 read the same ratio, 1752 of 2048 = 219/256, from
its own rounded table), at 67.5° 37 of 256; the chain at 22.5° steps
(the rotate 32, the read, the window 32) passes 187 of 256 (0.7305 against
`cos^4 22.5° = 0.7285`). At 45° and 90° the tables are exact (181 = 181,
0 and 256), so the three pinned counts carry no rounding at all.

**The fail clauses, as A12's.** World (c) passing 0 or 128 in the cell
0+ (the intermediate angle not carried); world (a) passing other than 128;
world (b) passing other than 0; any cell off its rung width (the wheel's
own statement of note 46, "every cell within one click of W × its weight
over the total", is met here exactly, the widths being the rungs'); the
books not conserved at every tick; a `split` line off the design's two
rows per row.

**Robustness** (the map's section 5): u covers the 256 residues once for
r = 1, 157, 159 and 255; if the rotate's re-creation counted a rebirth per
row (u stepping by 2 over the births), every even residue would be met
twice and the widths 64, 64, 64, 64 are even, so the counts are the same.
The multiplicity per path is `1 × 65 536 × 2^32 = 2^48`, within the
register's ceiling of `2^62` (the register's L5: three rotations fit, four
do not).

## 4. The three tests on the arrangement

- **Generic.** The entries are the law's (`lamp`, `rotate`, `read`,
  `phase_window`, `sum`), each with its declared integers (the setting s,
  the wheel [r, W], the tables of 2N); the family `light` appears only as
  the row of the tables it appears in for every world, no kind, no name
  branched on; the cosine table stays the declared input it is (the
  owner's decision, the Boss's record 328). No new integer, no new key.
- **Vector.** The rotation is the bilinear form of the declared matrix
  `U_s` on the label's two-vector at scale 256, its norm `n_s` kept as the
  multiplicity, no root and no float (the design's 2.2; record 105 settled
  the norm); the read is the evaluation at a label (the factor `256^2 e_0`
  selecting the label's column); the click is the Euclidean division of
  the rungs with the remainder kept (note 46). Three of the six verbs.
- **Local.** The rotate acts at its Node on the rows there; the read at
  its Node; the end's window at its Node; the click through the layer is
  the apparatus's, the one non-local operation at the one-way border that
  every registered click of L1 to L7 already is (the layer's principle 5).
  Nothing is kept at a Node beyond the events there.

## 5. What the run decides, and what it does not

The run reads the three worlds' cells against the integers of section 3.
As A12's deviation (i) said of the earlier model, the question the entry
asked ("whether a two-state property carries the intermediate polariser's
angle through the chain") is answered by construction, here by the read's
factor and the rotation's composition, and the run confirms the engine's
integers rather than deciding physics; what it establishes for NATURE row
9 is that the click as built reads Malus's fractions at the tables'
rounding (exact at 45° and 90°, 219/256 against 0.8536 at 22.5°), with the
caveat stated: a polariser here is a rotation and a which-path read whose
absorbed rows click in their own cells, not a body with a sink. Nothing
enters the law: no identity, no key, no rule.
