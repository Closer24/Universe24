# The massive record kind: the readings of the check worlds

The worlds of `examples/events/massive_record/` (its README the declarations;
`expectations.json` the pins written before any run by `pins.py`;
`readings.json` the numbers `read_runs.py` reads from the run artifacts), run
headless with `python -m event_universe` on this machine (numpy int64, one
process per world), each read against its pin: the engine's number against the
algebra's, by kind (DETECTOR a click; GAMEBOARD a diagnostic of the rows;
COMPUTATION a number of the declaration). Under the owner's gate (record 1468)
no exploratory reading is met or not met: it is the number beside the pin with
its kind and the condition named, a COMPUTATION; none is nature. The reader of
record of every clock row is THE CLICKS (DETECTOR, the count between clicks on
the block's own record over the window); the spectral peak of the summed
record and of the co-moving centre cell is a GAMEBOARD diagnostic beside, never
the pin (MASSIVE_RECORD.md section 11 item 7); both are written for every
world. THE PIN FILE: `expectations.json` was regenerated by `pins.py` from the
design's exact cone (gamma_m at c_eff^2 = cos omega_0 (omega_0 / sin omega_0)
c^2, MASSIVE_RECORD.md section 8 at the design's 14e3657e) AFTER the readings
below were taken and BEFORE any pinned run; the readings stay beside the
numbers they were read against (light's gamma, the prior file in the history)
and beside the exact cone's pin and its two controls. THE COST (the five cuts'
first act): 13.6 ms per interval on a periodic 200^2 layer (a block s = 14,
60 intervals, run.json's elapsed seconds; the design's numpy script 0.80 ms),
a moving layer pin world of 9500 intervals about 2 minutes; no vectorisation
(the Boss's word). The plan is BUILD.md (section 5 the worlds
and their pins, section 10 the findings); the design MASSIVE_RECORD.md (section
11 the order, item 4 the margin rule, item 6 the schedule of the pins).
