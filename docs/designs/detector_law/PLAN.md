## 1. What "approved" means

An experiment on the list is approved when the engine's clicks (DETECTOR:
a count between clicks on a body's own record over the hold, or a probe's
line read from a record that a detector holds) read the algebra's number,
computed before the run, within the band named for the row on the lattice
(the click's grain, the remainders' grain, the lattice's residual). A
GAMEBOARD reading (a spectral peak of a summed record, a subtraction of two
runs' block lines) stands beside as a diagnostic and never approves a row.
A mismatch is a finding, named on the exploration page, and the row is
either approved in form with the size open by name or set aside by name;
no pin moves because of it. Approval is the owner's word on the list, on
the Boss's recommendation below, after the readings in `EXPLORATION.md`
and `BUILD_READINGS.md`.
