An experiment on the list is approved when the engine's clicks (DETECTOR:
a count between clicks on a body's own record over the hold, or a probe's
line read from a record that a detector holds) read the algebra's number,

residual). A GAMEBOARD reading (a spectral peak of a summed record, a

never approves a row. A mismatch is a finding, named on the exploration
page, and the row is either approved in form with the size open by name or
set aside by name; no pin moves because of it. Approval is the owner's word
on the list, on the Boss's recommendation below, after the readings in
