# The pins under the local detector law, declared before any run under the rule

The table of the pass (the Boss's order of 10:08Z, items D, F and G;
Reviewer 3's MUST G: every pin against nature, the ray law's numbers
named history). It stands beside [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) and is read with
it: the rule is section 2, the receiver forms are section 5, the pins as
computed are section 6, the binding is 8b. No pin here is moved after a
run; a pin the script has not computed is marked so; a number whose
kind is not named is not a result.

**The kinds.** NATURE: a measurement with its one source, its medium
and, for light, its polarisation. COMPUTATION: a number of a stated
law computed on the host (the wave law's exact sums, the pins script,
a closed form), never compared with nature as if it were nature.
DECLARATION: a world's or an object's input (a width, a train, a pair).
FORM: a row without a nature source, entered by a pin. DETECTOR: a
click line's reading; GAMEBOARD: a host's reading of the board, a
diagnostic. HISTORY: the ray law's pin or reading, kept, never a pin
of this design.

**The medium column.** Nature's light rows were measured in air or in
vacuum; the rule has one medium, the lattice at the pair [1, 3], and
air enters only as declared foreign objects (DESIGN.md, the air
experiment, pending). A row whose source is in air carries "air" and
the air's index 1.000 27 for a check of the sensitivity of its pin
(none of the light rows here moves at that index by more than its
band's hundredth); a row in vacuum carries "vacuum"; a row that is not
light carries "none".
