## 1. What a closed radius is, in the law's integers (the six verbs; Bohr compared with afterwards)

    Delta A_a = N x (the sum over the Links stepped on the axis a of abs(p_a))        (an integer per axis, GAMEBOARD),

    Delta A_a = 0 mod h on each axis a,   and   (Delta A_x + Delta A_y + Delta A_z) / h = j N,   j whole        (the closure, exact, no grain: three congruences and one sum),

h). The per-axis statement is the exact one: a return whose SUM `Delta

## 6. VERDICT: NEEDS A NEW RULE

What follows from the six verbs and the declared rules, with the
numbers: the closure as an integer congruence (section 1 (a)); the

What does not follow: the transition. The law has no operation that
carries a body from the closed loop j to the closed loop i with `2 i^2 <
j^2` by one row, no rule that selects the row that would do it where it
can, no rule that returns an open loop to a closed one, no frequency per
