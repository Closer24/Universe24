## 1. The rule: when a bound body sends a paid strong message

### The two candidates, precisely

**(A) Once, at the contact (the formation).** The contact of note 31
(ix) is the moment two bodies are at one Link and one is refused its
step onto the other; today the occupant's `measure` TAKES the body's
momentum component on that axis. binding-v1 extends the same moment to
content, on the verb GIVE:

> **At a contact under `measure` (the hand-over as today), the refused
> body GIVES to the flight its held content of every paid family it
> holds: per such family with quantum h, `held // h` units of content h
> as one row (age 0, the body's phase and number) on the heading
> opposite to the refused step, away from the occupant; the body's held
> content of that family falls by the content given (the family's
> `spent` line, as a lamp's release books it), its momentum takes the
> recoil `-label` (Q x content per unit, toward the occupant); `held mod
> h` stays held. Nothing else changes: the rows then have the law's
> fates by the tables and the border.**

The rows' fates are the three of record 115, all existing rules: on a
heading a row makes its first Link at age 1 and its second at age 3 (the
flight table, T = 110, m = 0, 1, 1, 2, 2), so with the family's lifetime
L = 3 it clicks on the border `lifetime` two Links from its birth with
its content: **the released binding energy, measurable as clicks** (the
escape); a body on its line reads it by its entry, `measure` (the keys'
rule for a paid arrival) TAKES it, `rerelease` re-creates it on that
body's fan, `pass` and `read` let it go on to the border. A body that
holds no paid family gives nothing: the contact is the momentum
hand-over alone, bit for bit.

Why AWAY from the occupant and not toward it: a row given toward the
occupant meets the occupant's entry for the family, `measure` by the
keys, and is TAKEN: the content moves from p to n and, at n's next
refused step, from n to p, a swap with no defect; the defect would then
hang on a declared `pass`, and under a declared `rerelease` on the
290-direction fan a re-created unit returns to the giver (who takes it)
on 57 of 290 directions and escapes on 233, an age-dependent fraction.
Given away, the content leaves the pair whole, the keys' defaults give
the physics, and the picture is nature's: n + p -> d + gamma, the gamma
leaving, the pair recoiling inward. The alternative (toward) is stated
so the owner can choose it; it changes the integers of section 2 to "0
under the keys, 4 under a declared `pass`".

Why the row is born at the contact and not at the self-creation: the
contact is the one moment the law already treats a body as a paid
arrival at another body (note 31 (ix): "a body arriving at a body is a
paid arrival, its momentum its own label"); what a click of a paid
arrival takes is "the amount, its content and its label" (step 4), and
the contact takes the label's component alone because taking the content
would merge the bodies, which the no-merge decision forbids. binding-v1
lets the paid content the body carries LEAVE at that moment instead of
being taken: the click's content half, turned from a TAKE into a GIVE so
that two bodies stay two. A lone body never contacts and never gives.

**The closed loop of record 115** ("re-created by the partner for
ever") is NOT reachable with the rules as written, and this design does
not add the rule it would need: a re-creation (`rerelease`) goes on the
re-emitter's declared `directions`, the 290 fan, so a unit re-created at
the partner comes back on 57 of 290 directions and escapes at L on 233;
the loop empties geometrically within a few lifetimes (mean 1.24 bounces
on the axis). A loop that closes needs a re-creation on the REVERSED
arriving direction (a retro-mirror on the verb PASS, the contact's
`rerelease` mirror applied to rows), a second rule with its own
identity. Under binding-v1 the content in flight between the bodies is
transient (three intervals per row, or a few bounces under a declared
`rerelease`) and the steady state has no content in flight: the mass a
detector reads of the pair is p + n less what escaped, the books exact
(section 3). Record 115's formula holds with the in-flight term 0 in the
steady state and equal to the escaped-to-be during the three ticks of
the formation.

**(B) Continuously, at the family's rate, paid.** A bound body releases
the paid strong family at every self-creation as a lamp does, h x s per
unit; the rows that reach the partner are re-created (`rerelease`) or
taken, the rows that miss escape at L; the pair leaks, the leak a decay
rate. At the register's K: s = 1 once in 570 intervals, 290 units of
content h per turn, 57 of 290 on the axis toward a partner at one Link
and 233 escaping: **0.41 h per interval per body**, the pair's 3677 units
gone in about 4500 intervals at h = 1, and no strong push at all between
the turns (F1, F2). B keeps no deuteron stable at any h >= 1 and any K
the register uses, and it cannot bind (the paid column is refused, and
if the refusal were lifted the paid rows would exist one interval in
570). The decay rate it would read is not nature's (the deuteron is
stable to better than 10^31 years): B is refuted by the deuteron before
any run.

### The choice: A

A, for three reasons: it is the only candidate that keeps the deuteron
stable after the formation (the body gives once; afterwards it holds no
paid content and the contact is the momentum hand-over alone); it is one
condition on one verb (GIVE at the contact) with every other step the
law's; it reads no name, no family, no partner (the rule fires on any
body holding any paid family refused by any occupant under `measure`).

### The register's G = 7111 threshold under each

The binding condition of series I is the sign of the free push, `G^2 +
M_A M_B > Q_A Q_B` at one Link (the README: the least G that binds two
protons is 7111 with M = 1837). Under A the condition is untouched in
form; what moves is M_A, the reader's gravity charge, which is its held
sum and so falls by what it gave: two protons of 1837 (1834 `p` + 1
`nuclear` + 2 `bond`) read the threshold 7111 while free and, once each
has given its 2, `Q^2 - M^2 = 50 567 111`, the least binding G becomes
**7112**: a pp pair at exactly G = 7111 binds (push -6554 x 3008 inward),
gives, and reads +790 x 3008 outward: unbound by its own defect. A
prediction of A, marginal and exact, for the register's pp series. Under
B the threshold has no meaning (F1: no strong column on a paid family).

## 3. GIVING, TAKING, PASSING; what is bit-identical; LOCALITY-1; the books

- **GIVE**: the contact's give (the rule), the only new line of code on
  the verb; the rows born are ordinary paid rows (age 0, phase, number,

- **PASS**: unchanged. `pass` and `read` let the row go on; `rerelease`
  re-creates it on the fan (a leak toward the border in a few bounces).
- **Bit-identical**: every world without a body that holds a paid
  family and is refused a step. No registered world holds a paid family

  and weak worlds, all of free families); so the whole register,

  only for the new worlds of section 4, the register's deuteron and
  alpha entries kept as history.
- **LOCALITY-1**: the contact reads the destination Node's occupant as
  the step already does (one Link); the give reads the giver's own held
  content and the heading of its own refused step; the row born crosses
  one Link per interval; no partner content, no memory of partners, no
  host total. Fixed work: one row per paid family held per contact.
- **Integers**: `held // h` and `held mod h` exact (h >= 1 by the
  parser); the label `Q x content x amount` checked before it is formed
  as every born label is (`momentum_labels`); the recoil within the
  momentum bound as a lamp's.
- **The books**: the paid family's content line, initial + measured =
  current + spent + escaped, with the give on `spent` and the row on the

  into `escaped_content`); the momentum: the recoil on the giver's

  the click; `remainder`: none (`held mod h` stays held; nothing is
  rounded). The `contact` record gains `given` (the content given per

## 7. The generic form: "the field is content in flight", every family paid

The owner's question: in nature every message carries energy; a static
field is free to maintain and costs at a change; our free rows are the
idealisation whose price is three items of the gap list that are one (no
field energy, no binding energy, no radiation from an accelerated charge).
The generic form proposed: every family's rows paid, the in-flight sum
constant for a static configuration, the mass a detector reads = held +
in flight, a change of configuration changing the in-flight sum.

**(a) The deuteron's defect under the generic form: not the same; it
does not come out.** Under "every row paid" the rows that reach a
partner are TAKEN by the keys (a paid arrival is `measure`): in the bound
pair 57 of p's 290 `nuclear` rows per interval land in n's held and 57
of n's in p's, while free bodies lose all 290 at L. The pair therefore
keeps MORE of what it releases than two free bodies do: held + in flight
of the bound pair exceeds the free bodies' by 114 rows' content per
interval, the sign of a mass EXCESS, not a defect; under a declared
`pass` the in-flight sums are equal and the defect is 0. The defect of 4
appears only when the strong give of section 1 is added on top, and then
it is the same 4 by the same rule: the generic form contributes nothing
to it. The integers: with h s = 1 per row (a K small enough that a
nucleon turns every interval), the nuclear rows in flight per free body
are 290 x 3 = 870 in the steady state, the bound body's 233 x 3 + 57 =
756, the difference 114 per body; at the register's K the turn is 0 in
570 of 571 intervals and the field's rows carry content one interval in
571 (F2 again: the static field would flicker).

**(b) Series C and the bookkeeping of a static body.** Two changes, not
a re-interpretation. First, the push: a paid row pushes by its label with
kappa = +1 and carries no column (F1); to keep the registered gravity
and electric pushes, the parser's refusal must be lifted and the column
form must act on paid rows with the free label weight `amount x u_d`
(not `content x amount x u_d`), or every push of series C, 7, D, E, G,
H, I, K and Bohr changes; that is a new form of the push, the
mathematician's to verify. Second, the cost: a lamp pays h x s per unit
from its held content at every release, and a static body releasing
290 paid rows per turn loses 290 h s per turn to the border for ever;
nothing local refunds it. The refund at L is non-local (the row dies two
Links away and its emitter would have to learn of it). The local form
the question names, "the emitter's held content is the sum of its rows'
contents plus what it holds, the detector reading the whole", keeps the
in-flight sum constant but the whole (held + in flight) still falls by
what dies at L each interval: a lone body is not static under it. The
only static local form is a field row whose content is 0 at rest and
positive at a change: the content per row read from the body's own
change (its push taken this interval, |delta p|, a quantity the body
already holds: `by_clock(age, |delta p_A| x n, Q x d)` per direction),
which is the radiation-reaction rule, a rule on GIVE distinct from the
strong give and not designed here. So the exact bookkeeping that keeps a
static body's held content constant does not exist with "every row paid
at h s"; it exists with "every row paid by the change".

**(c) The cost: two rules at least, and a re-registration of
everything.** (i) The parser's paid-column lift with the label weight
question (the push form); (ii) the content per row read from the change
(radiation reaction), with its own bound, books and tests; (iii) the
strong give of section 1, unchanged. Every registered series replays
under (i) and (ii) with new integers unless the free label weight is
kept exactly, and the identity of the register moves. One rule it is
not.

**(d) Recommendation: the strong family first, the generic form the
stated direction.** Build binding-v1 as sections 1 to 5 (one rule, one
identity, the register byte-identical, the deuteron's 4 in the border's
clicks). Record the generic form as the direction with its one open
rule named: the content of a field row is a function of the emitter's
change, not of its clock, so that a static field costs nothing to
maintain (the free row of today, exactly) and an accelerated body pays
into its rows (radiation reaction) as a bound body pays at its contact
