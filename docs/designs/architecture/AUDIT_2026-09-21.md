# The architect's audit of 2026-09-21: the tests and the locality

The model owner's word of 2026-09-21 (16:2xZ, through the Boss): verify that
there are no redundant tests and that everything has a test that checks it;
verify that there are no registers on the GameBoard, because of the locality
we committed to. Two read-only audits on main at `93f46617`, after step (a)
of the speed-up of `massive-rows-fast`. Nothing here is changed: the
deletions and the additions of section 1 are proposals for the owner's word;
section 2 lists what persists and gives a verdict on each piece. Every count
in this page is a host reading of the tree (files, lines, test functions),
not a detector reading.

The rules read: [Highlights 5.5](../../HIGHLIGHTS.md#55-acceptance-tests-and-open-decisions)
and [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md) (one test module per generic
rule, one per feature of the ray-event model, the integers written before the
run; no test pins an example world's numbers, compares two worlds or
reproduces a known experiment; the suite short and parallel), the register
rule of 2026-09-21 in [TEST_EXPECTATIONS.md](../../TEST_EXPECTATIONS.md) (a
test reads a shipped world's numbers from the register beside the world,
never a literal in its body), [LOCALITY-1](../../../SIMULATOR_DEFINITIONS.md#locality-1-end-to-end-local-physics),
the change boundaries of [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) ("do not keep
anything at a Node beyond the events there: no register, remainder or draw")
and the law of events of Highlights 5.4 (a Node keeps nothing; the record is
the event's).

### 1.2 The redundant ones (proposals; nothing changed)

(a) **One assertion in ten modules.** Each of the ten readings-tools modules
(`test_coupling_readings.py`, `test_orbit_readings.py`,
`test_heisenberg_readings.py`, `test_hubble_readings.py`,
`test_hubble_stars_readings.py`, `test_lensing_readings.py`,
`test_buildup_readings.py`, `test_bohr_readings.py`,
`test_nucleus_readings.py`, `test_weak_readings.py`) carries one test of the
same rule, "a readings tool reads the engine's record and world and replays
no rule" (`test_read_run_reads_the_record_and_the_engines_world` or
`_the_runners_record`), 10 of the 30 functions in these modules. Proposal:
one module `test_readings_tools.py` with that test parametrized over the ten
tools, the tool-specific tests (the flight table's first arrival, the clock's
release, the label table, the Manhattan steps, the pointer per turn, the
exact forms) staying where they are. Keep: the parametrized one; the rule is
one and the suite reads it once.

(b) **The step rule twice.** `test_push_width.py` (4 tests, the step off the
clock with the width S, `by_clock(age, |p|, Q S M + |p|)`) and
`test_step_drive.py` (9 tests, the step drive of note 17 as amended, "the
same integers at a momentum of one sign as the whole part off the clock").
The rule in force is the step drive; the width S is one of its keys.
Proposal: fold the width's four tests into `test_step_drive.py` as its S
cases, delete `test_push_width.py`. Keep: `test_step_drive.py`.

(c) **A world's number in a test's body.** The register rule of 2026-09-21
says a test that consumes a shipped world asserts against the value
registered beside the world and never holds its literal. The series tests
hold literals (host count of `assert ... == <integer>` lines: `test_reader_clock.py`
9, `test_crowd_clock.py` 6, `test_two_stars.py` 5, `test_clock_word.py` 5,
`test_hubble_stars_readings.py` 13, `test_c_measured.py` 3,
`test_quarks_expectations.py` 3, `test_cluster_clock.py` 1). Some are
structural (a count of crowds, a length), some are the world's numbers:
`test_crowd_clock.py:114` (`lamp.presence == 4 * 82 == 328`, a GameBoard
reading of the shipped world), `test_crowd_clock.py:142`
(`round(generator.exit_tick(1.0)) == 69`), `test_reader_clock.py:111-116`
(`presence == 4 * 16384`, `4 * 8192`), `test_hubble_stars_readings.py:219-224`
(`run.c == 32 / 55`, `declared_speed == 0.5`, `initial_distance == 5`),
`test_amplitude_layer.py:360` (44 gathers with the first's cells). Proposal:
each literal that is a shipped world's number moves to the series'
`expectations.json` with a `derivations` entry, the test reads it there; a
structural count (a length, a key present) stays. Keep: the tests; move the
numbers.

(d) **Not redundant, though it looks so.** The three window modules
(`test_nature_beam_window.py`, `test_window_width.py`,
`test_nature_beam_window_reads.py`) test three keys with their own refusals
(the centre, the width, the read centre); `test_integer_arithmetic.py`'s
three `by_drive` tests and `test_fraction_free.py`'s array-form test are the
primitive and its row form; `test_hubble_readings.py` and
`test_hubble_stars_readings.py` read two tools. Keep all.

(e) **The size.** 34 of the 480 functions are `test_retention.py`'s and 20
`test_check_scope.py`'s: host gates of the artifact lease and the check's
selection. They are outside the law's rule of one module per rule and are
not proposed for change.

row (a ray's record), to a measured event (the external thing's record), to

violation.

### 2.2 What the existing gates catch and miss

The gates are `test_architecture.py` (the dependency direction; the integer
audit of `diagnostics/numeric_audit.py`: no float, no `math`, no output or
storage import in a physical module) and `test_locality.py` ((a) LOCALITY-1
documented without an exception; (b) the six-read identity of two Nodes on
one minimal world across one interval; (c) the causal bound at 1, 2 and 3
Links).

They catch: a float or a forbidden module in the engine; a rule whose
one-interval outcome at a Node depends on a row beyond one Link; a change
that travels faster than the flight.

They miss: state kept at a Node between intervals whose one-interval outputs
are unchanged (a per-Node array that persists would pass (b) and (c) if it is
written this interval and read the next); a cache keyed by Node; a rule
reading the books; the layer (excluded by design); an O(Nodes) host
allocation per interval (`node_event`, the dense diagnostics), which is a
cost and not a locality fault but is not measured by any gate.

### 2.3 The proposed additions (one line each; not made)

1. `test_locality.py` (d): after `nature_beam`, the simulation's persistent
   state is exactly the registered set (the stores, the measured events, the
   ledger, the layer, the tables, the run's identities) by name, so that a new
   per-Node field cannot enter silently.
2. `test_locality.py` (e): one interval with the ledger replaced by a
   write-only proxy (every read raises) gives the same rows and records; the
   books are never read by a rule.
3. `test_architecture.py`: a static scan of `src/event_universe/events/` for
   module-level mutable state (a dict, a list, a global assignment) and for a
   dataclass field typed by `Address3` or `Node` outside the allowed names
   (`DetectorSet.nodes`, the layer's `Offer`), the allowed ones listed in the
   test.
4. The host cost of `node_event` and of the dense diagnostics measured once
   per gate world through `run_series` (peak RSS, seconds), a GameBoard
   reading reported in the series README, so that an O(Nodes) allocation is
   seen when a GameBoard grows.

### 2.4 The branch `massive-rows-fast`, read the same way

On the branch (head `e6de713d` at the time of this audit) the additions are
`acc_turn` on the row (the row's own), the `FamilyFlight` tables (world
constants), the ledger's waiting lines (books), the thread pool and the slab
merge (host: every thread's result is one field's or one chunk's, joined in
order; nothing at a Node) and `born_recoil` (a bulk sum of what the loop
summed). None adds state at a Node.
