## 5. Why it works, and what refutes it (the algebra; nothing run)

**(a) A closed loop gives nothing and stays.** DESIGN.md section 1 (d),
untouched: r = 0 at every return of a loop whose action gain is `j N h`,
for every start; nothing is born, nothing is moved.

**(b) An open loop gives, and where it goes, exactly at first order.**
Let the loop have `phi = j + f` circles per return and give G at a
return. After the give `M' = M - G` and `p' = p - G n = p M' / M + G
rem / M` (section 2), so `p' / M' = p / M` to within the remainder's
share. On the lattice: the body's pace under either drive is a function
of `n = p / M` alone (form B: `n / (Q S + n T_D / Q)`; `main`'s drive:
`n / (Q S + n)`; PINS.md section 2), and the push it takes per interval
is proportional to its content (charge per unit of content, note 28:
`Delta p = M x (the crowd's flow read at its Node)`), so `Delta n` per
interval is unchanged by the give. Therefore the loop's Nodes and its
period are unchanged by a give, Link for Link, up to the remainders of
the law's declared divisions (the drive's accumulator below its
denominator, the push's rounding), while the action rows gain `abs(p_a)
N` per Link, proportional to M at fixed n: the action gained over the
next loop is the last loop's times `M' / M`. In the limit the same
statement is one line (ALGEBRA.md section 2 (b)): `kappa = M k_0`, `m_i
= Q S M`, the loop's constant `E' = (M' / M) E` at the same position and
the same pace, the semi-major axis `a' = kappa' / (2 abs(E')) = a`, and
the closure sum `J = 2 pi sqrt(kappa m_i a) = 2 pi M sqrt(k_0 Q S a)`
scales as `J' = J M' / M` EXACTLY, no second-order term:

    phi' = phi (1 - G / M),      Delta f = -(j + f) G / M   per return, DOWNWARD, first order, exact in the limit and Link for Link on the lattice up to the remainders;
    a' = a,   T' = T:            the loop is shrunk in action, not in space.

Compare DESIGN.md section 4 (b): the give of content moved f by `+2 (j +
f) G^2 / M^2` (second order, upward) and widened the loop by `2 G / M`
per return. The two rules differ in sign, in order (`10^-2` against
`10^-4` per return at r = 8) and in the loop's widening (none against
0.87 percent per return): two returns of a run tell them apart.

**(c) The staircase, and the band as the closure.** The give per row is
`g = floor(f n_g / d_g)`, a step function of f, so the give falls as the
loop closes and stops in the band `f < d_g / n_g`: the band is the set
of the rule's fixed points, and a loop enters it from above in a finite
number of returns and stays (nothing raises f: the give only lowers it,
and a closed loop's r = 0 gives nothing). At the atoms series' numbers
(section 6; `phi_0 = 2 pi p r / h = pi` exactly at r = 8 by the
generator's rule `h = 16 p(8)`, so `f_0 = pi - 3 = 0.1416`; `h_q = 1`,
`[n_g, d_g] = [16, 1]`, D = 4, `M_0 = 1836`), the recurrence `phi_{k+1}
= phi_k (M_k - G_k) / M_k`, `G_k = 4 floor(16 (phi_k - floor(phi_k)))`,
`M_{k+1} = M_k - G_k`, GAMEBOARD by formula:

## 9. What it is not

- No change to the six verbs: the rule composes verbs 1 and 6 and the
  law's paid release; no seventh verb, no root, no float.
- No change to the one reading set: the body reads its own momentum and
  its own content (its record) and never its own rows; the given rows
  are read by others as any rows, their momentum taken at a click.
- No change to the push or to the meeting's reading: a given row pushes
  nothing (a paid row) and its excess is read by no crowd.
- No change to the give's arithmetic of DESIGN.md (the give rows, r, g,
  the pair, the band, the one-sided band of section 5 (d)); the one
  change is the momentum the given row carries and the recoil the body
  takes.
- Not a fold: a row of momentum n per unit of content is not a ray of
  this law (note 18); the identity says so and stays outside the law
  until the owner's word, off by default, every registered world byte
  identical without it.
- The alternative of DESIGN.md (the give of content, `atom-give-v1`)
  stays designed there with its finding (a slow outward evaporation,
  never a closure) and is not built; a world declares one of the two
  identities or neither.
- The planets' correspondence limit (DESIGN.md section 6) holds: a
  body of content `2^24` gives at most 15 quanta per row per return,
  its fraction moving by at most `(j + f) x 60 / 2^24` per return; the
  rule's effect vanishes as `h_q n_g / (d_g M)`, compared with and not
  put in.
