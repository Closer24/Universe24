## 1. The identity and its keys

**The world key** `massive_rows: true`, absent by default: without it
no family may be declared massive, every registered world is byte
identical in `events.jsonl` and `state.json` (the meeting's and
optical-v1's precedent), and `run.json` carries `massive-rows-v1` under
`hypotheses` when a world declares it.

**The family's keys.** A paid family (`quantum` from 1: the content M of
one unit of amount, the existing key) with the flag `massive: true`:

| what | the key | the value in the pin | where it acts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M, the content of one row | `quantum` (existing) | 64 | the click's placed content; `E'_0` |
| `E'_0`, the rest energy | none: `Q S M` from the world's `width` S and the family's M, formed at load | 4096 (S = 1) | the wall `E'` |
| the pace wall `E'_D` | none: `isqrt(E'_0^2 + 3 p_D . p_D)` per direction at load (the class of `T_D`'s load-time `isqrt`, 17.6 M3's initial `E'`) | 4113 on every direction of the pin's table | the flight's accumulator |
| the turn | the world's `action` h (existing) with the flag: `by_drive(acc_turn, abs(p_a) N, h)` per axis Link | h = 1024: 13.75 steps per Link on a heading | the row's phase |
| the momentum label p | the lamp's key `momentum` (new): the magnitude of every row's label, in label units | 220 | `p_D` per direction at load |

Refused at load, naming the key: `massive` without `massive_rows`;
`massive` with `phase_per_link` (a massive row turns per Link, never per
interval: 23.2's reason, the temporal turn's fringes below the Link);
`massive` on a family without a phase circle or with `quantum` 0 (a free
family is never massive: its rows are a body's field, section 4);
`massive` without the world's `action`; a lamp of a massive family
without `momentum`, or `momentum` on a lamp of a family that is not
massive, or `momentum` below 1; `E'_D^2` or `abs(p_a) N` beyond `2^62 -
1`; a `rerelease` of a massive family's row on a direction lacking its
table entry (every direction of the world's table has one: section 2).

**The momentum label per direction.** At load, for every direction
**D** of the world's table (the six headings and the declared), `p_D` is
the integer vector nearest `p D / abs(D)` by `unit_label`'s own rule with
p in place of Q (`k(abs(a)) = (isqrt((2 p abs(a))^2 // n) + 1) // 2`, n
= `abs(D)^2`, the sign restored: `p_{-D} = -p_D` and `p_{gD} = g p_D` for
the 48 signed axis permutations), so that every row of a record has the
one magnitude p within the rounding: over the pin's table `abs(p_D)` runs
219.32 to 220.64 (0.29 percent, the map's 1) and `E'_D` is 4113 on every
direction. Why not `p = k D` (23.1's form for the identity with the
photon): on a fan whose `abs(D)` runs from 1 to 48 an integer k cannot
give one magnitude to every row, and the pin needs one (the same `abs(p)`
on every row of a record, section 23.3); the identity with the photon is
the primitive's (section 2), not the label's.

## 2. The flight and the turn of a massive row

**The flight.** Today's row walks its direction's Bresenham line by one
Manhattan accumulator formed off its age, the rate `2 S_1 Q` against the
wall `2 T_D` started at the half (`Flight.accumulator`, BEAM_LAW note 41
(viii); the count `by_drive_rows`, record 299). A massive row walks the
SAME line by the same primitive with the family's table in place of the
direction's: the rate `2 abs(p_D)_1` (the Manhattan norm of its label)
against the wall `2 E'_D`, started at `E'_D`, so its Manhattan pace is
`abs(p_D)_1 / E'_D` and its Euclidean pace `abs(p_D)_2 / E'_D = p /
E'` on every direction within the rounding (the map's 1: 0.05332 to
0.05364 over the pin's table, the mean 0.05349 = 0.0926 of the rows'
`1 / sqrt 3`); the deficits of the line are today's. The cap is the
square's own: `E'^2 >= 3 p . p` gives `p / E' <= 1 / sqrt 3`. The photon
is this primitive at `E'_0 = 0` with `p = Q D`: the rate `2 S_1 Q`, the
wall `2 isqrt(3 Q^2 abs(D)^2) = 2 T_D` to the integer (23.1). Today's
photon families keep today's table untouched (section 7); the identity
is a theorem of the primitive and a unit test, not a change of the
photon's flight.

**The turn.** At every Link the row crosses on the axis a its phase
turns by `by_drive(acc_turn, abs(p_{D,a}) N, h)` steps (7.2's turn by
momentum, the body's rule of `engine.py` on the row: the accumulator
gains `abs(p_a) N`, the count is the whole part in units of h, the
remainder stays), and by nothing per interval of age. On a monotone
digital line the sum of `abs(p_a)` over the Links stepped is `p . (x -
x_0)` exactly, so the phase at the Node **x** is `phi_0 + (N / h) p . (x
- x_0)` to the accumulator's remainder: the plane wave on the Nodes, de
Broglie's `h / p` (23.1). One new row column, `acc_turn` (an int64
accumulator, 0 on every row of a family that is not massive): the row's
phase column stays the phase as today.

**The birth.** A lamp of a massive family births as today's lamp does
(ENGINE.md, `lamp`: at a self-creation with a release, as many records as
the rate says units per direction, each one row of amount 1 per
direction, the multiplicity the directions' count, the identity the
lamp's number and the birth's ordinal, u by the wheel), the row's
momentum label its direction's `p_D`, its energy `E'_D`; the lamp's
momentum takes `-p_D` per row born (the recoil, as today's lamp takes the
photon's label). The birth phase is the wheel's u; the exact phase at
the click (note 45, `exact_phase`) reads today's row's phase per interval
of age and the flight's count: a massive row's phase changes at Links
alone, so at the click its phase IS the phase column after its last
Link, and `exact_phase` is bypassed for the massive family (the reading
`where` names it: no interpolation, no rate per interval).

**The re-release** (the openings of the pin world, a `rerelease` entry
with `weights` on the fan): an arriving massive row of amount w is
re-emitted as today's split re-emits it, the rows `(w a_i, m x A, p +
t_i)` on the directions, each new row at age 0 with ITS direction's
`p_{D_i}` and `E'_{D_i}` (the same p, the elastic re-emission of one
quantum over the fan), the re-emitter taking `p_{D_i} - p_D` per row on
its own momentum (a fixed wall: no step), as it takes the photon's
change of label. The map's 3: the openings of the pin world re-release
one row as 1327 rows of amounts 123 to 5619, the copies of one quantum,
as the photon's.

## 3. The click: the gathered quantum placed as a body

The click is the click as built (7e523c55: the Gram form on the
phase-count vectors, the exact phase, the wheel, the ladder over the
cells; BEAM_LAW notes 45 and 46): the massive record offers at the sets
it reaches with its rows' phases, completes when its live count reaches
0, and the ladder chooses one cell and one Node. What is new is what the
chosen row hands over at its Node under `measure`:

1. **the content**: ONE quantum, `M = quantum` (the content of the
   birth's one unit of amount), whatever the amplitude weight `w a_i` the
   split gave the chosen row; the record's other units, the chosen row's
   surplus included, are cancelled as the photon's are (the `cancelled`
   lines; the books' identity `initial + released = current + escaped +
   absorbed + cancelled` unchanged, the `click` line's `content` M);
2. **the momentum**: the chosen row's `p_D`, on the event's `momentum`
   (the books' momentum line as a `measure` click writes it today);
3. **the body**: the measured event at the click's Node gains M of the
   massive family in its `held` (the existing key: "the content the event
   holds of families other than its own") and `p_D` on its momentum. A
   fixed event (a screen) takes both without stepping, as series D's
   source takes the probe's push; a free event is a body from then on as
   series H's electron is, its content the sum, pushed and stepping by
   the drive, its self-creations releasing its own family's free rows as
   its field. A click on a set whose Node carries no event is refused at
   load as today (the validator's rule).

So the two representations meet at the click and nowhere else (23.5):
matter in flight a record of massive rows, matter after the click a body.
**The bound electron stays a body**: series H's electron (the free family
`e`, charge -15, content 1836 on three Nodes, turning its phase by the
action per Link, pushed by the proton's crowd) is untouched by this
design, and a body never becomes a record (no rule of the GameBoard
turns held content into rows of a massive family; a lamp is the only
birth). The standing record of rows under a potential (23.4) is named and
not designed here: the free particle first.

## 5. The three tests, on paper

- **Generic.** One primitive per rate with declared integers (**p**, M,
  h), the same accumulator for every row massless or massive (the photon
  its `E'_0 = 0` case), the flag `massive` a declared key of the family
  and not a name branched on; the click's placement adds declared
  integers (M, `p_D`) to the existing keys `held` and `momentum` of the
  event at the Node; no kind, no family name, no new verb.
- **Vector.** The flight the translation on the row's digital line with
  a wall (T); the exact square `E'_0^2 + 3 p . p` a bilinear form (B);
  `E'` by comparisons at load, the one `isqrt` per direction in the class
  of `T_D`'s (D); the turn the group-ring addition of steps on the circle
  with the remainder kept (Euclidean division); the placement the
  addition of content and momentum; no root at run time, no float, every
  intermediate within `2^62` (the map's 2).
- **Local.** The row's own record and its direction's table; a free row
  reads nothing of any Node; the re-release at its own Node; the click
  through the layer as every registered click (the apparatus's one
  non-local operation at the one-way border); the placed content and
  momentum at the click's Node alone. Nothing is kept at a Node beyond
  the events there.

## 7. The host cost, and one choice for the Boss

A massive row lives from its birth to its click about 940 intervals
where the photon's lives 86 (the map's 5: the pace 0.0926 c), so with a
birth per interval the rows in flight at once are about 11 times
`slits_huygens`', and the work per interval with them: `slits_matter` at
4096 births and 5250 intervals, about 4.9 hours on the host that ran
`slits_huygens` in 1334 s; at 1024 births (the wheel [633, 1024], the
golden rate at W = 1024) and 2200 intervals, about 2.1 hours, the pin's
Pearson and visibility read on 1024 clicks (the map's clicks at 1024 and
4096 births read the same cells within one, TWO_SLITS.md section 8) and
the cell counts' widths a quarter of the pin's. The choice of W is the
Boss's; the pin above is written at 4096.
