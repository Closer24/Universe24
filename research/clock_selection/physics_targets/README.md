# Physical targets and the present board evidence

These five notes translate physical questions into possible future detector
records. They are research requirements, not demonstrations of the five domains
in Universe24. Each note distinguishes established physical evidence, exact
board algebra, finite computational observations and proposed tests. Sources
and their reading scope are recorded in the individual notes.

| Domain | Operational target | First missing board capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Electric charge and electromagnetism](charge_em.md) | Charge-dependent deflection, local transported balance, retardation and a common distance/velocity response law | Independently identified mobile structures with a calibrated signed response; a conserved raw account alone is insufficient |
| [Electronic and chemical binding](electronic_binding.md) | Formation/dissociation, spectra, ionization, occupation and exchange-compatible records | A persistent interacting composite with identifiable products and calibrated probes; electronic statistics and energy remain separate requirements |
| [Strong interaction](strong.md) | Composite spectra, confinement-related behavior and scale-dependent scattering | Constituents and interaction labels that predict held-out collisions; the eight sign triples are not eight gluons |
| [Weak interaction](weak.md) | Species-changing decay records, lifetimes, angular/polarization response and parity asymmetry | Distinguishable persistent species, internal elapsed-time records and source-controlled transformations; clock period is not decay lifetime |
| [Gravity](gravity.md) | Universal motion, sustained clock-rate shifts, signal delay/deflection and one effective geometry | Mobile objects, operational clock comparisons and an independently calibrated source; a phase reset is not time dilation |

Chemical binding is primarily an electromagnetic and quantum many-body problem,
not an additional fundamental interaction. These headings organize research
responsibility; they do not assert five new forces or five implemented modules.

The separate [mass and motion families note](mass_families.md) specifies
prospective dispersion, inertial and moving-clock tests. It is not a sixth force
or an executed result. The [fixed-ratio mathematical candidate](../fixed_ratio_candidate.md)
has an additional immutable coefficient field; denominators above1 enlarge the remainder domains.
the baseline engine and Presenter do not implement it. A coefficient attached
to a lattice site is not automatically a property transported by a moving object.

## Shared algebra and observation contract

- [Clock and conditional-response algebra](../algebra.md) states the exact
  update identities and constraints on internal history-dependent mechanisms.
- [Arithmetic click classes and body boundaries](../click_algebra.md) defines
  C0/C1/C2, their event guard, core versus halo, and counterexamples to reading
  C0 as automatic route selection. Its old-structure trace count is explicitly
  passive reanalysis, not a newly replayed board experiment.
- [Interacting structures and binding](../forces_algebra.md) separates prepared
  recurrence, formation, disruption and identifiable dissociation. It also
  states why moving activity centroids cannot substitute for tracked objects.
- [Operational criteria](../physics.md) fixes the distinction between board
  updates, returned carriers, trial eligibility, approval and persistent records.
  Its effective-ratio section requires named input/output observables, frozen
  latency and a residual bound across a behavior family. Runtime `num=den=1`
  remains unchanged; one clock frequency or finite count is not an effective law.
- [Composition and recurrence](../dynamics.md) explains full interfaces, retained
  wakes and the limits of interpreting recurrent motion as a search.

Every candidate must evolve solely through unchanged synchronous Rule3 with
one fixed initial tensor. All actual sources, probes, receivers and records
must be board states. Remainder fields are part of each initial preparation;
removing only counters can leave a physically consequential apparatus defect.
No score, event class, plotted curve or Presenter control changes the trajectory.

## Evidence available in this research namespace

The [research README](../README.md) links the complete frozen studies, finite
results, independent audits and unchanged Presenter exports. Those records
concern prepared clocks, carrier transmission and recurrence. No entry above
has yet been validated as a physical interaction or a derivation of quantum
measurement, a Born rule or a preferred-complexity principle.

Exact whole-board recurrence and persistent local memory have different
implications. Once the complete state repeats under a fixed deterministic
update, all future board states repeat too. Continued local clicks on that
orbit do not by themselves demonstrate accumulation of new trials or elapsed
cycle count. Local settling can still preserve information in the unobserved
remainder of the full board; that information must be retained and inverted.
