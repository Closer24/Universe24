# Weak interaction: operational targets for Universe24

Status: a prospective physics program, not a demonstrated weak interaction.
Prepared clock phases, signed coupling entries and arithmetic C0/C1/C2 classes
have not been identified with weak charges, particle flavors or decay channels.
This note adds no decay law, random event generator or runtime state change.

## What the measured target contains

Weak-interaction evidence includes identified daughter particles and their
timing, energy and directional distributions. For example, the MuLan experiment
inferred a positive-muon lifetime from a large set of timed positron detections,
using distinct target arrangements and a segmented scintillator detector [1].
An automaton clock that repeats every four updates is a different observation:
its period alone gives neither an unstable particle nor a decay probability.

Parity tests compare physically defined mirror-related observations. Wu and
colleagues measured the angular distribution of electrons from polarized
cobalt-60, reversed the polarization and checked instrumental alternatives [2].
A preference for one lattice axis or a selected channel label is insufficient.

Muon-decay data test more than one lifetime. The PDG review expresses the
electron energy/angular distributions using measurable parameters and the
V−A interaction; this supplies a family of targets for a proposed effective
chiral interaction [3]. Separately, neutrino flavor is inferred through the
particles produced in weak detection. Propagation-induced flavor oscillation
and decay are distinct phenomena, even though weak production/detection makes
both observable [4].

## Necessary model objects

Before assigning weak-interaction language, register all of the following:

1. **Species and preparation.** Persistent, repeatably preparable internal
   structures with fixed full-state recognition and distinguishable detector
   responses. A name attached to a clock phase is not a derived species.
2. **Charge and accounting.** Explicit mathematical quantities with proved or
   bounded conservation laws and their mappings to measured particle labels.
   The existing algebraic Q and counter norm are not established physical
   energy. Daughter states and all outgoing unobserved information must be kept.
3. **Decay and records.** A parent recognition predicate, admissible daughter
   patterns, spatial registration regions, an event window and no-event outcome.
   A parent disappearing from one crop is not enough; test propagation out of
   the crop, temporary deformation and later return as competing explanations.
4. **Time and calibration.** Record board updates separately from a physically
   proposed internal elapsed-time register. Phase modulo four is not an
   accumulated clock reading. Freeze the conversion/calibration before fitting
   any lifetime or comparing differently moving preparations.
5. **Parity and chirality.** Derive how the proposed momentum, spin/polarization,
   source, apparatus and records transform. A spatial spinor representation or
   an allowed signed mirror of channel algebra does not by itself provide the
   measured physical transformation or a chiral interaction.

These prerequisites are not currently supplied by the clock-selection studies.
Those studies can develop causal interfaces needed by future detectors, but
cannot inherit the weak-interaction interpretation from their C labels.

## A minimal falsifiable sequence

The following is a proposed protocol template. It has not been executed and is
not permission to change observation definitions after examining trajectories.

| Gate | Fixed observable | Discriminating controls and failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local transformation | Parent leaves its declared state and specified daughter records appear | Isolated propagation, translated preparation, retained parent-only controls, full-board inverse; disappearance without identified output fails |
| Lifetime family | Survival curve S(k) and daughter-channel frequencies over a declared ensemble | Keep censored/no-decay runs and all preparation weights; compare stable orbit, deterministic-delay and nonconstant-hazard alternatives; one tuned decay time fails |
| Directional asymmetry | A=(N_forward−N_backward)/(N_forward+N_backward), relative to an internally defined polarization | Reverse polarization, exchange detector sides, mirror the entire preparation, rotate it through valid lattice orientations, and test unpolarized controls; a fixed apparatus/axis bias fails |
| Effective chiral response | Joint energy, angle and polarization-dependent record distributions | Calibrate the underlying observables, fit one declared parameter family and test unseen preparations; channel signs or one asymmetric histogram alone fail |
| Flavor-sensitive records | A fixed detector distinguishes prepared species and later outputs | Independently test source purity, misidentification and detection efficiency; distinguish propagation-induced oscillations from disappearance or decay |

For deterministic board dynamics, probabilities require an explicitly weighted
ensemble of initial preparations or a separately justified sampling procedure.
Preserve that procedure across all controls. An observed survival curve may be
compared with `S(k)=(1-h)^k` on a declared finite interval, but constant hazard is
a test hypothesis, not a permitted update instruction. Report residuals and
confidence intervals appropriate to the actual sampling; do not force an
exponential or drop runs which fail to decay. Operational time units and species
labels must not be refitted independently to each ensemble.

Weak-specific evidence would require several compatible distributions and
rates under one effective description with stated errors. A successful local
transformation alone earns only that narrower name. The first useful milestone
is therefore a reproducible parent-to-daughter-like internal transformation with
a persistent record and complete information accounting; numerical comparison
to measured weak lifetimes should wait for the prerequisite observables.

## Reversibility and apparent local decay

Rule3's full-state inverse must recover every complete preparation, including
fields outside the selected parent/detector region. A locally lost parent may
coexist with reversibility when its distinguishing information moves into other
states. Equal local daughter records do not imply equal full final boards.
Audit explicit outside-region differences and complete rewinds; never delete a
parent state or prescribe a decay probability to obtain an apparent arrow.

Invertibility is also distinct from physical time-reversal symmetry. Claiming
or rejecting the latter requires a derived transformation of the proposed
physical observables and their preparations. Calling `inverse()` is an audit,
not a new interaction or a simulated physical time-reversal experiment.
