# Gravity: targets expressed through clocks, signals and records

Status: sourced physical targets and a proposed research program, not a gravity
result for Universe24. Sources were checked on 2026-11-08. No new gravitational
simulation is claimed in this note. The model remains one board, 48 channels,
six spatial neighbors, fixed pre-run couplings and unchanged synchronous Rule3.

For subsequent mathematical and computational results, use the
[current clock and gravity status](../../../docs/GRAVITY_STATUS.md). It includes
the restricted all-48-channel two-probe `4/5` rate coincidence and the autonomous
finite-guide echo component. Neither result establishes the physical benchmarks
in this target sheet.

## What the physical evidence asks us to reproduce

Gravity is not established by attractive-looking activity or by naming a
coupling object a tensor. Relevant observations relate identifiable objects,
clock comparisons and propagating signals across many preparations.
No assignment of C0, C1 or C2 to gravity is made. Intrinsic skin events, delayed
events and coincidences must first be distinguished by their actual internal
and external input histories; their arithmetic names supply no force law.

| Target | Physical evidence | Required eventual click-level counterpart |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Universal free fall | MICROSCOPE compared titanium/platinum test masses and found no composition-dependent violation within its reported uncertainty [1]. | Different stable model-object families in a declared test-body regime, prepared with equivalent positions and velocities, yield the same calibrated free-fall response within a declared error, with no species-specific fitting. |
| Gravitational clock-rate shift | Optical-clock experiments resolve height-dependent rate differences and separately test motional time dilation [2]. | A persistent fractional rate difference between calibrated local clock/record systems, separated from phase reset, Doppler effects and detector latency. |
| Deflection and propagation delay | Cassini radio-link observations constrain the joint relativistic description of solar signal delay and deflection [3]. | Source-dependent changes in signal arrival directions and round-trip record intervals, with the same source parameters that predict object and clock responses. |
| Orbital dynamics and relativistic timing | The S2 study detected the combined gravitational-redshift and transverse-Doppler contribution near pericentre [4]. | Moving, identifiable objects and independent record-based timing must fit a common effective law, with kinematic and source-induced contributions separately controlled. |

These are examples of established experiments, not a claim that this short list
exhausts gravity tests or that each measurement separately proves all of general
relativity. In particular, the S2 observation does not isolate gravitational
redshift by ignoring transverse Doppler shifts.
Cassini measured radio frequency shifts associated with propagation delay;
the deflection connection is through the shared relativistic parameter, not a
claim that Cassini independently measured every lensing observable.

## Calibration must precede a gravity claim

1. Define stable objects, their identity under motion, local clocks and persistent
   records using the same counter/remainder states. A moving observation window
   is not a moving observer. A four-phase orbit does not itself store elapsed
   cycle count.
2. Fix the record rule, including which native C events are read and how absent
   or repeated records are handled. Retain every raw event. Do not change the
   rule separately for each trajectory or discard inconvenient no-click runs.
3. Establish signal speeds, lengths, round-trip timing and clock comparison
   procedures in a baseline preparation before introducing a candidate source.
   Calibrate motion and direction dependence separately. One edge per board
   update is a causal bound, not an operational measurement of physical c.
4. Identify a reproducible candidate source and an independently measured
   strength parameter. `sum(a*a+b*b)`, the signed account Q and sparse-cell count
   are not established mass or energy. Do not define source mass from the very
   deflection curve that the same fit then purports to predict.
5. Preserve the source, clocks, emitters, receivers and their disturbances in
   the full trajectory. A prescribed external metric, force, clock correction,
   site-dependent runtime coupling or selected path would add the phenomenon
   instead of deriving it.

The earlier prepared-clock study established a period-four orbit. Its
signal-written phase result changed the phase while retaining the later rate.
A lasting phase offset is not a gravitational frequency shift. A scheduled
pause/restart supplied by trains is not gravitational time dilation. The
periodic-return study deliberately uses wraparound; it cannot supply a
boundary-independent gravitational range or lensing law.

## Separate clock shifts, forces and effective geometry

For weak stationary fields near Earth's surface, the clock comparison tested
in [2] uses `delta_f/f = g*delta_h/c^2`; the same paper separately compares the
small-velocity shift `delta_f/f ~= -<v^2>/(2*c^2)`. These are reference physical
relations, not new Universe24 update instructions. A model comparison would
need its own independently calibrated units and domain before testing either
functional dependence.

A changed local oscillation rate could arise from a particular environmental
interaction. A bent guided carrier could arise from ordinary medium scattering.
Neither alone establishes gravity. First report the actual phenomenon and its
dependence on channel, amplitude, phase, wavelength proxy and apparatus type.

An effective geometric interpretation is a stronger, prospective inference:
infer one common geometry from a limited training family, then predict new
clock ratios, signal directions/delays and freely moving object paths without
refitting each species or trajectory. State the geometry's gauge/convention,
scale, fit parameters and maximum residuals. A tensor-valued representation
does not provide this cross-prediction by itself.

Nearby freely moving object trajectories also offer a differential test:
record whether their separation changes in a source-dependent way after
controlling common motion and calibration. A coordinate transformation or
acceleration of the observation frame can alter coordinate accelerations;
one cannot label that a measured tidal interaction without comparing actual
local records and object identities. A packet that loses its full template
has lost the current identity test, rather than proved accelerated motion.

## Proposed gates for a later preregistered experiment

| Gate | Fixed observation and controls | What a pass would support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G0: functioning measurement apparatus | Source-free operation, moving/stationary calibration, fixed clock ticks and receiver latency, repeated direction/size checks. | An operational measurement system, not gravity. |
| G1: source-induced response | Matched source-present/absent preparations; actual complete signal and object identities; record changes versus distance and source preparation. | A finite source-dependent interaction. |
| G2: sustained clock-rate response | A predeclared long comparison window, repeated source strengths/separations, comparison of distinct clock families and motional controls. | A calibrated clock-rate law in its tested domain. |
| G3: universality | Independently functional object families with different internal states; fixed initial kinematics; one response law with a preregistered error bound. | An equivalence-principle candidate over those families. |
| G4: common predictions | Fit a limited clock/object subset; predict held-out signal deflection, delays and object trajectories using the same parameters. | A candidate effective geometry, still requiring broader tests. |
| G5: range and dynamics | Several separations and increasing board sizes; source changes encoded initially and propagated causally; pre-wrap comparisons when claiming open-space behavior. | A finite range/dynamical law, not infinite-range behavior proved from one cube. |

Clock and receiver definitions, fit domain, training/validation split and error
bounds must be frozen before the validation trajectories. Candidate initial
data can be searched externally; after startup only Rule3 acts. Controls may
vary complete initial preparations, but must explicitly document every changed
counter and remainder, including source halos.

Useful refutations include species-dependent acceleration, rate shifts that
vanish after latency correction, one successful direction only, failure on new
source strengths, an effect caused only by periodic returns, or incompatible
parameters needed for clocks and deflection. Preserve these outcomes instead
of introducing separate fitted "gravity" rules for each component.

## Current decision

Current clock/gate/recurrence studies do not meet G0 for a general moving
measurement apparatus and establish none of G2–G5 as gravity. The immediate
use of this target sheet is to prevent premature naming and to specify what
later internal clocks, mobile structures and persistent records must enable.
A successful local history-dependent gate remains valuable toward measurement
machinery without being a gravitational result.
