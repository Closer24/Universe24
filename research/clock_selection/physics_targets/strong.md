# Strong interaction: operational targets, not an identification

Status: source-backed physics comparison and proposed research gates, dated
2026-11-08. No strong-interaction experiment has been performed in Universe24.
This note changes no dynamics, tensor, preparation or accepted observation.

## What established physics asks us to reproduce

QCD describes strong interactions using quark and gluon fields with SU(3)
color symmetry. Hadrons are color-neutral combinations; isolated quarks and
gluons are not observed as free particles. At large momentum transfer the
effective coupling decreases. These are several constraints, not a single
statement that nearby objects attract. The eight allowed Universe24 sign
triples do not provide the eight gluon fields of QCD: the mathematical objects,
their transformations and their measured roles differ. [1]

An example of quantitative experimental evidence is the CMS extraction of
the coupling from inclusive jet cross sections, combined with HERA scattering
data. Its published analysis tests scale dependence using jet momenta over
100–1600 GeV, with correlated uncertainties and QCD predictions. A proposed
board counterpart would need detector records, calibrated scales and many
cross-section measurements, rather than one visually similar outgoing spray.
The experimental result does not validate a deterministic board model. [2]

Confinement must be distinguished from an indefinitely increasing mechanical
force. In a confining potential, an approximately linear energy cost over a
range of separation corresponds to approximately constant tension. With
dynamical quarks, pair production and screening can change the relevant
states. A lattice calculation by Bulava and colleagues resolves energy levels
through light- and strange-quark string-breaking thresholds. It is a QCD
computation, not a direct observation of a literal string in a detector. [3]

Residual nuclear binding is a further scale and observable problem. It acts
between composite nucleons; it is not the same question as keeping colored
constituents confined inside a hadron. Jefferson Lab's deuteron measurements
use elastic scattering and polarization observables to constrain charge,
magnetic and quadrupole distributions. Coincidence measurements of knocked-out
nucleons separately constrain nuclear correlations. A bound pair in a board
must not be labeled a deuteron merely because it contains two objects. [4,5]

Lattice QCD already discretizes spacetime while preserving a specified gauge
theory. Its link variables, quantum path integral and controlled continuum
extrapolation are substantive ingredients. Sharing a cubic lattice does not
make Universe24 a lattice-QCD implementation or establish a common limit. [6]

## Proposed board-to-record tests

These are proposed future protocols. None is an accepted strong-force result.
Every run starts from a complete initial state. Prepared sources are installed
before startup; any later probes or messages must arise from ordinary Rule3
without external injection. A scoring function may analyze
records but cannot bind, remove or select an object during evolution.

| Gate | Fixed operational question | Necessary controls and rejection criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Persistent composite | Do at least two independently identifiable constituent patterns remain within a declared separation range while preserving a specified response to a probe? | Compare isolated constituents, joint preparation, phase changes and localized perturbations. Reject static placement alone, indistinguishable overlapping footprints, observer relabeling and confinement supplied only by periodic wraparound. |
| Interaction and separation | Do prepared encounters change relative motion or break up/recombine the composite in a reproducible way? | Use matched source-off trials, several separations and incident directions. Measure all outgoing records and full-state reversibility. A frozen pair with no mobile constituents is not a measured force law. |
| Internal state or charge | Can an intrinsic, reproducible state label predict interaction outcomes across new collision partners? | Fix the label and probe before outcomes. Require transformation and composition rules with independent evidence. Channel names, a spinor representation and a sign choice are not color charge. |
| Separation cost | Does increasing separation require a reproducible increase in a calibrated resource, followed by a defined breakup channel? | An initially prepared packet count can be a resource proxy, explicitly not energy. Physical tension requires a separately justified energy observable and balance law. Never identify counter norm or the algebraic account Q with energy by naming it so. |
| Effective law | Can one interaction law predict held-out separations, phases, speeds and board sizes within a registered error? | Hold the tensor and observation map fixed. Do not fit a new coupling or clock calibration to each case. Scale-dependent effective behavior must emerge from the same dynamics. |
| Spectrum and recorded scattering | Can the model predict calibrated resonance locations, stable-state ratios and multi-detector event distributions? | Register detector efficiencies, no-click outcomes, trial boundaries, uncertainty and independent test families. A period-four clock frequency is not a hadron mass, and raw arithmetic C is not already a calibrated detector response. |

The first executable gate should remain modest: select one candidate composite
and two documented constituents; preregister relative-position, identity and
probe-response windows; test separate and joint preparations at multiple
phases and on two cubes whose active causal regions do not wrap. Stop and
report if mobile constituents or an unambiguous identity test do not yet exist.
Only a passing, independently replayed component warrants a later separation
study. This plan does not authorize additional computation in the current
clock-selection study.

## Present gap and decision

Universe24's prepared clocks and guided carriers are useful research apparatus.
Neither their existence nor a history-dependent probe output derives SU(3)
color dynamics, confinement, asymptotic freedom, hadron spectra or residual
nuclear binding. There is also no established physical energy, scattering
normalization or photon-detector mapping in these studies. Every one of those
connections needs a separate derivation and measurable predictions.

A future stable composite would therefore be promoted only as a bounded
mathematical component. The strong-interaction hypothesis stays open until a
family of operational records distinguishes it from generic reversible
binding, lattice artifacts and engineered preparation.
