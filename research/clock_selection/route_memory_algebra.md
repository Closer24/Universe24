# Prepared competing routes and a proposed phase-memory interface

Status: prior evidence inspection, exact initial compatibility argument and
prospective interface only. No new joined trajectory is claimed here.

The existing [path-competition report](../../docs/PATH_SELECTION_BLOCK.md)
already establishes a finite OR/NO prepared competition block. Its separate
200-case confirmation includes lengths8/10, both spatial swaps and isolated
controls. For the registered positive source, OR transmits and NO remains
quiet at its outlet. This must not be described as absence of a demonstrated
route winner. The unestablished stronger properties are autonomous selection
based on a measured desirable property, an input-controlled winner flip,
internally computed uniqueness approval and a retained single-click record.
The registered higher-C_pair preference had ten eligible counterexamples.

The later16-case joined Receiver study retains the successful first response
and its failure: the winner has19 C-bearing updates12,14,...,48 containing29
individual channel events, and the initial

Inspection of the actual saved winning receiver trace shows old-state primary
inputs at t11,13,15,17,19 of `1,-2,2,4,-10`, respectively. The companion and
other inputs are retained in the original record. Therefore this waveform is
neither a single ideal carrier nor a declared sequence of identical unit
pulses. Feeding a hand-extracted list into a new clock at runtime would be an
inadmissible replacement of the board dynamics; it is not proposed here.

## Exact compatible initial placement

Retain an ordinary length8 PairedArm outlet with channel remainders
`(r0,r14)=(4,0)` and zero counters. Place a unit ShieldedOrbit centered at
channel2 one site in +Y above that outlet. Use base counters(a,b)=(0,1),
center remainder0, and all six base halo remainders2. Choose complete initial
phase1 or2. For both phases the halo prefix is1, so all six orbit-recipient
remainders equal4. In particular the −Y recipient is channel0 at the arm
outlet, exactly matching its required remainder4.

In the orbit's complete336-triple patch, set the untouched companion channel14
at that same contact node to `(0,0,0)` to match the ordinary arm companion.
This is a fixed initial assignment to a different, zero-counter channel, not
a second update rule. The core channel2 does not drive that companion address.
The full patch overlaps the arm at only this one spatial node. Every shared
triple then agrees, so an immutable composer can accept the joint preparation
without overwriting or summing full states.

Phases1 and2 have identical initial remainders throughout the complete patch
and different center counter pairs `(1,0)` and `(0,-1)`. Counter-off clock
ablations therefore use the same full initial remainders for both phase labels.
The two endpoint clock patches are disjoint. In a source-absent apparatus,
the isolated quarter-turn proof applies: only clock centers have counters,
their recipient counters remain zero, and the other prepared guide remainders
are stationary. Incoming route activity invalidates that isolated assumption
and must be measured in the complete joined run.

The earlier clean-carrier phase-writing theorem is not a behavioral contract
for this new interface. The contact is an O arm, the waveform is different,
and the clock can send feedback into the route. Initial compatibility proves
that this is a legal preparation, not that it writes a persistent memory.

## The quiet-memory obstruction and the useful next test

For the existing unit quarter-turn clock with a constant center remainder,
preserving its exact counter orbit requires center ell0. The remainder
identity then gives `r_next-r=2*u`, so a constant center remainder also requires
u0 at every such update. The stronger registered quiet-region criterion
additionally requires every individual external counter term to be zero;
cancellation of nonzero terms is insufficient.

Consequently an unchanged route that keeps driving the clock cannot satisfy
that same quiet-memory contract. The old trace establishes persistent drive
only over its measured window and only in its old receiver geometry. A new
clock can alter the waveform through allowed feedback, so this is not a
general impossibility proof for joined memory or for a different internally
driven recurrent component.

The exact passive-halo condition permits more than unit clocks. For a single
center with counters(a,b) following the zero-quotient quarter-turn, its emitted
b values are `b,-a,-b,a`. Accumulated prefixes are
`0,b,b-a,-a,0`. The four distinct values form the sum of the two sets `{0,b}`
and `{0,-a}`, so their maximum-minus-minimum is exactly `|a|+|b|`.
A recipient of weight±1 therefore has a remainder span `2*(|a|+|b|)`.
To keep that recipient's counters and quotient zero with r in0..5 requires
the integer bound `|a|+|b|<=2`.

Conversely, for a specified signed prefix list P, a compatible passive
remainder exists whenever this bound holds: choose an integer r satisfying
`-2*min(P)<=r<=5-2*max(P)`. This can be done independently at each of the six
distinct recipient addresses. Nonzero permitted counter families include the
unit axes, amplitude2 axes, and `(a,b)=(+/-1,+/-1)`. The condition is about a
single center with no other input and its correctly matched complete halo;
an arbitrary existing halo is not certified solely by the center amplitude.

A secondary activation-from-rest test should therefore distinguish unit
activation from any nonzero quiet activation with `0<|a|+|b|<=2`. Its matched
clock-counter-off/source-off control must remain at rest. Neither secondary
test changes the primary unit-phase-writing acceptance condition.

There is an exact native click consequence of activation from rest. At the
first update making an initially zero-counter center nonzero, the old pair is
still(a,b)=(0,0), so the new pair is `(0,ell)` with ell nonzero. The guarded C
condition therefore necessarily holds at that update. Its incoming u is also
nonzero, because valid r with u0 gives ell0. Record that first event and all
six actual donor terms. A matched entire board with every counter zero is
exactly stationary for any valid fixed remainders, so source-on activation
against a source-off/clock-off null is a genuine source-derived state change.
It does not yet prove that the resulting clock persists or can be composed.

A prospective bounded test can combine the two mixed swaps, four isolated
pattern/direction arrangements, source-present/absent controls, phases1/2 and
two new sizes. That gives48 primary preparations. Eight mixed clock-counter-off
ablations and eight bare mixed-route controls bring the total to64. The current
bounded proposal uses H80 with a fixed hold at states64..80; its final sizes
must retain a strict initial causal margin. Freeze the complete
clock region, input terms, route-output comparisons and a late hold window
before capture. Preserve all full states, raw C and inverses.

Success would require a changed clock phase relative to its matched source-off
control, a unit center, zero other regional counters, exact full-region
period4 and individual-term isolation throughout that hold, while the matched
joined route controls still support the declared comparison. A retained local
phase record is the permitted result; a later readout probe or internal approval
mechanism remains a separate composition problem. Failure should identify
invalid route input, continuous drive, lost clock identity or failed retention
separately, rather than redefining a series of clicks as one event.
