# Proposal: formation from two initially complete clocks

This is the proposal written before execution. The subsequently authorized [results](structure_formation_results.md) preserve the same protocol and criteria. The [protocol](structure_formation_protocol_v1.json) fixes 48 preparations, the readouts, and the budget. The [initial assembly preflight](evidence/structure_formation_preflight/initial_assembly.json) checked all 48 immutable native assemblies without advancing the engine.

## The question

Can two initially separate, individually valid clocks create an active bridge and leave a finite recurrent stationary composite? This differs from the previous study, which began with an already joined period-five composite. A positive here would demonstrate a local assembly process under Rule3. It would not demonstrate attraction between mobile bodies or chemical binding.

At each clock's center, channels 0 and 14 contain `(s,s,0)` and `(-s,-s,4)`. Every other triple in its complete seven-node neighborhood starts at `(0,0,2)`. The first sign is +1; the second is +1 or -1. The centers lie two, three or four sites apart along X. Their explicit complete templates compose without conflicts. At distance two they share one background halo node; at larger distances their neighborhoods are disjoint. Distance one is excluded because both isolated full templates would not be valid initially.

The native immutable composer validates the single global tensor, rejects conflicting assignments and aliased sources, and finalizes the initial board once. Four masks retain identical remainders while enabling neither source, the first, the second, or both. No runtime object inserts a force, connection, click, or stopping decision into Rule3.

## Exact algebra guiding the proposal

For one uncoupled center, zero incoming neighbor counters give the four-step channel-0 cycle

`(1,1,0) -> (1,-1,0) -> (-1,-1,0) -> (-1,1,0) -> (1,1,0)`.

Channel 14 has opposite counters and remainder four. The recipients of channel 0 have zero counters and remainders `2,4,2,0`; channel-14 recipients have complementary remainders `2,0,2,4`. These are full prepared phases, including every halo triple.

The only prospective nonlinear interaction in this family is distance two with equal signs. At the shared middle node, the first synchronous update receives `u=2` in channel 0. Rule3 gives

`divmod(2*u + r, 6) = divmod(6,6) = (1,0)`,

so the initially quiet middle becomes `(0,1,0)`. Its paired channel becomes `(0,-1,4)`. Both singleton controls keep the corresponding bridge counters zero. This predicts immediate bridge activation; it does not predict that the activity settles into a recurrent object.

| Distance and relative phase | Pre-execution prediction | Status to test separately |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2, equal signs | Counter-active middle at update 1 | Late full-region recurrence and isolation are unknown |
| 2, opposite signs | Shared inputs cancel; global period 4; no counter bridge | Shared halo differs from each isolated template, so report individual template loss separately |
| 3 or 4, either sign | Two independent complete period-4 clocks, no counter bridge | Exact noninteracting controls conditional on the fixed ports |

The existing historical 40-step study already recorded interaction at distance two and no full-state return through twelve steps. It recorded no core interaction at distances three and six. The proposed experiment is therefore a longer, controlled test of a new formation criterion, not a rediscovery of those earlier finite observations.

## What would count as formation

The fixed target contains all 48 channels on every node within one spatial step of the entire connecting segment: 17, 22 or 27 nodes. It includes the transverse halo of any intermediate bridge. Between states 64 and 96, the whole target must repeat with a registered period from 1 through 16, and every individual external incoming counter term must be zero. The bridge must recur with nonzero counters, remain absent in both singletons and the empty control, and affect counter trajectories at both original centers relative to their matched singleton trajectories.

The singleton controls must preserve their complete isolated clock templates throughout all 96 steps. An initial-state conflict or failed singleton makes the associated formation comparison ineligible. A lost constituent template is recorded explicitly and never replaced with a centroid or relabeled as motion. Radiation and all remainders outside the target remain in the complete reversible recording.

The run budget is 300 CPU seconds, 600 wall seconds and 64 MiB of recorded evidence, with per-state and cumulative native row limits separately specified. Budget stops are inconclusive and retain complete prior records. Every C class is kept as a passive event; no event class is called selection or binding by definition. Independent replay is required before publishing a formation claim.

At proposal time, the next decision was whether to authorize this frozen family after algebra, physics and implementation review. The decisive unknown was late recurrent localized organization in the distance-two, equal-phase case. The negative controls remain useful even when that candidate fails.
