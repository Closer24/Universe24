# Prepared routes activate resting centers but do not leave quiet clock memory

All 64 registered runs completed: 5,184 complete states, 5,120 transitions and 64 full rewinds. The bare route discrimination and every required incoming-interface comparison pass. Nevertheless, **none of the 24 active-clock comparisons writes the registered quiet phase memory, and none of the four resting-apparatus comparisons leaves a retained quiet clock**. Incoming signals do activate previously resting centers; late influence also reaches the nominal losing side. [Independent replay](evidence/independent_audit/route_clock_v1.json) confirms every native transition, inverse, raw C event and six-term input, complete region trace and registered assessment.

## What the experiment connects

An unchanged central source feeds length-eight OR/NO arms. The patterns are fixed initial remainders, not runtime route switches. Each included outlet contacts a complete 336-triple local clock patch one site above it. A single explicitly prepared contact companion remainder makes the full patches compatible with the ordinary arm outlet. Native immutable composition rejects conflicting assignments; it does not add or overwrite complete states.

The primary family uses two complete clock phases, both mixed spatial swaps, all four isolated pattern/direction arrangements, source-on/off controls and two cube sizes (289 and 321). Eight additional mixed clock-counter-off runs keep the complete remainder apparatus but begin with resting centers. Eight bare mixed-route runs supply fresh apparatus controls. The horizon is 80 updates and the fixed quiet-memory hold is states 64 through 80.

## What is observed

The winning route first produces an upstream counter at state ten. Its first source-caused contact input and center-triple departure occur at update thirteen. For every resting-apparatus winner, the first center counter activation occurs at update fifteen:

`(0,0,2) -> (0,-1,4)`, with input `u=-2`, quotient `ell=-1`, and a genuine native **C1** event.

This is a reproducible source-caused transition from a resting center to counter activity. It is not the registered quiet clock. In all driven winners, every quiet-hold predicate fails: the full region does not repeat after four updates, activity is not confined to the designated center, the center does not maintain a constant remainder and exact quarter-turn, its input/quotient are not always zero, and external individual inputs remain present.

Late influence also reaches the nominal losing side. In active phase-one preparations, the losing center can retain its original unit orbit while its surrounding region acquires activity and fails isolation. A center-only readout would therefore conceal the composition failure. In active phase-two preparations, losing centers first depart from their null trajectories at state 56. In the resting-apparatus runs, they first change remainder at state 57 and first activate at update 59, with the same `(0,0,2)->(0,-1,4)` transition and C1 event. All these statements hold in both spatial swaps and both tested sizes.

The original bare-route result remains valid over its registered window. What fails here is the stronger joined interface and late retained-memory contract. A valid incoming route does not guarantee that its ongoing output becomes a quiet memory. The observed early winner cannot be relabeled as permanent exclusive recording after the losing apparatus later becomes active.

## Definitions were kept fixed

The primary memory requires a unit center with remainder zero, an exact four-step recurrence of every triple in the full 336-triple region, no other regional counters, zero center input and quotient, and zero individual external incoming terms throughout the hold. The recorded phase must differ from its matched source-off phase at every hold state; the losing region must match its null.

The separately registered activation test allows constant center remainder 0, 2 or 4 and nonzero counter amplitude `|a|+|b|<=2`. Unit, axis-two and diagonal amplitudes are distinct reported classes. It retains the same full-region recurrence and isolation requirements. This broader prospective criterion also fails; no post-analysis threshold or hold-window change was used.

Every raw C0/C1/C2 event, quotient, address and six input terms is retained. A first guarded C event records the actual arithmetic transition. No event class is an approval instruction, an internal uniqueness test, or evidence for physical light or quantum collapse. The full reversible information outside the clock regions remains in every saved state.
