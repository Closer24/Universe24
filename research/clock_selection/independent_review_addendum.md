## What is established

The equal-channel +++ sector has the declared cosine dispersion and an explicit finite-time rounding-error bound. The independent internal-channel matrix calculation supplies exact integer eigenvectors with eigenvalue2 at zero spatial wave vector. The finite-H construction realizes that internal recurrence using initial amplitudes growing as 3^H. These are distinct from the unimplemented fixed-ratio candidate, physical mass, or a localized particle. The [coherent-wave algebra](coherent_wave_algebra.md) also separates its long-wave speed from the one-edge-per-update guided carrier and the board's dependence cone; no physical click metric was inferred.

It supplies no runtime oscillator law, signal injection or proof that an arbitrary joined component remains quiet. Its 384 initial outgoing-map checks and 384 producer orientation comparisons are static; the separate native dynamic family contains 48 cases.

**Finite causal ingredients.** The earlier V2 and crossed-region studies establish a prepared signal writing a local state that changes a later signal's transmission in their registered domains. ## What failed and what remains unestablished

The simultaneous orthogonal study never has exactly one joint registered carrier candidate: phases 0/1/2 have none, and phase 3 has two, including the second size. Thus its zero-exclusivity result is not merely a consequence of a strict click-signature calibration. The bare simultaneous crossing also has an extra C2 at each natural outlet. The frozen producer requires a clean carrier+C calibration, which is stronger than the protocol's brief calibration wording; its result was not relaxed after observation. Both complete carriers and the extra raw events remain visible.

The previous ghost-source preparation failure, structural observer-variable collision and its withdrawn product claim, recording-cap corrections, long-run carrier loss and closed-orbit findings remain negative evidence or documented implementation corrections. None has been converted into preferred-route selection by changing the observer.

No audited study yet supplies the complete autonomous chain of internally generating alternatives, comparing a predeclared property, retaining one result, computing unique approval and renewing the next trial. No higher-complexity preference, internal quantum collapse, Born probabilities, physical mass/interaction, or click-based time arrow is established. Long execution and repeated visits to a closed orbit do not supply that missing chain.

Omission from a derivative summary is not omission from the native recording or the full certificate. [compact_audit.py](compact_audit.py) makes such summaries without modifying the original.
