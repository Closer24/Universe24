# Source-driven paired clock: registered finite study

## Decision and scope

The registered family supplies a reproducible **signal-dependent phase reconfiguration of a prepared, stationary period-five clock composite**. It does not supply formation of a new composite, two separated clock products, attraction, repulsion, chemical binding, or physical dissociation. Four geometries lose the registered parent identity after interaction; loss of identity is not evidence for identifiable products.

The authoritative scientific assessment is [the corrected passive analysis](evidence/structure_v1_analysis/result.json). [Independent verification](evidence/structure_v1_analysis/independent_verification.json) links it to a separate replay of all 36 native captures, 1,764 complete states, 1,728 transitions, and 36 full rewinds. All eight corrected group assessments agree. The old capture result is retained to expose an observer error and must not be used for product claims.

## Preserved failures and corrections

1. The first capture attempt completed 20 of 36 cases, then hit a serialization budget because the native `max_cells` limit counts cumulative rows while the producer supplied a per-state cap. The original source, records, failure receipt, and exact correction remain under `evidence/structure_v1/`. The corrected capture uses a cumulative limit of 980,000 rows plus an explicit per-state 20,000-row check. All 980 complete states in the 20 overlapping records are exactly equal. No preparation or physical acceptance criterion changed.
2. An observer variable named `pairs` was reused for event-class tables and physical product pairs. This falsely made the product-pair field nonempty and produced four provisional source-induced product positives. The old executed source and faulty assessments remain under `evidence/structure_v1_corrected/`, with a bug receipt and exact patch. Those positives are withdrawn. A separately versioned passive reanalysis changes only the two affected observation fields and downstream assessment; all native record hashes and the Presenter bundle are unchanged. Independent replay confirms **zero** product groups. Regression tests cover both an empty product set with a populated event-class table and a genuine two-clock product fixture.

The complete corrected capture used 41.01 CPU seconds and 41.46 wall seconds. Passive correction used 20.47 CPU seconds; independent replay used 15.59 CPU seconds. The failed initial attempt has no trustworthy measured runtime, which is recorded as unknown. These corrections establish the integrity of the finite result, not a theorem about all preparations.
