# Interacting structures, motion and binding: mathematical scope

This additive note proposes a bounded follow-up. It changes no frozen run,
engine, observer or coupling. No new trajectories were computed for this note.

## Existing evidence inspected

`research/building_blocks/structure_protocol_v2.json` and its producer test
prepared one-site and adjacent two-site paired-channel motifs. The stored
`structure_results_v2.json` contains 128 cases, of which 72 meet its finite
radius-one recurrence predicate. These counts describe the existing artifact;
they are not new independently replayed results.

For +++ coupling, the recorded `(a,b)=(1,1)` single motif has period four,
while two identical adjacent motifs have period five. Their counter activity
remains at the initial motif sites; the surrounding halo changes remainders.
This is an interacting stationary oscillator cluster. It supplies no measured
approach, separation, source-created attachment or physical binding energy.

The old study's different initial counter pairs are not necessarily complete
phase shifts of one orbit. For example, its +++ `(1,-1)` isolated preparation
with the same background halo has period six and radius two. A genuine
prepared phase shift must include its changed halo remainders. Never infer
phase covariance by rotating only the two center counters.

channel0 is `(A,B,0)` and channel14 is `(-A,-B,4)`. Start with the canonical

A prospective producer should choose exact sizes, horizon, amplitude limits,
recurrence window, support envelope and budget before execution. This cheap
family can test interaction and stationary composite behavior first. It should
report attraction or repulsion as unestablished unless independently defined
moving structure identities are actually found. A failed template match is
loss of that identity, not permission to relocate a structure retrospectively.

For a genuine complete-phase construction, the zero-quotient halo formula is

```text
r_i(p) = r_i(0) + 2 * sum_j w_ij * sum_(s=0..p-1) b_j(s).
```

It applies only while those halo quotients remain zero. Joint preparations
must solve shared-address requirements once, check `0<=r<6`, and reject
inconsistent core assignments. They may not overwrite a conflicting value
or add complete evolving states. Even compatible initial assignments do not
prove that both components remain functional after joining. Singleton controls
must use the joint remainder preparation and be tested themselves.

## Distinguish motion from a changing display

Before a run, define a structure by its complete counter/remainder template,
allowed internal phases and surrounding interface. A native position `x_i(t)`
exists only when that predicate identifies the structure at a board location.
Track it consistently across successive native states. Mark ambiguity or loss
of identity explicitly. A moving camera or selected window does not move it.

For two identifiable structures, let `r(t)=x_B(t)-x_A(t)` using a registered
nonwrapping coordinate lift. Compare the joint trajectory with both matched
singleton trajectories. Two initially approaching carriers can decrease their
separation without any interaction; the relevant signal is a departure from
the matched free motion.

An attraction-like displacement requires the joint separation to decrease
relative to the singleton prediction over a predeclared window while both
identities survive. Repulsion-like displacement is the converse. If estimating
relative acceleration, use the native finite difference

```text
Delta2 r(t) = r(t+1)-2*r(t)+r(t-1),
delta_a(t) = Delta2 r_joint(t)-Delta2 r_free(t).
```

A negative radial projection `delta_a(t).r_joint(t)` is an attraction-like
kinematic diagnostic, and a positive one repulsion-like, only when the
tracking, time window and ambiguity rules were fixed in advance. A physical
force law would additionally need a reproducible family and bounded model
error; one collision or clock phase change is insufficient.

The centroid weighted by `a*a+b*b` is not a conserved-mass centroid. With
`ell` the actual local quotient, Rule3 gives the exact identity

```text
(a_next*a_next+b_next*b_next)-(a*a+b*b) = ell*ell-2*a*ell.
```

Activity can disappear from one stationary site and increase at another,
moving that centroid without translating either structure. Keep such a
centroid descriptive; do not relabel it force, acceleration or matter motion.

## Separate four binding-related outcomes

1. **Prepared recurrent composite:** a complete localized patch, including
   all retained halos, repeats over a registered finite horizon. Joint local
   behavior differs from the matched singleton behavior. This may be a
   stationary interacting cluster with fixed separation.
2. **Formation:** initially distinct identified structures interact and create
   that localized composite, with matched controls lacking the same outcome.
   Preparing neighboring clocks already joined does not establish formation.
3. **Disruption:** a prepared incoming signal breaks the registered recurrence
   or localization. This alone does not establish dissociation.
4. **Dissociation:** the initially verified composite releases independently
   identified outgoing structures whose separation grows under the registered
   tracking rule, while the matched absent-trigger control remains composite.

Finite recurrence and localization are scoped observations. They do not
establish stability against arbitrary perturbations or infinite persistence.
Phase synchrony alone establishes none of these spatial binding outcomes.

## Conservation and information constraints

The implemented algebraic account is

```text
q_i = 6*(b_i-a_i)+r_i-r_background,
q_i(next)-q_i = 2*u_i-12*b_i.
```

Summing over the periodic board gives

```text
Q_next-Q = 2*sum_j (sum_(six outgoing edges) w_ij - 6)*b_j.
```

Thus Q is conserved for +++ coupling. It need not be conserved for arbitrary
signed tensors. Q is signed and is not established as physical energy, mass,
binding energy or a positive stability functional. Counter norm and observed
click counts are likewise not automatically conserved.

Invertibility forbids distinct full states from merging into one winner or
entering an already closed full-board periodic orbit. A locally settled
composite can coexist with outgoing information. Retain the complete state,
all emitted disturbances and exact inverse checks when calling a local pattern
bound or selected. Do not erase the radiated information or interpret missing
template activity as destruction of the full state.

All selection/complexity scores remain passive measurements. A stationary
cluster with a longer period demonstrates a changed temporal organization;
it does not by itself show that the board prefers a more complex route.
