# Research decisions, hypotheses and evidence boundaries

This ledger records the current questions without converting an objective into
a result. The active computational candidate is the baseline: one board,
48 channels per node, six spatial neighbors, a tensor fixed before the run,
and synchronous Rule3 with `num=den=1`. All source preparations are initial
states. Experiments, analysis and the Presenter cannot inject approval, erase
an alternative or change a coefficient during a trajectory.

“Verified” below means the named finite records or mathematical argument were
checked in their declared domain. It never means physical validation of the
Universe24 hypothesis. Domain notes contain sourced physical requirements;
their proposed experiments are not recorded as completed.

Evidence status:2026-11-08. All15 completed native study families are audited;
future targets and unimplemented candidates are separated below. No image or
animation determines a scientific status.
