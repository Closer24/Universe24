# From reversible ingredients to internal choice

This is a research synthesis and decision plan, not a claim that Universe24
already performs autonomous search or evolution. The evidence below belongs
to the unchanged one-board,48-channel,six-neighbor baseline unless explicitly
marked as a separate candidate. Observation, optimization of initial conditions
and the Presenter never update the evolving board.

## The missing causal chain

The user's proposed “try, find, click, try again” mechanism can be made precise
without supplying those operations as extra rules. It requires all of the
following within the native state:

1. **Internal alternatives:** identifiable candidate signals or configurations
   are generated after initialization. A finite train placed in the seed is
   autonomous execution of a prepared sequence, not renewable generation.
2. **A local discriminating interaction:** different candidate states produce
   different outgoing behavior under fixed couplings and a fixed observer.
   The audited phase-mediated gate supplies a finite example of this ingredient.
3. **Retention:** a changed internal record persists after the encounter and
   causally affects a later encounter. A click label alone is not this record.
4. **Differential propagation:** the competing candidates are each functional
   alone, yet one has a different subsequent fate when both are present. All
   rejected information remains recoverable in the complete reversible state.
5. **Feedback and renewal:** the retained result changes which next candidate
   is generated or how it is tested. The update law continues through the same
   Rule3; neither an observer nor an optimizer issues a continue/accept command.

These requirements permit a finite internally adaptive circuit if realized.
An evolutionary interpretation additionally needs defined persistent or
reproducing units, internally generated heritable variation and a measured
difference in persistence or reproduction. No current run establishes that
stronger chain. Large activity, long duration and periodicity do not substitute
for it.

The separate [Bell and operational-time gates](quantum_time_gates.md) derive
the conditional CHSH bound, including settings, missing outcomes and observed
locality. They also separate hidden board updates, accumulated analysis counts
and native persistent clock records. Neither the choice mechanisms above nor
many board ticks between visible clicks establish entanglement, collapse,
zero physical elapsed time or a physical time arrow.

The registered cooperation proxy K in [physics.md](physics.md) is the minimum
number of source groups sufficient for one fixed output. The demonstrated
distance2 bridge has a two-source activation requirement in its declared
four-mask comparison. That is a useful interaction fact, not a preference for
K=2 over K=1. Such a preference needs two independently functional alternatives,
matched timing/amplitude/path conditions, and reversal of which physical route
carries the larger K. K is not entropy, algorithmic complexity, geometric size
or a fitness command. A global reversible trajectory cannot merge different
full initial states into one full final state; an apparently unique local
outcome must leave their distinction elsewhere.

## Closely related algorithms and reusable principles

These are concrete precedents, not implementations in Universe24. The
references below were checked2026-11-08. The comparison in the final column is
our assessment of the baseline contract.

| Precedent | Reusable principle | What must not be imported as an extra rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Toffoli–Margolus invertible cellular automata and lattice-gas constructions [1] | Test complete local interactions and composition, retain all outgoing information, and derive conservation and propagation laws from a specified reversible map. The paper also distinguishes invertibility from physical time-reversal symmetry. | Replacing Rule3 by a lattice-gas collision table or an alternating block-update scheme would define another model. Reversibility alone supplies no universal computer or physical continuum law. |
| Fredkin–Toffoli conservative logic and billiard-ball computation [2] | A conditional routing event can be implemented by a reversible collision while preserving control information. Composition requires timing, crossover and clearance, not only an isolated truth table. | Their balls, fixed mirrors and collision rules are not native Universe24 objects. Every analogous guide, reflector, control and timing resource must be realized by counters and remainders under the existing coupling. |
| Bennett's reversible computation [3] | Retaining intermediate history makes a computation invertible; an output can be preserved while a reversible construction uncomputes temporary history. This explains how a locally selected output can coexist with complete information retention. | Calling the engine's inverse from an external controller, deleting unwanted paths, or supplying an extra history tape is not an autonomous Rule3 mechanism. Any storage, copy into a prepared register and return signal must be an internal construction. |
| Dijkstra–Scholten termination detection for diffusing computations [4] | An outward branching computation can require returning acknowledgments before declaring completion. Local counts and pending obligations distinguish “one response arrived” from “all work has returned.” | Their algorithm has explicit messages, counters and signaling rules, and is not itself a reversible physical model. Absorption, acknowledgment emission and stack/cornet updates cannot be Python actions on the board. They must be implemented, including retained history and resource limits. |

The last precedent most closely resembles the proposed approval metaphor.
Its completion signal means that a defined distributed computation has
terminated. It does not mean that the most complex route wins, that a physical
light quantum collapsed, or that information disappeared. If used as a design
specification, each pending obligation and returned acknowledgment needs a
native state encoding and a tested local transition. Finite branching also
needs enough initially available storage/resources; it is not permission to
silently create another board for every path.

## Composition decision and retained follow-up options

The [completed composition](route_clock_protocol.json) used the already available OR/NO
competition block and a complete phase-memory clock beside each ordinary
outlet. It did not wait for the new orthogonal family to invent another
competition mechanism. The64case family comprised48 primary
source/phase/pattern/swap/size cases,8 mixed clock-counter-off controls and8
bare mixed-route controls. The new interface kept the ordinary outlet
remainders and matched the complete clock halo at the shared node; no state was
overwritten during the run. The final protocol fixed H80, a16-update hold at
states64..80 and a300CPU-second cap. Its executed SHA-256 is
`d0c2883260705631121be1efcd3111581d3db349ceac3d4e8f4bf02718d8a95b`.
All64 cases and inverses were independently verified. The complete workflow
used173.33CPU seconds within the cap.

Its question was whether actual route output writes a full local phase record
that remains isolated for at least16updates. All singleton pattern/direction
cases, both mixed swaps, source-off controls and new sizes are required for
any accepted phase preparation. Input validity uses the actual route waveform
and a predeclared upstream observation, not an assumed ideal carrier. The
clock-counter-off subset separately tests the registered activation classes
from a resting prepared apparatus. Neither a successful
phase write nor a new clock alone establishes a later nondestructive read,
internal acknowledgment or adaptive search. This tested native composition is
distinct from the abstract algorithms above; its quiet-memory criterion failed.

The [targeted32-case composition](carrier_memory_results.md) is now complete:
the [independent audit](evidence/independent_audit/carrier_memory_v1.json)
verified3,872 complete states,3,840 transitions and32full rewinds. It produced
**zero of8 strong terminal records** under the frozen H120 protocol and
full336-triple hold96..112. Both clocks start active. For receiver phase0 on
both sizes, the clean early writer sets effective phase2 and the later clean
conditional carrier restores phase0. This distinguishes joint input11 from
writer-only10, but all336 receiver triples equal null00 and probe-only01 at
every terminal-hold state. All32 terminal receivers are quiet; quietness alone
does not encode the requested distinct outcome. Phases1–3 also fail the joint
late packet/C contract, with every raw event retained.

The phase-0 encounter supplies a bounded, native pause/resume illustration:
update74 changes the center to(0,0,4) with C2, update75 leaves it there, and
update76 restarts it at(0,1,0) with C1. Those are ordinary Rule3 updates caused
by the packet's successive inputs. The resulting **weak write/cancel primitive**
does not implement an approval gate, unique detector record, creation of a clock
from rest, zero physical elapsed time or autonomous search. The complete board
remains reversible even though the final local region aliases the null control.
No criterion was relaxed after this negative strong-record result.

The two conditional options below were proposed before the orthogonal result.
They remain unexecuted future design options, distinct from the completed
two-clock composition. No further forward study is implied by this note.
Exact preparations and observation windows require registration before any
future validation.

**If valid simultaneous outlet exclusivity passes: test retained choice reuse.**
The single question is whether the first encounter writes a local record that
controls a later common probe. Use two eligible first-encounter preparations
with different registered winners, the same later initially prepared probe,
and all source-off/first-encounter-off controls. Both winner histories must have
an identified full record region, a declared quiet hold of at least16updates,
and the same valid late incoming carrier. Require the late output to follow
the record in both histories. New offline crossed record/exterior preparations
provide a separate mediation check; they never occur during the original run.
Freeze the record region and output identity before validation, and keep all
radiation. Use two new sizes and cap the family at64runs,H160,600CPU seconds.
Failure includes no second winner, loss of record, ineligible late input or an
output that follows only the exterior. A pass would justify a reusable finite
choice-memory component, not renewable search; the later probe was present
initially. Complexity property-reversal and internal probe generation remain
subsequent requirements.

**If exclusivity fails: test state-dependent turning before adding more trials.**
The single question is whether one valid incoming carrier can be sent straight
or through the orthogonal outlet by two different complete initial states of a
local interface. The failed current family must first identify whether input
delivery, singleton output or competition failed. A new bounded family may add
one complete clock preparation at a distance2 neighbor of the current clock,
using the six axial placements and four registered phases, with the existing
primary phase fixed. These are initial-state changes only. Reject incompatible
complete halos before running; preserve their rejection receipts. Test24
candidate interfaces with four packet/interface masks on one size, then the
same complete family on a new size: at most192runs,H88,600CPU seconds. Require
valid input in all relevant controls, no source-off emission, one exact full
carrier with its fixed C signature at the registered straight or turned outlet,
and retention of all other activity. A pair of interface states must switch
the destination under the same input. A pass supplies a routing primitive;
failure bounds this additional-clock family and calls for algebraic interface
analysis, not a longer run with unchanged acceptance. Neither outcome can be
renamed a complexity preference.
