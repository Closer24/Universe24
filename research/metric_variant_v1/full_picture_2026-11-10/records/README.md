of the metric_variant_v1 programme. The files are kept as historical evidence for the documents in this folder.

- They were sanitized for this repository: English only, local paths shortened to `scratchpad/...`, and owner quotes in
  Hebrew replaced by their English gloss. Paths of the form `s<N>/...` or `scratchpad/...` refer to research working
  records that are not in this repository.
- Earlier records can be superseded by later ones. Where they differ, `../../README_v1_draft.md`, `../README.md` (precedence
  note) and the code govern.
- Labels as in the rest of the package: THEOREM, NUMERIC, DERIVED, CHOSEN, NEW LAW, OPEN, POSTED, TUNED.
