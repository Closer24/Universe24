A15. Verification update — 8 November 2026

This update supplements H1a and A11, which already identify homogeneous effective 1/2 and 2/3 encodings. It adds exact preparations and independently replayed evidence. The fundamental rule remains num=den=1 in every channel: one board, 48 channels per node, six neighbors, synchronous previous-state reads and a tensor fixed before execution. No per-channel coefficient field or runtime intervention is introduced.

Exact invariant families. Let F be the fixed neighbor operator and let a full-address pattern v have entries −1, 0 or +1 with Fv=λv. Using the existing J sign conjugacy, encode a scalar triple (a,b,r) as (a,b,r) at positive entries, (−a,−b,4−r+2(r mod 2)) at negative entries, and (0,0,2) at zero entries. The signed remainder map preserves both parity sectors. The full board then exactly follows ell,r′=divmod(2λb+r,6), a′=b, b′=ell−a. Closure is an all-integer mathematical result; zero-pattern inputs may cancel without their individual donor terms vanishing.

With the unchanged +++ tensor, the uniform pattern has λ=6; the channel-uniform spatial profile (1,−1,0) has λ=3; and both the spatial profile (1,0,−1,0) and the spatially uniform internal pattern v_g=(R(g)e_y)_y have λ=4. The spatial profiles require cube sides divisible by 3 and 4, respectively, so their periodic boundaries preserve the patterns. These give α_eff=λ/6 equal to 1, 1/2 and 2/3. They encode homogeneous scalar recurrences, not arbitrary spatial fields or localized particles. For reduced p/q=λ/6, the generalized remainder correspondence is ρ=qr+j with fixed 0≤j<q. The native six-remainder batch tests j=0; it does not test all 6q generalized states or define a new effective click classifier.

Independent verification. Forty-eight runs cover four families, two counter seeds, all six remainders and 18 updates on compatible periodic cubes. Every complete state, input, raw C event and inverse matched. A separate four-control overlap study used side 4 and 12 updates. Together these studies independently verified 52 recordings, 964 complete states, 912 transitions and 52 full rewinds. Every λ3 case returns after six updates. The λ4 families share scalar traces but have preparation-dependent returns of 6, 8 or 15 updates, or no return within 18. Uniform seeds drift; one universal clock frequency is not established.

Interaction and its limit. Overlapping spatial and internal λ4 patterns with identical initial remainder 2 produces the predicted first-step counter-addition defect: +1 at 128 addresses and −1 at 128, with compensating remainder contrasts. This is native nonlinear interaction. A doubled single-mode amplitude can produce the same local carry, so the result is not complexity preference. Conversely, exact planar, λ3 and λ6 additions with input divisible by three leave an underlying trajectory unchanged after subtracting the added counters; a passed planar pulse cannot leave a residual local record through that mechanism. Transparent additions may nevertheless alter raw C events through the quotient and changed-triple guards. Previously verified guided-packet phase writing is a distinct signal construction.

Exact global sectors have a further limit: their reduced map is bijective, so each is invariant both forward and backward. A full-board state outside such a sector cannot enter it after finitely many updates. Formation targets must therefore concern local, approximate or coarse-grained structures, with distinguishing information retained elsewhere, rather than exact entry of the complete board.

The earlier two-clock composition still has 0/8 strong terminal records: joint-input receivers alias null/probe-only receivers. The next gates are localized moving preparations and a distinct retained native record under complete controls. Mass, autonomous selection and quantum measurement remain unproved.
