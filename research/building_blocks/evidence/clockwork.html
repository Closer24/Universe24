What this replay establishes — and what it does not

This is a read-only replay of finite computational runs. Playback speed and the scrubber change the view; they never evolve or edit the physical candidate. The source, guide, clocks and record are initial counter states advanced by the same engine.

The term “signal” denotes the tested activation pattern. Physical light, autonomous physical time, permanent storage and a quantum detector have not been established. A prepared guide can carry a front while leaving changing state behind; that is not a compact free particle. These clocks are signal-started: after activation they can cycle with net input u = 0, so continuous light-powered operation is not claimed.

The display exposes channels 0 and 14 at selected sites. Provenance and inverse checks refer to the full retained state, including all other channels. A zero displayed memory bit is not erased microscopic information.
