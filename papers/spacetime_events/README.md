The paper arrow.tex is built by build.sh (pdflatex, bibtex, pdflatex, pdflatex with the Springer Nature class in sn/), which then runs tools/run_clocks.py (the two clocks of Section 5 counted on the board with the program's own functions, written to run_clocks.json beside the paper), tools/run_momentum.py (the momentum at the click of Section 4, read as the click runs, run_momentum.json; it loads the run's world, which names the repository's examples/ files, so it runs from a checkout of the whole repository), the ensemble's check (run3d_fixed/ensemble/ensemble_seeds.py --check, the summary of the 1,200 runs over seeds recomputed from the saved rows; the runs themselves, about fifteen minutes, are rerun with the same script without --check, as are the run's own files, the world, the report and the rows of the fixed-board run, under run3d_fixed/) and the eight checks under tools/ in order, stopping at the first failure: check_numbers (every integer against the run's data), check_formulas (every derived number recomputed from the paper's own integers), check_words (the forbidden words, the mandated sentences, the eight assumptions, the Abstract's length), check_notation (every mathematical symbol defined in words before its first use, with one meaning), check_terms (every defined term used no earlier than its definition), check_circularity (results against assumptions), check_refs (the citations and the .bib files, every entry verified against its Crossref or Zenodo record, cached under refs_check/) and check_layout (the body ending by page 14, the figure's labels at 7 points or more, no overfull box over 5 points).

The run's report, run3d_fixed/report_e4.json, holds two no-click fields at the close of tick 48: unabsorbed_over_unit, the draw's rule (one less the shares now, 0.811, the number the paper prints), and no_click_weight_over_unit_as_engine (0.621), the program's own bookkeeping, which the draw did not use; the run's files are kept as the run wrote them.
